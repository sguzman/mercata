# itch.io game-selling research plan

**Research underway; 2026-10-26 source-backed checkpoint.** The existing `itch-io` specimen is already admitted and is **researching**.

**244 research tasks:** 84 original marketplace and game-domain tasks, plus 160 itch.io-specific questions across 13 workstreams and four phases. Research is now **85%** across the fixed 244-task inventory; the original inventory was 0%. No seller account operations or market experiments have been performed.

## Phases

### 📐 Phase 1: Scope the marketplace and measurement rules

Define units, access, charges, and evidence protocols; make no market observations.

- 🪪 Market identity and boundaries: 9 new research questions
- 🚪 Entry and eligibility: 11 new research questions
- 💳 Fees, costs and payouts: 16 new research questions
- 🔬 Verification and synthesis: 8 new research questions

### 🌍 Phase 2: Measure paid-game supply, competition and discovery

Gather bounded game-catalog, release, traffic and competition evidence or document unavailable datasets.

- 🌐 Market size and demand: 13 new research questions
- 🧭 Discovery and acquisition: 13 new research questions
- 🏁 Competition and prices: 12 new research questions
- 🎮 Game publishing: 16 new research questions

### 💸 Phase 3: Measure sales, profit, rights and continuity risks

Establish compatible paid-unit, seller-net and zero-earner measures without treating anecdotes as distributions.

- 📊 Income and outcome distribution: 16 new research questions
- ⚖️ Rights, products and portability: 10 new research questions
- 🛒 Sales, conversion and retention: 12 new research questions
- 🛡️ Risk, policy and continuity: 10 new research questions

### 🧪 Phase 4: Design viable earning experiments

Prepare falsifiable and bounded release strategies; do not conduct experiments without authorization.

- 🧪 Strategies and earning experiments: 14 new research questions
