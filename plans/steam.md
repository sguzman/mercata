# Steam game publishing economics — research inventory

**Research started October 28, 2026.** Initial findings are documented in the [Steamworks entry and payment checkpoint](../ontology/steam/entry-review-and-reporting-2026.md). Steam was already admitted on 2026-10-23. Its [existing 84 baseline tasks](../queues/steam.json) remain unchanged; this plan adds **209 Steam-specific atomic research questions**, for **293 total tasks** across 13 workstreams. **Current progress is 43% task-weighted.** Verified official-document findings advanced selected tasks; many remain unstarted, and no commercial sales have been observed.

[Machine-readable inventory](steam.json) · [Empty Steam dossier](../specimens/steam.json) · [Gallery](../gallery/README.md).

This is a research inventory with an initial source-backed **policy and payment** checkpoint, not a market revenue report. Actual seller earnings, release-cohort sales and buyer populations remain unknown. Steamworks partner access, authenticated seller transactions, developer contact, experiments and actual game publishing are outside this planning action.

## Four research phases

### 📐 Phase 1: Define Steam's commercial rules and measurement units

Specify independently verifiable definitions, contracting access and payment accounting; no market observations.

- 🪪 Market identity and boundaries: 12 dedicated questions plus 5 retained baseline tasks
- 🚪 Entry and eligibility: 14 dedicated questions plus 6 retained baseline tasks
- 💳 Fees, costs and payouts: 18 dedicated questions plus 8 retained baseline tasks
- 🔬 Verification and synthesis: 13 dedicated questions plus 7 retained baseline tasks

### 🌍 Phase 2: Measure supply, competition and discovery

Use dated cohorts and source-quality gates for game supply, visibility, launch formats and competition.

- 🌐 Market size and demand: 17 dedicated questions plus 6 retained baseline tasks
- 🧭 Discovery and acquisition: 18 dedicated questions plus 6 retained baseline tasks
- 🏁 Competition and prices: 16 dedicated questions plus 6 retained baseline tasks
- 🎮 Game publishing: 18 dedicated questions plus 5 retained baseline tasks

### 💸 Phase 3: Measure buyer transactions, seller outcomes and risks

Distinguish transaction records from estimates and measure zero earning, revenue and profit only from qualified cohorts.

- 📊 Income and outcome distribution: 19 dedicated questions plus 8 retained baseline tasks
- ⚖️ Rights, products and portability: 12 dedicated questions plus 6 retained baseline tasks
- 🛒 Sales, conversion and retention: 18 dedicated questions plus 6 retained baseline tasks
- 🛡️ Risk, policy and continuity: 16 dedicated questions plus 7 retained baseline tasks

### 🧪 Phase 4: Design viable game publishing experiments

Define falsifiable low-risk tests with stop rules, without performing any launch, paid campaign or account operation.

- 🧪 Strategies and earning experiments: 18 dedicated questions plus 8 retained baseline tasks
