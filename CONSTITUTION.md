# Mercata Constitution

## I. Purpose
Mercata is an independent public research project on digital marketplaces and realistic routes to income. It studies who can earn, on what terms, at what cost, with what demand, earnings evidence, ownership rights and risks. Mercata is not a marketplace operator and promises no income.

## II. Public-information boundary
Never include private conversations, private language or emotional venting, personal notes, identifying private information, secrets, unpublished research, internal prompts, nonpublic files, unpublished repository materials, or recognizable paraphrases of them. This applies to source code, comments, documentation, data, figures, screenshots, site text, metadata, examples, commits, Git history, issues, pull requests, releases, and logs. If provenance, rights or publication suitability are uncertain, exclude the material. Public artifacts must be independently authored in clear, ordinary professional English. Never import private repository history. Automated scanners supplement, not replace, publication review.

## III. Independent operation
The complete domain ontology, public evidence model, machine validation, gallery, research queue system, static website, and required build assets must be maintained in this repository. Normal use must not require private repositories, private paths, private services, unpublished data or private ontologies. Follow third-party terms, licenses and attribution requirements.

## IV. Ontology and gallery
Keep human-readable and machine-readable ontology files in `ontology/`. Keep a human-readable gallery and machine-readable index in `gallery/`. An empty gallery must say it is empty, not display fabricated examples. Research tasks are not findings.

## V. Research integrity
Date and cite all external factual claims. State jurisdiction, population, denominator, currency, period, method, selection bias and confidence where applicable. Distinguish mean from median, gross sales from net payout and profit, estimates from observations and unknown from zero. Preserve meaningful history of changed fees and eligibility rules. Do not present opaque earnings rankings without inspectable evidence.

## VI. Specimen admission
Do not create a named specimen or its tasks without owner authorization identifying the platform. Admission generates an empty factual record, one unique persistent OKLab-derived color and atomic tasks in all core and relevant specialist queues. Generated tasks begin at 0% and require acceptance evidence before reaching 100%. Do not use unapproved example names as live specimens.

## VII. Site and accessibility
Maintain a readable, responsive and keyboard-accessible public website. Place System / Light / Dark controls at the top right; use System by default. Preserve contrast and avoid using specimen color as an economic judgment.

## VIII. Tarium
The private Taria ecosystem and public Tarium registry are separate. Mercata may not depend on, link or expose private Taria resources. References to other Tarium member projects are allowed only for publicly accessible, explicitly registered members. Adopting the Tarium conventions never authorizes a central registry edit. Only the owner may explicitly authorize registering this named project. Mercata governance may strengthen, not weaken, Tarium's mandatory public-information boundary.

## IX. Operations
Work on the existing `main` branch unless directed otherwise. Verify changes and their public suitability before committing. Report test limits accurately. A later deletion commit cannot remove leaked information from Git history. Stop work at the user's authorized scope.
