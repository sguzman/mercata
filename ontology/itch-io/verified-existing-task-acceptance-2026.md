# Acceptance audit for four existing itch.io research tasks

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** [Machine decisions](verified-existing-task-acceptance-2026.json). This closes **four previously existing fixed tasks**, using their already collected dated sources and regression checks. There were **no new seller observations, source registrations, task admissions or revenue estimates**.

| Fixed research task | Why its stated acceptance condition is fulfilled | Primary audited evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Create market chronology | The 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2026 milestones have their original observation years and separate creation, publication, download-event and catalog-count units. No calculated comparable annual paid-seller growth is asserted. | [Historical operator source chronology](historical-game-supply-2015-2026.md) |
| Study development profit | The 2020 and 2025 developer postmortems explicitly distinguish gross/early cash, costs and partly described labor, with alternative cash/living-expense allocations. The sample **does not** yield a representative earnings mean or an audited hourly rate. | [Two bounded costed launch cases](release-cost-labor-cases-2026.md) |
| Study policy changes | Official July 2025 deindexing and reindexing statements are recorded separately by date and product/payment scope. Suspensions, payment availability and indexed visibility are not treated as identical failures. | [Platform policy chronology](policy-risk-2025-2026.md) |
| Define research acceptance | The existing ledger and test suite distinguish public rule evidence, bounded self-reported transactions, unmeasured populations and authorized private-only merchant records. Unsupported totals remain explicit unknowns. | [Decision guide](market-entry-decision-guide-2026.md), [first-party API access limitations](api-purchase-privacy-2026.md) |

The task score of **100% means that its narrow documented research acceptance condition is met**, not that an operational game was deployed or that private accounts were accessed. Separate research tasks still ask for an active paid-seller census, representative mean/median profit, zero-sale shares, buyer counts and genre performance. Their unknown values remain null and their scores are not raised by these decisions.
