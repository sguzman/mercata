# Bitiverse's free browser-play and payment events are not paid-game conversion

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** [Developer's August 9, 2026 post](https://fortisq.itch.io/bitiverse/devlog/1622835/from-empty-roads-to-real-neighborhoods) and its [dated devlog index](https://fortisq.itch.io/bitiverse/devlog). [Machine evidence](bitiverse-2026-free-browser-events-vs-paid-game-conversion.json).

The solo creator reported one **recent 30-day** period for the free/PWYW 2D browser MMO **Bitiverse**: **7,033 page views, 1,364 browser plays, 93 downloads, 26 collections, seven ratings, and three payments totaling US$57**. This is a single reporting window from one voluntary self-disclosing publisher, not an independent seller population. The player can enter as a guest without paying.

The descriptive **1,364 / 7,033 = 19.39 browser play events per 100 page-view events** is *not* a unique-visitor activation rate and **not** a paid-game sales conversion rate. A visitor may play or visit repeatedly; none of the counts identifies unique people or connects payments to page views. Three payment events are not necessarily three distinct paying humans or three mandatory-positive-price game purchases.

The developer described **30–50 continuing browser plays on ordinary days** after outreach spikes. That is a rough narrative activity level, not daily transaction records or controlled discovery effects.

| Metric | Reported | Can it establish positive-price game sales? |
|---|---:|---|
| Page-view events | 7,033 | No |
| Browser play events | 1,364 | No |
| Download events | 93 | No |
| Collections and ratings | 26 and 7 | No |
| PWYW payment events | 3; US$57 | **No** — donations/PWYW are not mandatory-price orders |

**Missing**: unique buyer identities, matched session-to-order funnels, actual mandatory-price paid units, refund-adjusted payouts, creator labor cost, causal promotion lift and a representative release cohort. No private records or individual customer data were collected.
