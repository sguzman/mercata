# Team administration and project transfer: documented capabilities (October 27, 2026)

**Population / unit.** Public itch.io project administration features and support policy statements, not actual team counts, paid sellers or transactions. Evidence spans a dated 2014 official announcement and a moderated February 2026 support thread; collected October 27, 2026.

- The [official game-admin announcement](https://itch.io/updates/let-collaborators-edit-your-game-pages-with-the-new-game-admins-feature) describes separately approved admins editing project details and appearance, viewing unpublished pages and seeing analytics. These powers are **not** proof of co-ownership or automatic revenue sharing.
- A [2026 moderated ownership-change thread](https://itch.io/t/5906538/cannot-switch-games-ownership) says project ownership changes require platform support and are delicate. This is a moderator statement, not a promised transfer timeline or result; no creator-side self-service migration is established.
- [Terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) preserve publisher ownership of submitted work. The [co-op-bundle guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/bundles) describes a separately consented commercial bundle arrangement; administrative project access is a different role.

**Practical release rule.** Pick the project owner account and document each collaborator's permissions and rights before publishing. Separately agree how income, labor and IP are allocated; do not infer financial or legal rights from an admin invitation. For owner changes use official support, and do not delete and recreate paid pages as a substitute. Preserve buyer entitlements and reputation. Unknowns include guaranteed transfer approval, elapsed support time, 2026 fine-grained admin UI permissions, actual team counts and team payments.
