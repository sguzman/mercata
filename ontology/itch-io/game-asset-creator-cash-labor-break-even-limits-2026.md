# Game-asset seller cost floors, not playable-game demand

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** [Akami July 3 expenses and break-even](https://itch.io/blog/1573030/what-would-actually-make-this-profitable-doing-the-math-out-loud.amp); [Akami June 12 one-euro zero sale checkpoint](https://itch.io/devlog/1551381/i-priced-this-pack-at-1-heres-whether-that-was-smart.amp); [Indie_Vova January 26 six-month review](https://itch.io/devlog/1331048/postmortem-6-months-after-releasing-my-first-pixel-art-asset-pack-on-itchio-50-anniversary-sale.amp).

**Scope:** Two voluntary reporting *game-asset pack* sellers, three dated source articles. They are **not playable videogame merchants or a representative seller cohort**. [Structured observations](game-asset-creator-cash-labor-break-even-limits-2026.json).

Akami Assets reported **€12.99 from May through June 30**, **three payment events across 13 project pages**, **€75 per month** in software/subscription costs, and **20–30 estimated production hours per 60-icon pack**. An earlier **€1 block/puzzle art pack reported zero sales** as of June 12. Both Akami articles refer to one publisher. The real billing duration, merchant payouts, full costs and order settlement are unknown.

With *hypothetical* **€6 gross per paid event** and **€10/hour valued labor**, the fee-free integer cost floors are:

| Modeled monthly cost | Hypothetical gross per payment | Minimum whole payments before fees |
|---|---|---:|
| €75 recurring cash subscriptions | €6 | **13** |
| €75 cash plus €200 valued labor | €6 | **46** |

The author described the latter as roughly 45 payments, while exactly €275 / €6 requires **46 whole payments**. These are **optimistic pre-fee lower bounds**, not observed merchant break-even, forecasts or validated income. Refunds, taxes, payment fees and shared-cost allocation are unobserved.

A separate publisher, Indie_Vova, reported **over US$500 revenue**, **over 10,000 page views** and **157 new followers** after roughly six months of a first paid pixel-art pack, including claimed sales spikes during seasonal promotions. The exact unit orders, fees, labor and control condition are unavailable. Do not claim a causal discount effect or convert visits/followers into buyers.

**Reusable research lesson:** Decide an external cash cap, creator-hours cap and dated measurement window before expanding an experiment. Cash-only and labor-inclusive targets are separate. Use *actual retained after-fee payments*, not listing counts, to evaluate a stop gate. The existing [no-spend pilot protocol](zero-spend-pilot-stop-gates-2026.md) remains operative; this evidence does not authorize real expenditure.

Unknowns remain the seller population, buyer counts, full cost, payout receipts, fee-adjusted contribution, discounted-vs-undiscounted causal lift and any playable-game seller profitability comparison.
