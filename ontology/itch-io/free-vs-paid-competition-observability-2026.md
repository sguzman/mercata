# Free versus paid games: competing attention, different economic demand

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** The [pricing rules](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing) distinguish mandatory positive-price full-game orders from free/pay-what-you-want access and optional voluntary payments. The [HTML5 rules](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5) explain why browser plays and free previews are not game orders.

| Offer | What public activity can show | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positively priced full game | Listing and buyer checkout eligibility | Sold copies without retained paid-order records |
| Zero-minimum PWYW | Free access, optional tips | Paid game sale from a free download |
| Free HTML5 game | Browser play events | Unique buyers or comparable conversion |
| Free demo plus paid full game | Trial/preview activity | Purchase of the full entitlement |
| 100%-discounted paid listing | Temporarily zero-price acquisition | Positive-price order during the free promotion |
| Selected free alpha feature | Attention and potential outside wishlists | Commercial demand for another paid game |

The existing [all-game and Paid browse snapshot](catalog-snapshot-2026.md) counts **1,780,861** general games and **49,588** Paid-filter results on October 26, 2026. These are **different public listing views**, not an audited free-versus-paid buyer market. It would be unsound to call their difference a free-game sales census. A creator may have free and paid versions, tips, DLC, or temporally varying discounts. The [selected SuperWEIRD alpha](free-alpha-frontpage-tail-2026.md) illustrates how free-game attention is not an itch.io paid-order outcome.

A competitive study can compare discovery exposure, product types and time costs, but must separately count validated refunds-adjusted positive-price game orders, optional donations, demo/download events, prices and development/support hours. This satisfies the existing task's distinction between **adjacent free discovery and real paid demand**. No causal displacement, payment conversion or genre profit is claimed.
