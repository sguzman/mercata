# itch.io game soundtracks, bonus files and limited reward tiers

**Source review: October 26, 2026.** itch.io's [pricing guidance](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing), [buyer checkout guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/how-buying-works) and [exclusive rewards documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/exclusive-content) support optional commercial extras beyond a game's minimum price. This is a description of seller capabilities, **not evidence of incremental paid orders, bundle uptake or profit**. See the [machine accounting crosswalk](game-bonus-entitlements-2026.json).

## Optional soundtrack or levels

A seller may publish a $5 downloadable game and attach an individual soundtrack file only unlocked if total payment reaches **$8 or more**. That $8 is a **total support/payment threshold**, not a claim that the soundtrack independently sold for $8, or that an additional $3 is guaranteed. Individual levels and extras can similarly be priced on otherwise free project pages. Tips, special file unlocks and base game paid orders must be separated when calculating retained income.

The [individual-file repricing warning](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing) is operationally important. Raising an individually priced file threshold after a buyer already paid can remove access to the file if the existing payment falls below the new threshold. By contrast, changing a game's project-level minimum price does not ordinarily remove prior ownership of the base project. Use additional file pricing for genuine supplemental materials rather than substituting it for the game's main purchase price.

## Limited rewards, early-bird and physical goods

[Exclusive content & Rewards](https://itch.io/docs/creators/exclusive-content) permits quantity-limited rewards at configurable minimum payments, including early-purchaser bonuses, optional physical merchandise, creator promises (for example a character named after a contributor), and even free limited key claims. These are **different event and fulfillment units**. A reward slot being listed does not prove a sale; a free reward claim does not prove any buyer paid; a successful base game order does not prove the seller successfully delivered an additional physical perk.

The platform says the seller bears responsibility for promised reward fulfillment and physical shipping, with possible refund at buyer request if delivery fails. The documented reward system does not provide a separate shipping-price add-on, so shipping costs must be considered in the offered total price. Purchased rewards remain recorded for fulfillment, even if archived from future purchase. It is wrong to treat every high-tier purchase amount as net profit when promised merchandise, labor or special-access obligations remain.

## Paid early access and preorders

Paid early-access downloads can receive future updates, and preorders may grant entitlement before real deliverable files are made available. A paid checkout at that stage is **not the same as having fulfilled all obligations** of a released game. In any future study, separate buyer-paid transactions from the file or reward delivery process, refund exposure, seller labor and customer support.

## Music and bonus-asset commercial rights

The game upload license and the right to sell a **separate soundtrack download** are not interchangeable. The game publisher needs independently adequate permission for the music composition, recording, cover art, third-party samples, and any other redistributed asset, including whether a collaborator granted separate-download rights. The operator's [terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) do not themselves grant rights to someone else's music.

Under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), properly attributed material may be redistributed commercially subject to its conditions. By contrast, [CC Attribution–NonCommercial 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) does **not** alone authorize the commercial soundtrack option; separate authorization may be needed. Do not infer a license from an upload tag or the game engine's license. The [component rights inventory](game-component-rights-register-2026.md) holds the evidence fields required before an actual commercial release.

Any attach rate, tip conversion, realized number of soundtrack buyers, incremental payout, asset-production hours and soundtrack profit remain unknown. The **$5 game / $8 soundtrack threshold** in this study is only hypothetical arithmetic, not a tested offer.

## What would establish profitability?

A representative eligible cohort with actual counts of paid base game orders, soundtrack and bonus tier selections, tips, seller fulfillment and returns, payment deductions, and labor/asset production costs. None of these are established by documentation or a sample price. The [economic break-even worksheet](break-even-scenarios-2026.md) can handle explicit seller cash/labor assumptions but does not forecast demand.

No project, storefront, reward tier, payment, account, music asset or personal buyer record was accessed.
