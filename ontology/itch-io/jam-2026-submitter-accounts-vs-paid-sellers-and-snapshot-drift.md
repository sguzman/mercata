# Game-jam activity is not the paid-seller population

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** [March 2026 collector report](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/yize-cheng_every-month-thousands-of-games-are-built-activity-7444878799294398464-uXyZ), [official GMTK jam rules](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-jam-2026), [live GMTK 2026 results](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-jam-2026/results), [itch jam directory](https://itch.io/jams/past) and [September 23 independent recap](https://rec0ded88.com/statistics/game-jam-participation/). [Structured observation](jam-2026-submitter-accounts-vs-paid-sellers-and-snapshot-drift.json).

## March 2026 — repeated participation within an observed jam slice

An independent collector's **first-pass scrape** described **191 jams, 5,120 entries, 4,175 distinct developer identifiers and 499 identifiers appearing as multiple-game submitters** in a roughly March 2026 window. The reported repeat share **499 / 4,175 = 11.95%** applies only to that self-collected identifier set. The collector acknowledged **1–2 days of possible date-window error**. This is **not** 4,175 distinct paid sellers, verified natural persons or a census of all active itch publishers.

## July 2026 GMTK jam — accounts, teams, joined users and projects diverge

The [organizer's rules](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-jam-2026) let one person submit multiple games, and **one account submit a game for an entire team**. Other team members can be listed as contributors, but this does not form a payout-entity identity registry. On October 28 the [itch past-jams directory](https://itch.io/jams/past) displayed **37.4k joined accounts** versus about **10.5k submissions**; these two numerators describe different behavior.

The [live results page](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-jam-2026/results) displayed **10,527 entries**, while a [September 23 retrospective](https://rec0ded88.com/statistics/game-jam-participation/) recorded **10,542**. The **15-entry difference** is real *between the two reported counter values*, but changes in curation, visibility, removal or counting definitions are **not identified**. It is not proven that 15 commercial titles were deleted, nor a negative release trend.

## Research boundary

| Observable public unit | Is it a paid game seller? |
|---|---|
| One jam entry | **No** — not necessarily sold, and may contain many people |
| One joined jam account | **No** — joining does not require a submission |
| One tagged contributor | **No** — does not prove ownership or payout eligibility |
| One distinct submitter identifier | **No** — may have free-only projects and no cash transactions |
| One settled positive-price game merchant | **Yes for that verified window**, but cannot be counted from public jam pages |

The available observations strengthen the evidence that **listed projects, active hobby creators, recurring uploaders and payment-receiving sellers are different populations**. They do **not** identify dormant-account shares, paying-buyer cohorts, paid game seller counts, or distributions of revenue. No account-level data was scraped or copied into Mercata.
