# itch.io — paid niche supply, not game sales

Three recently indexed official public directories were reviewed October 26, 2026. [Paid Puzzle genre](https://itch.io/games/genre-puzzle/store) showed **7,456** results, [Paid Horror tag](https://itch.io/games/store/tag-horror) **5,117** and [Paid Cozy tag](https://itch.io/games/store/tag-cozy) **857**. The pages were **not crawled simultaneously**; these are dated retrieval observations of recently crawled results, not precise current census counts.

Puzzle is a genre, Horror a theme, and Cozy an aesthetic/mood tag. Listings can appear in multiple results; sums and naïve fractions of the 2D game catalog are invalid. A Paid listing may show a temporary 100%-off price and does not prove the game has ever sold a copy. The [machine record](paid-genre-tag-frames-2026.json) leaves paid units, genre-specific profits, overlapping paid-2D intersections and success rates unknown. No buyer data was accessed.
