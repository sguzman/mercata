# One tabletop publisher's four yearly serial adventures

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** [Creator's January 6, 2025 postmortem](https://loottheroom.itch.io/advent2024/devlog/863862/post-mortem); [dated log index](https://loottheroom.itch.io/advent2024/devlog); [structured observation](four-serial-tabletop-release-sales-2021-2024.json). These are one established publisher's paid PDF/epub tabletop adventures, **not four independent sellers or paid 2D video games**.

| Release | First day (copies) | During active release (copies) | Live-period gross GBP | Cumulative copies at Jan 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2021 — Reivdene-Upon-The-Moss | 181 | 260 | £969.20 | 308 |
| 2022 — BLACKOUT | 113 | 171 | £807.47 | 174 |
| 2023 — A Month of Misery | 97 | 155 | £670.34 | 163 |
| 2024 — The Witch of Drithwyn Weald | 33 (soft launch) | 239 | £1,272.50 | Not separately reported |

The older titles gained **48, 3 and 8 copies** after their active release intervals, as measured by their January 2025 reported lifetime snapshots. Those post-live windows are **unequal** for titles published in different years, and do not form comparable 365-day or refund-adjusted lifetime earnings cohorts.

For the 2024 title, a November 26 soft launch yielded **33 copies / £173**, plus **30 / £164** during the other soft-launch days. November 29 Creator Day contributed **15 copies / £91 within those 30**, not additional copies to add. December 1's public announcement day brought **80 / £349.50**; December 2 **38 / £157**; December 3–4 together **23 / £117**. Changed price, product availability and marketing all confound any attribution of these differences to one channel or announcement.

The creator also offered a 50%-off coupon to a campaign with **over 700 backers**, reporting **three code-related purchases**. This does not say how many eligible nonbuyers saw or clicked the offer, nor who had already bought the adventure. Three divided by seven hundred is **not a legitimate repeat-customer conversion rate**.

The quantified 2024 live-period report says **239 copies / £1,272.50** (elsewhere the creator conversationally approximates 240 supporters). Gross payments and the creator's provisional 20% tax reserve are not settled net cash or profit after processor fees, development hours and other expenses.

**Scope:** richer same-creator volatility, delayed-sale and coupon-use observations, but no comparable market-wide 7/30/90/365-day order series, 2D video-game seller incomes, representative zero-earner rate, unique paying buyers or causal marketing effects. No private account, customer or payment records were used.
