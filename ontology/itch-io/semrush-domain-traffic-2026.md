# Modeled itch.io domain traffic — not game demand or revenue

[Semrush's September 2026 domain traffic report](https://www.semrush.com/website/itch.io/overview/), updated October 11 and reviewed October 26, provides a **third-party modeled estimate** for the **entire itch.io domain**, not creator-owned analytics or checkout records. [Machine frame](semrush-domain-traffic-2026.json).

| Calendar month | Semrush estimated domain visits (all devices) |
|---|---:|
| July 2026 | 161.31 million |
| August 2026 | 174.43 million |
| September 2026 | 162.37 million |

The same vendor estimates September's average 5.75 pages per visit, an approximately 8:59 session, and a 42.58% bounce rate. The model attributes **26.9% of visits to the United States** (43.68 million), **6.32% to Brazil** (10.26 million), and **4.21% to Bangladesh** (6.83 million). These are **modeled browsing sessions**, not unique people, paid-game customers or developer market share. The operator's site contains free games, game jams, game assets and various non-game products, so total traffic does not isolate demand for purchasing a premium 2D game.

A separate desktop referrer panel estimates **47.09% direct** and **31.97% google.com**, but these cannot be mixed arithmetically with the *all-device* geographic table. All metrics are modeled, not audited against platform's internal servers.

It would be invalid to infer that 162 million people shop for paid games, to multiply sessions by a typical price to obtain marketplace GMV, or to assign estimated browser origin to a publisher's actual paid customers. The provider does not report settled purchases, cohort eligibility, product type revenue, average net proceeds or the probability a first-time game developer will earn money. Those remain unknown.
