# itch.io sitewide traffic models versus paying game demand

**Research date: October 26, 2026.** Observed model month: **September 2026**. These are model-provider estimates, not itch.io checkout records. [Machine record](whole-site-traffic-model-comparison-2026.json).

| Source, September 2026 | Modeled whole-site visits | Modeled U.S. share | Details |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/itch.io/overview/) | **162.37 million** | **26.90%** | All devices; 47.09% direct and 31.97% google.com refer to its *desktop* incoming-traffic model |
| [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/itch.io/) | **about 60.6 million** | **32.51%** | Modeled all-site traffic; 41.14% direct is its *desktop* modeled source share |

The Semrush estimate is approximately **2.68 times** Similarweb's for the same website and month. We have no first-party site visits or methodological calibration to determine which number is closer to reality. **The two models cannot be averaged, treated as a formal confidence interval, or used to infer September paying-customer volume.** The models' U.S. location shares also disagree. An advertising/referral session is not equivalent to one unique person or one sale.

The domain hosts **free browser games, paid and donation-enabled games, downloadable assets, music, software tools, game jam events and creator dashboard visits**. Most measured sessions are not assigned to a game offer, and neither provider reports a representative valid paid-game checkout conversion rate. Therefore there is no legitimate path from 60.6–162.37 million modeled *sessions* to a count of paid game buyers by simply choosing a guessed rate.

For a meaningful paid 2D addressable-market estimate, the missing inputs are unique *eligible game buyers* who actually purchase, spend by category, exposure of paid games to those buyers, the game-level denominator and a consistent observation window. Geographic payout eligibility must be studied separately from a visitor's modeled country.

Both source links and the mismatch are retained in [the data](whole-site-traffic-model-comparison-2026.json). This new work adds **no claim of observed revenue, verified paid buyers, or platform-wide growth**.
