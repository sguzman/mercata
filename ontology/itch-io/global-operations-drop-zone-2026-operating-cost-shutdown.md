# Global Operations: Drop Zone — a 2D live-service shutdown

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** The creator's [March 13, 2026 shutdown postmortem](https://itch.io/devlog/1456982/closing-the-doors-on-global-operations-drop-zone.amp), for an itch-listed HTML5 top-down multiplayer shooter. [Machine record](global-operations-drop-zone-2026-operating-cost-shutdown.json).

The author reports shutting down the **last online server on March 13, 2026**. Hosting, bandwidth and maintenance were no longer affordable alongside concurrent player counts said to be **near zero**. The developer stated that **Stripe-mediated Gold Pack** income remained insufficient to cover operating costs. Matchmaking, leaderboards, Gold Pack purchasing and online play were disabled.

This is a concrete failed live-service operating-cost case, **not a verified zero-sale itch.io game**: the paid packs used **external Stripe checkout**, and the developer supplied no exact revenue, order count, monthly bill, lifetime profit, usage time series or cost ledger. Inadequate revenue is not synonymous with zero revenue. The author discussed possible open-sourcing, not a completed source release.

**Scope:** advances the existing technical and operating-dependency maintenance question by documenting a real operational shutdown. It does not estimate the platform-wide rate of failure or imply paid-game customer conversion.
