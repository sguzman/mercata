# Historical 2019 GDC merchant dependence on itch.io and Steam

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** [Machine record](historical-gdc-2019-store-revenue-dependence.json). GDC's [January 24, 2019 first-party press release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2019/01/24/1705090/0/en/Game-Developers-Conference-Releases-2019-State-of-the-Industry-Survey-Showing-Nearly-Half-of-Respondents-Supporting-Unionization.html) reported a voluntary industry survey of nearly 4,000 game professionals, with specific responses on PC/Mac storefront use and how much each responding seller relied on each store for **their own game-sales revenue**.

| Respondent subgroup | Reported share |
|---|---|
| **Itch-selling respondents** whose itch revenue accounted for **under 10%** of their own sales | **52%** |
| **Itch-selling respondents** whose itch revenue accounted for **75–100%** of their own sales | **28%** |
| **All storefront-question respondents** who said they sold games on Steam | **approximately 47%** |
| **Steam-selling respondents** whose Steam revenue accounted for **75–100%** of their own sales | **54%** |
| **Steam-selling respondents** whose Steam revenue accounted for **50–74%** of their own sales | **17%** |

The exact numbers of storefront-question respondents, itch sellers and Steam sellers are **not published in the cited announcement**. The percent shares are conditional on *different seller subgroups*, not the total nearly-4,000 participant count. A creator deriving nearly all game revenue from itch may earn very little money overall, and another earning a small share via itch may earn much more. These figures **are not market-wide revenue shares, seller medians, or game sales totals**.

The [Gamedev.js 2025 survey](https://gamedevjs.com/survey/2025/) separately reported that **67.4% of 439 publishing-question respondents** published on *itch.io and similar* sites. It measures a different self-selected web-development group, includes free publishing and similar platforms, and has no store-specific revenue-share question. Neither the 2019 GDC percentages nor the 2025 publishing figure can be combined into a time trend or active-merchant census.

**Research disposition:** existing detail task `itch-io/competition/create-cross-platform-benchmarks` advances from **98% to 99%** on this newly registered survey-level historical benchmark. It remains unfinished until representative contemporary, like-for-like store cohort revenues, prices and production costs can be reconciled. Current paid seller populations, market-wide buyer orders, income mean/median, zero-sales incidence and causal store profit effects remain **unknown**.

This audit used attributed short statistics only; no proprietary chart, private data or full survey PDF was republished.
