# Free-to-access itch.io game-artist revenue, 2015–2019

An independent artist's [August 2019 account](https://www.nathalielawhead.com/candybox/my-gross-revenue-on-itch-io-transparently-sharing-all-my-stats-earnings-and-speaking-on-how-supportive-of-a-base-itch-io-has) describes **34 free/pay-what-you-want game and interactive software projects**. The disclosed lifetime gross was **$7,276.70**, of which **$7,164.18** was classified as voluntary tips. A reported **$6,353** received was before income tax and excludes a complete labor-cost treatment.

The same source inconsistently reports **1,072** and **1,073** payments; its annual lines do not reconcile cleanly. We preserve both, without inventing a corrected count. The 2019 data were published in August and are not assumed to cover a full calendar year.

The creator attributes some attention to press coverage and serial work, but provides no causal test. **Optional contributions from a free-to-access creative catalog must not be mistaken for typical mandatory paid 2D game unit sales or a representative developer profit.** [Machine case](creative-free-games-pwyw-2019.json).
