# Different remedies for indexing, content bans, copyright and payouts

Reviewed October 26, 2026. [Machine remedy and timing crosswalk](content-indexing-dmca-payout-appeals-2026.json). The critical finding is that **not all itch.io restrictions are the same event**, and there is no universal appeal or guaranteed recovery clock.

| Event | Official route | Documented timing |
|---|---|---|
| New paid seller's published page not in Search/Browse | Verify metadata and search eligibility, then use [itch.io support](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed). The page can remain accessible through its URL. | New paid seller review often a few business days; guide requests waiting at least one day before contacting support. |
| Page deindexed due to misleading content or tags | Correct the page and contact support; do **not** recreate near-identical pages to evade ranking. | No guaranteed reinstatement interval. |
| Restricted adult-classified page in the UK | The [July 27, 2025 operator notice](https://itch.io/t/5133739/our-update-on-the-uk-online-safety-act) documents an appeal through **Distribute → Geo Restrictions** for a staff review. | No specified guarantee; 2025 statement is not proof of 2026 outcome or other countries' rules. |
| Copyright takedown (DMCA) | The [itch.io terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) allow a formal **counter-notice** when legally appropriate. It requires a signature, identification of removed content, good-faith statement and specified legal/contact declarations. | The operator describes **possible** restoration within **10–14 business days** after counter-notice, absent litigation and subject to discretion; not a guaranteed processing promise. |
| Payment restriction or account ban | Follow the terms, creator guidance and available support procedures. Some serious violations are explicitly subject to permanent action. | No general guaranteed appeal or repayment period. |
| Ordinary collected-payment review | Complete seller tax/identity validation and follow [payout review](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments). | Seven-day transaction hold plus at least seven days of staff review after payout request; bank arrival and exceptional waits are unknown. |

## Commercial consequences and source boundaries

A game can be **publicly accessible by URL but absent from discovery**. Separately, a publisher might be **unable to receive payments**, and still separately might face **copyright removal** or a **country-specific availability restriction**. None of those events by itself establishes how many paid purchases were lost. A successful review, reindexing, restored game download or reimbursement cannot be assumed.

For a creator risk register, record restriction type, effective date, affected project IDs, available support or appeal mechanism, access for existing owners, the merchant payment mode and actual refunds/held balances. Do not expose private buyer or seller identification records in public research. The current study has no observed actual enforcement cases, successful appeals, hold amounts or lost-revenue estimates.

The stated **DMCA legal declarations should not be submitted speculatively**. This is factual operator-procedure research, not individualized legal representation or permission to challenge a third-party complaint.
