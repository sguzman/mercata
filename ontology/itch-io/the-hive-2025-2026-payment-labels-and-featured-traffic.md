# The Hive: reported sales, payments, and ongoing featured traffic

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** The same publisher provided a [December 2025 sales report](https://skydome.itch.io/thehive/devlog/1142956/itchio-sales-reality-check-and-why-it-doesnt-mean-your-game-is-bad), [May 2026 financial report](https://skydome.itch.io/thehive/devlog/1532607/we-are-now-richer-than-jeff-bezos-on-itchio) and [July 2026 featured-traffic report](https://skydome.itch.io/thehive/devlog/1588098/we-got-featured-on-the-itchio-front-page-here-is-what-other-developers-can-expect). | Date | Developer's reported itch.io figures |
| --- | --- |
| December 14, 2025 | **17,000 page views; 414 sales; USD 706.73 total revenue** |
| May 24, 2026 | **24,800 page views; 141 payments; 510 downloads; USD 819.44 gross revenue** |
| July 16, 2026 | **50–150 usual daily visits; approximately 7,000 visits during a still-active front-page feature** |

**Core incompatibility:** The earlier **414 sales** are greater than the later **141 payments** for the same title. No public merchant export reconciles the differing event names or counting rules. These cannot be stitched into a cumulative sales curve or interpreted as negative sales, and do not provide a valid page-visit-to-purchase conversion rate. December's *total revenue* and May's *gross revenue* are not verified to use identical fee deductions, so a net cash change is also unavailable.

The developer also reported about **113,000 Steam copies**. The Steam and itch account periods, prices, refunds and channel conventions are not matched. One voluntarily reporting publisher is not a typical indie seller or a zero-sale-inclusive distribution.

July's approximately **7,000 feature visits** were reported before the feature ended, compared with a **daily** baseline, with no completed paid-order count. Other awareness channels were mentioned, preventing clean causal attribution to featuring.

Only the existing paid-purchase-demand task earns one evidence point. Total buyer counts, net profit, median creator income, and zero-sale prevalence remain unknown.
