# Content eligibility versus monetization and buyer access

Reviewed **October 26, 2026**. [Structured compliance categories](content-monetization-regional-policy-gates-2026.json). This audits existing itch.io publication rules, not an individual game's legal eligibility or actual enforcement likelihood.

The [creator FAQ](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq) permits a wide range of lawful fictional game content but explicitly forbids some abusive, exploitative, real-person sexual and photoreal AI real-person material. The [Terms of Service](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) separately require publishers to have rights to distribute their uploaded work. Serious FAQ violations can trigger permanent account enforcement and payout loss; **do not generalize that no-appeal language to every content review**.

**Four economically distinct gates** must be checked:
1. Whether the publisher may legally host the game under site and intellectual-property rules.
2. Whether the project qualifies for **Search & Browse** indexing under the [quality guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines).
3. Whether the selected direct or collected **payment method** permits selling the actual content, including gateway acceptable-use rules.
4. Whether a particular **buyer region** is lawfully able to access the relevant project.

A public page may remain directly accessible while deindexed; a lawful hosted game might not pass a particular payment processor's merchant eligibility. These are different states. None proves a sale, refund, account ban or transferred money without an actual case.

The [July 2025 UK operator announcement](https://itch.io/t/5133739/our-update-on-the-uk-online-safety-act) is an explicitly dated example of region-scoped adult-content access changes, with a project-level staff review/appeal route. Its existence does not prove the exact October 2026 country-level treatment of every game, nor does it justify extrapolating to Mexico or other regions.

**Measurement limits:** no affected-project census, actual merchant suspension outcome, regional paid-buyer traffic, refund loss or payment-processor genre-risk estimate was obtained. This audit does not provide personalized legal advice or authorize uploads.
