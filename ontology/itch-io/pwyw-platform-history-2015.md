# Historic operator pay-what-you-want payments: positive transfers versus free downloads

[June 20, 2015 itch.io operator research](https://itch.io/blog/2/running-an-indie-game-store-2015) · [Structured categories](pwyw-platform-history-2015.json). Reviewed October 26, 2026.

The platform operator reported that **roughly 30% of completed purchases of positive-minimum-price content** included an additional payment above the mandatory price. Those who did not add a tip still paid the mandatory price; they were **not $0 buyers**. For purchases with extra money, the report said mean extra amount was **$3.67**.

For **free-minimum-price content**, the mean donation **among those who chose to pay** was **$3.68**, with positive donations commonly between $1 and $5. This is not the mean across everyone downloading for free: the article does not give the count of people who skipped payment. A free player's eventual donation likelihood and contemporary conversion cannot be recovered.

The operator described **approximately 80% of purchase events originating externally** and **83% of purchases being initiated from project pages**. These attributes can occur on the same transaction, so they must not be added as disjoint traffic channels. The operator also reported approximately **$393,000 in total payouts accumulated by June 2015**, including $51,489 for a strong preceding month. These are historical whole-store totals, not earnings for a typical first commercial 2D game seller.

This is stronger observed evidence about the *amounts of positive payments* and *optional tipping*, but does not measure 2026 payment behavior, zero-dollar downloader frequency, median seller profit or active paid customer count. The fixed pay-what-you-want distribution task remains partly open for the missing nonpayer denominator.
