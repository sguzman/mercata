# A creator's two partial-year financial disclosures (2024–2025)

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** [Machine case](2024-2025-odds-ents-multichannel-receipts.json) uses the publisher's original [2024 finance retrospective](https://itch.io/blog/851643/2024-finances) and [2025 retrospective](https://itch.io/blog/1137874/2025-finances). This is **one publisher**, not a seller panel or representative paid-game population.

| Item reported in dollars | 2024, through Dec. 14 | 2025, through Dec. 8 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Itch channel (mostly digital assets/zines) | $378.99 | $820 |
| Total reported revenue, all business lines | $5,454.04 | $8,850 |
| Total reported expenses | $936.70 | $2,015 |
| Total reported all-business profit | $4,517.26 | $6,835 |
| Sum of listed revenue-category rows | $5,454.04 | $8,810 |
| Sum of categories minus stated total | $0 | **−$40** |
| Reported profit minus revenue-minus-expenses | **−$0.08** | $0 |

The **$441.01** difference between the two itch-channel labels is a nominal observation across **two unequal, incomplete annual windows**. The store product mix also changed. The 2025 list includes six asset packs, two zines and an update: these are not nine new paid digital-video-game releases. No itch-paid-game purchase counts, channel labor, refunds or bank-ledger reconciliation were observed.

**Arithmetic audit:** The 2025 listed revenue components ($6,480 + $1,338 + $115 + $820 + $57) add to **$8,810**, while the author separately reports **$8,850** overall. The difference is unexplained in the public categories; we do not invent a balancing entry. The art-market $1,338 gross and $761 after press royalties are the **same** channel with different deduction states, not separate income.

In 2024, $5,454.04 revenue minus $936.70 costs is **$4,517.34**, eight cents above the author's $4,517.26 profit. The source mentions subscription offsets as equal income and expenses, but does not explain the eight-cent mismatch. Both original and arithmetic figures are retained.

The 2025 creator estimates **20–24 weekly work hours** over roughly **45 weeks**, spanning freelance and press operations, so deriving an itch income per development hour would be unjustified. Reported all-business profit does not mean itch game profit.

This strengthens only the original **study sales volatility** task from **94% to 95%**. A paired *selected* creator account can illustrate nominal change and accounting ambiguity but not market-level sales volatility, mean or median paid-game income, genre profitability or a causal storefront effect. Unknown quantities remain null.
