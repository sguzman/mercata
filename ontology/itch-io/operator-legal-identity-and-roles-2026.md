# Legal operator and marketplace role boundaries — itch.io

Reviewed **October 26, 2026**. This audit establishes the legal operator and separates publication, hosting, discovery, checkout, delivery and contractual responsibilities. [Machine crosswalk](operator-legal-identity-and-roles-2026.json).

The [Terms of Service](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) name **Itch Corp.** as owner and operator of the service. The [privacy policy](https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy), last modified **March 26, 2026**, also names Itch Corp. as the company. The [general FAQ](https://itch.io/docs/general/faq) identifies **Leaf Corcoran** as the person who founded the platform in **2013**. Founder identity and corporate service identity are different facts. Neither source constitutes a separate search of incorporation records, ownership or corporate control.

| Function | Documented responsibility | Official evidence |
|---|---|---|

## Merchant of record is a conditional transaction role

The [official payment-mode comparison](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) says **the seller is merchant of record in Direct to you mode**, with funds directed to the seller's connected payment provider. Under **Collected by itch.io (Payouts)**, itch.io is merchant of record, receives buyer funds, and later remits eligible payout balances. The two modes assign different chargeback and VAT responsibilities. This distinction does **not** change which corporation operates the site.

## Population, observation limits and economic relevance

**Population and unit:** documented public platform functions and seller-configurable payment routes, not a probability sample of real checkouts. **Time:** observed October 26, 2026, with the privacy-policy revision dated March 26, 2026 and historical founder year 2013. **Source frame:** the official service and creator documentation linked above. No merchant configuration, real transaction, payment account, private analytics or country eligibility was sampled.

The public documentation supports legal-operator identity and role attribution but **not** actual purchase volumes, typical developer income, number of eligible merchants, geographic paying-buyer population or observed conversion. Those measurements remain unknown. A mode-specific role is not evidence that one party acts as merchant of record on every sale.
