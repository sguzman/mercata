# Historical optional-payment receipts: a five-year inconsistency

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** A [creator's August 6, 2019 public income breakdown](https://www.nathalielawhead.com/candybox/my-gross-revenue-on-itch-io-transparently-sharing-all-my-stats-earnings-and-speaking-on-how-supportive-of-a-base-itch-io-has) supplies five year-labeled payment totals for a **single** account with 34 free/pay-what-you-want works.

| Year label | Reported payment events | Gross USD, rounded |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2015 | 10 | $34 |
| 2016 | 139 | $139 |
| 2017 | 356 | $2,306 |
| 2018 | 361 | $3,016 |
| 2019 | 301 | $1,782 |
| **Sum of displayed rows** | **1,167** | **$7,277** |

**Payment counts do not reconcile.** The author gives **1,072** as the lifetime payment count in one section and **1,073** in another. Both differ from the arithmetic sum of the five yearly rows by **95** and **94** events respectively. Without transaction-level evidence the reason is unknown; the source values must remain visibly inconsistent.

**Gross amounts nearly reconcile.** The author reports precise lifetime gross **$7,276.70**, just **$0.30** below the sum of whole-dollar year figures. Ordinary display rounding can account for this small monetary difference; it does not explain the event count discrepancy.

The first payment occurred on August 19, 2015, and the writeup was published August 6, 2019. Thus **2015 and 2019 are partial periods**, and the intermediate year boundaries are not fully documented. The account's portfolio, press attention and game releases changed as well. We cannot claim calendar-aligned annual variance, an effect of publicity, platform-wide seasonality or expected income for a new premium game publisher.

This is a separate historical publisher from the [2023–2025 Not Jam account](same-creator-annual-payment-variability-2023-2025.md). They are **not pooled**, and the missing game-specific paid orders, refunds, labor costs, zero earners and representative population remain unknown.
