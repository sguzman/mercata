# Historical payout delays: seller cash availability is not promised

Reviewed October 27, 2026. [Machine case record](historical-payout-delay-tail-2025-2026.json).

The [official payment guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) describes a seven-day transaction wait, then an at-least-seven-day staff review after the seller requests a collected payout; **10–14 days** is its *typical review* description, **not** a guaranteed transfer to a bank. Extra review and payment-network delays may apply.

Historical 2025 source evidence shows that selected creators experienced slower settlement:

- A [June 9 itch.io support thread](https://itch.io/t/4968173/100-days-and-still-no-payout-does-anyone-else-has-the-same-issue-lets-discuss-it-here) reports a payout wait exceeding 100 days. An administrator later marked that thread resolved and stated on June 18 that **all participants in that thread had been paid**. This resolution is specific to the June thread.
- [Rascal's September 24 reporting](https://www.rascal.news/game-designers-are-experiencing-persistent-payout-issues-from-itch-io/) describes a completed **25-day** payout for one interviewed creator and independently reports another creator's **96-day** payout completed in May. These are voluntary selected accounts, not a sample of all payouts.
- [Game Developer's September 25 report](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/developers-report-delayed-payouts-from-itch-io) discussed complaints and cited Rascal. It is not another independent set of observations to add to the reporting frame.

The right commercial question is **when a seller can actually access net proceeds**, not merely whether a positive-price game got a sale. Under **Collected by itch.io**, payouts pass platform fraud/tax/staff review and subsequent transfer providers. With **Direct to you**, the configured seller payment provider gets funds directly, avoiding this particular platform-collected review but retaining its own eligibility, reserves, conversion and dispute risks.

For a theoretical liquidity test, consider conditional 14-, 30-, and 100-day cash-inaccessibility scenarios. The first is not an arrival promise; the last references a selected historical complaint, not a likelihood or normal expectation. A game studio's actual safe buffer would need real recurring expenses, payroll/contract obligations, source of funds and documented reserves. No user account was touched or bank or merchant records acquired.

**Evidence boundary:** no population denominator, actual 2026 payout distribution, delayed dollar totals, independent receipts verification, representative delay quantile, or causal revenue effect. The 2025 accounts establish a documented historical risk, **not** that payouts are currently failing for all sellers.
