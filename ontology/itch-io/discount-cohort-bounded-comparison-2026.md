# Discount conversion measurement: timing, audience, and prior-demand controls

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** The official [sale documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/sales) allows scheduled percentage discounts and limited coupons. [Pricing](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing) distinguishes a paid game from zero-price acquisitions; a 100% discount is not a paid order. The [Creator Day 2025 announcement](https://itch.io/blog/1124058/creator-day-black-friday-sales-this-friday) concerns a platform fee waiver, not necessarily a changed customer price.

For any permissioned experiment, compare the same game, build, full-game entitlement, underlying minimum price and currency in a controlled 0% full-price cohort and 1-99% promotional coupon cohort. Require the **same campaign dates** and comparable source/device/geographic audiences, plus a separately dated full-price **pre-campaign baseline**. Retained positive-price orders are completed orders minus refunds; report page views and marketing labor/cash separately. Without controlled allocation, a change is descriptive, not causal.

The [offline cohort auditor](../../site/itch-discount-cohort-audit.mjs) rejects mismatched windows, price, product or audience, 100% free-treatment purchases, missing seller consent, and inconsistent refunds. It reports event-denominator rates, never unique-buyer conversion probabilities. The prior [discount compensation model](game-sale-discount-compensation-2026.md) is a hypothetical break-even calculation and not an observation of actual buyer demand.

**Population:** potential authorizing paid-game publisher. **Unit:** eligible visit/order/refund event rather than person. **Observed experiments:** none; no real game, owner analytics, paid order or profit effect. This completes the existing task to document and test controls for **baseline demand, timing and audience mix**, not an empirical price-elasticity study.
