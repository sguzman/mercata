# Comparing the same independent game across storefronts

Reviewed October 26, 2026. [Machine records](crossstore-sales-comparability-2026.json) · [deterministic comparability validator](../../site/itch-crossstore-comparability.mjs).

Six independent developer disclosures demonstrate why a cross-store result needs a consistent denominator:

- [The Hive](https://skydome.itch.io/thehive/devlog/1142956/itchio-sales-reality-check-and-why-it-doesnt-mean-your-game-is-bad) reports 414 itch.io paid sales and $706.73 in platform-reported revenue, but its much larger Steam copy comparison has an unverified product-and-period basis.
- [Rose Seed Replica](https://lezliz.itch.io/rose-seed-replica/devlog/179880/rose-seed-replica-postmortem-mistakes-were-made) reports 13 itch.io units versus 130 across storefronts in its first week. Its roughly €1,000 total and larger grant-sensitive historic cost allocation are not an itch-only payout ledger.
- [AraCore Astromining Ventures](https://elcrabogrande.itch.io/aracore-astromining-ventures/devlog/1133828/aracore-astromining-ventures-a-personal-success-a-commercial-failure) reports approximately £190 multi-store revenue in the first five days but no separated itch.io income or units. Its reported 1,500 development hours cannot be allocated by storefront.
- [An experimental optional-payment game](https://alienmelon.itch.io/flower/devlog/1460326/she-danced-in-the-wind-like-a-holographic-dream-before-the-world-died-on-steam-and-epic) later appeared on Steam and Epic, but no matched additional paid orders or contribution margins were disclosed.

- [Mixolumia](https://mixolumia.com/postmortem/) separately reports 1,776 itch.io units ($17,906 gross; $14,980 reported bank receipts) and 713 Steam units ($6,202 gross; $3,680 reported bank receipts). Store totals reconcile, but the Steam launch occurred **114 days later**, updates changed the game, and receipt settlement lagged, so raw per-store cash is not a same-age experiment.
- [Crumbling World](https://danimarti.itch.io/crumbling-world/devlog/160435/crumbling-world-post-mortem-the-story-of-a-failure) separately reports 7 itch.io units, 132 Steam units, 2 Apple Store units and 0 Game Jolt units in roughly a month. The **141 total units reconcile**, but its store-by-store US-dollar amounts **sum to $1,374.82**, not the **$1,380.82** reported grand total. The **$6.00 discrepancy** remains unknown. Its four venues were not released on an identical clock; $10,000 in marketing-agency costs was cross-store rather than itch-only.

The validator requires **the same game and edition, product type, price currency and amount, discount treatment, time windows, release age, marketing cohort, receipt basis, paid orders, refunds, fees and store-specific distribution/support hours**. Under [itch.io's official pricing rules](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing), a zero-minimum game can produce free downloads and optional gifts, so it does **not** satisfy the positive-price game sample. A declared positive-price order paired with zero gross receipts also fails this audit's accounting consistency gate. It also rejects **invalid or reversed real UTC time windows**, fractional paid-order counts, fractional release-age days, malformed currency codes, missing required text and nonfinite costs. Two records bearing the same malformed date are not comparable merely because their strings match. A comparison fails if even one required dimension is unknown or inconsistent. Its synthetic passing fixtures demonstrate valid record alignment only: passing a schema does **not** prove stores expose identical buyers, or that additional sales were caused by choosing that storefront.

## How to compare if merchant records are authorized

Record separate 7-, 30-, 90-, and 365-day cumulative retained sales and merchant proceeds for each store, aligned by product and release age. Match whether the price is gross of VAT, what share and processor fees were charged, discounts/refunds, promotional activity, paid buyer eligibility, different build requirements, local tax/currency, and staff porting/maintenance effort. Identify whether each store release arrived simultaneously or after an existing audience had formed. Preserve eligible zero-sale products, otherwise survivorship favors apparent successes.

**Result:** The current voluntary cases are **not comparable revenue cohorts** and cannot establish expected additional sales, an incremental storefront profit, or which platform is financially better for a new developer. Two further same-game postmortems now provide source-bounded monetary arithmetic, but no comparable same-release-age and same-earnings-basis treatment cohorts. The research fully specifies the accepted comparison design; actual matched multi-store sales remain unobserved.
