## Distinguish source attribution from redistribution permission

The [itch.io terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) and [creator FAQ](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq) can be linked and summarized for their publicly stated platform rules. That practice does not imply a general license to reproduce all their text, illustrations or community-post images. Likewise, [the Di Sipio 2025 Zenodo research materials](https://zenodo.org/records/15545055) are **referenced as dataset metadata only**. The underlying archive materials have not been imported, and this task does not assert that all files share one reuse license or may be redistributed.

The source register's audit checks uniqueness, URLs, retrieval dates, publish-date syntax and nonblank authored notes. A source can satisfy those checks while the underlying copyright, figures, raw rows, contractual terms or license versions remain inaccessible or unverified. Reuse clearance would need separate inspection of the exact materials to be copied, appropriate permission and attribution.
