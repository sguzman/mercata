## Deposited-file copyright audit

[Zenodo policy](https://about.zenodo.org/policies/) makes public bibliographic **metadata** reusable under CC0 (except email addresses); it does **not** grant a universal license to the deposited data files. [Zenodo's Terms v1.3](https://about.zenodo.org/terms/) say uploading or downloading does not transfer intellectual property rights and that file-specific licenses govern use. The [earlier Di Sipio study record](https://zenodo.org/records/14173085) lists restricted files, while [the later v2 record](https://zenodo.org/records/15545055) lists downloadable files including a **5.8 GB selected-games archive**. Different access states are not copyright clearances. No files were downloaded or redistributed; per-game rights and data-file licenses remain unverified.
