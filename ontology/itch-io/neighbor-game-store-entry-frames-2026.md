# Neighboring PC game stores and direct-sale reference frame

**Research date: October 26, 2026.** [Machine crosswalk](neighbor-game-store-entry-frames-2026.json).

The comparable *channels* for a small downloadable PC game are [itch.io](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq), [Steam](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee), [Epic Games Store](https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/epic-games-store/publishing-tools/organization-management/manage-org), [GOG](https://docs.gog.com/quick-start/) and a publisher-operated direct-download/checkout site. They are not comparable samples of paying buyers.

| Sales channel | Source-documented entry model | Missing information |
|---|---|---|
| itch.io | Free game-page uploads; configurable platform revenue share with payment fees on transactions | Average revenue for eligible first-game publishers |
| Steam | $100 per new app; paid fee is non-refundable but may be recouped at $1,000 adjusted gross receipts for the app | Sales, developer profit and refunds |
| Epic Games Store | $100 submission fee per product plus any local taxes; product questionnaire | Actual approval, seller revenues and buyer exposure |
| GOG | Previously unsigned developers submit a game for review to receive developer-portal access | Universal application fee, approval frequency and profitability |
| Owner's own website | Direct distribution reference channel, not a staffed marketplace: developer must supply payments, bandwidth and marketing | All actual costs and sales outcomes |

A storefront open to anyone (itch.io) and an operator-reviewed release set (GOG) necessarily have different entry selection. Fees for Steam/Epic are product onboarding costs, **not proof of an eventual buyer or return**. Nor does a zero-upfront itch.io page imply zero production, distribution or compliance cost. The direct web route is conceptual and does not inherit a marketplace's visitors.

## Comparative evidence requirements

An interpretable store-versus-store sales assessment needs a common game/version, release window and age, paid minimum price, discounts, buyer geography, refund treatment, platform/processor fees, marketing exposure and per-store QA labor. Distributors may launch on different dates or reach distinct audiences. Even raw paid unit ratios for one game do not identify a causal discovery advantage. A Steam key issued to an itch.io buyer is an entitlement, not a fresh Steam purchase.

This research fulfills the fixed task **to define eligible neighboring stores and direct-sale alternatives**. It does not establish a buyer census, first-launch mean/median profit, switching rate or causal superiority of any channel.
