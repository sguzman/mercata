# Fixed-task acceptance: checkout events, discovery, selection bias and permissions

Reviewed **October 27, 2026**. [Machine source and task crosswalk](core-economic-event-access-and-rights-audit-2026.json).

Six pre-existing tasks ask for operational definitions and source attribution, **not** a representative income survey. They are complete on their narrow documented acceptance criteria:

| Existing task | What the dated evidence establishes | What remains unknown |
|---|---|---|
| Seller disclosure bias | Public developer postmortems are voluntary and incomplete; selected genre/tag samples lack known paid-merchant inclusion probabilities | Population mean, median and zero-sale fraction |
| Transaction mechanics | A game price/payment mode determines which merchant and gateway collect payment; payout and refund obligations can differ | Observed purchases or profit for a new seller |
| Traffic analytics | Page views, downloads, browser plays, paid orders, refunds, follower reach and payouts use different units and may need private owner authorization | Per-person paid conversion and causal promotion |
| Search/indexing | Public page, eligible indexed page, ranking slot and paid purchase are not the same; the **October 19, 2026** reform changed the eligible page frame | Incremental paid game sales or organic search uplift |
| Dated discovery change | The [Oct 2026 operator announcement](https://itch.io/t/7172464/october-updates-on-indexing) follows earlier review policies without making every former page a new paid game | Current ranking benefits to any exact publisher |
| Copyright and media rights | itch.io's [terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms), software licenses, Creative Commons media and OFL fonts impose separate grants, notices and commercial-use conditions | Clearance of any actual game's media inventory |

The [buying guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/how-buying-works), [payment mode table](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments), [indexing guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed) and [quality guidance](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines) establish platform behavior. They do **not** supply seller-bank payouts or complete transaction datasets.

Actual bundles require review of the *specific* files, author permissions, notices, derivative conditions and software linking implications. A public research citation does not clear game art for commercial use.

The internal acceptance concerns **documented distinctions**, not empirical claims of income. All actual active paid-seller populations, buyer conversion probabilities, mean/median profit and audited game asset licensing remain unknown, and no seller or buyer records were accessed.
