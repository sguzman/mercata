# One first solo videogame: three-year cross-store sales versus free itch demo traffic

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** Source: [December 20, 2025 first-person developer postmortem for *No More Snow*](https://www.reddit.com/r/gamedev/comments/1prgrto/3_years_after_my_first_solo_game_launch_6k_copies/). [Machine record](no-more-snow-2025-one-game-crossstore-paid-and-free-demo.json).

This is **one selected independent game**, not a representative sample of positive-price itch sellers or buyer conversion. The developer supplied approximately three years of cumulative self-reported copies and money across storefronts:

| Distribution venue | Copy events reported | Revenue label in USD |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Steam storefront | 1,231 | $4,465 |
| Fanatical **(Steam keys sold by a third-party retailer)** | 4,443 | $2,137 |
| GOG storefront | 446 | $1,409 |
| itch.io | **8** | **$32.30** |
| **Arithmetic total** | **6,128** | **$8,043.30** |

The developer separately summarized the revenue as **$8,043 before taxes**, rounded to whole dollars. Fanatical Steam keys **must not** be relabeled as Steam storefront purchases, even though they activate on Steam. Neither the listed money nor summed copies is verified against owner transaction exports; refunds, tax, store fees, payout cash and production labor costs are not reconciled.

## Earlier same-creator checkpoint (October 2025 discussion)

In a [2025 community discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/itchio/comments/1oks4ip/people_whove_priced_their_games_on_itch_how_often/), this **same developer** reported **6 itch.io copies**, **422 GOG copies**, roughly **5,000 loosely labeled Steam copies** and **about 20,700 free itch demo downloads**. The thread began October 31, 2025, but the individual comment's precise day is unverified. Compared only arithmetically with the later December report, the reported cumulative itch and GOG unit tallies differ by **2 and 24**, respectively; these are not matched reporting windows, measured sales rates or representative longitudinal outcomes.

Crucially, the earlier ~5,000 **Steam-labeled** figure cannot be compared directly to the December **1,231 Steam storefront** count: the later report additionally specifies **4,443 Steam keys sold through Fanatical**. Neither the earlier channel mix nor its accounting cutoff was independently verified. The free demo download count cannot be converted into checkout conversion.

## Free demo exposure is a different population and observation period

The author described an early itch prototype drawing approximately **2,000 free downloads**, growing to **around 20,000 demo downloads by Steam launch**, and identified itch as a large early traffic source to the Steam page. Those are **download events before or near full-game launch**, not the same roughly three-year cohort as the eight purchased itch copies or storefront paid orders. No common unique visitor denominator, buyer-level join or cross-store attribution was provided. Ratios of demo downloads to any later store sales would **not** be verified checkout conversion or proof of a marketing effect.

The game had seasonal Christmas demand, years of intermittent creator development, and changing Steam discounts. Even the same title cannot be treated as a controlled comparison of store purchase likelihood when launch windows, pricing, visibility and store buyers differ.

## Research disposition

This source contributes a direct positive-price **itch paid-unit count** for a modern commercial videogame, rather than downloads, creator tips, artwork packs or tabletop subscriptions. The existing paid-purchase-demand detail task advances **71% to 72%**; it remains incomplete. Cross-store benchmark evidence improves qualitatively but its task remains **99%**, because equivalent price, time and exposure conditions are still absent.

**Unknown:** all-platform paid buyer denominator, 30/90/365-day matched store receipts, actual game net profit, typical itch paid-title order volume, mean/median income, zero-sale fraction and causal demo-driven purchases. No buyer records or copyrighted source media were copied.
