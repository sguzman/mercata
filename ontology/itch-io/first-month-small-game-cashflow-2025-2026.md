# First-month game income: one $10 report and one itemized $49.99 donation

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** These are two *voluntary, individually described* developer outcomes, not a sample of every commercial game. [Machine observations](first-month-small-game-cashflow-2025-2026.json).

## Basement Raid — $10 reported, but not necessarily settled revenue

The developer's [public project development thread](https://itch.io/t/3851203/basement-raid-a-top-down-shooter-about-raiding-a-giant-futuristic-basement) describes a small 2D/top-down shooter developed since May 2024, first commercially released on **August 15, 2025**. In the final update, the creator reports the game **made $10 in its first month**. That $10 has no audited definition: it is **not known whether it represents gross orders, developer receipts or other accounting**, and its denomination cannot identify how many $5 copies sold. The game had a 20% first-week promotion, preliminary demos, early access and more than a year of intermittent hobby development. The author describes early weeks of roughly 12 hours, then 4–6 hours nearer completion, but no total hours, production costs, payout statement or final profit. The source is **one nonrandom title** and does not determine typical income for shooters or first commercial releases.

## Genetic Collections - Axolotls — an actual creator-reported payout waterfall

In a [February 23, 2026 first-month disclosure](https://msreds.itch.io/geneticcollections/devlog/1412711/the-first-month), the author of a first game in the 2D simulation category reported **over 100 page views and one $49.99 voluntary donation**. The author itemized the payment as follows:

| Author's cash-flow description | USD |
|---|---:|
| Gross contribution | $49.99 |
| Payment processing charge | -$1.75 |
| itch.io platform share | -$4.99 |
| New-account tax-document adjustment | -$3.00 |
| Tax withheld | -$10.38 |
| **Owed to publisher** | **$29.87** |
| Publisher's later discretionary charitable gift | -$15.00 |
| **Publisher's reported remainder** | **$14.87** |

The cash deductions reconcile exactly in cents. The author's stated 24% withholding describes this specific reported transaction and **does not determine another seller's applicable rate or final taxes**. The $3 tax-document charge is an account-level adjustment, not a cost assumed on every sale. The $15 charity contribution is a **discretionary use of received funds**, not a platform service fee or development expense. Neither the $29.87 account amount nor $14.87 remainder is **profit after development labor, assets, equipment or all personal taxes**.

This was **one donation**, not an observed positive-minimum-price sale, so it must not join a mandatory-paid game unit denominator. The 100-plus views are events, not independent known buyers.

## What remains unknown

The observations differ in years, genre, price mechanism and financial disclosure detail. Basement Raid is a paid title with an ambiguous earnings basis; the axolotl prototype received a voluntary contribution with a detailed fee waterfall. Neither establishes average new-seller income, zero-earning prevalence, paid-buyer conversion, causal marketing effects or genre profitability. Platform-wide estimates remain `null` without a properly sampled cohort.
