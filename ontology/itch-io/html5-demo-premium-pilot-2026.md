# HTML5 demo versus premium downloadable game: pre-execution experiment plan

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** The official [HTML5 creator guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5) establishes that browser-embedded HTML games are available to play with optional donations, rather than a built-in paywall. A positively priced full game can be a **Downloadable** project. The official [getting-started guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started) also permits a separate free *downloadable demo file* on the same paid game's page.

For an optional browser demo, compare the **same positive-price downloadable game** with (variant B) and without (variant A) a separate freely playable HTML5 project linked to the premium product. There is no verified cross-project native checkout or visitor-identity join. The [owner-authorized aggregate funnel validator](../../site/itch-matched-funnel-audit.mjs) only accepts a common attribution frame when separately verified; having a demo link does not prove it.

**Development effort evidence:** [selected real-world small-game reports](small-game-production-effort-2025-2026.md) describe a roughly two-calendar-week and nine-minute free browser game, a different 140-person-hour solo jam project, and 51 reported team phase-hours without a total team-person-hours measure. They cannot be combined or used as a representative HTML5-demo cost estimate. The planned worklog instead separates demo engineering and export, art, controls/accessibility QA, browser compatibility, publisher copy, acquisition and maintenance. Only actual nonoverlapping timed sessions can establish hours; the seller must specify any cash and labor budget.

**Funnel comparison:** fix game, version, positive price, observation horizon (30/90/365 days), acquisition source and buyer eligibility. Record eligible page-visit events, demo-play events, checkout starts, completed real positive-price paid-game orders, refunds, actual payouts, marketing cost and valued work. A browser play is **not** a paying purchaser. Without verified cohort linkage and randomized or valid counterfactual assignment, the comparison is descriptive rather than causal.

**Acceptance:** this is a source-backed plan with specified funnel controls and how to measure development effort; not a released game, an authorized experiment, a completed QA suite, real owner cash spend or a predicted return. [Machine protocol](html5-demo-premium-pilot-2026.json).
