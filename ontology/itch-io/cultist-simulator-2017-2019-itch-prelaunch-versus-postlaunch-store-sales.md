# Cultist Simulator: pre-launch itch sales versus post-launch cross-store totals

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** Historical first-party [Weather Factory year-one financial report](https://weatherfactory.biz/state-of-the-factory-year-1/), published February 12, 2019. [Machine record](cultist-simulator-2017-2019-itch-prelaunch-versus-postlaunch-store-sales.json).

The publisher reports **1,070 itch.io pre-purchases** during the December 2017–February 2018 exclusivity window. After its May 31, 2018 full launch, with other storefronts available, the same publisher reports **178 itch purchases over seven months**. Those windows have different durations, game availability and promotion: they are not a natural experiment isolating a Steam substitution effect.

The publisher's cross-store *post-launch* sales table separately reports around **170 itch units / USD 3,500 gross**, **93,000 Steam units / USD 1.46 million gross**, **7,400 Humble units / USD 135,000 gross**, and **3,100 GOG units / USD 58,300 gross**. The table's roughly **170 itch copies must not be presented as lifetime itch sales**: it excludes the earlier 1,070 pre-purchases. The seven-month 178 observation is not assumed to share an identical exact cutoff with the rounded 170 table row. The approximate total across reported storefronts is **105,000 units and USD 1.76 million gross**, with free Steam keys and paid storefront purchases needing different accounting.

The studio also reported an approximately **USD 60,000 publisher advance** and meaningful platform, publisher, refund, operating, staff and marketing deductions. Cross-store gross is **not personal developer income** and must not be modeled as profit. This studio described its game as an unusually successful outlier, with established experience and publishing support; the results do not estimate the median first-time itch videogame seller.

**Scope:** advances one existing purchase-demand task with a dated, named, store-specific case while preserving missing paid-buyer denominators, comparable cohorts, after-cost income and zero-sale outcomes.
