# itch.io first-game selling experiments — design, not execution

**Research plan only (October 26, 2026).** Machine design: [commercial-strategy-protocol-2026.json](commercial-strategy-protocol-2026.json). No account, game, purchase, seller data or advertisement was used.

## Seven falsifiable comparisons

| Offer | Question | Real observation needed |
|---|---|---|
| Paid downloadable | Will retained per-sale proceeds recover production and testing labor? | Settled paid orders, refunds, fees, cash cost and time |
| Free HTML5 with donations | Does browser usage convert into voluntary cash? | Separate plays, visits and actual donations |
| Free demo + paid full game | Does demo access yield incremental paid orders? | Matched launch exposures and payer outcomes |
| Positive-minimum pay what you want | Do extra contributions offset changes in order count? | Actual paid amount distribution and purchase count |
| Sale or coupon | Does lower price produce better *net* returns? | Comparable windows with/without promotion and costs |
| Jam release leading to paid game | Do jam views translate to later real purchases? | Subsequent matched release cohorts, not ratings alone |
| itch plus another store | Which storefront produces more proceeds for a common game/time period? | Store-specific sales/costs, marketing and launch timing |

Sources include [pricing](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing), [HTML5 limitations](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5), [payments](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments), [sale mechanisms](https://itch.io/docs/creators/sales), and [a known differently timed cross-store case](https://mixolumia.com/postmortem/). The last is an example of a confounded comparison, not a general result.

## Experiment integrity

Record each game's eligible audience and release age before comparing. Track **actual paid orders, refunds, final seller receipts, and development/marketing labor**; do not treat view counts, page visits, demo downloads or jam votes as customer sales. A page may be unindexed during launch even though accessible by direct URL. Randomized exposure may be unavailable in marketplace ranking; in that case mark estimates *observational* and report confounding instead of pretending causal inference.

All budget and time ceilings, personal publishing choices, game source files, payment account access and exact release date remain **unspecified**. The contract therefore keeps those values null. This stage requests no account credentials or purchase and has no authority to create commercial experiments. No hypothesis was confirmed; income and conversion remain unknown.
