# Paid-game supply, popular listings and site visits use different denominators

**Reconciled October 27, 2026.** [Machine audit](paid-game-supply-vs-attention-denominators-2026.json). Original evidence: [platform/type catalog](platform-specific-paid-game-supply-2026.json), [ranked 2D prices](paid-2d-listed-price-observation-boundary-2026.json), [site-visit models](whole-site-traffic-model-comparison-2026.json), and [referrer categories](referral-source-channel-denominators-2026.json).

## Supply observations (not additive)

The recorded public catalog snapshots list **12,691 Paid + 2D-tagged game pages**, **7,589 paid-filter HTML5 game pages**, and **34,058 paid-filter downloadable game pages**. Platform-label views separately show **7,456 browser**, **7,350 Linux**, and **25,265 Windows** paid-filter pages. They were captured from different cached dates, and one title can qualify for multiple tags, types or platforms. **Do not add these counts or convert them into shares, sellers or sales.**

The existing 15-title Popular-ranked 2D sample has a displayed-price median of **$5.99**, but includes **2 temporarily 100%-discounted pages**. Popular sorting oversamples visible projects and does not provide seller revenue, price paid by an actual buyer or a representative premium-game median.

## Audience observations (not buyers)

For September 2026 the recorded third-party models reported **162.37 million all-domain sessions (Semrush)** and **60.6 million (Similarweb)**. They disagree substantially, are estimates of visits rather than unique people, and cover browser plays, assets, free downloads and other website functions. Neither gives how many customers purchased games.

The official platform's [indexing documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed) distinguishes a published page from Search/Browse appearance. The [source register](../../specimens/itch-io.json) also includes historical purchase-referrer and domain-referral descriptions. Those do not produce a 2026 merchant dashboard or a complete first-party visitor-to-paid-order funnel.

## Reproducible comparison gate

Compare game pages only when the same eligibility filters, project identifiers, capture dates, release cohorts and temporary discount state are available. A complete paid-game study then needs owner-authorized aggregate impressions, page visits, free play/download events, settled/refund-adjusted paid orders and privacy-preserving unique buyer deduplication, each with its own denominator.

**Unobserved:** unique publishers, project overlaps, genre GMV, actual paid-game customers and income. The existing source evidence closes the **definition and bounded sample interpretation** work, not the population-wide financial and demand tasks.
