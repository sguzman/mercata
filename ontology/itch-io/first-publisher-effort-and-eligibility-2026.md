# First game onboarding: required work and unmeasured costs

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** [Structured evidence](first-publisher-effort-and-eligibility-2026.json) · [Conditional estimator](../../site/itch-first-publisher-effort.mjs).

The official [first-project instructions](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started) permit a free account and a free game page. That establishes a **zero platform listing fee**, not zero engineering, assets, QA or support cost. A game release requires six identifiable work stages: secure account setup, rights-cleared build, packaging and device testing, accurate page metadata, price/access configuration, and release/discovery review. Each has skills and tangible required artifacts in the structured record.

A Direct-to-you **paid** release adds connection to an eligible PayPal or Stripe account and checkout validation, with the publisher as merchant of record. A collected-payment release instead adds seller tax identity verification and payout configuration, with itch.io as merchant of record. The [operator's payment documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) details these distinctions and a **one-time USD 3 account-balance adjustment** in the collected pathway. That debit is not a listing charge or universal required cash expenditure before creating a free page.

The official Direct Stripe currency table includes United States, Canada, Ireland and United Kingdom examples. It is not a global eligibility census; Mexico's absence from that list cannot prove an account will fail, nor does general Stripe country support prove itch.io integration. Publisher age/consent, ownership rights, local tax identity, account approval and checkout must be established for each real case.

The estimator accepts **minutes, external cash and optionally an hourly labor valuation for every applicable stage**. If one measurement is missing, total work and cost remain **unknown**, rather than incorrectly reporting zero. Paid-release readiness additionally requires explicitly verified seller, tax and checkout gates; merely filling the table is not approval.

No game build, seller account or payment was operated. The number of days until first publication, typical publisher hours, actual effective country eligibility, merchant bank proceeds and the probability of any paid game sale remain unknown.
