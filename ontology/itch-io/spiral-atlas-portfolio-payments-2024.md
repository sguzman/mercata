# One creator's 2024 mixed-portfolio payments and download-event ratios

[Developer report, November 9, 2024](https://spiralatlas.github.io/how-much-i-get-paid-for-my-games-and-game-dev-tools-on-itchio/) · [Structured counts](spiral-atlas-portfolio-payments-2024.json). Reviewed October 26, 2026.

The developer reported roughly **USD 100 per year** from a mixed set of visual novels, puzzle games, demos, backgrounds and tools. The per-product figures below are **cumulative lifetime counts as of November 2024**, not 2024 sales or one matched launch cohort.

| Item | Download events | Payment events | Reported receipts |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Northanger Abbey, complete visual novel | 3,779 | 36 | $339 |
| SOON, complete visual novel | 614 | 13 | $86 |
| Perpixeled, puzzle game | 181 | 6 | $32 |
| Pride or Prejudice, demo | 947 | 0 | $0 |
| A Hungry Heart, demo | 71 | 1 | $6.70 |
| Pay-what-you-want background pack | 1,301 | 4 | $30 |
| Paid-only background extension | 8 | 2 | $24 |

The writer noted potential errors in their ratios. For **Perpixeled**, 6 divided by 181 is approximately **3.31%** in event arithmetic, not the **0.3%** reported in the original table. The structured record preserves both the published figure and the arithmetic inconsistency. Neither number is a verified unique-downloader-to-paying-customer conversion.

The creator explicitly reported **two owner accounts and eight download events** on the paid-only background pack, demonstrating repeated download events rather than eight separate buyers. They also listed three separate bundle allocations of **$324.25**, **$56.66**, and **$55.15**. Those correspond to bundle programs, not independent full-price sales of a specific title.

The products have different ages, business models, audiences and purposes. Bundle allocations and voluntary tips do not establish mandatory-price game units. No owner profit after labor, complete fee/tax series, unique customers, genre-average income, median seller income or platform-wide zero-sale fraction can be recovered. This is one voluntarily published creator case, **not** a representative market survey.
