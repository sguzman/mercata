# First-game launch economics: cash costs, development labor and store scope

**Source date and study scope:** Developer-published postmortems reviewed October 26, 2026. These are two volunteered game stories, not a representative first-time paid-game sample. Machine records: [release-cost-labor-cases-2026.json](release-cost-labor-cases-2026.json).

## Rose Seed Replica (2020 narrative 2D game)

In the [publisher's itch.io postmortem](https://lezliz.itch.io/rose-seed-replica/devlog/179880/rose-seed-replica-postmortem-mistakes-were-made), Rose Seed Replica sold **13 itch.io copies** during the launch week and received **€27 in itch.io tips**. The author separately reports approximately **130 copies across storefronts**, roughly **€1,000 of total launch-week revenue**, and **three refunds**. It would be incorrect to attribute the 130 units to itch.io.

The publisher describes **more than €26,000 in combined personal living and operational outlays across roughly three years**, and derives alternative cost-allocation assumptions of approximately **€17,000** for two development years or **€8,700 after deducting one-time grant funding**. These figures are the author's livelihood-inclusive budget scenarios, **not a standardized estimate of direct out-of-pocket engine and asset expenses**, and include non-game financial support. The postmortem does not supply a complete verified hours ledger or final itch.io game profit.

## AraCore Astromining Ventures (2025 2D sci-fi game)

The [developer's launch postmortem](https://elcrabogrande.itch.io/aracore-astromining-ventures/devlog/1133828/aracore-astromining-ventures-a-personal-success-a-commercial-failure) reports **about £190 in first-five-day sales** and lists approximately **£185** for business incorporation, Steam registration, music, sound effects and graphics, producing an approximately **£5 cash-accounting profit**. The source reports around **1,500 hours of development**. Division of £5 by 1,500 gives about £0.0033 per hour **only as an arithmetic illustration** of why labor valuation changes the interpretation; it is not the author's contracted wage, nor a full audited profit calculation. Its first-five-day sales span the game's release context and are **not itemized as itch.io transactions**.

The developer suspects that genre expectations, custom engine construction, production scope, and limited marketing/pre-release testing affected commercial performance. These are single-case interpretations, not controlled estimates of engine choice or genre revenue potential.

## Decision relevance

These cases demonstrate three boundaries essential to selling indie games: **store-specific paid units**, **initial cash recovery**, and **compensation for development work**. They cannot be combined into an itch.io-wide mean or median, a zero-earner percentage, or a recommended genre. A first-time seller can recover a few cash purchases and still fail to recover production labor or living costs; conversely no mandatory storefront listing fee says nothing about the full cost of actually producing a game.

No seller account operation, experimental release, financial transaction or personal data collection occurred.
