# Accepted measurement and platform-rule research tasks

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** [Source-by-source machine acceptance ledger](accepted-measurement-and-policy-tasks-2026.json). These are **ten tasks already present** in Mercata's fixed itch.io research plan. No specimen, project experiment, seller login, payment, income data or task has been added by their closure.

The research goal for each of these tasks is a **documented measurement rule, source-bound commercial policy, or evidence-gap report**. A documented rule can be complete even though the entirely separate question *what proportion of commercial developers earn money* cannot currently be answered.

| Existing task | Grounded acceptance |
|---|---|
| `itch-io/sales/separate-buyer-interest-from-purchase` | [Supporting document](../../ontology/itch-io/api-purchase-privacy-2026.md) — A public view, collection or download-key check is not a completed paid order or verified unique buyer. |
| `itch-io/economics/check-payout-delays-and-thresholds` | [Supporting document](../../ontology/itch-io/collected-payout-costs-2026.md) — The policy conditions distinguish the seven-day hold, five-dollar available minimum and separate staff review, without promising bank arrival. |
| `itch-io/rights/review-buyer-entitlement` | [Supporting document](../../ontology/itch-io/resale-transfer-2026.md) — Account, title copyright, paid transaction, download key, gift and post-delisting access are separately represented. |
| `itch-io/sales/study-bundles-and-cross-sell` | [Supporting document](../../ontology/itch-io/skwish-direct-versus-bundle-2023.md) — The game developer explicitly separated direct paid copies from bundle allocations; no overlap or standalone bundle sale is manufactured. |
| `itch-io/income/compare-monthly-and-lifetime-sales` | [Supporting document](../../ontology/itch-io/notjam-game-category-series-2023-2025.md) — Three cumulative annual snapshots from one publisher are kept as changing lifetime stocks, not independent calendar-year revenue flows. |
| `itch-io/income/separate-game-and-developer-revenue` | [Supporting document](../../ontology/itch-io/developer-income-cases-2026.md) — Game-category earnings, other product lines, cooperative bundles and outside work fees remain distinct from a publisher's total take-home. |
| `itch-io/review/define-publication-privacy-rules` | [Supporting document](../../ontology/itch-io/api-purchase-privacy-2026.md) — Credentialed buyer records and shipping details are excluded from public releases; only authorized, nonidentifying aggregate research is permissible. |
| `itch-io/review/prepare-evidence-gap-summary` | [Supporting document](../../ontology/itch-io/research-synthesis-2026.md) — The published synthesis explicitly retains unknown market mean, median, zero-sale frequency, active paid publishers and causal effects. |
| `itch-io/economics/verify-platform-revenue-share` | [Supporting document](../../ontology/itch-io/commercial-rules-2026.md) — Documented 0–100 percent configurable share, ten-percent illustrative case and separate processor/settlement liabilities are accounted for. |
| `itch-io/market/measure-genre-supply` | [Supporting document](../../ontology/itch-io/genre-rating-attention-2025.md) — The study's non-exclusive genre tags, overlapping filters and incomplete publication-date frames forbid additive genre market-size estimates. |

Each task's original source-backed documentation and regression test was already present and its listed source IDs already appeared in the dossier. This increment makes the acceptance explicit, connects it to the task evidence register, and tests the ten decisions as a fixed set. **100% of the narrower task's documented analytical acceptance is not 100% confidence in empirical market data.** Transaction-level buyer demand, first-paid-game median and mean seller income, genre profit differences, and zero-sale publisher rates remain unknown and have not been upgraded.

The [main synthesis](research-synthesis-2026.md) continues to report unknown quantities, rather than treating method completion as observed sales.
