# Paid platform and file-type game supply: nonadditive snapshots

Reviewed October 26, 2026. [Structured filter record](platform-specific-paid-game-supply-2026.json). A **platform compatibility filter** and a **project file-type filter** are different dimensions.

These **type-specific observations are real public catalog-filter counts**, not a measurement of actual game sales. The snapshots have differing crawl times and cannot be used for exact same-date market shares, catalog growth or buyer conversion.

A page can contain both an HTML5 browser version and downloadable files. **HTML5-only and Downloadable-only counts are still unknown.** The separate browser platform tag is not necessarily identical to the HTML5 file-type tag. A Paid filter can include discounted-to-$0 projects and does not establish a positive-price order or completed payment.

The existing [all-price 2D](https://itch.io/games/tag-2d) and [Paid+2D](https://itch.io/games/store/tag-2d) observations of 426,103 and 12,691 listings were captured at different times. Those tags overlap categories and must not be used as a paid-purchase-rate denominator.

This research supports the original task to **document separate platform and file-type supply denominators**, while preserving the fundamental limits: no synchronized complete-project deduplication, seller counts, positive-price unit sales, market GMV, or publisher income was observed.
