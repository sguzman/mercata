# Paid browser and desktop game supply: a nonadditive snapshot

Reviewed October 26, 2026. [Machine frame](platform-specific-paid-game-supply-2026.json).

| Paid browse filter | Results | Source index age |
|---|---:|---|
| [Web](https://itch.io/games/platform-web/store) | 7,456 | About five days |
| [Linux](https://itch.io/games/platform-linux/store) | 7,350 | About two weeks |
| [Windows](https://itch.io/games/platform-windows/store) | 25,265 | About one month |

These are **not simultaneous observations**. A game can advertise all three platforms and appear in each filter. The results cannot be added, divided into platform market shares, or used as unique game, merchant or buyer populations.

The separately indexed [all-price 2D tag](https://itch.io/games/tag-2d) displayed 426,103 listings; the previously recorded [Paid+2D tag](https://itch.io/games/store/tag-2d) displayed 12,691 at a different capture. Neither the ratio nor a sales conversion can be inferred. Tags and temporary discounts do not ensure that any particular buyer paid.

**HTML5-only and downloadable-only counts remain unknown.** Browser compatibility is a platform label, not necessarily an exclusive file-type designation. Attempts to inspect those separate type filters did not yield usable counts.

The study therefore distinguishes supplier *listing availability* from actual unit sales, gross merchandise value, operator platform revenue, developer cash payout and publisher earnings. Those commercial measurements remain unobserved.
