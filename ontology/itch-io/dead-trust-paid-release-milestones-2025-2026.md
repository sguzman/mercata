# Dead Trust: dated paid-game copy milestones without a receipt ledger

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** [Structured evidence](dead-trust-paid-release-milestones-2025-2026.json). This is one voluntarily reporting developer of a **second** 3D horror game, not a first-time 2D publisher or a representative commercial release sample.

The [November 29, 2025 release announcement](https://hexnut-studios.itch.io/dead-trust/devlog/1128066/dead-trust-out-now) precedes two publisher disclosures. The [December 13 post](https://hexnut-studios.itch.io/dead-trust/devlog/1142149/thank-you-for-150-copies-sold) says the game sold **150+ copies in its first week**. The [January 4, 2026 post](https://itch.io/devlog/1307279/thank-you-for-300-copies-sold) says it surpassed **300+ copies sold** cumulatively. These are approximate threshold disclosures, not exact transaction counts.

| Disclosure | Stated threshold | Unknown |
|---|---|---|
| December 13, 2025 | First week: 150+ copies | Exact number, start/end of first-week window, refunds, store-specific units |
| January 4, 2026 | Since release: 300+ copies | Exact number, positive-price settled orders, buyer identities, retained cash |

The disclosure posts were about **14 and 36 calendar days** after the November 29 announcement. Because the first post discusses an earlier unspecified first-week window and both figures are **lower thresholds without upper bounds**, **300 − 150 is not a verified 150-copy increment**. The developer does not explicitly identify both figures as itch-only reconciled purchases. Voluntary copy claims must not be silently converted into a store-specific order ledger.

The [December 5 promotion](https://hexnut-studios.itch.io/dead-trust/devlog/1134564/-dead-trust-is-now-on-sale-50) advertised **50% off**. The [live paid storefront](https://hexnut-studios.itch.io/dead-trust) showed a USD 1 minimum against a USD 2 regular price on October 27, 2026. A present-day price cannot prove any historical buyer's paid amount; do not multiply either milestone by USD 1 or USD 2 to invent gross receipts, after-fee earnings or profit.

**Research consequence:** these dated observations add a second-game milestone trajectory to existing paid-demand and long-tail research, but do **not** establish completed 7-, 30-, 90- or 365-day retained-order cohorts, incremental demand, market-wide seller income, causal promotion lift or game profitability. No private payments or customer records were accessed.
