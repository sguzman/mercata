# Long-horizon game returns and cancellation — one bounded 2026 account

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** [Machine-readable observations](six-year-low-return-cancellation-2026.json) · [Developer's September 17, 2026 retrospective](https://itch.io/devlog/1667716/the-manifest-shadows-over-manston-project-cancelled.amp).

The publisher of *The Manifest: Shadows over Manston* disclosed **11,200 downloads**, **48 people who paid**, and **USD 199.50 in gross receipts** over approximately six years of itch.io availability. These are self-reported cumulative figures. The income is before the marketplace's share and tax. The same author announced that development was abandoned and reported that, in no month, income exceeded project costs. The author did not give monthly revenues, cost totals, refunds, paid orders or labor hours.

## Measurement boundary

- **11,200 = downloads**, not unique people or paid orders. A buyer can download repeatedly and a free download can be recorded without payment.
- **48 = people reported as paying**, not necessarily 48 independent purchase transactions. The author did not provide a matched downloader-to-buyer funnel.
- **USD 199.50 = six-year gross**, not net proceeds, profit, typical annual salary, or a first-month earned amount.
- Dividing 48 by 11,200 would combine unlike units and cannot establish a meaningful checkout conversion rate.
- The source does not establish whether a strictly positive price was required for download throughout the six years; this must **not** be included in a mandatory-paid-launch median cohort.
- The announcement of cancellation cannot imply that all first-game projects fail or quantify the platform-wide game survival rate.

## Decision relevance

The case gives a measured *example* of considerable download activity coexisting with very little reported cash. It also gives a developer-described case of maintenance and asset-obsolescence costs making further development unattractive. It supports separate recording of download events, paying people, gross receipts, support effort and abandonment in the fixed research tasks. It **does not** fill the unresolved representative paying-buyer population, median seller income, zero-sale rate or genre-profitability gaps.

No seller-only analytics were accessed, no estimate was invented, and no probability sample was claimed.
