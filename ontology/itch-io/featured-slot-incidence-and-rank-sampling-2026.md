# Featured-feed placement incidence and sampled slot positions

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** The [Pelipulssi independent public-feed report of June 12](https://pelipulssi.com/article/superweird-itch-io-featured-feed-112-days) monitored 118,017 first-seen mixed-price game pages over 152 sampled days, of which **2,477 (about 2.1%) were ever observed in its featured-feed catalog**. This is a selected public frame, **not** the chance for a paid game or first-time publisher to be chosen.

Among 296 games first entering that featured catalog since February, only eight had at least 112 sampled days of membership by the report's June cutoff. Games entering at different dates have different opportunities for observation: the fraction **cannot be used as a stable feature-duration survival rate**.

The report's detailed free-alpha case has **652 featured sightings over 112 consecutive monitored days**, initially at rank 24. Its first five weeks averaged rank around 29–73, while later ranks were typically about 400–1,400. The featured catalog extends well below the actual front-page viewport; rank, continued membership and on-screen impressions are **different units**. Its selected-publisher background prevents generalizing to new paid sellers.

The [official indexing guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed) describes page eligibility and browse signals, not a merchant-revenue experiment. The [2018 analytics announcement](https://itch.io/updates/updates-to-project-analytics-filtering-collections-impressions-and-more) separately identifies impressions and clicks.

**Acceptance:** this directly samples rank, duration and bounded inclusion for the existing feature-slot concentration task. It does **not** estimate the complete platform's slot impression share, causal sales lift, paying customers or commercial winner concentration.
