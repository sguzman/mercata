Plan created **October 27, 2026** based on the dated official sources last reviewed October 26. [Machine review schedule](policy-payment-research-review-calendar-2026.json). The dates below are **planned future checks**, not automated monitoring or completed source reviews.

| Research area | Next proposed review | Why it is time-sensitive |
|---|---|---|

| Merchant, payment gateway, payout and FX policy | **November 26, 2026** | Provider/country support, tax validation, dispute adjustments, fees and review waits can change without the site catalogue changing. |
| Content rights, regional restrictions and payment-policy overlap | **November 26, 2026** | July 2025 restricted adult-content handling and later operator rules have different effective periods. |
| Comparable catalog and site search counters | **January 26, 2027** | Indexing reform and dynamic third-party crawlers change the eligible underlying population. |
| Historical feature/terms changelog | **January 26, 2027** | A new announcement must not overwrite previously dated sale, bundle, payout or licensing rules. |

Official anchors: [Payments](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments), [October 2026 indexing update](https://itch.io/t/7172464/october-updates-on-indexing), [Quality guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines), [Terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms), and [2026 platform feature history](store-feature-history-2026.md).

If an official policy or payment announcement arrives earlier, **the relevant subject should be reviewed before the planned date**. Each check should record the retrieval timestamp, policy effective date, old/new wording reduced to a factual summary, affected units/markets, assumptions requiring revision and regression tests to rerun. Historical claims keep their original dates and are not silently overwritten.

This plan **does not create a background automation**. It requires a future explicit source lookup; no account credentials, buyer data, merchant actions or spending. It only completes the existing Mercata maintenance-*schedule* task and does not resolve unavailable population earnings or paying-customer counts.
