# itch.io commercial-game research — evidence synthesis and open gaps

**Snapshot: October 26, 2026.** The specimen has 158 source records and 433 bounded claims. This is a research synthesis, **not a representative seller-income survey**. [Machine register](research-synthesis-2026.json).

The market has **no mandatory fee to list a game**, but publication, indexing and acquiring purchasers are separate. Payments can use configurable platform share and processor charges, and the illustrative $10 buyer payment becomes $8.41 **before** refund, tax, labor or other obligations. The public 1.78 million all-game directory, 49,588 paid-game directory and 12,691 paid-2D filter are counts of **listings**, not distinct active paid publishers or buyers.

Voluntary developer records demonstrate different outcomes but do not establish a typical first-commercial-release profit. Mixolumia reported 1,776 paid itch copies; The Hive reported 414 and $706.73 in itch revenue; an artist's free/pay-what-you-want portfolio amassed $7,276.70 mostly in tips; Not Jam's free-game-heavy category generated much less cash than its non-game product lines. These observations have different source periods, price models, genres and developer costs. They **cannot be pooled to estimate the median or probability of success**.

A 2025 engineering study and the 2026 open-source game corpus use related underlying game metadata: their sample counts cannot be added or treated as paid-seller counts. [Research ancestry](open-source-reproducibility-lineage-2025-2026.md). Free browser plays, paid downloadable copies, demo downloads, bundle game entitlements, discounts and game-jam ratings each require distinct denominators. The Skwish case separately records individually bought game units and community-bundle allocations, with unknown final profits. [Bundle receipts and limits](skwish-direct-versus-bundle-2023.md). Two older developer postmortems add a costed 2015 cross-store contrast and a 2017 visual-novel discount case, without converting them into contemporary income statistics. [Historical costed cases](historical-costed-game-outcomes-2015-2021.md). ## Missing evidence required for the goal

- **representative paid game median income:** First paid release publisher cohort including zero earners and delisted games; *currently unavailable*
- **zero sale rate:** All eligible paid-game launches observed at matching 30/90/365-day horizon; *currently unavailable*
- **paid game 2d buyers:** Unique paying buyers with anonymized, approved matched unit counts; *currently unavailable*
- **game development profit:** All development labor, taxes, sales refunds, fees and cash costs; *currently unavailable*
- **organic vs external acquisition:** Actual purchase records by matched exposure/acquisition route; *currently unavailable*
- **genre profitability:** Genre-specific selected publisher cohorts with compatible cost methods; *currently unavailable*
- **platform gmv:** Public audited aggregate seller receipts or validated first-party release; *currently unavailable*

28 of the fixed 244 original tasks had **no scored evidence yet** when this interim register was created; they remain pending, not declared completed. Other tasks have only partial evidence. No merchant or buyer accounts were accessed, no game launched, no test spending occurred. The next work must pursue only the existing unanswered research requirements, especially real paid-game income including zero earners, rather than creating unrelated new specimens or assuming missing values are zero.
