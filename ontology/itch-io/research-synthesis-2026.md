# itch.io commercial-game research — evidence synthesis and open gaps

**Snapshot: October 26, 2026.** The specimen has 142 source records and 395 bounded claims. This is a research synthesis, **not a representative seller-income survey**. [Machine register](research-synthesis-2026.json).

The market has **no mandatory fee to list a game**, but publication, indexing and acquiring purchasers are separate. Payments can use configurable platform share and processor charges, and the illustrative $10 buyer payment becomes $8.41 **before** refund, tax, labor or other obligations. The public 1.78 million all-game directory, 49,588 paid-game directory and 12,691 paid-2D filter are counts of **listings**, not distinct active paid publishers or buyers.

Voluntary developer records demonstrate different outcomes but do not establish a typical first-commercial-release profit. Mixolumia reported 1,776 paid itch copies; The Hive reported 414 and $706.73 in itch revenue; an artist's free/pay-what-you-want portfolio amassed $7,276.70 mostly in tips; Not Jam's free-game-heavy category generated much less cash than its non-game product lines. These observations have different source periods, price models, genres and developer costs. They **cannot be pooled to estimate the median or probability of success**.

A 2025 engineering study and the 2026 open-source game corpus use related underlying game metadata: their sample counts cannot be added or treated as paid-seller counts. [Research ancestry](open-source-reproducibility-lineage-2025-2026.md). Free browser plays, paid downloadable copies, demo downloads, bundle game entitlements, discounts and game-jam ratings each require distinct denominators. The Skwish case separately records individually bought game units and community-bundle allocations, with unknown final profits. [Bundle receipts and limits](skwish-direct-versus-bundle-2023.md). Two older developer postmortems add a costed 2015 cross-store contrast and a 2017 visual-novel discount case, without converting them into contemporary income statistics. [Historical costed cases](historical-costed-game-outcomes-2015-2021.md). A September 2026 developer retrospective additionally records 11,200 downloads and 48 paying people over roughly six years but does not identify matched purchases; this cannot establish a conversion rate or a representative income distribution. [Six-year developer account](six-year-low-return-cancellation-2026.md). Platform policy and payment rules create additional risks, including historical indexing changes and conditional treatment of old unclaimed payouts. The existence of a storefront or discovery feature never proves causal sales uplift.

The accessibility QA planning task has a complete evidence-backed matrix for controls, captions, visibility and supported input, with no actual product certification or paid-demand evidence. [Release accessibility checklist](accessibility-release-qa-matrix-2026.md).

The source-bounded creative-comparison design and matching exposure-to-click planning tasks are complete, without conducting an experiment or inferring sales uplift. [Creative comparison design](creative-comparison-audit-2026.md).

The listing/file/channel/build identity distinction is now a completed source-backed fixed task, with a tested offline cardinality model that never converts revisions, demos or keys into sales. [Project unit audit](project-file-build-identity-2026.md).

Two further 2025 first-person disclosures describe a zero-copy game at a roughly two-month observation window and a separate five-copy game at $1; free downloads of earlier titles and follower counts cannot form paid conversion denominators. [Dated cases](zero-five-paid-game-snapshots-2025.md).

Twelve source-linked genre-classified case studies have now been cross-checked for price, period, payment channel and labor-adjusted profit. **None** supplies a representative comparable genre-profit outcome. [Genre income comparability audit](genre-profit-commensurability-2026.md).

Refund and dispute sensitivity is separately modeled using explicit per-order reversals and merchant fee inputs; no market chargeback incidence is assumed. [Refund/chargeback cost audit](refund-chargeback-unit-cost-2026.md).

An April 2026 publisher case records sustained featured discovery of a free game alpha and roughly 2,500 Steam wishlist additions, but no paid itch sales or attributable buyer conversion. [Free-alpha discovery limits](free-alpha-frontpage-tail-2026.md).

A September 2026 first-game creator reported zero sales alongside a self-described optional-donation/paid-tier price configuration mistake, with no evidence the error caused the outcome. [First-game price incident](first-game-pricing-misconfiguration-2026.md).

The [documented buyer checkout route/region crosswalk](buyer-checkout-route-eligibility-2026.md) separates merchant processor configuration from actual accepted payment instruments and leaves unverifiable buyer-country access and actual buyer counts unknown.

The fixed multi-store legal/contract boundary task is now source-verified: itch.io licenses publisher content non-exclusively and lets developers opt into external-key distribution under separate restrictions, without certifying the publisher's outside licenses or actual offline operation. [Rights and DRM distinction](multistore-rights-external-keys-2026.md).

The [game source/asset licensing crosswalk](game-code-art-commercial-licenses-2026.md) distinguishes MIT/Apache/GPL software distribution from Creative Commons media permissions and itch.io's separate publisher license, with no game-specific compliance or profit falsely asserted.

The [first-month developer cash observations](first-month-small-game-cashflow-2025-2026.md) add a 2025 $10 reported shooter outcome with no defined gross/net basis and a 2026 first-game donation with a reconciled $49.99 gross-to-$29.87 payout-to-$14.87 remainder sequence. The latter is not a mandatory-price game sale, and neither establishes owner profit after labor or representative income.

The [neighboring game store entry analysis](neighbor-game-store-entry-frames-2026.md) records the distinctive onboarding models of itch.io, Steam, Epic, GOG and a conceptual self-hosted route; it does not infer commercial income from admission fees.

## Missing evidence required for the goal

- **representative paid game median income:** First paid release publisher cohort including zero earners and delisted games; *currently unavailable*
- **zero sale rate:** All eligible paid-game launches observed at matching 30/90/365-day horizon; *currently unavailable*
- **paid game 2d buyers:** Unique paying buyers with anonymized, approved matched unit counts; *currently unavailable*
- **game development profit:** All development labor, taxes, sales refunds, fees and cash costs; *currently unavailable*
- **organic vs external acquisition:** Actual purchase records by matched exposure/acquisition route; *currently unavailable*
- **genre profitability:** Genre-specific selected publisher cohorts with compatible cost methods; *currently unavailable*
- **platform gmv:** Public audited aggregate seller receipts or validated first-party release; *currently unavailable*

28 of the fixed 244 original tasks had **no scored evidence yet** when this interim register was created; they remain pending, not declared completed. Other tasks have only partial evidence. No merchant or buyer accounts were accessed, no game launched, no test spending occurred. The next work must pursue only the existing unanswered research requirements, especially real paid-game income including zero earners, rather than creating unrelated new specimens or assuming missing values are zero.
