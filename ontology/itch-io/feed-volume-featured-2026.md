# itch.io release feed, featured visibility and game competition (Jan–Jun 2026)

[Pelipulssi's June 12, 2026 public-feed monitor](https://pelipulssi.com/article/superweird-itch-io-featured-feed-112-days) followed new games and front-page/featured placements for approximately **152 days**, recording **118,017 games**. It reports approximately **776 first-seen games per day**. This is a *public all-price game feed*, not a complete register of new paid commercial releases, active developer businesses or paid buyers.

## Observed feature concentration

Across the observed corpus, **2,477 (about 2.1%)** appeared in the featured feed at least once. In one late-January first-seen cohort of **6,121 games**, the tracker found **4,897 without ratings (about 80%)**, and only **57 ever featured (under 1%)**. Missing player ratings must **not** be interpreted as no sales; curated featuring is not a payment event. The source's sample consists of public game pages, not a random cohort of positive-price games.

The unusual case *SuperWEIRD: Idle Automation [Alpha]* appeared in the featured feed for **112 consecutive days and 652 observed snapshots** through June 11. But appearance anywhere in a large curated feed is not necessarily first-screen exposure. The author also records the game reappearing in chronological new results after an update; this creates an important measurement boundary between **first upload, first public appearance, page update and paid release**.

## Commercial interpretation

These data show intense *supply and attention competition*, especially against established projects with existing fans. They do **not** measure the probability of being profitable or the revenue concentration of best-selling games. Counts of featured pages and ratings cannot identify median units sold, repeat purchases, developer profit or paid-game market-share concentration.
