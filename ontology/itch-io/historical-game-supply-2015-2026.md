# itch.io supply history — creation, publication and browse are different denominators

Reviewed October 26, 2026. This compares operator-published historical figures and a present-day game browse snapshot, **not annual paid-game sales or counts of sellers**.

| Period | Reported figure | Meaning | Official source |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 2015 calendar year | 23,708 | Games **created that year**, annual event flow | [itch.io 2015 review](https://itch.io/updates/itchio-year-in-review-2015) |
| 2015 calendar year | 5,581,159 | File **download events** across platform reporting; not paid units | [2015 review](https://itch.io/updates/itchio-year-in-review-2015) |
| 2015 calendar year | 27,061,715 | Views of game and project pages; not unique buyers | [2015 review](https://itch.io/updates/itchio-year-in-review-2015) |
| 2017 calendar year | 100,864 | Project pages **created** that year; all marketplace categories | [itch.io 2017 review](https://itch.io/blog/19998/itchio-year-in-review-2017) |
| 2017 calendar year | 68,526 | Those 2017 project pages **published**; all marketplace categories | [2017 review](https://itch.io/blog/19998/itchio-year-in-review-2017) |
| 2017 calendar year | 13,361 | Entries submitted to game jams, not game purchases | [2017 review](https://itch.io/blog/19998/itchio-year-in-review-2017) |
| November 2019 | 200,000 | **Cumulative games available** milestone, free or paid | [itch.io 2019 announcement](https://itch.io/blog/108659/there-are-200000-games-on-itchio-heres-how-to-find-your-new-favorite) |
| October 2026 | 1,780,861 | Result count of the public game **browse filter** | [current archive](catalog-snapshot-2026.md) |

The 2017 created-to-published page ratio is **67.94%**, but this measures *page publication within a reported annual flow*, **not the share of created games that become commercial or earn anything**. The 2019 game count and 2026 filter count imply a very large catalog expansion if considered informally, but they are **not verified comparable censuses**. The catalog itself can include updated, private, unindexed, restricted and non-game pages on different bases. We therefore do not calculate a defensible exact annual compound growth rate from them.

The historical data support two narrow conclusions: creators have been publishing many projects for years, and the site serves multiple product and attention channels. There is **no published representative count of first-time paid game publishers, unique paying customers, typical income or annual platform-wide game revenue in these sources**.
