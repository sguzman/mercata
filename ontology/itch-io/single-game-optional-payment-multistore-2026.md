# One optional-payment game that expanded beyond itch.io

Reviewed October 26, 2026. [Structured case record](single-game-optional-payment-multistore-2026.json).

On [February 14, 2026](https://alienmelon.itch.io/flower/devlog/1379934/bug-fixes-no-more-blinking-main-menu), the creator of an experimental downloadable interactive-fiction game reported **USD 155 made on itch.io**. Its listing offered name-your-own-price downloads. The developer did not publish the number of positive-payment transactions, their sizes, fees, refunded transactions or bank proceeds. The stated amount is **not evidence of 155 full-price game orders**, nor a measure of profit.

In a [February 15 postmortem](https://alienmelon.itch.io/flower/devlog/1382599/postmortem-she-danced-in-the-wind-like-a-holographic-dream-before-the-world-died), the creator described over a year of development and substantial production complexity but supplied no reliable total labor hours. A second, less precise statement that the game made more than USD 100 refers to the **same game and publisher**; it cannot be counted as a second independent payment or added to the earlier report.

On [March 16](https://alienmelon.itch.io/flower/devlog/1460326/she-danced-in-the-wind-like-a-holographic-dream-before-the-world-died-on-steam-and-epic), the creator announced that the game was on Steam and Epic and credited preceding itch.io payments with enabling the listings. This is a dated publishing sequence, not an audited financial transfer or a comparison of store conversion rates.

## Existing first-game commercialization decision route

**Offer:** optional-payment download. **Audience:** readers/players of an interactive-fiction game, uncounted as unique paying buyers. **Promotion:** public project page and updates without a matched exposure-control group. **Payment:** developer-reported 155 US dollars, exact gross/net basis unavailable. **Fulfillment:** downloadable build on itch.io, followed by public announcements of third-party distribution.

The publisher has earlier works, so this is not a representative newcomer cohort. No individual work hours, labor-valued game profit, mandatory paid units, storewise sales, recurring buyers or counterfactual promotion outcomes can be inferred. The [commercial decision guide](market-entry-decision-guide-2026.md) still treats these market-level measures as unknown.
