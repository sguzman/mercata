# Two historical paid-game seller disclosures: itch.io versus Steam (2018 and 2021)

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** [Machine evidence](historical-itch-vs-steam-low-paid-game-outcomes-2018-2021.json) · [Realm of the Ghost King postmortem](https://mantiseyelabs.itch.io/rotgk/devlog/44297/realm-of-the-ghost-king-postmortem) · [Yerba Mate Tycoon retrospective](https://donislawdev.com/my-first-indie-steam-game-revenue-first-month-in-early-access-yerba-mate-tycoon/).

This is a bounded comparison of **two voluntary developer disclosures**, not a statistically sampled population of itch.io paid-game publishers.

| Reported game and period | itch.io paid-game units | Steam units | Revenue information |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Realm of the Ghost King, 2018 author postmortem (roughly January–early July) | 12 cumulative | 121, Jan. 16–July 5 | Neither store's seller-net receipts established |
| Yerba Mate Tycoon, 2021 retrospective | 0 in the game's first itch month; 1 cumulative by the later report | 410 in the first Steam early-access month | Roughly $3.99 itch gross for one sale; $632 Steam after Steam share/VAT, before local income tax |

## Source-reported temporal detail

For **Realm of the Ghost King**, 9 of the 12 reported itch purchases occurred in January and February **combined**. Another 2 were sold during a 25%-off late-June sale. The remaining purchase is not precisely allocated. These are overlapping lifecycle descriptions of the same **12** orders, not independent cohorts or a first-30-day conversion estimate.

The corresponding Steam sequence was **72 January, 17 February, 9 March, 4 April, 2 May, 14 June and 3 July**, totaling **121**. January starts on the 16th and July ends on the 5th. The June change includes a sale, but no counterfactual, matched exposure counts, full pricing history, or cross-store spend records permit estimating a discount effect.

For **Yerba Mate Tycoon**, the author's initial itch sales observation precedes the post's 410 Steam first-month copies. The later single itch purchase does not identify a consistent 30-day, 90-day or 365-day cohort. The Steam **$632** cannot be transferred into itch revenue or classified as after-cost profit.

## Acceptance and limits

These two public cases add genuine paid-game order observations and one within-game seasonal trace, while preserving missing time alignment and accounting. They do not establish a publisher selection frame, total paid-game demand, genre earnings, representative store ratios, zero-sale rates, median income or causality. No private seller records were opened. Exact original post publication days were not verified, so source metadata records them as unknown rather than inventing dates.

The fixed 244-task study remains unfinished. The only task-scoring advances are paid-purchase-demand **68→69**, cross-store benchmarks **97→98**, and sales-volatility evidence **92→93**. None reaches verified completion.
