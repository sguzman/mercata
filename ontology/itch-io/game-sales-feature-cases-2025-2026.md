# itch.io game sales: reported launch orders and featured-placement case studies

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** Both datasets are voluntary and unverified publisher reports; they have no representative sampling frame. [Machine case register](game-sales-feature-cases-2025-2026.json).

## One-week paid launch: *no signal*

The developer's [July 31, 2025 first-week write-up](https://exodrifter.itch.io/no-signal/devlog/1021575/one-week-after-no-signal) reports **three itch.io game-and-soundtrack bundle orders**, **$75 gross** and **$63.61 estimated after store/transaction deductions**. It separately reports **239 Steam base-game units**, **$2,808 gross** and **$1,965.60 estimated after deductions**, alongside 38 soundtrack sales and further other-game sales on Steam.

The source calls those after-deduction amounts *estimates* because payment settlement was still pending. The itch SKU bundles soundtrack with game while the Steam baseline lists the game separately. Differences in product packages and buyers mean these are **not matched equivalent-unit prices or causal store comparisons**. The developer reports that overall launch receipts could approximately pay a collaborator for game art but not the developer's own living costs for a week. These observations are **not an audited profit-and-loss statement**; no comprehensive production labor ledger was given.

The developer describes a prerelease outreach campaign, but contacts selected by prior relationship and audience preferences are not randomized marketing treatments. There is no proven attributable paid order from a particular streamer, and the broader sales table spans multiple products and stores.

## Same game, different disclosed counters: *The Hive*

The [December 14, 2025 disclosure](https://skydome.itch.io/thehive/devlog/1142956/itchio-sales-reality-check-and-why-it-doesnt-mean-your-game-is-bad) reported **414 sales, $706.73 revenue and about 17,000 views**. The [May 24, 2026 update](https://itch.io/devlog/1532607/we-are-now-richer-than-jeff-bezos-on-itchio.amp) instead reports **141 payments, $819.44 gross, about 24,800 views and 510 download events**. The smaller *payments* number despite a later observation is a clear metric-definition or scope problem: it does not imply sales reversed. The unit and time-window definitions cannot be reconciled from these public sources, so **no cumulative unit-growth curve is calculated**.

In a [July 16, 2026 note](https://skydome.itch.io/thehive/devlog/1588098/we-got-featured-on-the-itchio-front-page-here-is-what-other-developers-can-expect), the author said the game's normal traffic was roughly **50–150 visits each day**, compared with approximately **7,000 visits accumulated thus far** during a front-page feature. Those numbers have **different time units**, so they cannot safely yield a feature multiplier. The author also mentioned roughly **32,000 views on an outside short video** during the period and did not publish the feature's completed paid-order result.

## Business interpretation

These records demonstrate distinct but easily confused units: paid checkout events, bundles containing games, downloaded files, *lifetime* page-view events, daily traffic rates, unsettled estimated receipts, and true labor-adjusted developer profit. None equals a representative probability that a new 2D game will sell, nor do they establish that featured placement caused purchases. Outcomes that lack aligned transactions and windows remain unknown.
