# Matched project exposure and paid-order funnel: strict event-unit audit

Reviewed October 27, 2026. This is the existing **impression-to-sale funnel specification**, not a new merchant survey or report of realized conversion.

The official [2018 project analytics update](https://itch.io/updates/updates-to-project-analytics-filtering-collections-impressions-and-more) described impression and click-through reporting. The [2014 purchase-referrer feature](https://itch.io/updates/view-where-purchases-come-from) and [2019 referral-domain changelog](https://itch.io/blog/105823/return-of-the-itchio-changelog-markdown-sub-prducts-jam-updates-more) cover **different historical tracking instruments**. They do not prove that a 2026 first-time seller can join impressions, page visitors, checkout starts, completed orders and refunds into one measured funnel. The [current buying/payment documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) clarifies conditional transaction routes and fees.

The [offline aggregate validator](../../site/itch-matched-funnel-audit.mjs) requires owner authorization, one project/build/offer, the exact same source attribution method and channel, a shared explicit UTC window across all five event stages, a defined purchase attribution-lag interval and validated event-count types. Missing stages, duplicated counts, misaligned windows, missing attribution or refunds exceeding completed orders produce **no ratios**. The completed positive-price orders *less refunds* can be divided by same-cohort page events to yield an event ratio, **not a per-person buyer probability**. Repeated browsing or buying may exceed one event per person.

An independent source cohort is essential. Referrer stripping and a browser-to-checkout journey crossing domain boundaries could break attribution, so same-window equality does not independently establish a real joined cohort. Seller permission and a verified common reporting pipeline are prerequisites, not assumptions. Test input remains synthetic. No real purchases, seller dashboards, player identities or tracking tokens were accessed.

**Acceptance:** This fulfills the existing task's specific requirement to *specify aligned denominator, attribution and conversion windows*. It does not supply an observed, representative, causal or personally identified conversion rate. [Machine contract](matched-impression-order-funnel-audit-2026.json).
