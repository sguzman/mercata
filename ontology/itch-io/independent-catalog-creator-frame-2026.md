# Third-party tracked creators are not active paid sellers

Reviewed **October 26, 2026**. [Structured observation and limitations](independent-catalog-creator-frame-2026.json).

The independent [ItchDB catalog](https://itchdb.com/) displayed **400,641 indexed games, 65,273 creator profiles and 215,920 ranked tags** at capture. It follows public rating trends and rankings, not confidential game sales. The publicly displayed totals are **dynamic** and the complete catalog inclusion frame, source crawl schedule and identity linkage were not independently verified.

Compare carefully with the [October 19 official itch.io indexing update](https://itch.io/t/7172464/october-updates-on-indexing), which described **well over two million indexed project pages**, including many non-game project types. That figure and a third-party game-only index are **not comparable denominators**. It would be invalid to divide 400,641 by the platform total to infer game market coverage, active sellers, or the share of games earning money.

| Visible unit | What it represents | Not established |
|---|---|---|
| Indexed game entry | A project in one external crawler's observed frame | Verified positive-price title or new commercial release |
| Indexed creator profile | An account/profile indexed by the third party | Active, unique, tax-onboarded, or paid merchant |
| Ranked tag | A discoverability classification | Human search demand or purchases |
| Rating growth | Audience/feedback activity | Retained paid orders or actual profit |
| Public followers | Potential notifications | Distinct paying customers |

For the existing **active-seller** study, a credible count would need an eligible merchant account frame, separate dormant/free-only/paid sellers, deduplication across team contributors and portfolios, and at least one actual retained paid order in a declared period. An independent ranking site's creator counter is not that. This research does not access seller accounts or buy-side analytics.

**Status:** meaningful extra public coverage evidence; actual active paid seller counts, paid game demand, country reach and earnings remain **unknown**, so those tasks remain incomplete.
