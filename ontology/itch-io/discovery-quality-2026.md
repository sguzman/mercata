# itch.io — discoverability, community and release-quality boundaries

Reviewed October 26, 2026 against the [official quality guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines), [followers guide](https://itch.io/docs/accounts/followers), [fan interaction guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/interact), [indexed discovery guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed) and [limited playtest guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/limited-releases). [Machine crosswalk](discovery-quality-2026.json).

**The difference between publication and customer acquisition extends to compliance and community activity.** itch.io permits tags and accessibility/language classifications, but the guidance requires them to describe the game accurately. Platform checkboxes should be set only for builds directly tested on those platforms; 2D tags should describe actual content, not be abused to capture unrelated browse traffic. A text-free title should use a no-text accessibility classification rather than claiming every translated language.

## AI-generated game materials and repeat uploads

Current quality guidance asks publishers to disclose generative AI materials with an AI Disclosure field. It distinguishes generative tools from conventional NPC pathfinding, procedural generation, dynamic difficulty and similar game algorithms. Its *strict enforcement* statement is specifically about **game asset pages**, not a universal ban on AI-assisted indie games. The guide also discourages mass-generated near-identical projects and minimally curated automated content. These are documented publication and indexing risks; there is no measured AI-related conversion or seller ban rate in the evidence.

## Early audiences and community channels

Followers can receive project launch, devlog and sale notifications, while discussions, press links and ratings offer separate social evidence. These are not interchangeable with **paying customers** or independent app-store revenue. Ratings need not track paid purchases; a follower email notification is not confirmation of delivery, open or payment. The [limited playtest guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/limited-releases) supports restricted preview access and limited distribution keys, separate from early public release. It cautions that publishing a visibly unfinished early-access game may affect later launch attention, but does not quantify a causal revenue penalty.

## Sales and jam promotion boundaries

The quality guide discourages perpetually discounted products and artificial original-price inflation followed by a sale. It treats ongoing bundle-price reductions differently from fake standalone discounts. Unrelated game-jam submissions and promotional-only jams can lead to enforcement. Genuine jam participation can supply playtests and attention, but jam ratings and page visits do **not** establish paid game purchase intent.
