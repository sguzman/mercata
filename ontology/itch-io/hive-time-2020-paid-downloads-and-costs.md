# Hive Time: costed pay-what-you-want game and unmatched download events, 2020

[First-party developer financial retrospective](https://cheesetalks.net/hive-time-finances.php) covering a December 2019 game release through about September 2020. [Machine case](hive-time-2020-paid-downloads-and-costs.json). Reviewed October 26, 2026. The page does not provide a reliably established publication day, so it is recorded as **unknown**, rather than inferred from its discussion of 2020.

## Cash expenditures and valued work are different

The author itemized the initial release's costs in **Australian dollars** (unless otherwise noted), including approximately 1,050 hours of pre-release development at an imputed hourly rate of AUD 19.49:

| Cost allocation reported by developer | AUD |
|---|---:|
| Initial total economic-cost account | 25,131.37 |
| Valued noncash/in-kind labor and contributions | −20,956.85 |
| **Adjusted initial out-of-pocket cash costs** | **4,174.52** |
| Subsequent major update total cost account | 3,910.42 |
| Valued in-kind component of update | −3,860.84 |
| **Adjusted update out-of-pocket cost** | **49.58** |

The numbers reconcile arithmetically but **must not be subtracted indiscriminately from USD receipts**: different currencies, timing and valuation bases apply. Some testing and ordinary overhead remain unpriced. The budget uses an imputed labor value for work that was not paid as cash wages, so low actual cash spending is **not zero development cost or profit**.

## Buyer payments are not equivalent to player downloads

The developer reported **500 paid sales around July 2020** and just under **550** by the September narrative, alongside **40,018 download events**. The source described a **1.36% paid-sales-to-download-event ratio**, but acknowledged that repeat downloads and updates made this denominator unreliable as a count of distinct users. Halving the denominator to suggest **2.72%** was an *illustrative assumption*, not a verified buyer conversion rate. The author had initially targeted 10% full-price-equivalent sales per download, which was a business forecast rather than an observed rate.

The reported mean positive payment was approximately **USD 9.15**, but the precise retained cash after all platform fees, currency conversions, project expenses and total labor was not disclosed as a complete audited profit. The creator also mentioned roughly **815,000 charitable bundle owners**, explicitly **excluded** from standalone game sales; bundle entitlements do not mean that many fresh commercial transactions for the title.

## Economic and research boundary

This is a particularly useful named case because it provides both developer-valued work and a separate cash-spending adjustment, as well as concrete paid and free events. It remains **one 2019–2020 pay-what-you-want simulation game** with nonrandom publicity, one creator's support audience and multiple software updates. It cannot establish median or mean 2026 new paid-game profit, representative sales conversion, paid-buyer population, first-release zero-sale fraction, or a causal benefit from homepage placement or promotions.
