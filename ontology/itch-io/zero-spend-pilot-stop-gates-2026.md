# Commercial-pilot downside and stop-condition gate

**Policy reviewed October 27, 2026.** Population: a prospective consenting seller-authorized itch.io game trial. Unit: one pilot, a nonduplicated paid-order/visit event or recorded cash/labor amount. No actual account, spending, game publication or user-level measurement was undertaken.

Before owner approval, the proposed project's executable budget is **$0 external cash and 0 commercial pilot work minutes**. Do not open accounts, spend advertising money, publish a paid release, collect buyer records or test paid checkout merely because a research document describes an experiment. This is an **internal execution gate**, not a claim that itch.io charges $0 for every possible transaction.

The [platform terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) require distribution rights; [quality guidance](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines) requires truthful disclosure and warns of account/payment sanctions. [Payment guidance](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) establishes merchant-route, tax, dispute and withdrawal constraints. [Pricing](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing), [buyer mechanics](https://itch.io/docs/buying) and [HTML5 guidance](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5) distinguish paid game orders from discounted/free plays. Account security and consented evidence handling are separate prelaunch checks.

An owner-approved pilot must first record **finite explicit** maximum cash, creator minutes, calendar days, minimum eligible-view events, and minimum *refunded-adjusted positive-payment orders* for any evidentiary sufficiency claim. The [offline guard](../../site/itch-commercial-pilot-guard.mjs) refuses an unapproved pilot; missing caps or unresolved rights, payment/tax eligibility, QA, honest listing or research consent blocks execution. Any positive cash limit reached, or an attempted expense under an approved $0-cash limit, stops further execution. Labor and calendar caps stop at the exact boundary, not only after an overrun. Calendar and labor caps, and minimum page-view and retained-order evidence thresholds, must be strictly positive; zero evidence is never sufficient. A separately approved no-spend pilot can continue below its positive labor/calendar caps without spending money. Invalid refund totals also stop the protocol. Passing thresholds does **not** certify profit, representative market success or causal lift. Missing volume is inconclusive, not proof the game cannot sell.

**Acceptance status:** the existing *set downside limits* task is completed at the research-policy level, because legal/time/cash exposure is explicitly bounded. The related *set stop conditions* task is advanced but remains open pending an owner's numeric approval thresholds. Commercial execution remains unauthorized; no numerical labor cap beyond zero preauthorization is invented. [Machine policy](zero-spend-pilot-stop-gates-2026.json).
