# itch.io buyer game entitlement, gifting and account resale

October 26, 2026 — platform sources: [Terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms), [checkout](https://itch.io/docs/creators/how-buying-works), [download keys](https://itch.io/docs/creators/download-keys), and [buyer access](https://itch.io/docs/buying/already-bought).

Publishers retain copyright and users receive a non-exclusive license for the files they have rights to access. The Terms describe a perpetual content license that survives a publisher's later removal from itch.io; buying does **not** transfer the game's underlying copyright.

A customer may purchase a new game *as a gift* to another recipient. That checkout feature is different from reselling an already-purchased entitlement. A unique download-key URL is intended for its legitimate holder and should not be publicly shared. Buyers can later redownload updated files without generating a second sale.

The Terms expressly bar the **sale, assignment or transfer of user accounts**. This contract rule should not be extrapolated into a universal finding that a particular digital game license can or cannot be resold under all jurisdictions and product-specific licenses. The public sources did not establish a generic secondary-game-entitlement transfer mechanism; that question remains **unverified**.

[Machine model](resale-transfer-2026.json) maintains separate counters for buyer payment, entitlement, game copyright, gifts, download keys, repeated downloads and the unresolved secondary market. No actual customer or account data were accessed.
