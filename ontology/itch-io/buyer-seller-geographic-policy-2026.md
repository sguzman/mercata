# itch.io geographic reach — distinguish buyers, sellers and past entitlements

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** The source is a [July 27, 2025 official operator policy announcement](https://itch.io/t/5133739/our-update-on-the-uk-online-safety-act), not a live publisher-account check. Machine crosswalk: [buyer-seller-geographic-policy-2026.json](buyer-seller-geographic-policy-2026.json).

## A documented geographic buyer-access restriction

In July 2025 itch.io announced restrictions on **UK visitors' access to adult/NSFW-classified project pages**, responding to UK online safety legislation. The announcement described classification through publishers' sensitive-content settings and certain adult tags, with possible human overrides. It did **not** state that all game pages, all UK purchasers or all international buyers had been banned. Nor did it enumerate how many eligible positive-priced games or paying customers were affected.

The operator described a **Geo Restrictions** dashboard section for a publisher to view the reason and appeal erroneous classification to staff. A published route to request review does not establish a successful appeal or a response-time guarantee. The same announcement said **existing buyers could still retrieve files through their library or the itch app**, even though a restricted game's storefront page would not be available in the UK. This is a time-bounded statement, not an assurance that every future policy change preserves access.

## Distinct commercial geography populations

For research, keep four populations separate: (1) anyone in a country who can visit a public page, (2) people legally able to purchase a specific game, (3) previously paying people able to redownload it, and (4) developers in a jurisdiction approved to receive seller payouts. UK category-specific content classification says nothing by itself about seller eligibility in Mexico, US buyers or an international game's entire market.

The [payment guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) establishes merchant-of-record and checkout differences between direct and collected payment routes. It does not solve the full country matrix of payout approvals. Without authorized seller observations and comparable game-level sales data, neither a UK revenue-loss amount nor a countrywide paid buyer denominator is defensible.

## Limits

The **exact October 2026 UK classification state is not independently confirmed here**; the policy source was published in July 2025 and may have been revised. No account was accessed, restricted project examined, buyer profiled or eligibility inferred. Counts of affected paid developers, denied UK buyers and realized lost profits remain explicitly unknown.
