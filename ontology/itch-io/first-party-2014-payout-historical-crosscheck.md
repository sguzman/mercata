# December 2014 founder interview — payout and catalog denominators

[December 1, 2014 interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/q-a-itch-io-interview-with-leaf-corcoran) with itch.io's founder, read with the [September 16, 2014 operator study](https://itch.io/blog/1/running-an-indie-game-store). [Machine facts](first-party-2014-payout-historical-crosscheck.json). Reviewed October 26, 2026.

The September operator note stated that **more than $100,000 had been paid to developers** since launch, while the later interview gave a roughly **$170,000 cumulative developer payout** figure and identified **6,666 published active game pages** in early December 2014. The founder explicitly said he treated dollars of different supported currencies at nominal parity in the latter estimate.

These are valuable **dated operator-reported historical scale measurements**. They cannot be subtracted to get an exact September–December transaction flow because the opening amount is a strict lower bound, the closing figure is approximate, and currency conventions were not normalized. They also do not describe the same population as an eligible paid-game publisher cohort.

Most importantly, **published active game pages are not unique people selling games**, and free and browser games contribute to the directory. Dividing $170,000 by 6,666 would misrepresent average seller earnings: cumulative payments are a money flow across different publication dates, while the page count is a one-day stock of active listings including free pages. The interview gives neither zero-sale publisher counts nor creator labor, median paid-game income, profit, platform GMV or current revenue.

This extends the already registered 2014 founder baseline without constructing a current paid-market income estimate from unrelated quantities.
