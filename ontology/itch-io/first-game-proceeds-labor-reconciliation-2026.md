# Gross game receipts are not the seller's wage

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** [Structured accounting and labor audit](first-game-proceeds-labor-reconciliation-2026.json). It reconciles Mercata's existing [case labor register](developer-labor-and-small-game-cash-2026.json), [first-month reports](first-month-small-game-cashflow-2025-2026.json) and [payment rules](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments). No new merchant payment records were accessed.

**Eight quantities must not be conflated:** buyer gross charges; retained charges after refunds; seller proceeds after processing; actual paid-out cash (after withdrawal FX and withholding); project cash surplus after explicit expenses; economic profit after appropriately valued labor; cash surplus divided by *known* development hours; and fully costed return divided by *all* project work hours.

## Three bounded creator examples

| Disclosure | Reported amounts and period | Why full labor-profit remains unknown |
|---|---|---|
| AraCore, first five days across stores | Approximately **£190 receipts, £185 costs, £5 cash-accounting surplus**, and **1,500 development hours** | £5 ÷ 1,500 is **£0.0033 per known development hour**. Not a wage or total-hour return; QA, marketing, support and tax hours are missing |
| Rose Seed Replica, first week across stores | Around **€1,000 receipts**, with a separate roughly **€17,000 two-year cost allocation** that changes with grants | Different accounting periods, financial support and allocation rules; real platform-specific receipts and labor hours unavailable |
| Basement Raid, first month | Author described approximately **$10 made**, with incomplete weekly work schedule descriptions | Gross/net payout basis and complete project work hours unavailable; cannot infer two $5 units or profit |

AraCore illustrates the difference between observed cash and hypothetical opportunity cost: if a person values its 1,500 disclosed development hours at **£10/hour**, the cash balance *after that assumed labor valuation* becomes **negative £14,995**. This is a **what-if sensitivity**, not an audited accounting loss or evidence of the actual person's wages. Additional undocumented hours stay unknown.

## Complete work-log requirement

Measure time for creative conception, design/coding, third-party art/music and license review, build packaging and operating-system QA, localization/accessibility, promotional copy/social outreach, tax and seller administration, patches, and postlaunch customer support. Exclude no hours because the game is small or the engine has a free license. Assign one explicit valuation or distinguish cash-only surplus from unpriced labor; maintain the same currency and release period.

Never average GBP, EUR and USD anecdotes, early launch outcomes and multi-year lifetime money into a paid-seller median. The actual 2026 itch.io average or median hourly profit, representative developer earnings and complete costs **remain unknown**. The tasks accepted here concern definitions, observed per-case arithmetic and required cost completeness, not population profitability.
