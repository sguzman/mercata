# Operator-verified 2026 summer-sale gross-to-payable example

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** In the public [July 2026 seller support discussion](https://itch.io/t/6596992/pending-balance-not-updating), a publisher questioned why its sale tracker and pending balance appeared inconsistent. The itch.io administrator **examined that seller account** and publicly explained the financial waterfall. [Structured evidence](2026-operator-verified-summer-sale-gross-payout-waterfall.json).

| Measurement | Reported figure | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Summer sale purchase events | **113** | Confirmed by site administrator |
| Sale tracker gross | **$620.79** | Seller-reported screen figure; operator rounds to about $620 |
| After processing and itch.io revenue share | **about $535** | Operator-reconciled estimated net, before withholding |
| This seller's tax withholding | **30%** | Account-specific tax interview outcome |
| After withholding, amount payable | **about $375** | Operator-reconciled estimated balance, **not a verified bank deposit** |

**Do not fabricate exact fee amounts by subtracting an approximate $535 from the cents-precise $620.79.** The sale tracker contains **bundle and individual-item sales**, not a controlled cohort of positive-price digital video games. A purchase is an event, not necessarily a distinct buyer, and the public thread is a selected accounting question, not an all-seller sample.

The sale ended **July 6, 2026**. The operator explained that recent earnings remain in **pending for about seven days**, then move to **available**. Comparing the entire multi-day sale's *gross* with only *pending net* is mathematically invalid. Other account sales also enter the balance buckets. Taxes withheld are not the buyer's VAT or the author's eventual individual income tax; settlement, bank fees and production costs remain unobserved. The [official payment guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) supports the seven-day hold, fee deductions and individual withholding rules.

This case supplies unusually strong evidence of the **gross → after-fee → after-withholding** distinction because the site operator checked the specific account. It **does not** identify marketplace GMV, seller income distribution, game-only paid purchase demand, refunds, settled bank receipts or profitability. No linked private seller spreadsheets, buyer records or account information were accessed.
