# itch.io paid 2D game counts: *Blobun* at launch and after one year

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** These are independently authored interpretations of public publisher disclosures, not platform-verified transaction records. [Machine snapshot](blobun-and-pwyw-copy-counts-2025-2026.json).

## The same commercial puzzle game, observed twice

*Blobun*'s [March 2025 developer report](https://cyansorcery.itch.io/blobun/devlog/912359/how-blobun-did), stated as current to March 25, recorded **143 outright itch.io purchases** and **$1,635 gross itch.io purchases**, which included earlier early-access income. It separately reported approximately **1,984 charity-bundle library additions**, **2,059 full-game download events** and **470 demo downloads**. These are completely different counts. A user can receive a bundle entitlement without purchasing the game individually, and a person may download files more than once.

The [March 11, 2026 one-year report](https://itch.io/devlog/1455287/one-year-of-blobun.amp) recorded **271 itch.io direct paid copies** plus **1,420 Steam copies**, excluding itch.io bundle recipients from the direct paid tally and excluding itch-to-Steam key claims from new Steam purchases. This is one developer's evolving game rather than two independent cases. Subtracting the earlier 143 from the later 271 produces 128 additional reported direct units **conditional on the categories being defined consistently**; a full original transaction ledger was not available.

The same one-year report described roughly **$11,700 received after certain fees and taxes from Steam, itch.io and selected bundle allocations together**. It does not disclose *itch.io-only* retained proceeds or labor-adjusted game profit. The publisher described a **$14.99 original price**, a later **$9.99 price**, promotional periods and subsequent fixes/updates, but no comparable control group exists to estimate the causal benefit of a price reduction.

## A large optional-price copy milestone is not a paid-revenue census

A different developer's [August 11, 2026 ERROR143 note](https://itch.io/devlog/1624789/error143-recently-hit-100000-copies-sold.amp) celebrates **100,000 copies sold**, but the game page offers **Name your own price** and the post does not specify how many copies were *actually paid for* or how much money the developer received. We preserve the publisher's own label, while keeping actual positive-payment orders, zero-price downloads and net income **unknown**.

## Research implications

Direct purchases, bundle entitlements, download events, external-store key redemptions and optional-payment copies must be separated before studying income, active customers and the long tail. *Blobun* supplies concrete observations for an eligible commercial game and a one-year time horizon; it cannot estimate the market-wide median or zero-sale rate because its publisher voluntarily disclosed results and no sampling frame includes unsuccessful games.
