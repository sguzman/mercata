# Public Top Sellers position turnover is not economic concentration

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** An independent monitor's [itch.io Top Sellers chart](https://www.gameindustrytrends.com/chart/itch/top-sellers/all) provided a **2026-10-23 12:00 UTC** observation, reporting **367 distinct project pages** within the top 100 across **182 ranking reads** over approximately 30 days. It also reported **39%** of that day's top 100 was not in the top 100 on October 16. These observations describe **chart positions**, not copies sold. 

The monitor separately provides a [dated October 23 archive](https://www.gameindustrytrends.com/chart/itch/top-sellers/all/on/2026-10-23), whose **18:00 UTC** final top-100 walk reports **7,197** games in its chart display. This is an independently crawled subset of ranked public game pages and not a census of eligible paid-game sellers. The 12:00 dynamic summary and the 18:00 archive are **different observation times**, so they are kept as separate rows.

The [collector methodology](https://www.gameindustrytrends.com/methodology) states that itch.io's published browse ordering is captured separately from the site's own engagement-based *Velocity* score. Historical rank tracking starts when a game first enters the collector and missed days are not backfilled. A game may be a demo, free release, playtest or paid title. The visible public ranking carries **no individual settled order totals, gross revenue, fee-adjusted proceeds, or buyer counts**.

Therefore **367 unique chart entries / 182 observations** is a direct measure of *listing churn in a sampled leaderboard*, not 367 active paid sellers or evidence about inequality in game earnings. The **39%** seven-day top-100 turnover similarly says nothing about what proportion of payments went to recently ranked games. No cumulative ranked-page count can substitute for a revenue numerator and complete paid-seller denominator.

**Research acceptance remains partial:** the ranking-visibility side of bestseller concentration is more precisely documented, but a statement about economic dominance still requires actual compatible paid-game receipts including zero sellers, time horizons, refunds, fees and game-to-publisher deduplication. No content, personal data, or full scraped listing was copied into Mercata.
