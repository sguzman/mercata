# Three game titles across the same creator's October 2020 and November 2024 snapshots

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** Primary [October 24, 2020 developer disclosure](https://spiralatlas.github.io/downloads-versus-payments-on-my-pay-what-you-want-projects/) and its [November 9, 2024 follow-up](https://spiralatlas.github.io/how-much-i-get-paid-for-my-games-and-game-dev-tools-on-itchio/). [Machine pairings](spiral-atlas-four-year-game-payment-deltas-2020-2024.json).

The posts identify the **same publisher account and the same three game titles** at two dates. Rather than stacking them as six observations, the following table computes differences between self-reported **lifetime cumulative** figures.

| Title | 2020 downloads / payments / revenue | 2024 downloads / payments / revenue | Difference in downloads / payments / revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northanger Abbey: The Game | 1,848 / 12 / $138 | 3,779 / 36 / $339 | +1,931 / +24 / +$201 |
| SOON | 452 / 12 / $81 | 614 / 13 / $86 | +162 / +1 / +$5 |
| Perpixeled | 122 / 6 / $32 | 181 / 6 / $32 | +59 / +0 / +$0 |

The comparison shows **heterogeneous longer-horizon receipt changes within one developer's own catalog**: one title continued receiving multiple payments while another received no additional reported payment despite more download events. It cannot demonstrate platform seasonality, universal paid-game conversion, paid-launch survivorship rates, annualized earnings or an indie game genre ranking.

**Source arithmetic cautions:** the original October 2020 post prints a 0.5% purchase/download ratio for six Perpixeled purchases over 122 downloads. Those event counts give approximately **4.92%**, not 0.5%. The November 2024 table similarly prints 0.3% for six over 181, instead of the arithmetic **3.31%**. Both published ratios remain preserved in the structured evidence alongside the calculated differences, so numerical corrections are not silently attributed to the author.

The reported payments are predominantly optional pay-what-you-want contributions. Download events can include repeat downloads, and neither snapshot provides unique matched buyer identities or total production labor. Calculated four-year revenue changes are a difference in cumulative *reported receipts*, **not** profit, a four-year net paycheck, or an average seller outcome.

The creator also describes the project's earlier sales being influenced by purchases from familiar supporters. This is a relevant selection/confounding factor for first-game acquisition, not proof of causal effects across other developers. No private account data were accessed.
