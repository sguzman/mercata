# Search, social, referral, newsletters and paid traffic: acquisition-channel comparison

**Unit of comparison:** evidence quality, source visibility, cash/labor obligations and observation units, **not** unmeasured channel success or platform-wide sales. Sources include itch.io's dated [2014 purchase-referrer announcement](https://itch.io/updates/view-where-purchases-come-from), [2019 referral analytics update](https://itch.io/blog/105823/return-of-the-itchio-changelog-markdown-sub-prducts-jam-updates-more), [Search & Browse guidance](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed) and [follower notifications](https://itch.io/docs/accounts/followers).

| Channel | Evidence for possible discovery | Required independent measurement | Cash/time and uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform Search & Browse | Public indexing and creator exposure reports | Same-title dated eligible listing, impressions, page visits and positive-price orders | Rank is not revenue or guaranteed inclusion |
| External search | Historically supported referrer-domain reporting | Verified engine referrer; distinguish ads from unpaid results | Browser suppression and mixed organic/paid referrals |
| Social/forums | Externally referred page links and posts | Dated campaign URL with separate view, click, order and labor units | Same domain contains unrelated marketing sources |
| Followers / newsletters | Follower opt-in feed and platform email notices | Eligible recipients, notification delivery/open and attributable paid orders | Followers are not unique customers; outside newsletters not built in by implication |
| Press/influencer mentions | Public coverage may refer viewers to a store | Specific article/video attribution, not domain guess | Earned exposure vs paid sponsorship must be declared |
| Paid acquisition | Externally purchased campaigns can link to project URLs | Actual invoices/spend, attributed checkout events and refunds, ideally randomized holdout | Neither native paid listing inventory nor profitable ROI established |

**Historical instrument constraint:** the 2014 purchase interface reported purchase location and referrers, while a 2019 seller dashboard update described referral views by domain and time. These are distinct event units; dividing mismatched event counts does not estimate conversion probability. No live 2026 seller dashboard was accessed.

For an actual owner-authorized comparison, fix game ID, offer, price and currency, discovery/indexing state and a common time window; log source/medium and attributable visits, verified positive-price settled orders, refund status and development/marketing hours and cash. A missing referrer is **unknown**, not confirmed direct. The [2025 voluntary traffic benchmark](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/12/benchmark-itch-io-traffic/) had 169 contributors but no representative paid-sales ROAS frame.

**Acceptance:** This finishes the existing source-backed task to **map and compare** channel mechanisms and measurable units. It does not assert that any channel outperforms another, an actual customer acquisition cost, email success probability, or a causal sales lift.
