# Two 2025 game-sales snapshots: zero copies and five copies

Sources reviewed October 26, 2026. [Machine evidence](zero-five-paid-game-snapshots-2025.json).

## Risk & Riches

The [May 30, 2025 developer postmortem](https://growlerygames.itch.io/risk-and-riches/devlog/956226/postmortem-risk-riches) states that the 2D roguelite deckbuilder, launched March 27, had sold **zero itch.io copies** by the report date, roughly 64 days after release. The solo publisher described spending **several days** creating and updating the itch build despite no corresponding sales at that checkpoint. This was the developer's first itch.io/Steam launch, **not** the first software or game project of their career. The author reported that some itch page metadata had initially been underused. Neither exact hours, paid price history, financial loss, lifetime final sales, Steam sales nor the causal effect of metadata or promotion was disclosed. The page is now downloadable without a mandatory listed price, which cannot establish whether payment was mandatory at all earlier dates.

## End of the Night

In a [June 2, 2025 public itch.io forum disclosure](https://itch.io/t/4942942/im-done-thank-you-and-goodbye), a second game creator reported **five copies of End of the Night** at a stated **USD 1 price**. The same creator mentioned **over 10,000 free downloads largely of other Liminal Leap chapters** and **over 300 followers** of the publisher account. These units cannot be divided into a paid conversion rate because they concern different titles and populations. Five multiplied by a $1 price is **$5 of list-price arithmetic**, not audited gross receipts, after-fee payout, production profit or take-home income. Later commenters described new purchases after the opening post; five is therefore **not the lifetime upper limit**.

## Interpretation

These selected reports add dated low-sale examples to the existing research. They cannot estimate the overall zero-sale fraction, median or mean first-launch income, conversion, or genre profitability without a complete comparable publisher cohort, aligned horizons, matching price history, nonresponding titles and reconciled transactions. The counts remain individual voluntary observations only.
