# itch.io paid 2D — public catalog and price pilot (2026-10-26)

The [Paid 2D tag intersection](https://itch.io/games/store/tag-2d) showed **12,691 public browse results**. On the same date, the [all-2D filter](https://itch.io/games/tag-2d) showed 428,856 results and the [all paid-games filter](https://itch.io/games/store) showed 49,588 results. The paid-2D intersection is about **2.96% of the all-2D results**. This is the relationship between directory counters, **not the fraction of 2D developers who make sales**.

The public page's **Popular** sorting exposes sale-badge prices, but does not show actual paid units or realized publisher earnings. We recorded the first 15 visible games and their displayed USD amounts in [machine data](paid-2d-popular-prices-2026.json), including items temporarily discounted to **$0** within the Paid filter. This is an **intentionally biased top-ranking convenience sample**. Its price range and genre mix must not be reported as the median or average price of all commercial itch.io games.

## Interpretation limits

- A listing returned under Paid is not necessarily a product that made a single sale.
- Some temporary 100% discounts remain in the paid results; thus filter membership does not guarantee a positive **displayed** sale price at the observation time.
- The Popular ranking overrepresents visible and established titles; the count is not a representative newcomer sample.
- A game's visible price is not the realized price paid per buyer after promotions, tips, refunds or bundle allocation.
- The page provides no seller labor, refunds, retention or profit information. Estimates remain unknown.

No purchase or account access occurred. The complete source URLs, filtered counts and dated listings are preserved for repeatable comparison, but this alone does not complete the task of measuring the market-wide paid-game price distribution.


## Exact descriptive math for these fifteen visible results only

Sorting the original **15 observed USD listing prices** produces a **sample median of $5.99**, including two temporarily **$0 displayed** listings with 100%-off sale badges. Removing just those two produces **13 positive displayed prices**, with a sample median of **$10.00**. The displayed positive-price range is $0.74–$25.00. Four listings were below $5 but above $0, two were $5–$9.99, and seven displayed $10 or more.

These are **medians of a biased 15-title visible Popular sample, not medians of all paid games**; there is no random sampling, sale-volume weighting, paid-order evidence, refund record or creator-net income. The conditional $10 median says nothing about what typical games earn, even among the 12,691 paid 2D browse results. The exhaustive price-band counts are stored in the existing [machine study](paid-2d-popular-prices-2026.json) so they can be rederived without guessing from summary prose.
