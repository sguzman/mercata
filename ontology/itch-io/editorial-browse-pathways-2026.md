# Official editorial and ranking discovery mechanisms — October 27, 2026

The [Search & Browse guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed) distinguishes initial release, indexed title search, accurate category/tag browse, followers, collections and devlogs. Initial publishing cannot be repeated just to reset Most Recent; false metadata and synthetic promotion are disallowed by [quality guidance](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines).

A separately documented **editorial pathway** exists: the [page-design guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/design) describes a staff preference for thoughtful page customization when considering features. It does not promise promotion, ranking weights, or revenue. The [2018 impressions feature](https://itch.io/updates/updates-to-project-analytics-filtering-collections-impressions-and-more) reported visibility/clicks, not paying customers. The already recorded [2026 independent feed sample](feed-volume-featured-2026.md) counted 118,017 first-seen games and 2,477 ever-featured entries; this is neither a paid-game feature rate nor a causal experiment.

**Population:** source-described projects and selected feed. **Unit:** eligibility rule, impression or listing, not human paying buyers. Genuine measured order lift needs eligible paid game cohort, surface/time, existing fans, paid traffic, click events and owner-authorized refunded-adjusted orders. **Unknown:** algorithm weights, editorial acceptance rates and incremental publisher profit.

This closes the existing *document publicly described editorial and ranking mechanisms* task, not any actual placement trial. [Machine study](editorial-browse-pathways-2026.json).
