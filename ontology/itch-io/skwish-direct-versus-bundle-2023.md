# Skwish: separate paid game orders and game-bundle allocations

[January 21, 2023 developer report](https://pawprints.itch.io/skwish-playdate/devlog/479222/releasing-my-sales-figures) · [Structured evidence](skwish-direct-versus-bundle-2023.json). Reviewed October 26, 2026.

The publisher identified *Skwish for Playdate* as a first paid game released July 23, 2022, following earlier web-game work. In a six-month retrospective with a few preorder days, the publisher reported **591 individually purchased copies** and approximately **USD 2,300** from these sales, including discounts and tips. Separately, **852 copies delivered within a community bundle** corresponded to approximately **USD 2,000** allocated to the developer. The publisher rounded the combined quantities to about **1,400 copies and over USD 4,000 in receipts**.

A bundle entitlement is **not** an additional standalone purchase and is not an independent unique paying customer. There is no basis to assign a uniform revenue-per-copy figure to these two differently priced channels or determine cross-route buyer overlap.

The report states that collaborator compensation for music and sound, marketplace share, and payment processor fees reduced the developer's actual receipts. Exact deductions, labor time, taxes and profit remain **unknown**. The author also reported **188 demo download events** but disputed the accuracy of that counter; no observed demo-to-customer conversion can be reconstructed.

This example is a voluntarily published early Playdate-device title that benefited from promotional attention and bundle participation. It does **not** estimate what other new publishers, genres or current 2026 games typically earn.
