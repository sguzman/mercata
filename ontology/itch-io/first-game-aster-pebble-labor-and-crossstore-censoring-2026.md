The authors describe substantial branching dialogue, testing and debugging work, and acknowledged scope growth. That establishes real **engineering work categories**, not measured development costs or profitability. Even treating ten months as full-time does not provide a per-person hours ledger, and the source does not supply an agreed value of time or a wage.

A useful distinction for the existing **first-time versus established creator** question is that the first-game label applies to this product/team, not necessarily the developers' first coding experience. No matched experienced-publisher comparison controls price, genre, source traffic, promotional audience, or launch windows. The observed upper bound is pooled across two stores and cannot be multiplied by the live listed price to infer itch.io gross receipts.

This is one additional real-world first release, not evidence that most paid 2D games sell fewer than 50 copies. Neither representative expected income nor the causal impact of earlier portfolios can be identified without an eligible seller cohort and compatible accounting.
