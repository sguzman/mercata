# Two bounded indie game earnings and costs: Playdate and PC

**Research date: 2026-10-26.** [Machine data](small-paid-release-finance-2022.json). These are voluntary public first-party developer statements, not sampled outcomes from all itch.io sellers.

## Fish'n Feathers for Playdate (July 2022)

[The publisher's post-launch disclosure](https://itch.io/devlog/404938/launch-sale-1-week-left-post-launch-stats.amp) reports **127 sales, 369 download events and $401 gross receipts**. Paid units and downloads differ because the creator gifted about two dozen copies to earlier supporters and participated in two charity co-op bundles; the latter generated download-key claims even when the title did not sell as an individual $3 order.

A 40% first-month offer priced the title at **$3 rather than the planned $5**. The developer described historical processor fees of **$0.39 Stripe / $0.59 PayPal per $3 payment**, in addition to the optional itch.io revenue share. These are 2022 creator observations, not guaranteed 2026 checkout fees. The disclosure provides no comparable control launch without a discount, no complete unit refund accounting and no production-hour ledger. Thus gross revenue cannot be converted into creator profit or a demonstrated beneficial launch-discount effect.

## Escape from Life Inc (historical first paid game)

The [postmortem](https://powerburger.itch.io/life-inc/devlog/180595/escape-from-life-inc-postmortem) describes **approximately $651 in Steam receipts plus itch.io income yielding roughly $740 across stores**, alongside **$300–$400 in cash paid for the soundtrack**. That does not constitute exact itch.io-only receipts, and the soundtrack is not the full development expense. The creator described about 1.5 years making the game, with paid sales concentrated soon after launch and some exposure through outside posts. No actual total work hours, seller taxes, direct itch.io paid units or final hourly profit were verified.

## Consequences for interpreting the marketplace

A paid game can generate downloads from donations, co-op bundles, complimentary copies and ordinary file redownloads. File downloads therefore **cannot serve as a proxy for direct paid unit sales**, and a sale banner cannot prove incremental net income. Different game platforms and release dates cannot establish the earnings distribution for a typical first-time 2D developer.

Neither postmortem is representative, and none of the missing population income, profit or zero-sale rates is estimated. No account was accessed, purchase made, or game launched.
