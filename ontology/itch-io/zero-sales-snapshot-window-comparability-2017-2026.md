## Newly isolated 2D year-long zero report

The [original FUMES forum statement](https://itch.io/t/6533997/fumes-pvp-dogfighting), dated June 26, 2026, says a year passed without sales. Its [June 24 anniversary devlog](https://spacecat-studios.itch.io/fumes/devlog/1564807/fumes-1-year-anniversary) advertised a temporary **USD 1** discounted price; the [observed game listing](https://spacecat-studios.itch.io/fumes) later showed **USD 2 minimum** and a **2D** tag. Prices at different times are not a fixed treatment; no later purchase count or response to that discount is known. The page's one visible rating is **not** a verified paid order, and cannot rebut the earlier zero-sales statement.

## Why these are not five observations of a shared zero-sales rate

A zero requires a **particular title, checkout route, source timestamp and observation horizon**. The games differ in genre, development team, marketing, pricing, free/paid access, visibility and censoring. In one case a checkout route was incomplete; in another the same game did sell on Steam; in a third a zero first day eventually became positive paid orders. Combining them into a fraction of sellers with zero lifetime earnings would be mathematically and economically unjustified.

Even the year-long FUMES report is approximate and author-declared, **not** a transaction-by-transaction 365-day owner export. It does not fill the missing 7/30/90/365-day cohort, zero-inclusive denominator, median net creator income, after-labor profit or genre comparison. Sources are public reports; no private receipts were used.
