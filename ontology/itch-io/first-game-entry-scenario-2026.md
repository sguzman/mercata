# First paid-game entry, team roles and geographic comparison frame

Source review October 26, 2026. Machine record: [first-game-entry-scenario-2026.json](first-game-entry-scenario-2026.json). This is an **unexecuted, hypothetical study**, not a publication or user-specific account instruction.

## Publishing teams and developer income

The official [game-jam documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/game-jams) allows multiple contributors and administrative roles. In a [2018 operator support response](https://itch.io/t/351041/team-projects), itch.io described granting page admins the ability to display a shared project in their own profiles, while a separate team account could be created manually. That historical response has not been revalidated for current seller finance permissions. The [co-op bundle](https://itch.io/docs/creators/bundles) model allocates proceeds to participating accounts, but a collaborator on a shared game is **not automatically a separate revenue recipient or salaried developer**.

## Three distinct geographic/economic frames

For global buyers, potential market reach is not a census of paying people. For a seller, payout eligibility and currency support depend on jurisdiction and payment route, not simply on a page being globally viewable. For cross-store analysis, select Steam, Game Jolt and direct-owned distribution as **comparison targets only**, not extra Mercata specimen admissions; verify matched genre, period, fees, release age and marketing source before making any claim about sales differences.

## Zero-upfront-cash first-game illustration

A hypothetical solo publisher who already owns a capable computer and uses permitted free software plus self-created/appropriately licensed assets could target a small **5–30-minute 2D single-player game with one or two mechanics**. Those are **scenario choices**, not evidence this format is profitable. itch.io's mandatory listing charge is **$0**, but seller labor, debugging, game design, localization, refund risk, transactional processor charges, support and eventual taxes remain separate. Any free browser HTML5 build supports donation-style rather than mandatory pay-to-play checkout; a downloadable premium build has different entitlement economics.

An entry check must distinguish public-page preparation, honest screenshot/tag metadata, tested Linux and other target builds, buyer access, monetization verification, licensing, accessibility and later updates. No external spending or commercial activity is approved by this research scenario. Production hours, initial sales, paid buyer probability and final profit remain **unknown**.

This exercise advances the study's setup and team-population tasks but does not answer how often such games sell. [Related break-even assumptions](break-even-scenarios-2026.md).
