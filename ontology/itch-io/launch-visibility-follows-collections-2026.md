# itch.io launch visibility, following, collections and timing (October 27, 2026)

**Population:** publicly documented itch.io game publication and discovery processes. **Unit:** an event, eligibility rule or notification condition, not a unique buyer, wishlist, order or seller profit. Source documentation was checked October 27, 2026; no private seller account or live sale experiment was operated.

## Separate launch clocks

- **Publication** means a project is made Public; its page is available directly and through a publisher profile. **Indexing** is separate Search & Browse eligibility. [Official guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed). A new paid seller may face a review queue after first publication; neither review duration nor organic listing exposure is guaranteed.
- Search & Browse requires the project to be public, have a cover image, and be purchasable, downloadable or browser-playable. An empty teaser page without these capabilities is not a commercial indexable listing. Accurate genre/language/accessibility metadata matters; spam can lead to removal from index.
- The original **publish date does not reset** after a build update or late indexing. The operator explicitly discourages deleting/recreating a page to obtain another Most Recent placement. Record first publication, first actual index observation and first positive-price sale as different dated events. See [quality guidance](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines).

## Wishlists versus documented on-platform interest

The [follower guide](https://itch.io/docs/accounts/followers) describes account follow events, feed updates and notifications to interested followers when new games or sales are posted, subject to preferences. The [indexing guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed) also encourages users to add a project to their collections. These are **observable interest/visibility pathways**, but neither is proven to be a native wishlist count comparable to Steam or an estimate of buyers, paid intent, purchases or conversion. The full native-wishlist equivalence and actual sale attribution are not verified.

## Launch and promotion periods

The [sale guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/sales) describes seller-configured local start/end times, preannounced sale pages and active sale badges. Track those separately from first project-publication and discovery-indexing dates. An audit of 7-, 30-, and 90-day *paid order* windows requires the same population, unit, refund definition and verified first-party order records, not only exposure, ratings, follows or collection events.

## What this task accepts and does not measure

The existing task requested dated public evidence and implications for visibility, wishlists and launch timing. This audit fulfills that documentation acceptance. It **does not** establish typical wait to index, follower-to-buyer conversion, launch-period purchases, ranking uplift, median seller earnings or the existence of a verified native wishlist-to-purchase event schema.
