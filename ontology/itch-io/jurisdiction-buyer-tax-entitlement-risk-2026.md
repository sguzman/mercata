# International game sales: visibility, payouts, taxes, and previous-buyer rights

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** [Machine jurisdiction separation](jurisdiction-buyer-tax-entitlement-risk-2026.json). This is a **first-party rule matrix**, not legal or tax advice and not an estimate of paid customers.

The official [itch.io payment guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) makes **merchant of record** the critical distinction: direct-to-merchant sellers connect processors and handle their VAT and chargeback obligations, whereas collected payments are sold by itch.io as merchant of record and have separate tax interview, fraud checks and later payout approval. Its stated **30% default withholding without valid tax identity** cannot be treated as the final tax rate for every country; valid documents and applicable treaties may change treatment. The seller’s bank country, the buyer’s payment/card country and the buyer’s VAT-relevant location are distinct units.

The operator’s [July 27, 2025 UK Online Safety Act announcement](https://itch.io/t/5133739/our-update-on-the-uk-online-safety-act) described a restriction on **certain adult/NSFW classified project pages for UK visitors**, not every UK buyer or all games. It documented a way for publishers to review/appeal restricted classifications and said existing buyers could still retrieve their purchased files through the library/app at that time. No later policy guarantee, appeal success rate, revenue loss, or definitive current status for every game was provided.

The [legal terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms), [download-key guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/download-keys), and [access-state guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/access-control) distinguish a new checkout from an earlier entitled buyer: draft, restricted, unlisted and no-new-purchases settings can affect page reach and existing access differently. Manual access keys and bundle claims are not independently paid purchasers. A geographic storefront restriction does not necessarily cancel legal rights previously acquired.

## Research risk dimensions

| Question | Applicable unit | Not inferable from public rules |
|---|---|---|
| Can a buyer see a game page? | Game classification, visitor jurisdiction, date | All consumers in that country or paid-game demand |
| Can the seller accept funds? | Seller merchant account, connected processor, tax eligibility | Approved countrywide seller population |
| Who remits applicable VAT? | Merchant-of-record mode and buyer’s transaction location | Every country’s rate or seller’s personal tax |
| What withholding applies? | Publisher's payout tax interview and treaty records | A universal 30% effective tax rate |
| Can a prior buyer retrieve files? | Purchased entitlement, page state and event | Guaranteed lifetime availability after any future policy |
| What happens after disputes? | Store mode, specific refunded transaction | Country-specific refund or fraud rates |

This completes the fixed **international compliance risk mapping task** by identifying the precise jurisdiction, transaction, time window and party on whom a rule acts. It does not certify a particular seller or game, state countrywide permission, or establish cross-border income. All real approval and market outcomes remain `null`.
