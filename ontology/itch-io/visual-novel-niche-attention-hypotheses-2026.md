# Which game niche has attention, and which has paying demand?

Reviewed October 26, 2026. [Structured observational data and preregistered hypotheses](visual-novel-niche-attention-hypotheses-2026.json).

An [independent April 6, 2026 analysis](https://www.indiegameinsights.dev/blog/itch_visual_novels_2025) constructed an approximate **2025 itch.io visual-novel frame of 10,427 projects** with **3,195 distinct tags**. The researcher grouped frequent co-occurring tags into manually interpreted themes. These are **public catalog projects**, not verified positive-price games, distinct publishers, paying readers or merchant receipts.

The article estimates about **15–20% zero-rating incidence among three leading VN theme categories**, compared with **81% zero-rating pages** in the author's [earlier broad study](https://indiegameinsights.dev/blog/itch_2025). Those fractions are neither zero-*sales* percentages nor independent probability samples. A game may have sales without reviews, receive free-play ratings without sales, or arrive with an existing audience.

A separate [paid-filter snapshot](paid-genre-tag-frames-2026.json) records **7,456 Puzzle-genre listings, 5,117 Horror-theme listings and 857 Cozy-mood listings**. They use *different, overlapping* classifications and source capture periods. A lower listing count does not automatically mean less competition or higher profit.

## Three predeclared, falsifiable product-positioning hypotheses

**Compact mystery VN:** a short, complete detective story with clear text/UI access could compete on comprehension and narrative focus. The test would compare first-time, similarly scoped narrative publishers' real retained positive-price orders and authoring/QA hours, controlling for price, release window, outside audiences and localization.

**Dark romance VN:** a matching theme may attract attention, but paid readership must be tested separately. A defensible cohort would include low-attention and zero-sale publishers, restrict comparable content labels, and compare actual net order receipts alongside development and moderation costs.

**Short offline puzzle:** a readable 2D puzzle download with robust keyboard/controller access might differentiate on practical usability. The measured commercial endpoint must still be a real paid purchase less fees and labor, not a public rating or Puzzle-tag ranking.

Each is a **hypothesis**, not a recommended genre or commercially validated need. None has been developed, marketed, randomized or purchased as part of Mercata. No causal lift, genre-conditional buying rate, developer median revenue or first-game profit is inferred.

## Comparison gates

For any later authorized research, pin identical 30/90/365-day windows, include eligible zero-sale and delisted titles where observable, deduplicate project versus creator entities with permission, identify first-time versus established publishers, and keep direct spend and valued developer labor. Unmeasured populations remain unknown. This closes the *public attention and falsifiable niche hypothesis* work; **genre earnings and paid addressable demand remain unresolved**.
