# One first paid game with an existing free-game audience

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** First-party [May 6, 2024 postmortem](https://edmango.itch.io/rc-moushley/devlog/727086/resident-cheesel-moushley-postmortem); [paid product listing](https://edmango.itch.io/rc-moushley); [machine observation](edmango-2024-first-paid-release-purchases-and-referral-boundaries.json).

The creator reported **more than 600 paid purchases** of one **$3-minimum 2D point-and-click visual novel**. The same retrospective counted about **31,300 itch page-view events**, including referrals of **28,452 from itch**, **1,059 from Twitter**, **565 from Google**, and **122 from Cohost**. These are self-reported, unverified seller figures for one selected, commercially successful, adult-niche project.

**First paid is not first ever.** The developer had previously published free games and already had platform followers and an external niche audience. Peer promotion and top-selling placement were also mentioned. The 600-plus result does not represent a newly created publisher with no earlier catalog or following.

**No inferred conversion or traffic causality.** A metric labeled **2.62% “Click Through Retention”** has no published numerator, denominator or definition and must not be treated as purchase conversion. The author also stated **55.6 average purchases per day after the initial spike**, without defining the days included; it cannot be extrapolated to a monthly income. Page views and referrals are events, not unique paying customers. No orders were assigned to particular referrers. Top-seller ranks of **#3 then #9** do not disclose market revenue concentration.

**No inferred profit.** The creator disclosed **$10 of marketing cash spend** and **three weeks plus six days** of concept/writing/coding development. Full art labor, other spending, tax, payments, refunds, platform fees and support were not accounted for. The Mac and Android versions were experimental, with some creator-described Godot crashes and freeze reports; limited device-testing capacity was an additional uncosted burden.

**Scope:** adds a positive-price paid-unit outcome and a documented first-paid-vs-prior-free creator distinction. It does not supply unique buyers, a market median, after-cost profit, measured acquisition causality or a zero-sales-inclusive sample. Public numerical disclosures only; no private data or copyrighted media included.
