# One creator's annual payment volatility is not the paid-game market

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** Population: one voluntarily reporting itch.io account, Not Jam. Unit: calendar-year **gross payment USD** across games, fonts, music and other projects, plus all-category page-view events. Source posts are [2023](https://itch.io/blog/656364/2023-in-review), [2024](https://itch.io/blog/858848/2024-in-review) and [2025](https://itch.io/blog/1284030/2025-in-review). The reports are dated December 31 of their respective years and are not independent seller samples.

| Year | Annual gross, all products (USD) | Annual all-category page-view events |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2023 | $641.00 | 25,500 |
| 2024 | $1152.22 | 53,681 |
| 2025 | $464.66 | 71,659 |

The **same account's reported gross receipts** rose **79.75%** ($511.22) in 2024, then fell **59.67%** ($687.56) in 2025, while reported page-view events increased in both years (**110.51%** and **33.49%**). These comparisons demonstrate **co-occurring changes**, not revenue-per-buyer conversion or the causal value of traffic.

The account's separately published **cumulative lifetime game-category revenue** was $0 at end-2023, $0 at end-2024 and $24 at end-2025; see the already verified [game-only series](notjam-game-category-series-2023-2025.md). Those cumulative figures cannot be mixed with the **annual all-category gross** series or used to calculate a mandatory-price game seller's net earnings.

The 2024 post cites a viral font pack as a driver of account attention. In 2025 the author attributes part of the lower gross to fewer new fonts, and describes a single game responsible for a large share of the higher page-view/play events. Those are **author explanations, not tested causal effects**. The shifting portfolio makes the sample unsuitable for measuring normal month-to-month fluctuation separately from outliers, release mix and within-year seasonality.

**What remains unknown:** 2023–2025 game-only annual orders and profit, paid-game refund-adjusted proceeds, intra-year seasonality, typical seller volatility, and whether page-view changes precede or cause payments. Three annual data points from one account cannot establish any of those. [Machine audit](same-creator-annual-payment-variability-2023-2025.json). No new research tasks, sellers, accounts, datasets or private buyer information were introduced.
