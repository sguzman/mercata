# itch.io October 2026 indexed-page reform and temporary revenue-share waiver

**Source review: October 26, 2026.** Two first-party announcements address distinct but important commercial mechanics. [Machine crosswalk](indexing-reform-creator-day-2026.json).

## October 19, 2026 — search/browse eligibility changed

In an [official operator update](https://itch.io/t/7172464/october-updates-on-indexing), itch.io acknowledged automated indexing restrictions had incorrectly flagged many legitimate pages and that humans could not clear the review backlog promptly. The operator said the filters were modified, allowing **about 215,000 previously restricted project pages** to regain indexing eligibility over the preceding week. It also said the platform had **well over two million indexed project pages** and that **more than 10,000 accounts attempting malware/spam distribution** were suspended or banned over that period.

Each number has a **different unit**: project pages made eligible; the current overall multi-category indexed page inventory; accounts receiving enforcement. None represents newly created paid games, active paid publishers, unique developers, customers or orders. The 215,000 were **already-existing pages previously prevented from qualifying**, not necessarily all observed in search now. It is incorrect to add 215,000 to earlier game-only browse result counts as though they were new commercial game launches.

The operator described the revised thresholds as experimental and subject to adjustment, and did not provide a statistical before-and-after time to indexing. They reaffirmed that uploading projects and becoming eligible for indexing remain free when requirements are met. An eligible page still has no guaranteed ranking, visits or purchases.

## Creator Day — fee rate is time-dependent

The [November 29, 2025 official explanation](https://itch.io/updates/what-is-creator-day.amp) describes a recurring **24-hour Creator Day** when itch.io automatically waives its own platform share for qualifying transactions. Sellers do not need to change settings, and their configured shares resume afterward. **Payment processing fees and taxes remain.** The documentation does not establish a specific upcoming event date as of this research snapshot.

Under the original [payment guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments)'s hypothetical $10 checkout, 10% platform share and 2.9% plus 30-cent processing, ordinary proceeds before other deductions are **$8.41**. With the same inputs but an automatically waived platform share, they would be **$9.41**, a **$1** conditional per-order increase. This is arithmetic assuming the *same order*, not a forecast of incremental customer traffic, actual payouts, income tax or developer profit.

## Research implications

Market population series must separate **newly created game pages, pages newly index-eligible after a classifier change, all indexed projects, paying customers and merchant accounts**. Seller profit modeling should allow rate changes by actual transaction date, rather than applying today's default share to historic Creator Days. Both new phenomena are important; neither answers the representative median developer earnings question.
