# First itch.io publisher setup, free page and conditional paid checkout

Reviewed October 26, 2026. [Machine entry/work register](first-publisher-entry-and-zero-cash-2026.json). This audit distinguishes **first free publication** from **qualifying to receive cash for a game**. It does not represent an actual game, seller account, payment or observed production-time benchmark.

## Account and first page

The official [getting-started guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started) says a basic project page requires a **free itch.io account**. From the dashboard the publisher creates a new project and supplies a meaningful title, cover image, suitable content classification, accurate game metadata, description, screenshots if applicable, downloadable build or HTML5 playable files, and appropriate price and access settings. A free project may be published publicly without connecting a payment processor.

The [service agreement](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) requires publishers to be over 18 or possess appropriate legal parent/guardian consent and be competent to accept its obligations. It also requires rights to distribute every included file and asset. This is a platform eligibility rule, not proof any individual minor or seller is approved. An account owner should also consider security, recovery, and the [platform's documented account limits](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq). Publishing a page does not grant guaranteed [Search & Browse indexing](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed); first-time paid sellers may be reviewed, and operator-level content moderation may limit discoverability.

## Paid entry has two different gatekeepers

| Seller mode | Onboarding and merchant of record | Money and jurisdiction conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Direct to you | Individually connect eligible PayPal or Stripe accounts and provide identity/tax details to those payment providers. Seller is merchant of record. | Provider-specific local availability, supported currency, payment processing, VAT/other taxes and disputes. No blanket country approval assumed. |
| Collected by itch.io | Accept platform seller terms and complete the operator's [tax interview](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments). itch.io becomes merchant of record. | Source states a one-time **$3 account adjustment** for tax identity processing, **$5 minimum available payout balance**, seven-day transaction hold and a subsequent staff payout-review period. The adjustment is not a cash fee demanded to create a free game page. |

Cash deducted after a payment, a refund or a bank withdrawal is distinct from the cash needed before a game is published. A free listing can require **zero new cash** under restricted assumptions without guaranteeing zero tax, labor, asset, support or technical cost for a paid title.

## A bounded zero-new-cash first-game scenario

The proposed example presumes an existing computer and internet connection, a game built with freely usable or already-owned tools, self-made/commercially licensed assets, no paid ads, and a free download or free HTML5 playable project. Before listing, record **game-development hours, art/license compliance hours, build QA hours, page preparation time and continuing support hours**, along with a declared hourly labor valuation. All of those are **unmeasured**, not zero. Actual build suitability, user testing, merchant approval, discoverability and sales are also unknown.

**Task scope:** This documentation establishes the steps, publisher rules, distinct payment modes, presence/absence of an up-front platform listing fee, and an auditable zero-upfront-cash workflow. It does not establish how fast ordinary developers finish games, which countries can receive money without additional checks, actual dollar profitability or whether any particular person can pass onboarding.
