# A Year of Springs: five years of itch.io payments and multi-store sales

**Source:** [developer's May 14, 2026 retrospective](https://tnywndr.cafe/office/2026-05-14-five-years-of-springs/), including earlier 2021 week-one launch notes. This is one voluntarily disclosed game, not a representative sample.

| Unit | itch first week (May 2021) | itch cumulative (May 2026) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Payment events | 324 | 1,789 |
| Download events | 458 | 5,929 |

The first week's 458 downloads split into **382 Windows**, **35 Linux** and **41 macOS**. Some downloads came from free supporter codes and community copies. A payment event and a download event do not represent unique customers and cannot be used as a per-person purchase conversion probability.

By May 2026, the author also reported **19,830 Steam units**, **over 25,000 console units**, and **over 10,000 Android downloads**. These reflect different sale windows and incompatible measures; **do not add them into a uniform total of paid game units**. The current itch game price was reported as **$4.99**. The game's **3.2% Steam refund rate** and **107-minute median Steam playtime** are *Steam-only case statistics*, not itch.io return rates.

The developer said **itch alone was insufficient to make game development full-time**, while subsequent Steam sales materially helped. This is a stated personal business outcome, not audited earnings, hours or profit. The earlier free game *one night, hot springs* had **50,600 itch downloads and 991 payments**, but that is another title and a different optional-payment offering.

[Machine case](year-of-springs-2026.json). The unknowns remain genuine: yearly new buyers, retained itch proceeds after refunds, costs, hours, first-release commercial medians, and causal effects from adding a store. No live account or financial transaction was accessed.
