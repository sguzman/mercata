# itch.io economic and discovery feature history

Reviewed October 26, 2026. This is a history of documented **platform tools**, not a history of observed developer earnings.

In [March 2013](https://itch.io/updates/welcome), itch.io announced customizable pages and optional minimum game prices. In [January 2015](https://itch.io/updates/a-new-payout-system-and-an-update-to-handling-vat), collected payment/payout mode addressed EU VAT administration. The [March 2015 open revenue share](https://itch.io/updates/introducing-open-revenue-sharing) announcement established the variable 0–100% seller-set platform share with 10% default, scheduled to activate March 23.

The [February 2018 owner analytics change](https://itch.io/updates/updates-to-project-analytics-filtering-collections-impressions-and-more) added impressions and CTR. An impression counts a project title/thumbnail displayed on browse, feed, recommendations or home pages; CTR represents clicks to the project page. **At rollout** the impressions/CTR figures covered the last week, whereas other graphs could cover up to a year. The current 2026 impression window was not established by that historical source. Lifetime game orders cannot be divided by weekly impressions to estimate conversion.

The [July 2, 2026 product update](https://itch.io/blog/1572473/itchio-changelog-sale-explorer-bundle-hosting-revamp-jam-theme-editor-patreon-integration-more) introduced a seasonal Sale Explorer with tag exclusions, final-price filtering, Staff Picks and personalized sort methods. Its Summer Sale had **over 30,000 discounted projects**, including non-game categories. This is *not a number of purchases or paying buyers*. The update also introduced a refreshed co-op bundle revenue split editor and enhanced Patreon membership-tier entitlement grants; the latter is external funding rather than native itch.io checkout revenue.

The [machine event register](store-feature-history-2026.json) preserves event dates and different measurement units. No active-publisher growth series, paid customer counts, market GMV or representative earning distribution has been inferred from feature announcements.
