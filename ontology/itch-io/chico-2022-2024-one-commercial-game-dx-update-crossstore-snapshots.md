# Chico: one 2022 paid game, 2023 free DX upgrade, and 2024 sales by venue

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** This case has three complementary public sources: the [creator's first-person April 2024 sales comment](https://www.reddit.com/r/IndieDev/comments/1btb1md), the [developer's release history](https://www.daikon.games/press/chico/), and the [March 2024 itch.io promotion](https://itch.io/s/119383/spring-sale-2024). [Machine record](chico-2022-2024-one-commercial-game-dx-update-crossstore-snapshots.json).

## Bounded reported paid game volume

The developer described *Chico and the Magic Orchards* as its **first commercial game** and reported these approximate cumulative copy counts in April 2024:

| Store | Creator's approximate copy count | Time comparability |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| itch.io | **60** | Originally released August 8, 2022 |
| Steam | **800** | Originally released August 8, 2022 |
| Nintendo Switch | **600** | Port only released December 29, 2023 |
| **Arithmetic total** | **approximately 1,460** | Not a release-age-matched or independently audited paid-order count |

The creator's personal account is a first-party anecdote, not a verified merchant export. Approximate copies do not establish buyer-unique counts, refunds, store fees, profit, publisher income or any marketwide distribution. In particular, the Switch port had been sold for much less time than the original itch/Steam release at the April 2024 reporting point.

## Important release-count distinction: DX is not another paid game

The publisher's timeline says the game first launched on itch.io and Steam on **August 8, 2022**. *Chico DX* was a **free enhancement to the existing game** on **January 13, 2023**, featuring new post-game material and visual/accessibility enhancements; that does **not** constitute a second new paid game page or another seller. The separate Nintendo Switch port arrived **December 29, 2023** but is still a port of the same game, not a newly admitted itch paid-game title.

The developer's [Spring Sale 2024 page](https://itch.io/s/119383/spring-sale-2024) records a historical **$3.49 discounted offer (30% off $4.99)** ending March 21. The listed $4.99 must not be multiplied by ~60 reported copies to produce a made-up itch gross-revenue estimate: buyers may have purchased on different dates at different prices, or received refunds.

## Study status

This adds a new **named, positive-price game** whose publisher reports actual store-specific approximate paid copies. It also provides an independently cross-checked example of why version updates and ports must be excluded from counts of new itch commercial game launches.

Only existing tasks advance: **estimate paid-purchase demand 73% → 74%** and **measure release frequency 96% → 97%**. Both remain incomplete. The example supplies neither a representative population of all paid-game sellers (including zero-sales and delisted ones) nor aligned store prices, payouts, production costs, fixed-window purchases or audited earnings. All remain unknown.
