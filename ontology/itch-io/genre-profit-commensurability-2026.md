# Why recorded itch.io genres cannot yet be ranked by profit

**Research reviewed October 26, 2026.** [Machine crosswalk](genre-profit-commensurability-2026.json) · [pure evidence gate](../../site/itch-genre-profit-audit.mjs). This directly supports the fixed question of whether game genres offer meaningfully different retained earnings to **first commercial game publishers**.

Twelve already-source-linked developer game outcomes span puzzles, handheld games, narrative releases, RPGs, 2D deckbuilders and platformers. The recorded facts are **different economic objects**, not a reusable 12-game profitability dataset:

| Selected case | Store-specific numeric observation | Why it does not establish genre net profit |
|---|---|---|
| [Mixolumia](https://mixolumia.com/postmortem/) | 1,776 itch paid units, USD 14,980 after transaction deductions | No full production labor or complete developer profit |
| [Skwish for Playdate](https://pawprints.itch.io/skwish-playdate/devlog/479222/releasing-my-sales-figures) | 591 direct paid copies, ≈USD 2,300 direct gross | Community-bundle allocations separate, no final labor and tax deductions |
| [Risk & Riches](https://growlerygames.itch.io/risk-and-riches/devlog/956226/postmortem-risk-riches) | Zero itch copies as of 2025-05-30 | Historical price and future sales unconfirmed, build upkeep described only as several days |
| [Rose Seed Replica](https://lezliz.itch.io/rose-seed-replica/devlog/179880/rose-seed-replica-postmortem-mistakes-were-made) | 13 itch copies first week | Euro costs are multi-year and include alternative living-grant allocations |
| [Lemma](https://gamedev.net/blogs/832/entry-2261203-the-poor-mans-postmortem-lemma/) | 46 itch sales, USD 701 gross in 2015 | Game-wide budget spans Steam and other outlets; no itch-only after-labor profit |
| [Blobun](https://itch.io/devlog/1455287/one-year-of-blobun.amp) | 271 itch copies, 1,420 Steam copies | Net receipts total includes several shops and some bundles |
| [AraCore](https://elcrabogrande.itch.io/aracore-astromining-ventures/devlog/1133828/aracore-astromining-ventures-a-personal-success-a-commercial-failure) | ≈1,500 developer hours; only combined-store money | No itch-only settled proceeds or labor-valued net outcome |

The other five machine rows record additional store-specific or partially combined observations with the same methodological boundaries. Some are one-week samples, others 64-day, six-month, ten-month or cumulative reports across several years. Genres/tags are **nonexclusive**, release formats and currencies differ, and seller selection is voluntary. Therefore comparing gross copies, views or ratings across these reports **cannot estimate genre-specific mean/median developer profit or the relative likelihood of commercial success**.

The executable evidence gate requires a fixed comparable observation horizon, dated product eligibility, platform-specific settled receipts, after-expense and labor-valued profit and a representative seller sampling frame before it permits a genre profit comparison. All current rows fail. Even a future matched set of selected games could only describe that sample, not the platform.

**Result:** no defensible genre net-profit league table is available from the currently recorded evidence. The source-linked study is substantially better specified, but the fixed task remains partially complete until adequate actual income observations exist. No owner accounts were accessed or source records invented.
