These decisions concern **what the platform documents** or **how to avoid invalid comparisons**. Dated operator sources have known policy-revision risks; they are not promises that an individual merchant is approved. The game-sale cases are selected individual observations and cannot infer market income distributions. The study keeps actual purchases, unique buyers, settled proceeds, publisher profit, active commercial publisher counts, zero-sale rates and matched genres **unknown** where unavailable.

The accepted task statuses have evidence paths in the existing ledger and are automatically checked for source existence, unique IDs and exact completion status. This is not certification of a live game, execution of an experiment, or an estimate of representative buyer demand.
