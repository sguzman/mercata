# Refunds, discounts, and geographic currency conditions (October 27, 2026)

**Population:** public itch.io seller policies for paid-game listings; no actual purchases, merchant accounts or refund events. **Unit:** a documented rule, eligibility gate or reporting distinction, not a transaction. Sources are the public creator documentation and [2023-dated Terms](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) as viewed on October 27, 2026.

## Refunds and net sales

The [Terms: refunds](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) allow requests where a purchase fails to work, cannot be accessed, or substantially differs from its listing. Technical support may be routed to the publisher first. Some older payment methods may require publisher handling and third-party keys may require cancellation confirmation. No general unconditional refund window or actual 2026 refund frequency is established. [Payment guidance](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) identifies seller/itch.io merchant distinctions and adjustments. Report gross orders, refunds, disputes and actually settled proceeds separately.

## Sale rules and entitlement risk

A seller's [sale configuration](https://itch.io/docs/creators/sales) can specify a start/end, a 0–100% discount, a seller-account bundle price, coupon access, previous-product-ownership conditions and an optional redemption cap. Discounts apply to base project, individually priced files and reward tiers. A **100% discount** can make a normally paid game free temporarily; this is not evidence of a positive-payment order. A regular seller-account bundle differs from a separately consented multi-account cooperative bundle. Badges and featured sale pages are exposure features, **not measured incremental paid sales**.

The [pricing documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing) distinguishes a paid buyer's durable ownership of the base game after a price change from conditional access to individually priced files that become more expensive. A normal free download is not equivalent to a paid entitlement.

The [creator quality rules](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines) discourage continuous or excessively frequent discounts and forbid deceptive inflation of the reference base price. An indefinite discount associated with a multi-item bundle is distinguished from a perpetually discounted individual item.

## Currency is not verified country-by-country base pricing

The [payments guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) says collected payouts accept buyer payments in USD, while direct-selling creators select a supported currency compatible with their processor account. It also documents buyer-location VAT adjustment and separate fee/FX risks. These are **not** evidence that each buyer receives a locally calibrated discretionary game base price. Although platform terms reference prices applicable to geography, our reviewed public docs do not establish a platform-wide seller-selectable automatic regional price-tier mechanism. That implementation remains **unverified**.
