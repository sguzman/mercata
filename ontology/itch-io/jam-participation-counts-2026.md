# itch.io jams: production, ratings and commercial conversion gaps

Sources retrieved on 2026-10-26: [official jam directory](https://itch.io/jams) and [Gamedev.js Jam 2026 results](https://itch.io/jam/gamedevjs-2026/results). [Machine evidence](jam-participation-counts-2026.json).

The sitewide directory states **613,796 games have been created for jams**. This cumulative operator description is not a unique creator or first-paid-game census and cannot be divided directly by a paid commercial listing filter: jams and commercial release identities can overlap, and many games remain free.

The Gamedev.js Jam 2026 results list **10,642 rating events**, with a **median of 17 ratings per game** and a reported mean of 22.1. Its published sentence says **482 submitted entries** but **483 rated entries**. That discrepancy exists in the official published source and remains unresolved, rather than silently changing either number. Submissions took place April 13–26, 2026; ratings followed April 26–May 9.

**Ratings are community activity, not customer payments.** A jam submission may generate valuable playtesting, feedback, press or community attention, but these two sources give no tracked positive-price sales, unique paying buyers, game revenue or labor-adjusted profit. They therefore do not establish a jam-to-paid-game conversion probability or the causal effectiveness of participating in a jam.

No jam account, participant identity, sales dashboard, individual rating data, or paid transaction was accessed. The figures are included as explicit engagement/production denominators, separate from monetization.
