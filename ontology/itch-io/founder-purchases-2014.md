# First-party itch.io marketplace sales — historical 2014 baseline

The founder's [September 16, 2014 public transaction study](https://itch.io/blog/1/running-an-indie-game-store) reports more than **$100,000 paid to developers since launch**, roughly **13% of registered accounts with a public game**, and **7% with a linked payment account**. Of those with **both** a public game and a linked payment provider, about **30% had received at least one purchase**. This included contributions on free games. It does not mean 30% of *today's* positive-price game developers have sales, much less a living wage.

Of purchase events at that early point, **84% originated off-site** and roughly 16% from itch.io internal surfaces; 73% of externally initiated purchases used a game page and 27% the embed widget. These are **2014 purchases**, not web visits or unique customers. Later discovery surfaces changed substantially, so this is no current causal marketing forecast.

About **11% of purchase events** occurred while a title was on sale. The historical **$2 median listed game price**, **$2 extra median among buyers already paying above minimum**, and **$3 median amount among paying supporters of free games** have different denominators and cannot be pooled to project current pricing or a new game's likely revenue.

[Machine data](founder-purchases-2014.json) deliberately leaves modern marketplace GMV, game sales distributions, newcomer zero-earner fraction and developer net profit as unknown. It is a valuable early operator-admin source of actual purchase patterns, but not a 2026 market survey.

[December 1, 2014 founder interview crosscheck](first-party-2014-payout-historical-crosscheck.md): approximate $170,000 payouts and 6,666 active pages, with no defensible per-seller average.
