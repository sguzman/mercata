# Free game attention, one voluntary donation, and actual cash categories

Reviewed **October 26, 2026**. [Structured observed case](voluntary-donation-versus-game-sales-2026.json) · [integer-cent reconciliation](../../site/itch-donation-cash-waterfall.mjs).

A [February 23, 2026 developer disclosure](https://msreds.itch.io/geneticcollections/devlog/1412711/the-first-month) concerning *Genetic Collections - Axolotls* reported **more than 100 page views** and **one voluntary donation of $49.99** during the game's first month. It did not establish a mandatory-price game purchase or a unique paying-buyer conversion rate. Those view events do not provide a full denominator of exposed human buyers.

| Reported movement | USD |
|---|---:|
| Optional donation | +$49.99 |
| Processor fee | −$1.75 |
| Platform share | −$4.99 |
| One-time tax identity processing | −$3.00 |
| Withholding on seller funds | −$10.38 |
| **Reported amount due to publisher** | **$29.87** |
| Separate later voluntary conservation contribution | −$15.00 |
| **Money remaining after charity** | **$14.87** |

The developer's charity contribution occurred **after** the calculated platform settlement and is not an itch.io fee. The one-time $3 adjustment cannot be multiplied by a number of paid transactions. The withholding is a payout deduction, not proof of final personal income tax. All values are self-reported, not audited.

For free or browser projects, count **views, free downloads/plays, optional donations, resulting payout balances, refunds and post-platform personal expenses as separate events**. A free download or a donated $49.99 is not proof of one premium product sale. The author's development hours, software and equipment expenses, unique human readers, actual bank receipts, tax credits and project profit were not observed.

The model accepts integer-cent inputs and returns null for mandatory-price orders, unique donor conversion, tax liability and economic profit. Its arithmetic verifies **$49.99 − $1.75 − $4.99 − $3.00 − $10.38 = $29.87**, and **$29.87 − $15 = $14.87**; these are limited to this one disclosure.

**Research acceptance:** a reproducible distinction between free engagement and cash gifts. **Unanswered:** platformwide donation likelihood, paying buyer demand, representative first-game seller earnings and any causal benefit of using a free offer.
