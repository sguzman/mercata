# Engine licenses, royalties, assets and export work for an indie game

Reviewed October 26, 2026. [Machine cost model](engine-asset-and-export-cost-crosswalk-2026.json). This completes the existing task of **mapping** engine and asset costs; it is not a sample of real publishers' expenditures.

| Engine | Applicable engine cost basis | Independent obligations |
|---|---|---|
| **Godot** | The [official MIT license documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/about/complying_with_licenses.html) allows commercial game releases with required notice text and no engine royalty. | Third-party libraries, textures, music and fonts need their actual licenses and notices. [Export presets and templates](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/export/exporting_projects.html) still impose packaging and QA work. |
| **Unity** | In its [2026 pricing update](https://unity.com/products/pricing-updates), Personal is free subject to a **USD 200,000 revenue-and-funding** financial threshold; Pro is priced **USD 2,310 per seat/year prepaid or USD 210/month** in the published USD plans. | Personal eligibility uses the [applicable total finances criteria](https://unity.com/products/unity-personal), not one title's itch.io receipts. Unity's proposed Runtime Fee was canceled, but subscription, proprietary assets, cloud services and porting may still cost money. |
| **Unreal** | The [official release guidance](https://www.unrealengine.com/release) and [EULA](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/unreal) describe a standard **5% royalty on qualifying worldwide product gross revenue above the first USD 1 million lifetime threshold**. A 3.5% program rate requires special multi-store qualification. | Release notice, royalty reporting, product terms, engine acknowledgments, third-party licenses and additional contractual exclusions require review. Worldwide product gross is not the same as itch.io seller payout. |

## Cost inventory for a real independent game

**Fixed or subscription:** purchased engine seats if ineligible for free use, recurring middleware, purchased assets or licensing deals, platform/signing costs when applicable, advertising spend.

**Variable or volume-dependent:** itch.io share configured for that creator, actual transaction processing, refunds/disputes, conditional royalties, delivery/hosting expense when separately applicable, currency exchange.

**Labor:** build and game design, asset compliance, version-specific export and QA for advertised Windows/Linux/macOS/web platforms, localization, marketing, patches and customer support. Engine selection changes the tools required but does **not** establish how many person-hours any project will cost.

The formula for an actual title is: **retained publisher proceeds minus engine fees/royalties, third-party assets, release and support expenses, and explicitly valued labor**. A royalty-free engine is not equivalent to a cost-free or profitable game.

The [existing component-rights audit](game-component-rights-register-2026.md) controls third-party asset reuse, and the [existing seller payment rules](commercial-rules-2026.md) control itch.io deductions. There is **no authorized real game**, merchant-specific pricing quote, verified component manifest, tested export, billed engine seat or labor ledger in this research. All corresponding empirical amounts remain unknown.
