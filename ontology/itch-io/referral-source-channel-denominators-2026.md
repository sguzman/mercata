# Seller referral and paid-purchase channels — keep attribution units separate

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** [Machine categories](referral-source-channel-denominators-2026.json) · [Offline aggregate channel checker](../../site/itch-referral-channel-audit.mjs). The existing fixed task to classify external referral sources is now source-backed and complete; **no creator analytics or payment records have been accessed**.

The operator's [July 31, 2014 purchase analytics announcement](https://itch.io/updates/view-where-purchases-come-from) described two distinct fields: **Buy location** (project page, embedded widget or purchase popup) and **Referrer**, including internal browse/search/home/profile and external sources such as game blogs, forums and social networks. The [October 23, 2019 analytics update](https://itch.io/blog/105823/return-of-the-itchio-changelog-markdown-sub-prducts-jam-updates-more) introduced referral-view graphs by external domain and time. These announcements provide a basis for designing a channel map, **not proof that every seller sees exactly the same historical UI in October 2026**.

## Categories and counting rules

A minimally interpretable owner-authorized aggregate needs mutually exclusive attributed records at the row level, each assigned its measurement unit, page/game, date window and evidence for its channel label. This study distinguishes *internal search* from other *internal browse/recommendations*; *external search engines* from *social/forum posts*; and *press/streamer/influencer coverage* from social sharing. It also preserves **verified direct** separately from **unattributed/unknown** activity. An absent HTTP referrer does **not** establish that a visit was typed, bookmarked or direct: privacy filters, mobile apps, link redirects and missing tags can hide attribution. A domain alone also does not prove that a particular visit was referred by an influencer.

A seller's **referral-view events** and the operator's **purchase-referrer events** can be summarized separately, but they are not automatically a matched purchase funnel. A repeat visitor can create several page views and one customer can pay multiple times. No ratios are reported for observed paying people, because actual 2026 owner-side data and deduplicated person-level cohorts are unavailable. Checkout location, referrer domain, organic source, paid promotion and eventual cash settlement are separate dimensions.

The [SuperWEIRD selected 2026 free-alpha case](free-alpha-frontpage-tail-2026.md) describes overlapping editorial featuring, social posts, community invitations and outside press. It cannot causally assign visits or Steam wishlists to one traffic source, nor turn free play into paid game demand. The operator's [2018 impressions notice](https://itch.io/updates/updates-to-project-analytics-filtering-collections-impressions-and-more) similarly defines thumbnail impression/CTR events, **not customers**.

The pure checker accepts only already aggregated non-identifying counts, rejects mixed time windows or units, and returns **null** for customer counts, paid conversion and causal lift. It does not classify user URLs, track buyers or contact a seller. Comparative income and audience demand remain unmeasured.
