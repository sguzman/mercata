# itch.io — promotions, co-op bundle revenue, and game jam outcome units

**26 October 2026.** These are official creator-platform mechanisms, not measured paid outcomes.

[Sales and Promotions](https://itch.io/docs/creators/sales) allows a seller to discount one or multiple games, create a single-account bundle price, and restrict some promotions by coupon link or existing buyer ownership. The seller has private promotion analytics. Neither a discount nor a dashboard field shows that promotion **caused** higher net profit.

[Cooperative bundles](https://itch.io/docs/creators/bundles) combine projects from multiple accounts at an agreed price and require contributor approval. Receipts are allocated to eligible participants by relative agreed weights, subject to price tiers. A recipient must qualify for collected itch.io payouts. The entire bundle checkout must **not** be credited as full standalone revenue to every title.

For bundles containing **more than 15 projects**, itch.io defers per-game download key creation until individual claims. Buying a bundle grants ownership of all eligible games, including those never claimed. Therefore a bundle purchase, entitlement, key claim and participant's allocated income are fundamentally different quantities.

[Jam documentation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/game-jams) describes submissions, optional rankings and ratings. The operator warns that voting is sensitive to popularity and potential manipulation. A jam rating or submission cannot establish a paid order, conversion, or profitable project.

The [machine evidence](bundle-sale-jam-2026.json) defines these units, boundaries and unknown outcomes. There were no seller account operations, downloads, purchases or live experiments.
