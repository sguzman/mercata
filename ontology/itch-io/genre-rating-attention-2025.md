# Digital itch.io game genres in 2025 — public ratings and paid-listing patterns

An [independent February 11, 2026 article](https://indiegameinsights.dev/blog/itch_2025) analyzed publicly available metadata for itch.io **digital games released roughly in 2025**, using sitemap order to infer the release window. The researcher explicitly warns that this can omit or misdate projects. The underlying **game count is not stated in the article**, so Mercata does not invent a sample size. [Machine study](genre-rating-attention-2025.json).

## Ratings measure some attention, not transactions

The article reports **81% of included digital game pages had no public ratings**. It also states the median rating count **within every analyzed genre was zero**, with strongly skewed means. These facts show that receiving public rating activity is far from universal; they do **not** measure the fraction of games with zero purchases, downloads, earnings or profit. Games can be purchased, played or given away without anyone rating them.

The author identified stronger **mean rating activity** for Visual Novel and Interactive Fiction titles and weaker relative ratings for Action and Platformer games. Story-focused titles stood out on the article's chosen attention metric, but because ratings are self-selected and genre membership overlaps, this is not proof narrative games are best for making money or that the typical title in a genre obtains any minimum number of buyers.

## Paid versus free category composition

The article observes Visual Novels, Adventure/Simulation and Role Playing among genres comparatively frequent in **paid listings**, with Platformer and Rhythm comparatively frequent among **free listings**. After fixing an original classification error involving tabletop games, **Racing** had the highest paid-versus-free listing share among the author's digital genre groups. The corrected ranking is retained; it does not indicate Racing titles sold best. The article itself notes that one highly rated paid-filter project on itch.io was only a **demo** of a full game available on Steam.

Game genres are self-assigned and nonexclusive. Multiple genre associations, free offerings, demos, and sale-price configurations require careful filtering. Even a perfect ranking of rated games would still not be a ranking of retained developer revenue.

## What remains unresolved

This source improves competition and product-positioning context, especially for small 2D games, but supplies **no representative per-genre paid transaction distribution, developer production costs, income median, income mean, zero-sales fraction or paid buyer count**. It must not be used as a substitute for the [first-commercial-title income protocol](transaction-cohort-protocol-2026.md). The [other paid genre supply filters](paid-genre-tag-frames-2026.md) cover different dates and definitions and cannot be pooled with this 2025 rating frame.

No private dashboard, individual developer profile or purchase was accessed.
