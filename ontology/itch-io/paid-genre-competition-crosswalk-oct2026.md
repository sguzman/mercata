The separately recorded **October 26 Puzzle figure was 7,456**. Do not overwrite that historic observation or infer that the difference measures a day's market growth: crawler times and indexing can differ. Genres overlap, and 2D is a tag, so these totals cannot be summed to count distinct games. Priced DLC, temporary 100% discounts and multi-project creators complicate the interpretation of commercially competing titles.

A properly matched study of rival games would need to align genre, platform, first release date, product/DLC status, active price, audience origin, buyer eligibility, seller population and actually settled paid orders. The existing [2025 game-rating study](genre-rating-attention-2025.md) establishes that visibility and ratings are not substitutes for orders. The public pages have no sale/revenue/production-cost denominators, and no typical profitability is computed.

This completes the existing *mapping of genre-specific rivals and public paid supply frames*; income distributions, actual competitive sales and owner profits remain unknown. [Machine record](paid-genre-competition-crosswalk-oct2026.json).
