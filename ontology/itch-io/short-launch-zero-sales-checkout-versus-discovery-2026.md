# Two short-launch zero-sale reports: unconfigured checkout versus low click exposure

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** [Structured observation](short-launch-zero-sales-checkout-versus-discovery-2026.json). These are two **selected public first-person reports**, not a census of failed itch.io games, and their clocks and denominators differ.

The [July 17, 2026 seller post](https://itch.io/t/6635856/sale-ends-in-20-hours-203-views-0-sales-thank-you) described **203 page views, one download and zero sales**, labeling the launch period seven days. Crucially, the seller said **Stripe was not connected** for the individual paid games; a **$14.25 PayPal bundle** was available via another route. The [official payment guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) says Direct to you publishers must connect their payment providers. Thus this seller's zero-sales count cannot distinguish missing purchase intent from an unavailable single-game checkout. It is not evidence that no one would purchase with a working route. Other dated posts give inconsistent elapsed-day and view summaries; they are not combined into an exact time series.

The [October 25, 2026 first-game announcement](https://itch.io/t/7220162/nobody-leaves-700-impressions-under-1-click-0-sales-what-makes-you-skip-it) concerned a two-person horror-game team. Its author reported **approximately 700 browse impressions, fewer than 1% clicking**, and **zero sales** roughly two days after launch. A seven-room browser demo was free, with a stated temporary **$1.99 full-game price** against $3.99. That disclosure did **not** provide the exact page visits, browser plays, checkout attempts, configured payment methods or confirmed settled orders.

| Scope | July browser-games portfolio | October first horror game |
|---|---|---|
| Observed exposure | 203 self-reported page-view events | About 700 self-reported browse impressions |
| Other recorded event | One download | Less than 1% click-through (no exact count) |
| Seller-reported sales | 0 | 0 |
| Known checkout limitation | Single-game Stripe unconnected; PayPal bundle separately offered | Checkout readiness unverified |
| Valid paid-buyer conversion estimate | **None** | **None** |

An impression, an actual page visit, a browser play, a checkout attempt and a retained order require **different denominators and verification**. In particular, the evaluator's rule for a disabled payment route may conclude the route cannot be used; it may **not** claim that it has observed *zero successful merchant transactions*. Where no transactions are measured, the correct numerical field is **null (unknown)**, even if a source separately reports zero sales.

These cases advance the existing **buyer payment access** and **acquisition-measurement** tasks by specifying a real observed route-configuration confound and a separate discovery-stage attrition report. They do not establish seller-income averages, zero-sale fractions, causal promotional effects, or a marketwide paid-buyer population. No merchant credentials, accounts, personal information or purchase records were accessed.
