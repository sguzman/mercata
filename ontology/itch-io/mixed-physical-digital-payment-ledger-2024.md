# 2024 mixed-format itch.io sales: physical purchases are not digital-game demand

**Reviewed October 27, 2026.** The creator's [2024 annual account review](https://buttondown.com/Binary/archive/2024-year-in-review-by-numbers/) is a voluntarily published, selected single-publisher report covering tabletop RPGs and supplements, not a representative digital-game seller sample. Currency is USD; the observation period is the 2024 calendar year. [Machine-readable reconciliation](mixed-physical-digital-payment-ledger-2024.json).

The publisher reports **$8,026.85 gross in 358 itch.io payments** and **approximately $6,662.29** after operator share and payout deductions. The latter is not verified cash deposited in a bank, nor profit after printing, postage, marketing or time. A breakdown for two titles supports the following reconciliation:

| 2024 account segment | Gross USD | Payment events |
|---|---:|---:|
| APOCALYPSE FRAME physical | $3,965.00 | 94 |
| APOCALYPSE FRAME digital **and tips** | $1,243.30 | 53 |
| Valiant Horizon physical | $440.00 | 14 |
| Valiant Horizon digital **and tips** | $706.00 | 33 |
| Other itch account activity, composition unspecified | $1,672.55 | 164 |
| **Reported itch account total** | **$8,026.85** | **358** |

The two **identified physical editions** alone comprise **$4,405.00 and 108 payment events**. That is **at least 54.88% of the full account's reported gross**, although just **30.17% of its payment-event count**. The other account activity is not classified, so the *total* physical share might be higher. The $1,949.30 specifically labeled digital **and tips** is not automatically mandatory positive-price digital game purchases. Even real itch.io gross receipts cannot simply be assigned to digital game downloads.

The author's [2023 annual review](https://buttondown.com/Binary/archive/2023-year-in-review-by-numbers/) reported **$12,556.99** before platform share but included an allocated **$4,605.50** collaborator share and **$825.74** in bundle revenue. The 2024 totals include new physical editions and different collaborative accounting. Differencing the year totals therefore **does not isolate year-over-year digital-game sales changes or normal demand volatility**. The author separately reports about $4,210.94 of 2024 business profit across multiple sales channels and print-related costs; it cannot be converted into an itch-only profit margin.

**Implications for the existing tasks:** transaction *counts* differ from USD gross, merchant proceeds, bank settlement and labor-adjusted profit. Even a precisely broken-down creator's annual statement is not a platform GMV total or an eligible seller census. Complete first-paid-release cohorts, refunds, distinct paying buyers, digital-game income and comparable seasonal observations remain unobserved. No private buyer data or payout API was used.
