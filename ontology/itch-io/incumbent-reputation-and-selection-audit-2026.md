# Reputation and incumbent advantage: observed signals versus unmeasured premium

**Review date:** October 27, 2026. A marketplace reputation study should separate (1) public ratings/rank, (2) account follows, (3) editorial or algorithmic placement, and (4) preexisting creator audiences and publication-age effects. All are **possible attention signals**, not observed commercial advantage by themselves.

The independent [2025 public rating study](genre-rating-attention-2025.md) reports about **81%** of its examined pages without ratings and median ratings of zero in each examined genre. The study's frame is not a representative paid-merchant census, and zero ratings do **not** imply zero paying customers. itch.io's [indexing guidance](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-indexed) indicates that discoverability can respond to activity and accurate classification, not a guaranteed ranking or conversion rule. [Follower features](https://itch.io/docs/accounts/followers) may generate notifications, not proven sales.

Selected publisher observations show why matching is necessary. The [The Hive](game-sales-feature-cases-2025-2026.md) report includes 213 followers, approximately 24,800 page views and 510 download events in May 2026; its separate payment count uses a different reporting unit and **cannot** be treated as 141 distinct converting customers. Its later featured appearance occurred with other promotion. The [SuperWEIRD free-alpha report](free-alpha-frontpage-tail-2026.md) describes prominent visibility but concerns a free preview supported by offsite attention, not retained paid itch.io orders.

To measure a genuine incumbent advantage, compare equally priced same-format paid games at matched release ages, genre, language, traffic source and build version, controlling for previous portfolio size, follows and editorial placement. Include overlooked/unranked games and verify positive-price refunds-adjusted orders, costs and labor. The selection correction is essential: taking only visible winners greatly exaggerates apparent commercial success.

**Accepted scope:** the existing task required *studying rankings, reviews, follower counts or legacy positions*. These dated sources establish the corresponding distinct signals and reporting limitations. A **causal incumbent profit, revenue, or conversion premium remains null**.
