# itch.io — publisher ownership, refunds, withheld earnings and buyer data

Reviewed 2026-10-26 against [itch.io Terms of Service](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms), [Creator FAQ](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq), and [quality guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines). The posted Terms page identifies its most recent displayed update as **April 15, 2023**; no account-specific contract was examined. [Machine data](publisher-rights-refunds-2026.json).

## Game rights and multistore distribution

The publisher retains ownership of submitted content, but the platform receives a **worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable** license for distribution, publication and promotion in connection with the service. This is wider than merely allowing a cover image to appear. The grant is **non-exclusive**, so itch.io itself does not impose blanket exclusivity. A publisher still needs independent rights to every incorporated third-party asset, track, font, library, trademark and contracted work. Do not infer automatic right to sell a game elsewhere solely from itch.io's own terms.

The published agreement distinguishes a user (age over 13) from a publisher (over 18, or with legally sufficient consent and capacity). This is general policy, not approval of a real seller.

The [multi-store and external-key scope crosswalk](multistore-rights-external-keys-2026.md) separately documents the non-exclusive itch.io license, external-key stock/availability and the absence of itch-added DRM; game-specific third-party rights and compatibility are not presumed.

## Refunds are separate from payment disputes

The Terms permit buyers to seek refunds when the purchased product cannot run, is inaccessible, or misrepresents its offering. External keys sometimes require the publisher to confirm that the entitlement was cancelled. Therefore gross sales and even captured payment amounts are not necessarily finally retained revenue. No refund share or average order-loss rate can be inferred from the policy.

## A material overlooked financial condition

For **itch.io-collected seller payouts**, the published Terms say amounts from purchases *older than 12 months* remaining unclaimed can be debited at **10% of each original purchase's revenue per month**, until that aging amount is exhausted or the balance is withdrawn. The provision does not make balances negative and permits requesting an extension through support. **This is not a normal platform sales commission, ordinary payment fee, or blanket charge on fresh withdrawals.** No evidence was found here establishing how commonly it is assessed. A serious cash-flow model should account for the conditional risk and request settlement promptly.

## Buyer information is not research inventory

The Creator FAQ forbids publishing or sharing personally identifiable buyer dashboard fields with third parties: buyer names, emails, IP addresses, **country codes** and transaction IDs are explicitly enumerated. It also prohibits adding customers to marketing lists automatically; marketing subscriptions require affirmative consent. Seller email functionality is intended for messages related to a customer's transaction.

Mercata's future seller-cohort research must request only approved, aggregated **non-identifying** summary measures, suppress small cells and not store individual buyer records, even if a seller volunteers them. Any access to private seller reports would require explicit permission and separate review of publication rights. The project has not accessed any such records.

## Policy continuity

Misleading content, rights problems or other guidelines breaches can lead to deindexing or, for more serious violations, an inability to receive pending payments. Search visibility removal and complete removal/payment suspension are distinct. Copyright takedown and appeal workflows exist but do not guarantee restoration or sales recovery.

**Remaining unknown:** actual refunds, lost revenue from disputes, median licensing costs, typical seller data access, and how often unclaimed-earnings maintenance is imposed. No real transactions, legal disputes, buyer records or accounts were accessed.
