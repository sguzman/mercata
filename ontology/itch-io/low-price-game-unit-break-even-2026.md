# Low-price downloadable game unit economics — no demand extrapolation

Reviewed October 26, 2026. [Machine scenario matrix](low-price-game-unit-break-even-2026.json) · [existing tested calculator](../../site/itch-break-even.mjs).

The [itch.io payments guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments) gives a **hypothetical** 10% platform-share configuration and typical 2.9% plus $0.30 per-transaction processor deduction. Mercata's conditional model expresses those fees in cents and rounds them per order. Neither amount is a binding quote for every country or payment mode.

For illustration only, assume **$100 in upfront cash costs**, **20 development hours valued at $20 per hour** (another **$400**), and a goal of **$500 pretax surplus after all those declared expenses**. No sales, traffic or actual developer labor were observed.

| Posted price | Illustrative contribution per retained sale | Paid orders to cover cash + valued labor | Paid orders to cover those costs plus target |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $0.25 | -$0.09 | Not attainable | Not attainable |
| $0.35 | $0.00 | Not attainable | Not attainable |
| $0.50 | $0.14 | 3,572 | 7,143 |
| $1.00 | $0.57 | 878 | 1,755 |
| $2.00 | $1.44 | 348 | 695 |
| $5.00 | $4.05 | 124 | 247 |
| $10.00 | $8.41 | 60 | 119 |

A low-price title with **nonpositive marginal contribution** has no finite paid-order count that can cover fixed costs under these assumptions. This is a calculation about costs, **not a claim that low-priced games cannot succeed** or that any listed title will attract the required buyers.

The table deliberately excludes local tax treatment, additional disputes, payment withholding, bank FX, postlaunch costs not entered, and any undetected change to actual gateway fees. Amounts are in USD and are not currency-converted. Money collected is not profit before those other costs.

**Acceptance scope:** concrete fee basis, calculation, testable thresholds and missing evidence are established. Real title sales, customer price sensitivity, net proceeds, buyer demand and representative developer income remain unknown.
