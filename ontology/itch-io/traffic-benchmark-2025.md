# itch.io traffic benchmark — 169 voluntarily submitted developer reports (2025)

**Source:** [How To Market A Game, May 12, 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/12/benchmark-itch-io-traffic/). Reviewed October 26, 2026. [Exact bounded machine record](traffic-benchmark-2025.json).

This study is genuinely itch.io-specific: the author invited developers to provide their project traffic and promotion information, receiving **169 volunteer developer submissions**. It measures **lifetime view/play/download events**, *not paid orders, unique buyers or developer earnings*. Because developer recruitment was voluntary and likely tied to marketing interest, the results are **not representative platform-wide medians**.

## Reported project activity

| Source section | Mean | Median | 30th percentile | 70th percentile |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Views | 54,483 | 1,582 | 397.5 | 12,150 |
| Downloads (downloadable subset) | 6,017 | 113 | 32.6 | 966.4 |
| All stats: views | 54,482 | 1,582 | 418 | 13,380 |
| All stats: browser plays | 24,428 | 590 | 113 | 4,982 |
| All stats: downloads | **58,976 as printed** | 110 | 26 | 809 |

**Publication discrepancies:** the article prints **6,017** as mean downloads in one section but **58,976** in its All stats table. It also reports two different 30th and 70th view percentiles. Both conflicting versions are retained; without access to the dataset, we cannot fix them by guessing. The 58,976 figure in particular must **not** be cited as a reliable mean download count.

The author describes browser-play activity as substantially higher than downloading, with a comparison table listing median 590 browser plays versus median 110 downloads. These groups may differ in developers, titles, game types, marketing effort and launch dates. The reported percentages are **ratios of page-view and interaction event counts**, not confirmed per-person buyer conversion. The article includes anecdotes about influencer amplification and storefront features, but their causal effects and expected income cannot be estimated from self-selected cases.

## What this improves and what it does not

This offers a useful **traffic-scale reference for some voluntarily reporting itch.io developers** and justifies collecting page views, browser plays and downloads separately. It does not prove that itch.io creators generally earn nothing, and does not establish the median paid-game buyer count, successful release probability, developer income or profit. The [authorized seller cohort protocol](transaction-cohort-protocol-2026.md) is still necessary to answer those questions.

No private account or original underlying spreadsheet was accessed; this work uses only public text with attribution and explicit discrepancies.
