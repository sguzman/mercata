# Public price snapshots are not paid-game earnings

Reviewed **October 26, 2026**. The [machine audit](paid-2d-listed-price-observation-boundary-2026.json) uses Mercata's existing [dated 15-row sample](paid-2d-popular-prices-2026.json), collected from itch.io's [Paid + 2D Popular listing](https://itch.io/games/store/tag-2d). It does not add games, sellers or new research tasks.

The observed filter had **12,691 visible results**, but the recorded sample consists of only the **first 15 Popular-ranked listings**. This is intentionally *not random*. Popular sorting favors attention and is unsuitable for estimating the typical paid game price, income or new-publisher outcomes.

| Sample definition | Rows | Displayed USD median |
|---|---:|---:|
| All 15 ranked listings | 15 | **$5.99** |
| Excluding two 100%-discounted zero-price listings | 13 | **$10.00** |
| Plain-price badge only, excluding every sale badge | 11 | **$10.00** |

The complete visible range was **$0 to $25**. There were **11 plain price badges, two regular sale badges, and two 100%-discount badges**. The $0 promotions remain listed under the Paid filter; they are not proof of permanently free games or positive-price purchases at that moment. Every figure above is a **description of these sampled rows**, not a population price parameter.

The relevant comparator set for a 2D downloadable first-game proposal includes paid-filter games, but it must separately record current buyer price, price changes, platform compatibility, release recency and whether an individual game has genuinely completed any paid orders. The filter does not supply transactions, publisher identities, sell-through, game labor or buyer country. No median profit, competitive rank by revenue, paying-buyer probability or typical earnings is supported.

These observations meet the original sampling-query, sample price range, denomination, currency and discount-handling tasks while preserving unanswered commercial questions as unknown.
