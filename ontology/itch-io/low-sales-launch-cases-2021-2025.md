# Low and zero itch.io unit-sales postmortems — bounded cases

Reviewed October 26, 2026. Three *developer-published self-disclosures*, not a randomly sampled group of paid game publishers. [Machine cases](low-sales-launch-cases-2021-2025.json).

## Dream Warrior — one itch.io unit, not zero expense

[The Dream Warrior postmortem](https://high-tower-games.itch.io/dream-warrior/devlog/860725/dream-warrior-postmortem) describes a small retro RPG that began in a 2023 jam, with later improvements and an August 2024 Steam launch. It reported **one itch.io unit**, **107 Steam units**, **$486 total gross game revenue**, **six returns** and **about $140 expenses**, despite describing its *production cash budget* as **$0**. The author estimates about **$120** deposited as profit from the game after expenses/fees, still without valuing the many development and marketing hours. Neither $486 nor $120 is an **itch.io-specific revenue or profit figure**. The account is not a matched comparison of buyer exposure between Steam and itch.io.

## What's Left Behind — zero itch.io units at a particular date

In [What's Left Behind, February 2021](https://brian-hoovler.itch.io/wlb/devlog/218277/whats-left-behind-a-postmortem), the publisher reported **zero itch.io units**, compared with **64 Steam units** before **17 Steam refunds** and **47 net Steam sales**. It reported about **nine months of development**, **seven post-launch patches** and a one-off **~8-hour delay** before its paid itch.io page became visible through the new-release index. The snapshot is about two weeks after publication: it **does not mean the game never later sold an itch.io copy**. The page describes a 3D city-builder, not a narrowly matched 2D game.

## Amadeus Episode 1 — more than $100, but mixed routes

A [late-2025 visual-novel release postmortem](https://arcanaxix.itch.io/amadeus-ep1-waltz/devlog/1128359/thank-you-launch-week-postmortem-2026-plans) reported **more than $100 cumulative itch.io receipts**, combining earlier free-prototype donations with sales/tips from a new commercial episode. The creator also reported **40+ Steam copies**, separately. No exact positive-price itch sale count, release-week itch gross in isolation, development hours or creator net profit can be extracted.

See also [two 2025 low-sale individual game snapshots](zero-five-paid-game-snapshots-2025.md), which preserve source dates and distinguish other-title free downloads from paid units.

## Why this matters

These cases counterbalance the previously recorded 1,776-unit Mixolumia success and small Hive earnings. Together they demonstrate that publishers can have sharply different individual outcomes. However, voluntary postmortems are *selected by willingness to write a post*, and exposure windows, genres and charging models differ. **No platform-wide zero-earner fraction, positive-price newcomer survival rate, median earnings or causal advantage of Steam over itch.io can be inferred.**

A mandatory-paid-title cohort with a fixed 30/90/365-day observation horizon, including deleted and unsold games, remains a separate and unresolved task.
