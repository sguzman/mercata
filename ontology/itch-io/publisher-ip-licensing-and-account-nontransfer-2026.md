# Publisher ownership, platform license, external keys and account transfer

Reviewed **October 27, 2026**. [Structured rights crosswalk](publisher-ip-licensing-and-account-nontransfer-2026.json).

The [itch.io Terms of Service](https://itch.io/docs/legal/terms) distinguish three separate rights. **The publisher retains ownership** of uploaded content. The publisher grants the operator a **worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, sublicensable and transferable** license for hosting, distributing, displaying and promoting that content as part of the service. An authorized buyer has a **non-exclusive, perpetual license** for uses permitted through the service, including surviving removal under the terms. None transfers the copyright in the game to the platform or buyer.

**Non-exclusivity is not a blanket distribution guarantee.** itch.io's ordinary hosting terms do not bar the original rights-holder from releasing the same owned game through another store. But a commissioned soundtrack, art asset, proprietary engine license, external store marketing agreement or individual publisher contract might be exclusive, time-limited or region-limited. No project-specific contract was inspected. For those sources the duration, territory and authorization remain unknown.

## Accounts, buyers and files have different transfer rules

| Object | Operator-stated rule | What must not be assumed |
|---|---|---|
| User/publisher account | Terms prohibit selling, transferring or assigning accounts to others | A game-asset sale necessarily includes transfer of the existing store account |
| Existing project and buyers | Buyers may retain rights to eligible files added to the same project | Creating a fresh project page migrates buyer access or ratings |
| Game copyright | Remains with legitimate IP owner | Purchaser or platform becomes new copyright owner |
| Purchased copy/license | Nonexclusive service-permitted use | Commercial redistribution or secondary resale is automatically permitted |
| Original purchase gift | Buyer can gift under purchase features | This equals a verified secondary resale market |
| Download/external key | May grant access without new money changing hands | Every key redeemed represents another paid-game order |

The [Creator FAQ](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq) says itch.io doesn't add DRM. The [download-key guide](https://itch.io/docs/creators/download-keys) and [quality guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines) prefer actual downloadable files on itch.io; external Steam keys may be offered as additional entitlements, but a key-only page normally violates the standard upload expectations. An untested game binary might still contain its own DRM and should not be advertised as verified offline-capable.

This is **public-rule research**, not a copyright clearance for an actual game. No private project assets, publishing agreement, secondary resale transaction, account transfer or refund was tested. Actual third-party rights, secondary resale legality and exclusive geographic/duration restrictions remain specific unknowns.
