The initial two-product comparator totaled **9 payment events / 350 page-view events**, or **2.57 payments per 100 views**. The later comparator reported **11 / 501**, or **2.20 per 100 views**. Those are **event ratios**, not deduplicated-buyer conversion rates. The observations are across different product ages and dates; the earlier and later cumulative windows **must not be summed**.

## Why a zero direct-payment count is not a zero-purchase entitlement

The author later reported that the unsuccessful icon pack had reached a buyer through a **two-pack bundle**. That entitlement was separate from the pack's **zero direct project-page purchases**. The author also described a 13-payment account ledger in which bundle purchases appeared under a sale page while the other two pack payments appeared under project pages. An account payment, a bundle purchase and an individual project-page payment are different accounting units.

## Why the comparison cannot prove a pricing or marketing effect

The [current storefront](https://z3er1n.itch.io/icons-vol2) shows **454 icons** and a **USD 4.95** regular price with a **USD 3.71** promotion. The original September report described a **381-icon** version. The author also described changes to free samples, thumbnails, page copy, and file contents over time. Their visitor populations and timing are not matched.

One forum response calculated a 3.2% chance of seeing zero sales if every page view were an independent identical purchasing trial at a comparator rate. That **conditional numerical model is not an observed significance result**: visitors can return, have different intentions, buy through bundles, and interact with changing pages. The audit intentionally computes no p-value or causal uplift.

**Unobserved:** unique paid buyers, purchase/session joins, independent auditing of the seller's ledger, per-product settled cash and fees, production labor, and matched paid-game sales. The three pages belong to **one creator** and sell **assets**, so they cannot populate any representative game-publisher mean, median, or zero-earner rate. No account access, transaction downloads, or personal buyer data were used.
