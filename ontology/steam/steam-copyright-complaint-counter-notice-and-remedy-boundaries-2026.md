# Steam copyright complaints, DMCA counter-notices and publisher remedies: separate routes

**Reviewed October 29, 2026.** [Structured primary-source audit](steam-copyright-complaint-counter-notice-and-remedy-boundaries-2026.json) · [synthetic route classifier](../../site/steam-dispute-route-guards.mjs) · [verification tests](../../tests/steam-dispute-route-guards.test.mjs).

This is **public legal-process research, not legal advice, an appeal service, or an analysis of actual complaints**. No dispute, identity details, private correspondence, Steamworks contract or legal filing was accessed or submitted.

## Which official channel addresses which situation?

| Situation | Public route or authority | What cannot be assumed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A rights holder alleges Steam-hosted copyright infringement | Valve's [copyright notice form](https://steamcommunity.com/dmca/create/?l=english) | That filing proves infringement, causes guaranteed removal, or creates an automatic game publisher appeal |
| Material disabled after a qualifying U.S. DMCA notice | [17 U.S.C. §512(g)](https://copyright.gov/title17/92chap5.html) provides a **conditional** counter-notice/replacement framework | That every removed Steam game, developer agreement or content review decision automatically falls within that statute |
| A rights holder alleges trademark misuse | A [separate route described by Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey?language=english) | That trademark complaints use the same DMCA counter-notification process |
| A Steamworks partner has a publishing question | [Steamworks contact/support](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/help/contact?l=english) | A guaranteed independent appeal tribunal, timed reinstatement or waived contract obligations |
| A store or build fails Valve's release review | [Store Review Process](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/review_process?l=english) | Proof that a copyright takedown notice or judicial decision occurred |
| The publisher wants to stop new sales | [Retiring a product](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/retire_app?l=english) | A third-party enforcement action, automatic refund of every prior sale or loss of all existing library entitlements |

## The copyright form is a legal notice, not private support chat

Valve's form asks a copyright holder or authorized representative to identify the allegedly infringing Valve-hosted material and copyrighted work. The page warns that notice details and contact information **may be shared** and that knowingly materially misrepresenting infringement can have consequences under **17 U.S.C. §512(f)**.

Those are public process and disclosure warnings. They do not establish that any particular product is infringing, that a complaint was accepted, or that a developer has an ordinary no-risk appeal path. No personal identification or private allegation should be collected or published in this repository.

## The 10–14 business-day rule is conditional

Under the U.S. **§512(g)** safe-harbor process, an effective counter-notice is a legally consequential statement involving identification of disabled material, a good-faith mistaken-removal assertion under penalty of perjury, contact information, and consent to appropriate U.S. court jurisdiction and service. Where the specified statutory process applies, a service provider's replacement obligation has a **10–14 business-day** framework after receipt, **subject to notice of a lawsuit seeking a court order**.

This is **not an unconditional promise by Valve** to restore a full commercial game within 14 days. It must not be conflated with Steamworks support tickets, a rejected store page, voluntary retirement, disputes over signed Steam Distribution Agreement terms, bank payment holds or ordinary consumer account sanctions. The full scope and remedies for an actual product depend on the facts, applicable law, contractual terms and qualified legal review.

## Machine-readable negative guarantees

The [pure classifier](../../site/steam-dispute-route-guards.mjs) accepts only labeled invented fixtures and preserves separate public procedure categories. The **conditional U.S. statutory window** is attached only to the explicitly qualifying synthetic counter-notification route. All cases have **unknown actual removal status, no guaranteed game reinstatement, unknown merchant sales and unknown publisher profit**.

This protects against the common but economically consequential error of converting a published legal remedy into a measured reinstate-rate or expected payout schedule. Nothing in the linked public sources supplies representative counts of copyright takedowns, successful counters, time to restoration, withheld partner revenues or lost sales.

**Research gate:** a meaningful empirical study would require legitimately obtained, anonymized case dispositions, exact policy/notice grounds and time periods, contract-controlled outcomes, store and game availability, authentic order data, and support/recovery costs. No such dataset was available in this audit.
