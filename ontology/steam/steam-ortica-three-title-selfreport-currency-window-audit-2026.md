Store pages independently support these identities and dates, **not** the private sales or tax figures.

The author's per-developer allocations are illustrative divisions, not proven payments.

The reported approximate portfolio totals are $350,000 gross and €84,000 after deductions. Adding the displayed per-game figures gives $354,957 and €84,682, respectively. These are approximate, non-synchronized reports, not a reconciled transaction sum or a profit statement.

## Publication chronology requires qualification

The page is dated **September 30, 2026**, yet it describes the *Loan Shark* 71,080-copy checkpoint as **October 26, 2026**. That observation is later than the page's visible publication date. The public text may have been edited, or a date may be wrong; an authenticated edit history was not found in the cited text. **Do not relabel this checkpoint September 30, assume the three title snapshots are simultaneous, or calculate a fixed-horizon median from them.** The *Nuraga* article includes an explicitly later September 28 unit update but retains the September 8 gross figure; an updated gross is not supplied.

The author's observations about festivals, short-form video, and front-page placement concern attention and wishlist changes. They do not isolate causal sales uplift. The launch-day wishlist stocks are not fixed purchaser cohorts and cannot be used as exact lifetime conversion denominators.

## Scope and inference

The three titles belong to **one self-selected studio**. They cannot be pooled as three independent publishers, treated as representative Steam developers, or used to estimate a zero-sale fraction. Work durations are approximate (roughly 100 working days for *Loan Shark* and 140 for each of the other games), without enough contractual, expense, or individual labor evidence for hourly profit. The small sample illustrates title-to-title economic variability and the importance of separating gross USD, author-defined after-tax EUR, and actual legal-publisher cash.

No private records or paid partner credentials were accessed.
