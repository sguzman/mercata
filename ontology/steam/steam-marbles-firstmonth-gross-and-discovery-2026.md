# Marble's Marbles: a short-production first-month gross disclosure and an unmatched launch funnel

**Reviewed October 30, 2026.** [Source-linked study](steam-marbles-firstmonth-gross-and-discovery-2026.json) · [regression tests](../../tests/steam-marbles-firstmonth-gross-window.test.mjs).

## Product identity and observation boundary

The [official Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4137920/) identifies *Marble's Marbles* (AppID **4137920**) as a game developed by **James Oliver**, published under the **Destined** label, and commercially released **January 7, 2026**. This public metadata does not establish the legal ownership/settlement relationship, and a developer name or publisher label is not evidence of a specific person's bank receipts.

In a [February 13, 2026 first-person postmortem](https://www.reddit.com/r/gamedev/comments/1r3ahi2/), the developer reported approximately **4,300 copies sold** and **over $30,000 gross** in the *first month*, alongside an approximately **6.5% refund rate**. The blog-style report arrived **37 calendar days after commercial release**, and the exact cutoffs for sold units, gross and refunds are not independently reconciled. The developer says the game was built in **under two months**, but that is not an audited labor-hours or expense statement.

| Public disclosure | Reported | What it does *not* establish |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Rough first-month sales | ~4,300 copies | Complete 30-day reconciled net paid units |
| First-month author-labelled gross | Over $30,000 | Partner payouts, personal income, after-cost profit |
| Refund rate | ~6.5% | Exact return counts, same unit/revenue cohort, refund causal explanation |
| Launch wishlist stock | ~5,800 | Matched original wishlisters who eventually bought |
| Week-before-launch wishlist stock | Less than 3,000 | Verified acquisition attribution over that particular week |
| Post-launch outstanding wishlist stock | ~22,000 | Additional paid units or unique incremental buyers |
| Steam discovery impressions | ~2.5 million | Unique players, clicks, purchases, or revenue attributable to discovery |
| External trailer views | ~30,000 | Sale conversion from verified viewers |

The story describes a short, recognizable physics game with an approximately two-hour playtime, developed quickly without a playable demo, alongside successful trailer coverage and an unusual late wishlist rise. These events **overlap**. No randomized exposure, comparable unexposed launch or linked buyer-to-traffic dataset establishes that its visuals, game length, lack of demo, external video, or Steam discovery *caused* a particular number of purchases.

## Why a higher-sales example does not contradict a lower one

Compare the separate [Lost in Art first-game audit](steam-lostinart-early-launch-wishlist-window-2026.md): its author reported about **14,000 launch wishlists** and **187 sales** after roughly **one and a half days**. *Marble's Marbles* reported about **5,800 launch wishlists** and **4,300 sales** over a loosely dated **first month**. These are **not equal observation horizons**, and the games differ in genre, price, trailer distribution, team, demo strategy, page age and audience. The ratios 187/14,000 and 4,300/5,800 are mechanically computable but **neither** measures purchase conversion among matched wishlist accounts. Their contrast cannot quantify typical developer odds, launch-page quality, or any single marketing intervention.

The post is one selected positive self-report; the public store only corroborates release identity. No original Steamworks transaction ledger, complete regional discount history, authenticated gross-to-net settlement, personal bank data, contractor expense records or representative frame was examined.
