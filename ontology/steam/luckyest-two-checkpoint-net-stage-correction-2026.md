# Luckyest: one game's first-day milestone, later growth and corrected "net"

**Reviewed October 29, 2026.** [Machine record](luckyest-two-checkpoint-net-stage-correction-2026.json) · [regression tests](../../tests/steam-luckyest-net-stage-and-window.test.mjs).

## Two reports describe one game

The [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3803930/Luckyest/) dates *Luckyest* (AppID 3803930; Mobmonks developer and publisher labels) to **September 23, 2025**. In an [early author post](https://www.reddit.com/r/IndieDev/comments/1nq2j1r/i_sold_100_copies_of_my_game_on_steam/), the developer said the game crossed **100 units within the first 24 hours** and had four reviews and about **90 launch wishlists**. "Crossed 100" is not an exact 100-unit reading and the post's exact public publication date was not verified. The author describes roughly two and a half months of development, but no audited labor hours.

A [December 21 follow-up](https://www.reddit.com/r/IndieDev/comments/1ps1jy0/update_from_100_copies_to_10000_copies_sold_on/), **89 calendar days after** the store release, reports **10,525 cumulative units**, about **$20,000 gross**, and roughly **7% refunds**. These are two *right-censored, cumulative checkpoints of the same title*, not two independent earnings cases. The larger later sales number cannot be extrapolated to other early low-wishlist releases, and no matched wishlist purchases are supplied. A new inventory of around 3,000 pending wishlists is a stock measured much later than the launch's around 90.

## A material clarification about "net"

The December post initially presents about **$16,500 as net** with wording that could be read as including the Steam cut. In a direct author reply, the developer clarifies that this is **after some return and tax deductions but before the Steam platform share**, additional U.S. withholding, and any subsequent local personal income taxes. The author estimates that approximately 55% of gross might ultimately be available after specified deductions; this remains a forecast, not a demonstrated bank transfer. [Valve's reporting FAQ](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/finance/payments_salesreporting/faq) gives the formal accounting-stage distinctions.

Do not automatically present the $16,500 as after-Valve proceeds, subtract 30% twice, or report the author's hypothetical $11,000 as a verified personal receipt. The precise region-level sales mix, contract, refunds cohort, payouts, foreign-exchange, and applicable tax treatment are not independently audited. Steam's live store price cannot reconstruct historical gross because transactions can be discounted or regionally priced.

## Cohort and source-integrity boundary

The [source-family register](source-families.json) groups the two public developer updates into **one selected game**. Two URLs do not imply two independently sampled sale histories. The observed single-game growth shows one possible path, but not its frequency among all first paid releases or a causal recipe for market success. There are no verified zeros in these selected positive reports.

**Unknown:** matched first-7/30-day orders, bank payout, labor-adjusted net profit, legal partner/payee distribution, full first-game population median, and actual zero-paid-sale fraction. No private merchant records were accessed.
