# Steam Deck Verified as a competitive position: design without causal claims

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** [Structured methodology](deck-verified-competitive-positioning-2026.json).

Valve's [Verified landing pages guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/compat/verified?language=english) states that qualifying Verified games receive a special page if the store has an age-appropriate published trailer. This provides another merchandising surface, not a published conversion or sales figure.

Valve's [Deck compatibility categories](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/compat?l=english) are **Verified, Playable, Unsupported, and Unknown**. Unknown is unreviewed and cannot be treated as an incompatible game. Non-Verified titles can still sell on Steam. A causal comparison cannot simply compare badge medians: genres, launch dates, marketing, controller support, production budgets, and preexisting interest influence both badge pursuit and results.

A credible first step is a same-month, same-genre, similarly priced cohort of originally paid games, fixed 90-day outcomes, contemporaneous badge snapshots and tracking of relevant QA labor and hardware usage. Even matched public sales *models* could at most show correlation without a defensible counterfactual. **No real device benchmarks, verified paid sales, Deck buyer mix, badge-associated sales gains, or development-hour costs were measured.**
