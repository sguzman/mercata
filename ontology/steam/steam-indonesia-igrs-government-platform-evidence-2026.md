# Indonesia's April 2026 IGRS dispute: rating badge is not a game delisting

**Reviewed October 31, 2026.** Primary sources: [Indonesian ministry's rating-label concern](https://eppid.komdigi.go.id/berita/detail/kemkomdigi-rating-gim-di-steam-bukan-rating-resmi-berpotensi-langgar-ketentuan-di-indonesia) · [April 7 ministry follow-up](https://portal.komdigi.go.id/kanal-publik/berita-kini/10138) · [Valve's Indonesian rating instructions](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey/indonesia?l=english) · [structured chronology](steam-indonesia-igrs-government-platform-evidence-2026.json) · [tests](../../tests/steam-indonesia-igrs-government-platform-evidence.test.mjs).

## Cross-check two authorities rather than treating ratings as a single equivalent badge

Valve's public Steamworks instructions describe **two rating routes** relevant to displaying a game to new Steam buyers in Indonesia: an official IGRS/Komdigi-issued rating or a Valve/Steam-generated rating based on the developer content survey and other review inputs. Valve cautions that content restrictions still apply and that truthfully completing a content survey does **not** guarantee sale approval.

In a statement referring to **April 5, 2026**, Indonesia's Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs **disputed the use of some IGRS ratings displayed on Steam**, saying that internal self-declared classifications had appeared with official government IGRS labeling despite lacking government verification. This is a first-party **regulator allegation/concern** over classification authenticity and presentation. It is not a public item-by-item adjudication or evidence that *all* Steam self-ratings are government-issued IGRS certificates.

On **April 7, 2026**, the same ministry reported investigating age/content mismatches, communicating with Steam and that Steam had **taken down the problematic displayed age-rating badges** while reviewing the matter. The ministry did **not** establish in that statement that the associated games had been removed from sale. **A removed rating display, a game hidden from new purchasers, and an already-owned license no longer launching are three different outcomes.** Valve's documentation separately says an existing customer keeps access to their game when the rating affects Indonesian storefront visibility.

Neither this dispute nor the German age-rating rule quantifies Indonesian missed revenue, verifies a zero-sales game or measures causal buyer demand. Regional sampling must retain the *original paid eligible cohort*, track individual storefront eligibility over time and reconcile authenticated partner financial data. No app lists, private user records or merchant documents were collected.

**Method:** This is a comparison of distinct contemporaneous public institutional accounts (government regulator, platform operator). The government's two statements are updates from **one** regulator, not two independent statistical samples. Claims about regulatory approval should be rechecked against current primary official documents.
