# Steam versus itch.io: first paid-game launch channels

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** [Structured comparison](steam-versus-itchio-first-game-channel-2026.json). This compares publicly documented commercial mechanics, **not** buyer demand or observed platform earnings.

| Dimension | Steam | itch.io |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial product fee | $100 USD per new app; potentially recouped when adjusted gross reaches $1,000 | No mandatory hosting/listing fee for project pages |
| Pre-release process | First-title 30-day post-fee public minimum, 14-day public Coming Soon and store/build reviews | Creator can publish a free project page and choose visibility; no corresponding Steamworks review clock |
| Nominal platform share | Actual signed royalty terms and applicable history need verification; do not apply a guessed universal rate | Open revenue sharing: creator-selected 0–100%, default 10%, plus separate payment costs |
| Checkout/payout | Valve-managed merchant reports with refund/tax and bank-payment scheduling | Either direct PayPal/Stripe settlement or itch.io-collected later payouts, with distinct fee/tax rules |
| Products and signals | Steam paid games, demos, Playtests, retail keys, wishlists | Paid, free and pay-what-you-want downloads, browser HTML5 and page analytics |

Official sources: [Steam Direct fee](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee), [Steam onboarding](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/onboarding), [itch.io creator FAQ](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq), [itch.io payments](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments), and [itch.io project creation](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started).

## A lower nominal fee does not prove higher earnings

Steam charges a per-product entry fee and operates a defined review/release gate. itch.io lets creators choose their platform share and supports voluntary payments, but processor fees and tax/withholding vary by payout mode and country. An itch.io zero-price download or donation isn't a paid Steam game sale; Steam key activation via an off-store retailer isn't a Steam checkout event either.

Steam's discovery infrastructure and wishlists may be valuable, but no current public policy demonstrates a guaranteed launch audience or conversion rate. itch.io can host a free HTML5 playable prototype without a paid-game registration fee, but no public seller guide makes browser plays equivalent to paying customers. A cross-platform comparison must not simply use the nominal share or consumer site size as the expected profit.

## Future same-game measurement

A credible game-level comparison would specify paid/free status, exactly matched product builds and content, launch timing, base prices and region currencies, coupon/discount treatment, cross-store links and Steam key distribution, advertising/referrals, service costs, refunds and settlement dates. It would reconcile both channels' real paid orders and after-share net cash separately. It also needs legally authorized commercial terms and player privacy safeguards.

**No same-game sales panel, merchant records, randomized channel test, distribution contract or developer profit was observed.** The [synthetic comparison guard](../../site/steam-channel-comparison.mjs) refuses to rank platforms by profit when net reports are absent.
