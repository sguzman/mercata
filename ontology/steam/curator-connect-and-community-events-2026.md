# Steam Curator Connect and community updates: outreach, rights and false conversion denominators

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** [Structured source record](curator-connect-and-community-events-2026.json). Official public Steamworks documents; **no review copies sent, campaigns run or community posts published**.

## Complimentary curator copies are not retail sales

Valve's [Curators and Curator Connect documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/curators) describes Curators as Steam groups or individuals who recommend, criticize or explain games to their followers. Through Curator Connect, a developer can offer **complimentary licenses** directly to selected Curators without sending redeemable keys. The developer needs the partner permission **Generate CD Keys**, a public Coming Soon page (or released store page) and a playable game build.

A partner can offer a title to **up to 100 Curators**, with **up to five copies** for each. This is a capacity limit, **not** evidence of 500 accepted copies, reviewed games, audience impressions or purchases. Curators have no obligation to accept, play or review a provided license. Unaccepted offers can expire; once accepted, the license cannot be revoked via this feature.

Selection filters include supported genres/tags, language, operating system and stated audience reach. A Curator Connect group may be absent because it has not reviewed or been active recently. Follower numbers and niche fit are screening signals, not paid conversion or causal reach measurements. A complimentary license is not a Steam checkout transaction and must never enter the paid-units numerator.

## Steam Events and Announcements have multiple surfaces

Valve's [Events and Announcements Tools](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/event_tools) let authorized product/group representatives publish posts or time-limited events for game updates. Events can surface on the store page, community hub, player Library, follower activity feeds, Workshop hub if tagged, and Steam's RSS feed. Those are possible channels, **not** independent unique readers; one customer may appear in several.

Posting requires appropriate publisher/Community-hub rights, and the author's public profile can be displayed. Steam supports localized event images and text, with English fallback where translations are unavailable. Event categories affect exposure, end dates and content expectations; unrelated cross-promotion requires correct classification. Publication may precede user visibility, and Valve warns that moderation can delay Library appearance by about an hour. Events cannot be newly published with their start date in the past or have their already-passed start time moved retroactively.

## A research-grade outreach experiment needs different measurements

A future study should separately record: Curator offers, accepted complimentary licenses, voluntarily published Curator recommendations, followed/clicked referral links, UTM tagged visits, wishlists, **merchant-verified purchases**, refunds, and actual promotion labor/cost. For announcements, record post category, first-visible timestamps, localization, eligible surfaces and prior owner/follower segments. Controls must account for festival seasons, patch updates, genre, review exposure and existing purchasers.

The actual impact of an extra Curator recommendation or Steam event on *new buyers*, *repeat paid orders*, support burden or developer profit remains unknown. This study is a measurement and eligibility checkpoint; **no publisher account, audience profile data, private traffic reports or monetization records were accessed**.
