# First-game two-week gross versus the author's undefined net stage

**Reviewed October 29, 2026.** [Machine case](steam-author-sim-firstgame-net-stage-2026.json) · [stage audit](../../site/steam-author-net-stage-audit.mjs) · [tests](../../tests/steam-author-net-stage-audit.test.mjs).

## One first-person report, not a market sample

A self-identified solo developer [reported a first Steam game, Author Sim, released August 12, 2026, in an August 26 retrospective](https://www.reddit.com/r/gamedev/comments/1vz3t5u/postmortem_of_a_solo_devs_first_game_7k_gross_in/). The author gave **820 copies sold**, **82 returns**, **USD 7,047 gross** and **USD 5,812 labeled net**. They also reported 5,175 wishlists at launch and 7,069 subsequently. These are voluntary, unauthenticated disclosures; the calendar dates are 14 days apart but exact query timestamps are not available.

## Why “net” does not identify deposited publisher earnings

The source does not adequately define the deductions behind its **USD 5,812 net** label. An illustration applying a flat 30% deduction to the **same reported USD 7,047** gross amount produces **USD 4,932.90**, not USD 5,812. This mismatch warns against treating the displayed net label as already after Steam's share. The calculation does not establish the actual contractual share basis, refunds, taxes, chargebacks, currency conversion or remittance timing. The [auditor](../../site/steam-author-net-stage-audit.mjs) records the comparison but retains actual bank cash and after-labor profit as unknown.

The 82 divided by 820 figure is 10% **within this author's reported units**; it is not a purchaser-cohort refund probability. Wishlist stocks and an author's festival/trailer/store-calendar attribution are not matched paid customer conversions or causal channel effects.

A first-game story from a developer who voluntarily posted figures remains selected evidence. The true median Steam publisher's bank receipt and exact zero-sale fraction cannot be inferred from it.
