## A selected first-party disclosure and a different-day official observation

The [official Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4825270/Crane_Game_Master/) lists *Crane Game Master* (AppID 4825270), a June 30, 2026 release date, developer/publisher labels MAgaming and a paid store offer observed October 30. A current paid offer and display release date do not independently verify the game's original checkout price, first paid playable minute, legal payee or genuine paid orders.

The [July 9, 2026 developer-authored post](https://www.reddit.com/r/IndieDev/comments/1urmmu2/my_first_steam_game_sold_only_5_copies_how_do_you/) links to that title and reports **about five copies sold so far**. Follow-up author replies describe a first commercial Steam release and solo project. July 9 is **nine calendar days** after the public June 30 date, not an authenticated full nine-day sale ledger. The figure is approximate, voluntarily self-reported and unverified against Steamworks reports.

At October 30 observation, the game's Steam page displayed **“No user reviews.”** This is the public review display, **not** an independently verified count of all submitted reviews, and importantly **not observed on the same day as the July sales disclosure**.

## Implication for low-end estimates

If the author's positive sale disclosure is accurate, this title illustrates why a currently missing public-review signal cannot be equated with lifetime zero paid orders. The [existing IndieList methodology audit](indielist-v1-reviewless-excluded-vs-zero-paid-sale-2026.md) correctly retains missing review-based estimates as missing, rather than imputing zero. A separate [second-person Crazy City Golf case](crazy-city-golf-dayone-four-sales-wishlist-conversion-denominators-2026.md) reports a similar observation but is less direct evidence. **Neither case contains audited transactions** and neither validates a review multiplier.

This one selected title does **not** identify an exact number of gross paid checkout units, whether any refunded, net publisher receipts, profit, the stock of wishlists, the number of unique purchasers, purchase access by country, or the share of all Steam paid releases with zero sales. Do not project the early report to a lifetime total or treat the later display as proof that the game had no reviews on July 9. The post is an opt-in anecdote, not a probability sample.
