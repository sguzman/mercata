# Valve's 2025 Steam aggregates: annual gross, payees and buyer denominators

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** [Structured study](valve-2025-platform-and-gdc-2026-aggregate-bounds.json). The evidence comes from [Valve's own March 6, 2026 Steam Year In Review](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/steamworks/announcements/detail/528746884222682053) and separately identified **journalist reports of Valve's GDC 2026 presentation**. No merchant records or account data were obtained.

## Official first-party platform aggregate

Valve reports a **76% share paid across all non-Valve games on Steam in calendar 2025**, excluding what a publisher may make by selling Steam keys outside Steam. This is a **revenue-weighted all-game aggregate**, not an individual publisher's royalty rate or median independent developer share. Valve references the historic marginal **75% and 80% share tiers** announced in 2018. An aggregated percentage without total platform revenue does not establish Steam-wide gross dollars, mean publisher payout or actual developer profit.

Valve also reports **100 exabytes** delivered to Steam users in 2025 (compared with about **80 exabytes** in 2024). This comprises installs and updates; it is not a count of paid downloads or a publisher-specific infrastructure cost. Its March 2026 retrospective says peak concurrently connected Steam clients reached **42 million**, which is not the number of unique annual buyers, purchasers or simultaneous game players.

## Valve-attributed game revenue thresholds, reported secondhand

[Game Developer's account of GDC 2026](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/valve-says-5-836-titles-earned-over-100-000-on-steam-in-2025) reports that **5,863 Steam games earned above $100,000 in calendar-2025 revenue**. It describes roughly **3,000** crossing that threshold in 2020. [GameDiscoverCo's reporting on another presentation slide](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-steams-2026-biz-update-tells) reports **2,395 games exceeding $500,000** in calendar-2025 gross Steam revenue.

These are **nested annual gross-revenue counts across all game release ages**. A game released in 2014 can have revenue in 2025. They are **not** 5,863 or 2,395 games that *first launched in 2025*, and they cannot be divided by a contemporary new-release total to estimate the odds that a new studio succeeds. Neither threshold represents a developer's annual salary, net royalty, or after-cost profit.

This also prevents mixing them with the [GameDiscoverCo January 2026 first-release model](january-2026-modeled-paid-release-distribution.md), whose population, calendar period, estimation method and horizon are different.

## Daily Deal customers are only one segment

Valve's GDC presentation, as reported by [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/valve-says-5-836-titles-earned-over-100-000-on-steam-in-2025), featured about **1,500 games** in 2025 Daily Deals, around **69%** for the first time, with **8.2 million purchasers of a Daily Deal** (about **125%** more buyers than the prior year). Those are *Daily Deal purchasers*, not the full Steam paying population.

Valve's first-party Year In Review says expanding the Daily Deal program increased **program-wide revenue 274%**, while **median revenue per Daily Deal fell about 8%**. These figures describe a changing, selected advertising/merchandising program, not a controlled causal lift for an ordinary game or its net profit.

## Reconciliation

| Reported number | Valid denominator | Does **not** establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **76%** revenue share (2025) | Non-Valve all-game aggregate | Typical first-game royalty or developer profit |
| **5,863** games over $100k (2025) | Games of **any** launch year with 2025 Steam revenue | Success rate of 2025 launches |
| **2,395** games over $500k (2025) | Same all-age annual-revenue concept | Profitable publisher legal entities |
| **8.2m** Daily Deal purchasers | Purchasers of a selected promotion in 2025 | Total Steam annual buyers |
| **42m** peak Steam-client concurrency | Simultaneously connected clients, as reported by March 2026 | Distinct people or buying accounts |
| **100 EB** (2025) | All Steam update/install delivery | Game-specific bandwidth bill |

The Year In Review is first-party. The game-revenue and Daily Deal thresholds are **Valve-attributed presentation metrics relayed by third-party analysts and journalists**, not directly audited by Mercata. No source provides a representative cohort of verified after-cost publisher incomes or exact zero-sale legal entities.
