# Crazy City Golf: four reported day-one copies are not a wishlist conversion denominator

**Reviewed October 29, 2026.** [Machine record](crazy-city-golf-dayone-four-sales-wishlist-conversion-denominators-2026.json) · [synthetic guard](../../site/steam-short-launch-wishlist-metric-guard.mjs) · [regression tests](../../tests/steam-crazycity-short-launch-guard.test.mjs).

## First paid launch versus free demo

The [Steam storefront](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5347600/Crazy_City_Golf/) identifies *Crazy City Golf*, AppID **5347600**, as first paid-released on **October 26, 2026**. The separately listed free [demo, AppID 5382250](https://steamdb.info/app/5382250/), became available on **October 12**. The demo is a distinct free entitlement, not an earlier paid launch or proof of paid units. Steam publicly labels Nulpoyo and Era Lab developers and Nulpoyo publisher, not necessarily the legal bank payee.

In an [October 27 Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/gamemarketing/comments/1xgthsz/launched_yesterday_with_300_wishlists_sold_only_4/), a person describing a **friend's game** reported **four paid copies after more than a day** and **over 300 wishlists**. The poster also said an attached screenshot showed **0.3% wishlist conversion**. This is second-person public disclosure, not an independently verified creator ledger. The exact eligible wishlist cohort, converted-wishlister count and reporting-period boundaries for the claimed percentage were not provided for independent reconciliation.

Four *total sales* divided by a rounded *300+ wishlist stock* is **not necessarily Steam's wishlist purchase conversion**. Some buyers may never have wishlisted the game; an individual may add or remove it; post-launch and different age cohorts may be counted differently. [Valve's official reporting documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/wishlist/reporting?l=english) tracks wishlist additions, removals and purchases and records the original wishlist month cohort separately. Neither the 0.3% dashboard figure nor a general hypothetical sales-to-stock ratio can be validated from this anecdote.

## Zero reviews, temporary prices and causality

The storefront displayed **zero user reviews** at the observation, but this is **not zero actual paid orders**. The current US temporary launch offer was **40% off, $5.99 instead of $9.99**, expiring November 2. It is not a signed sale price for each of the four reported purchasers, whose countries, currencies, refund status and financial reporting period are unknown. Multiplying four by the displayed price cannot establish the legal publisher's gross, net or deposited cash.

The poster mentioned press coverage, but no counterfactual or complete purchase attribution is available to measure the press release's causal effect. This one-day snapshot is right-censored and reports **positive**, not zero, sales. It cannot be used as a representative zero-sales release, developer income estimate or evidence that a particular wishlist count guarantees an outcome.

**Still unknown:** authenticated orders, unique matched wishlist purchasers, refund-adjusted units, realized regional sale prices, legal payee income, labor-adjusted profit, and market-wide zero-sale incidence.
