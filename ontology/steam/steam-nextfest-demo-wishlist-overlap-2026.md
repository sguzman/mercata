# Steam Next Fest: demo players, wishlist overlap and unreconciled reporting periods

**Reviewed October 29, 2026.** [Machine-readable report](steam-nextfest-demo-wishlist-overlap-2026.json) · [aggregate audit](../../site/steam-demo-wishlist-overlap-guards.mjs) · [tests](../../tests/steam-demo-wishlist-overlap-guards.test.mjs).

## June voluntary survey

The [June 30, 2026 Chris Zukowski survey analysis](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/06/30/nobody-plays-demos-and-that-is-ok/) reports the following game-level proportions of Steam Next Fest **Played & Wishlisted / Total Players** among developers supplying the relevant Steamworks figures: **15.9% at the 30th percentile, 19.3% median, and 23.2% at the 70th percentile**. The exact valid respondent count for that specific question is not supplied in the consulted article. The voluntary survey cannot be treated as a representative Steam participant census, a player-weighted conversion rate or evidence of sales.

Played-and-wishlisted is an **intersection of two user sets**. It does not by itself establish which action happened first, what would have happened without the demo, or whether the user eventually paid for a full game.

## One October 2026 developer disclosure

In an [October 26, 2026 public game-developer report](https://www.reddit.com/r/gamedev/comments/1xg7yic/i_went_into_steam_next_fest_with_8k_wishlists/), the author describes **1,280 demo players**, **296 played-and-wishlisted users**, and **1,898 wishlist additions**. The descriptive player-overlap ratio is **296/1,280 = 23.125%**. The seven daily demo-player counts (172, 129, 174, 200, 198, 231, 176) sum exactly to 1,280.

The eight reported daily wishlist-addition counts (134, 342, 282, 325, 237, 275, 213, 179) total **1,987**, or **89 above** the separately stated 1,898 additions. The author says the wishlist day series includes an extra day. Different cutoffs or exports could explain the discrepancy, but the source does not reconcile it. Mercata retains both figures, explicitly flags the mismatch and does not choose a preferred replacement.

The same author approximately describes 8,000 outstanding wishlists before the festival and 10,000 after, but the rounded stocks are not a matched chronological cohort. The survey and individual case represent different festivals, populations and sampling methods, and cannot be pooled into a global rate.

**No paid game order, net remittance, profit or incremental festival effect is observed here.** The study uses only public aggregated statements; it does not import private Steamworks records, individual player identities or keys.
