# First-person Steam two-week launch: regional concentration and revenue stage

**Reviewed October 29, 2026.** [Structured figures](steam-two-week-china-concentration-and-preshare-report-2026.json) · [independent arithmetic guard](../../site/steam-regional-selfreport-stage-guards.mjs) · [tests](../../tests/steam-regional-selfreport-stage-guards.test.mjs).

## One game's regional sales disclosure

A small team developing *One Last Hand* [reported](https://www.reddit.com/r/gamedev/comments/1w49lyh/we_launched_with_11k_wishlists_and_sold_12k/) a Steam launch on August 14, 2026 and an early sales account on September 1. The six-person team reports approximately **16,500 gross units**, **12,000 net copies**, around **USD 59,000 gross revenue**, and **USD 47,000 after returns, chargebacks and taxes but before Steam's share** for the first two weeks. These are author-provided rounded figures, not independently audited financial exports or received bank funds.

The author described **around 90% of game sales as coming from China**. Because the source does not explicitly identify a mutually exclusive unit-order, customer-location, or dollar-denominated denominator for this statement, it must not be multiplied by gross revenue or used to infer regional realized prices. In particular, 90% is not a share of the entire Steam market, nor is it a report of the publisher's country of residence. The game is a multiplayer party title: its regional appeal need not generalize to other genres.

## Gross and net are stages, not a refund or profit calculation

Gross-versus-net unit differences combine distinct reporting stages and rounded author figures; they do not directly expose the number of refunded individual customers. The USD 47,000 figure is explicitly **before the platform share**, and excludes any proof of transferred cash or full development, marketing and operating costs.

The team reports about **11,000 prelaunch wishlists** and more than **4,500 launch-day copies**, with a separate account of roughly **2,000 peak demo concurrent users** and initial paid regional streamer outreach that preceded larger unpaid creator coverage. The post does not contain a user-level join between wishlists and purchases or a controlled attribution model. Selling more net copies than the number of launch wishlists is **not** a wishlist conversion rate above 100%, and the streamer sequence does not identify a causal return on advertising spend.

## Generalization barrier

This is one voluntary developer disclosure, selected for publication after a successful opening period. No historical roster of originally paid launches, comparable geographic purchase cohorts, legal payee ledger, complete adjustment waterfall, or developer costs was observed. The [tested audit](../../site/steam-regional-selfreport-stage-guards.mjs) deliberately returns **null** for actual regional unit proportions, realized regional prices, matched wishlists, verified refund rates, bank receipts, after-cost profit, and representative Steam market shares.
