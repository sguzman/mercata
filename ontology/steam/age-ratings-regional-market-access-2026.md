# Steam age ratings and regional sale availability: Germany and Indonesia

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** [Structured evidence](age-ratings-regional-market-access-2026.json). Public Steamworks policy, not a measurement of country-level demand, regulation compliance of any named game, or developer losses.

## Germany: an effective cutoff

Valve's [German ratings guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey/germany?l=english) states that from **November 15, 2024**, Steam titles without a valid age rating are hidden from customers in Germany. This includes older titles, not just newly submitted games. Ratings can be obtained directly from USK or through Valve's questionnaire-based Steam rating workflow. An honest, completed questionnaire is necessary for that workflow; it does not guarantee all content can be sold.

This affects the *eligible storefront denominator* of German games. It does not tell us whether a particular developer lost orders, how many buyers were seeking the game, or how much money was lost.

## Indonesia: mandated ratings, but no verified cutoff date here

Valve's [Indonesia ratings guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey/indonesia?l=english) describes a requirement for a valid age rating, either through the Indonesian IGRS/Komdigi route or Valve's own content-survey review. IGRS uses **3+, 7+, 13+, 15+, 18+ and Refused Classification**. Steam documentation warns that unrated games **may become unavailable to new Indonesian purchasers**, but it does **not** provide a fixed enforcement date in the current source. The German November 2024 date must not be copied to Indonesia.

Valve states already-licensed Indonesian customers keep playing even if storefront visibility is limited. **Existing ownership and current sale eligibility are separate events.**

## Why this matters for the income study

A game can be globally released but unavailable to new purchasers in a particular country at a particular time, because of rating or content rules. Neither a missing store listing nor a zero-region sale observation establishes a lack of demand. Future regional cohort comparison needs historic rating status and availability, period-matched prices and FX, tax and refunds, and permissioned checkout data.

**No German/Indonesian revenue totals, restricted product counts, buyer populations or profit changes were measured.**
