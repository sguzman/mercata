# Steam discovery, wishlists, and the meaning of a commercial signal

**Reviewed October 28, 2026.** [Machine observations](discovery-wishlists-and-visibility-2026.json) · official [Steam visibility guidance](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/visibility) and [wishlist guidance](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/wishlist).

## What the recommendation system actually says

Steam describes New Releases and All New Releases as basic full-launch discovery surfaces. Appearing in personalized recommendations, New and Trending or top sellers is **conditional**, not an exposure guarantee. Early Access launch and a later 1.0 full launch may be separate visibility events, but one game's AppID cannot become two independent first-game sellers.

Purchases and play are strong qualitative positive signals. Supported languages and descriptive tags help the store find compatible customers. **Raw page traffic, store-page conversion rates and wishlist totals are not direct general ranking factors** under Valve's published description. There are exceptions, notably Popular Upcoming for wishlists and visibility personalized to customers who have wishlisted a game. Review positivity generally does not affect featuring while Mixed or above (40%); falling below may reduce featuring. Valve states that publishers cannot buy a better organic store position.

## Wishlists are interest, not checkout receipts

A visible store page lets customers wishlist a Coming Soon title. Valve describes launch notifications, transition-out-of-Early-Access notifications and discounts at least **20%**, lasting **more than eight hours**, including the lowest-priced package. Typical per-AppID notification cooldown is two weeks, with verified-email and preference restrictions. A free demo can prompt a **single manually triggered reminder** during the first two weeks after demo release.

The owner-only reporting portal shows wishlist additions, purchases and removals, with daily updates and geographical breakdowns. The public platform does not disclose each creator's wishlist funnel. Valve explicitly rejects both a minimum wishlist requirement to receive exposure and a universal formula predicting purchases from wishlist totals.

## Research conclusion

Wishlists, page views, notification messages, impressions, paid orders, refunds and net payout are separate economic units. We have **not observed any game-specific** wishlist count, private portal analytics, genuine paid buyer, conversion rate or measured ranking lift. A future validated study requires authorized aggregates with aligned release windows and explicit denominators.
