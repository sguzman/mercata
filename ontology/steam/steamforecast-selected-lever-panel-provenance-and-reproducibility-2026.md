# SteamForecast's public 10,403-game lever panel: what its estimated outcome can establish

**Reviewed October 29, 2026.** [Source metadata and reported values](steamforecast-selected-lever-panel-provenance-and-reproducibility-2026.json) · [independent schema arithmetic](../../site/steamforecast-lever-panel-audit.mjs) · [tests](../../tests/steamforecast-lever-panel-audit.test.mjs). This audit covers the [public CC BY 4.0 dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/steamforecast/steam-launch-lever-panel-2026), its [companion lever study](https://steamforecast.app/reports/steam-launch-lever-benchmarks) and the provider's [methodology](https://steamforecast.app/methodology). **One operator and underlying derived-data pipeline, not three independent confirmations.**

## The measured population is selected by model-observable estimated revenue

The dataset documents **10,403** currently non-free, positive-price rows with *derived* week-one revenue greater than zero. The provider openly warns that this is not all Steam launches; games with too little visible ownership/review history are missing. Its own approximately **$189,600 reported median week-one estimate** therefore belongs only to this selected **modeled-positive** group, **not** the Steam launch population. This project did not independently recalculate the full CSV median.

The source quality groups are **7,652** rows from a geometric midpoint of a third-party SteamSpy owner estimate; **2,734** from review multipliers capped by SteamSpy limits; and **17** from the review heuristic alone. They sum to 10,403. To obtain a synthetic first-week estimate, the provider combines estimated *lifetime* ownership, current price and a presumed lifetime-to-week-one fraction of **0.40 / 0.30 / 0.20 / 0.12** by size tier. None is a verified first-week paid transaction, historical local price, refund-adjusted amount or settled publisher remittance. Its 6,422-row held-out validation comes from another 32,106-row modeling corpus and refers to **derived revenue ground truth**, not independently paid sales.

## Two reproducibility gaps in the published panel

**Logarithm scale.** The dataset card labels `y_log_week1` a base-10 logarithm of `y_week1_cents`. But three publicly displayed pairs, including `353376223 → 19.683043837714514`, match the *natural logarithm* of cents to a small numerical rounding tolerance; the base-10 result for that example is approximately **8.548**, not 19.683. The included [pure check](../../site/steamforecast-lever-panel-audit.mjs) reproduces that mismatch. This finding is **based on three displayed rows**, not a full CSV computation, and may indicate a mislabeled transformation rather than erroneous underlying estimates.

**Localization and covariates.** The public lever report describes a treatment of **5 or more languages**, while the panel card describes `T_languages_high` as **10 or more languages**. The report says its causal model adjusts for follower count and tag profile, but the public 19-column file does not contain those variables; the dataset card confirms they are unpopulated/excluded. These two ambiguities block independent reproduction of the exact reported treatment and adjustment set from this public file. It is possible the provider used internal data not included in the public panel; that remains **unverified**, not categorically denied.

## What the causal multipliers do *not* show

The [provider's July 2026 report](https://steamforecast.app/reports/steam-launch-lever-benchmarks) gives median model-effect multipliers of **×1.26** co-op, **×1.09** demo and **×1.02** localization. They may be worth investigating as **conditional model comparisons**. They do **not** establish that adding each feature would causally increase real Steam publisher revenue by those ratios. Selection into treatments can reflect studio capacity and prior demand; the outcome itself is an estimated lifetime-owner and current-price projection to launch week; model adjustment features are only partially published; and implementation/labor costs are missing. A held-out fit against another *derived* outcome is not a randomized real-transaction experiment.

The [synthetic integrity guard](../../site/steamforecast-lever-panel-audit.mjs) checks source-quality counts, inconsistent language thresholds and sample log-scale calculations, never accepts merchant identifiers, and always returns null for merchant-verified cash, all-release median or identified treatment effects.

**Still unknown:** complete initial paid-release denominator, exact no-sale share, first-year verified gross, legally retained publisher proceeds, after-cost profit, and actual incremental sales caused by any of the three features. No private forecast accounts, raw full panel file, merchant ledger or customer identifiers were accessed.
