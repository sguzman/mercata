# RollScape, June 2024: creator attention, a corrected zero-sales headline and country checkout restrictions

**Reviewed October 30, 2026.** [Structured report](steam-rollscape-vietnam-creator-regional-access-correction-2024.json) · [regression tests](../../tests/steam-rollscape-video-country-access-correction.test.mjs). This is a selected public launch case, not a controlled conversion experiment or authenticated sales report.

## Three independently attributable public source roles

The [official Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2904290/RollScape/) identifies *RollScape* (AppID 2904290), developer/publisher label **Leonardo the Vinchy**, and a **May 22, 2024** release date. Valve's public page does not verify the historic payment partner or order records.

In a [June 4 developer-authored discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/gamedev/comments/1d7szns/a_vietnamese_youtuber_played_my_game_with_800k/), the author reported a Vietnamese YouTube creator with approximately **800,000 subscribers** and a game video with approximately **100,000 views**. The author described a large rise in Steam direct navigation but initially interpreted the campaign as yielding no new sales. These are voluntary self-reported observations; creator-view and visit figures were not independently audited, and no matched referral-to-purchase data was shared.

**Crucial correction:** The same author subsequently commented that there had been **one Vietnam-attributed sale** in the relevant day, and speculated that the buyer might have been the creator. This corrects the original **“zero sales”** wording; the purchaser and channel are **unknown**. Do not repeat the headline as an established zero-sale observation, equate a seller-reported country with physical buyer residence, or claim that the video caused the one sale.

The [May 31, 2024 VnExpress report](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/business/companies/gaming-platform-steam-blocked-in-vietnam-for-refusal-to-cooperate-with-authorities-4752391.html) quotes a Vietnamese communications regulator about Steam access restrictions and reports that some people could not browse the Store or pay from Vietnam since early May. It is evidence of a **contemporaneous country-level checkout-access shock**, not proof that every viewer, ISP or individual buyer was unable to purchase. The store-access difficulties predated the June 4 developer report.

## Why the case changes interpretation

The denominator of creator-video views is not the population of distinct potential paid Steam purchasers. Subscriptions are different from views; views are different from unique store visits; visits are different from geographically purchasable checkout opportunities; and successful paid orders require merchant proof. Country access restrictions can confound promotion conversion, but their exact causal role is **not identified** by this case. The author's direct-navigation observation cannot produce a defensible conversion percentage or no-uplift conclusion without tagged sessions, matching periods, actual purchase-country access, attributed sales and a counterfactual.

This example belongs alongside Mercata's [synthetic game-country purchase-availability bounds](permissioned-ledger-coverage-and-zero-identification-2026.md) and [external acquisition uncertainty methodology](discovery-wishlists-and-visibility-2026.md). It neither establishes a whole-game zero, nor measures publisher gross revenue, transferred remittances, refund-adjusted cash or labor profit. It must not be added as a second independent sales observation if the original developer headline and correction describe the same title and event.

No individual viewer locations, partner transaction records, customer identities, account access, video assets or private data were imported.
