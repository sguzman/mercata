# Steamworks onboarding, release and payment rules
**First source-backed research checkpoint: October 28, 2026.** [Machine-readable record](entry-review-and-reporting-2026.json) · [Public evidence and bounded claims](../../specimens/steam.json).

## Enrollment and entry costs

The official [Steam Direct enrollment page](https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect), [onboarding guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/onboarding), and [fee document](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee) describe signed distribution paperwork, identity, name-matched bank details, tax declarations and a **USD 100 per new-app fee**. The fee cannot be paid from Steam Wallet, is not refundable, but may be **recouped separately** after the app reaches **USD 1,000 adjusted gross revenue** on qualifying Steam sales/in-app purchases. This threshold is **not** break-even profit or a guaranteed return. Local consumption taxes and other costs are not comprehensively measured.

## Contradictory official wait rule

Steam Direct's [public landing page](https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect) specifies **30 days** from fee payment to earliest release for initial titles. Its separate [onboarding document](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/onboarding) specifies **21 days**. Both sources were accessible on this review date, so Mercata records **two incompatible observations** rather than choosing one or claiming an unverified policy-change date. A prudent hypothetical launch plan can reserve the longer period, but the operative requirement needs primary clarification. The separate [Coming Soon page guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/coming_soon) requires public availability for at least 14 days; it is not safe to simply add both waiting intervals into one mandatory total.

## Reviews and release control

The [release guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/releasing) requires separate store-presence and game-build checklists, store-review submission before build review, and explicit publish/release controls after approval. The [review guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/Review_Process) indicates typical **3–5 business-day** reviews and recommends at least **seven business days** of lead time for contingencies. Supported OS builds must launch and promised features must exist. Review approval alone does not establish release or sales.

## Payouts and report semantics

The [reporting guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/finance/payments_salesreporting) gives a **USD 100** payout minimum and generally describes payment at the end of the month after sales, using **ACH in the U.S.** and **USD SWIFT abroad**. The [reporting FAQ](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/finance/payments_salesreporting/faq) explains that near-real-time sales figures precede deductions, whereas monthly reports account for taxes, returns and chargebacks before applying the agreement's share. A displayed gross figure is not an actual received merchant payout.

The [tax FAQ](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/finance/taxfaq) distinguishes VAT included in listed prices in collecting jurisdictions from applicable U.S./Canadian sales taxes added at checkout. U.S.-source royalty withholding may vary between **0% and 30%** by tax and treaty status. No specific publisher's tax outcome or full revenue-share contract tiers have been verified.

## Remaining limits

- Exact operative waiting period and revenue-share tier schedule not established.
- Country-specific bank availability and exchange costs not yet studied comprehensively.
- No authenticated merchant reports, real buyer identities, seller net cash receipts, release-cohort distribution or after-labor profit observations.
- Zero-selling, delisted and low-revenue paid-game publishers remain unmeasured.

All claims are linked to nine public documents in the Steam dossier. Research did not create accounts, buy games, run experiments or access partner-only information.
