# YouTube opportunity and risk matrix: rules versus unknown outcomes

**Checked:** 2026-10-25. This matrix extends the already established [opportunity comparison framework](opportunity-framework.md), [official content-policy impact matrix](content-policy-risk.json), [product-specific eligibility matrix](eligibility-matrix.json), [revenue-stream settlement crosswalk](revenue-stack-accounting.md), and [monthly cash-flow protocol](monthly-creator-cash-flow-protocol.md). It is a research decision protocol, not a model claiming the probability of success for any creator.

| Risk axis | Grounding type | Verifiable gate or missing evidence |
|---|---|---|
| YPP access and channel review | conditional entitlement | Approval and modules vary by channel, region, product and dated requirements |
| Child-directed content feature restrictions | documented restriction | Correct audience designation may disable memberships, comments and certain advertising or shopping features |
| Third-party rights and Content ID allocations | documented restriction | Rights owners may claim, block or monetize qualifying reused media |
| Advertiser-suitable video context | documented restriction | Individual-video reviews can limit ad suitability |
| Shopping affiliate commissions, returns and delays | documented settlement | Eligible native Shopping affiliate commissions settle via AdSense after returns window |
| Payment thresholds, holds and transfer delays | documented settlement | Threshold and account holds can postpone issuance; issued is not deposited |
| Sponsor fee and paid-content disclosure | contract specific | Brand agreements need actual written terms, disclosure and fulfillment accounting |
| Unobserved research and production labor | measurement gap | Surveys and format comparisons do not reveal typical all-creator hourly returns |
| Public views versus unique people and buyers | measurement gap | View events, Shorts engaged views, ad impressions and unique viewers have distinct definitions |
| Creator income dispersion and zero-earner coverage | measurement gap | Selected creator surveys can exclude non-earning channels, abandoned work and inactive creators |

## Why these rows must remain separate

**Documented restrictions** come from [YouTube's Partner Program](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en), [Made for Kids feature rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9632097?hl=en), [Content ID documentation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797370?hl=en), [advertiser guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278?hl=en), and [paid promotion rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=en). They are conditions that **can** apply to a real video or account; they are not proof that an unnamed creator violates a rule.

**Settlement restrictions** reflect published [AdSense monthly payment timing](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7164703?hl=en) and [native Shopping affiliate payments](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en). Finalized platform revenue, issued transfers and cleared bank receipts must be reconciled separately. Fee reversals and refunds should not become two independent profit deductions.

**Measurement gaps** arise because [YouTube Analytics metrics](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/metrics) describe events and selected creator surveys such as [NeoReach's 2025 earnings study](https://neoreach.com/reports/creator-earnings-report-2025/) are not a census of all earners and non-earners. Production surveys such as the registered creator-work evidence cannot establish a representative after-cost hourly wage for YouTube.

## How the matrix is used

For each candidate content category, define country, language, publishing format, intended audience and original/licensed assets. Check applicable platform gates without guessing account approval. Enter **actual** production minutes/hours, cash expenses, permissions and settled receipts once they exist; until then, the respective inputs remain unknown. Keep selected surveys, public view events and particular channels in their original observation frames. If there are no comparable first-party results, the decision stays *insufficient evidence*, not a made-up high/medium/low success score.

All ten rows explicitly retain **null probabilities, financial losses and personal profit outcomes**. A hypothetical risk possibility is not a realized event. Distinct policies may apply simultaneously; categories are not summed as estimated monetary risks.
