The [Omdia video supply and viewing-time study](video-attention-culture-2026.md) reports that the top **1% of videos** received **91% of studied watch time** in a broadly global frame. This is neither the top creator earnings share nor a directly measured statistic for any one topic, market, or channel cohort.

A legitimate niche comparison requires a defined, same-window topic cohort, format, country, language, non-selected sampling mechanism, complete eligible video denominator including zero counts, and a consistent view or watch-minute metric. Public video views cannot be relabeled as owner-only watch time. Relevance-ranked search results alone are not a census.

The attached executable calculator gives video-level top-fraction share, HHI, and Gini using **explicitly supplied** matched records. Its fixture is synthetic and validates arithmetic; it is not a study of real creator performance. Profit, unique viewers, and all niche-level concentration results remain **unmeasured**.
