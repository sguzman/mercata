These reports **do not constitute a full niche upload census**. Video titles containing a phrase and the platform's broadly *related to* topic classification have different inclusion rules. Their populations may overlap, so even same-year, global video counts cannot be added as independent categories. The September cutoff for cake decorating, six-month cutoff for morning routines and full-year windows for other observations are distinct.

Each bound concerns uploads, not distinct channels, unique viewers, minutes watched, a channel's publication cadence or revenue. Creator supply requires deduplicated active channel counts and a fixed time window; channel income requires consented earnings and cost records including zero earners. No niche ranking or fabricated normalized annual volume is provided.
