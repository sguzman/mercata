# YouTube legal operator and economic-role boundaries

**Reviewed October 24, 2026.** [Machine taxonomy](operator-market-roles.json). This is a contract-and-product interpretation, not a census of market participants or a universal legal opinion.

## Legal service provider

The [YouTube general Terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) identify **Google LLC** as the provider of the covered service. The [Paid Service Terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms_paidservice), effective February 11, 2026, separately name Google LLC as provider of covered paid services. Paid products include subscriptions, rentals, purchases and channel memberships, subject to additional terms. This establishes the **named contractual service provider** at the cited time, not all historic corporate ownership, advertiser invoicing entities, tax payees or contracts under other jurisdictions.

## Who provides, who pays and who receives

| Economic route | Supplier | Payer | Recipient or beneficiary | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public video and livestream viewing | Creator provides content; YouTube hosts/distributes | Viewer may pay **nothing** | Viewers consume media; creator may or may not earn | Viewing is not itself a purchase |
| Ads on eligible content | YouTube/platform advertising inventory | Advertiser | Platform and conditionally eligible creator through revenue sharing | Advertiser and viewer are different populations |
| Paid video/services | YouTube's paid-services offering and applicable contributors | Paying viewer | Service provider, with creator compensation as applicable to product | Subscriptions/orders are not the same as free views |
| Brand sponsorship | Brand and creator contract directly | Sponsor | Creator, possibly via external payment | A placement may not be processed by YouTube |
| Copyright/Content ID | Eligible rights holder owns copyrighted material | Not automatically a buyer | Claimant may monetize, block or track based on rights/settings | Claim is not a payment or necessarily a strike |
| Channel operations | Owner and delegated managers/editors | Not necessarily any | Team operational workflow | Delegated permissions are not legal ownership |

Sources: [YPP and partner earnings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en), [Content ID](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797370?hl=en), [channel permissions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9481328?hl=en), and [branded-content policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/17596007?hl=en).

## Different products mean different economic units

Public Watch content, Shorts, Live, Music, podcasts and community posts represent attention and publishing surfaces. Monetized advertising slots are a different product purchased by advertisers. Premium subscriptions, memberships and rentals/purchases are monetary transactions by users who opt in. Merchandising and affiliate links may be settled by third-party commerce operators outside YouTube's own payment ledger. Rights management and creator sponsorship are additional legal/economic arrangements, not automatically creator sales in a single marketplace.

A creator may operate multiple channels; a channel may have several delegates; viewers can watch signed out and subscribe to many channels; payment accounts and legal creator businesses are distinct. Consequently **marketplace operator, publisher, rights owner, viewer, ad buyer, subscriber and payee must never share an undocumented denominator**.
