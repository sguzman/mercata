# Country YouTube advertiser-planner reach: two distinct 2025 change windows

**Reviewed October 25, 2026.** [Machine four-country change panel](country-planner-change-windows-2026.json) is derived from the existing [DataReportal country evidence](country-ad-reach-comparisons.json) and a fresh review of the four public Digital 2026 reports. The displayed periods refer to **late 2025**, even though the report edition is called Digital 2026.

| Country | Oct 2025 potential ad reach | End 2024 → late 2025 (publisher-reported) | July → October 2025 (publisher-reported) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| [India](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-india) | 500 million | +9m (+1.8%) | -9m (-1.8%) |
| [Indonesia](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-indonesia) | 151 million | +8m (+5.6%) | +6m (+4.1%) |
| [Japan](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-japan) | 78.5 million | -0.2m (-0.3%) | +3.1m (+4.1%) |
| [Philippines](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-philippines) | 59.6 million | +1.9m (+3.3%) | +1.8m (+3.1%) |

**Why separate windows matter:** India has a *positive* longer-window planner change but a *negative* July–October change. Japan has a slightly negative longer-window change and a positive July–October change. Neither difference is a contradiction; the periods differ, and planner estimates can be revised. No intermediate trend points are manufactured by subtracting the two.

## What this comparison cannot measure

DataReportal explicitly distinguishes **Google advertising reach** from actual YouTube **monthly active users**. The reported reach counts are estimates of ad-addressable audiences and can shift due to platform estimation or eligibility revisions. They are not verified unique humans, deduplicated logged-in accounts, realized video views or creators.

Because the sources describe country reach and **not the same person's attendance**, neither change series provides a valid count of people starting/stopping YouTube, a language-specific demographic shift, a first-time creator trend, or an amount of creator revenue. Treat the four countries as a **selected source-based panel**, not an exhaustive worldwide frame or a country's verified viewer growth.
