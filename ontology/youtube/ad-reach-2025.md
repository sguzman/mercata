# Estimated YouTube advertising reach: Mexico, United States and Brazil

The following are **advertising reach estimates**, not platform monthly active users. DataReportal's Digital 2026 country reports use Google's ad-planning outputs from **late 2025**.

| Country | Estimated advertising reach | Adult ad audience: female | Adult ad audience: male |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| [Mexico](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mexico) | 85.0 million | 51.5% | 48.5% |
| [United States](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-united-states-of-america) | 254.0 million | 50.9% | 49.1% |
| [Brazil](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-brazil) | 150.0 million | 51.9% | 48.1% |

The reach population is not necessarily a distinct count of people who logged in or watched something within a given month. The female/male percentages are reported for the **adult advertising audience**, not all YouTube viewers and not the population of each country. These figures must not be confused with Pew's self-reported U.S. ever-use prevalence rates. Do not add country estimates into a global count without checking coverage, duplication and the meaning of the measure.
