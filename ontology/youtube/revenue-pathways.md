# YouTube revenue pathways

**Reviewed 2026-10-23.** The [machine pathways](revenue-pathways.json) describe 16 distinct ways money might flow to a creator. They are not 16 equally accessible features. Some are official YouTube payments; others are independent sales or contracts for which YouTube provides discovery but does not arrange or guarantee payment.

## Two eligibility thresholds, several extra gates

The published YPP eligibility path for earlier access to commerce/fan-funding features is different from the higher path for advertising and Premium. YPP acceptance and product-specific approval follow the number thresholds. Channel memberships require adult creators, region access and the Commerce module; other products have additional restrictions. Always check the latest [feature requirements](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en).

The format and contract matter. Ads on regular watch pages, advertising allocations for Shorts, and native fan payments do **not** have an interchangeable revenue basis. YouTube Premium is distinct from ads. Gifts use Jewels and Rubies under the Virtual Items rules, not the standard Commerce module percentage. Shopping with one's own products and affiliate tagging for participating merchants are separate businesses.

## Shopping and brand partnerships

The [Shopping affiliate policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en) lists Mexico, alongside other eligible territories; the [July 2026 TeamYouTube announcement](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/449002752?hl=es-419) describes a Mexico/Argentina launch with Mercado Libre product tagging. This is available only for qualifying channels, with participant merchants, returns and country restrictions. An affiliate commission is not the same as gross merchandise value. Own-product Shopping requires an eligible connected merchant store ([documentation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12258186?hl=en)).

[YouTube Creator Partnerships](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9385307?co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DMX&hl=en) helps eligible channels be discovered by sponsors. Brand discovery is not a guaranteed paid sponsorship or a posted price list. The Mexico help page describes advertiser inquiries by email or social contacts for qualifying creators; the exact Studio interface can differ elsewhere.

YouTube's [September 2026 announcement](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-creator-monetization-shopping/) outlines changes and planned experiments. A future announcement cannot be counted as a currently usable revenue mechanism without deployment evidence.

## Payout accounting

A useful opportunity study must follow each stream from the qualifying viewer action or sale through platform and merchant deductions, refunds, taxes, payout thresholds and finally the creator's labor and cash costs. The [payment gates](payment-gates.md) document the official currency-specific thresholds.

No route in this inventory has a representative mean or median earning estimate. The separate [small-channel study](small-channel-income.md) and [Patreon study](creator-income-evidence.md) have different sample populations and revenue channels; they must not be combined to invent a typical payout.

## Unanswered research

Each route still needs local entry conditions, age and channel restrictions, accepted formats, merchant or buyer access, upfront cash and time costs, platform charges, withdrawal restrictions, settlement timing, churn, refunds, gross-to-net examples, and credible cohort income distributions. Null means not yet measured; it does not mean free or zero revenue.
