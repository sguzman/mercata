# YouTube comparison-set boundaries: video competitors are not interchangeable

**Observed:** October 23, 2026. **Scope:** YouTube's research plan only. This document does **not** admit Twitch, TikTok or Vimeo as Mercata specimens, create separate research queues, or claim earnings rankings. [Machine comparison scope](comparison-set.json).

## Why these three candidates

A competitor is in scope when a creator could plausibly use it to distribute video or monetize an audience, *not* because its subscriber total, video views or income statistics are numerically interchangeable with YouTube's.

| Adjacent service | Most relevant YouTube research surface | Officially documented earning mechanism | Key incompatibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Twitch streaming](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/streamer-benefits) | YouTube Live, memberships, Supers and community support | Streamer subscriptions and Bits after monetization onboarding; ad access has further Affiliate/Partner tiers | Livestream support, subscriptions and ads use different units; Twitch monetization setup is not the same as qualification for cash payouts |
| [TikTok Creator Rewards](https://support.tiktok.com/es/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/creator-rewards-program) | YouTube Shorts discovery and qualified short-video views | Rewards on qualifying original videos at least one minute long; country, age, account and view conditions | TikTok's eligible For You views cannot be converted into YouTube's public first-frame play starts or Shorts Creator Pool shares |
| [Vimeo OTT](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/12427212660369-Getting-paid-with-Vimeo-OTT) | Products, paid video distribution and external transactions | Customer purchases of paid video/OTT products, with documented monthly sales payouts | A direct buyer, subscription or transaction is not a public video viewer, ad impression or Shorts engaged view |

Twitch's official help distinguishes **all streamers**, **streamers with monetization setup completed**, **Affiliates** and **Partners**; it explains that ordinary creators may receive virtual on-platform support while only further-qualified participants receive cash payouts. This is a 2026 product structure, not a recommendation to join Twitch.

TikTok's Creator Rewards Help includes country availability, personal-account requirements, minimum audience thresholds and video quality/length and qualified-view definitions. It is an eligibility description, not an unconditional creator revenue per view or a representative success probability.

Vimeo OTT's official payment guide describes payouts for paid content sales on a monthly cycle. This is a storefront/service comparison, not a direct organic video-discovery comparison. Sales conversion, refunds, plan costs, buyer support and customer acquisition all require independent verification.

## Required normalized comparison dimensions

Before claiming one service is more accessible or profitable for a defined person or cohort, independently document:

1. **Video format and audience surface** — live, public long-form, short feed or paid OTT content.
2. **Creator entry requirements** — age, residence, product access, good standing, account review and separately required payment onboarding.
3. **Money flow and units** — advertising inventory, qualified video engagement, viewer payments, tips, subscriptions and store receipts, gross vs net.
4. **Country and time period** — creator residence, viewer market, payment country and account currency are different axes. Do not compare outdated policy snapshots to current terms.
5. **Rights and audience ownership** — hosted content, portability, off-platform offers and whether access to a customer list actually transfers.
6. **Production costs and hours** — labor, third-party fees, licensing, equipment already owned, failures and support.
7. **Representative outcomes including zero** — nonmonetized, rejected, abandoned and inactive creators must be represented for a valid newcomer income comparison.

## Exclusions and unresolved fields

No normalized earnings median, zero-earner rate, hourly profit distribution, creator population count, cross-platform audience overlap, or causal best strategy has been measured. The [newcomer-income sampling protocol](newcomer-income-cohort.md) defines the necessary empirical standard. The [YouTube geographic scope](geographic-scope.md) guards against comparing regional figures with different denominators.

These candidates remain only a **comparison methodology within the YouTube dossier**. Any separate specimen research requires explicit authorization and independent admission.
