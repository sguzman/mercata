# Meme, reaction, remix, sketch and language-specific short humor formats

**Research date: October 25, 2026.** [Machine typology](meme-format-typology-2026.json) · [bounded audit](../../site/meme-format-audit.mjs).

Six overlapping public content forms are distinguished: **Shorts Remix**, **tier lists**, **animated audio memes**, **reaction commentary**, **sketch/parody**, and **language-specific humor**. These are not six disjoint communities, six sets of unique creators or six separate audiences.

[YouTube's 2025 tier-list editorial](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/tier-list/) reports **over one billion global view events** in the first six months of 2025 among videos with *tier list* in the title. That is a topic-crossing template and does not prove that all such videos are comedy, or identify one billion unique people.

[Official Shorts Remix guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/18736733?hl=en) documents Green Screen, Cut and Collab video editing. [TeamYouTube's May 2026 announcement](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/433730507?hl=en&msgid=451265493) describes original attribution links and source-creator opt-out/removal controls. These editing remixes are not the same feature as Studio's named **Collaborations** credits and do not authorize assuming shared ad revenue or a right to retain remixes after a source removes permission.

YouTube's [2025 editorial cultural examples](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/end-of-year-summary-2025/) and [animation-meme coverage](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/trending-now-march-2025/) motivate reaction, parody, animation and fan remix distinctions but do not measure incidence. A local idiom or language-specific joke describes content; it does not establish a viewer's nationality, ethnicity, belief or identity.

**Unmeasured:** active creators in each format, unique viewers, shared cross-format fans, meme origination, commercial conversion and typical profits. Public content links, rights and non-identifying samples are required for any follow-up.
