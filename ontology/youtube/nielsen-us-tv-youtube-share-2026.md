# Nielsen 2026 U.S. TV time: YouTube is not an all-device audience count

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-25. Primary monthly releases: [May 2026](https://www.nielsen.com/news-center/2026/streaming-embarks-on-annual-summer-ascent-in-nielsens-may-2026-gauge-reports/), [July 2026](https://www.nielsen.com/news-center/2026/tv-usage-kicks-usual-summer-slowdown-fueled-by-world-cup-and-streaming-in-nielsens-july-gauge-reports/), and [August 2026](https://www.nielsen.com/news-center/2026/tv-viewing-cools-off-in-nielsens-august-gauge-reports-as-sports-take-a-breather-and-school-resumes/), published respectively July 28, September 10 and October 22.

Nielsen’s *The Gauge* and *Media Distributor Gauge* describe television screen viewing for **U.S. persons aged 2+**, using a TV watch-time denominator rather than a count of phones, personal accounts, global internet users or YouTube creators.

| Observation | YouTube % of total US TV watch time | All streaming % of US TV watch time |
|---|---:|---:|
| May 2026 | 13.8% | 48.6% |
| July 2026 | 14.2% | 49.0% |
| August 2026 | 15.8% | 49.6% |

Nielsen’s reported YouTube U.S. TV share therefore increased **1.6 percentage points** from July to August. It is *not* a 1.6% relative percentage increase; nor is it an identified individual viewer or creator payment effect. May and July are not adjacent months, and missing June values are deliberately **not interpolated**.

YouTube is counted *within* the streaming category, so adding its 15.8% August figure to the all-streaming 49.6% would double-count some television time. All three monthly YouTube measurements share the same broad TV-device denominator, but live event cycles and total television usage can change dramatically between months.

## Missing device comparison

The **U.S. mobile YouTube watch-time share remains unknown here**. These public Nielsen observations do not provide a matched phone-versus-TV sample, the number of unique viewers, persons switching between screens, average minutes watched per person, global viewing shares, creator ads receipts or mobile subscriptions. The recorded U.S. television percentages cannot be combined with GWI past-month YouTube participation surveys or YouTube ad reach figures as if they were one audience denominator.

To compare mobile against television, obtain the *same market, age universe, month, creator format and watch-time unit* from a compatible measurement instrument, then document overlap and what each device panel omits. Otherwise label the mobile column unknown rather than inventing a ratio.
