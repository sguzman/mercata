# Fashion, beauty and lifestyle video genres without gender assumptions

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-25. Original YouTube Culture & Trends materials: [GRWM format](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/grwm-2023-trends/), [prom and GRWM](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/prom-youtube/), [fashion design and beginner sewing](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/fashion-design-creators-youtube/), plus [Halloween makeup](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/halloween-makeup/) for creative transformations.

## Content-based categories, not the identity of viewers

Mercata now codes six overlapping content families: **GRWM conversational routines; makeup artistry and tutorials; sewing, upcycling and fashion production; occasion styling; historical fashion interpretation; and product evaluation/hauls**. A person's viewing of any category is not proof they are female, male, a teenager, a particular ethnicity or a paying beauty customer. Different communities may occupy multiple families, and a GRWM may be both a makeup tutorial and a personal essay.

## Three source-defined public view-event observations

| Topic definition | Geographic and calendar window | Publisher's figure |
|---|---|---:|
| Videos with GRWM title variants | Global, January 1–June 5, **2023** | More than **6 billion video views** |
| Videos with both prom and GRWM in title | Global, calendar **2024** | More than **25 million video views** |
| Videos related to sewing machines and with beginner in the title | Global, calendar **2025** | More than **100 million video views** |

**None of these three numbers establishes a year-over-year growth series.** They have different inclusion terms, date windows and cultural subjects; their audiences may overlap. Even where a subset conceptually resembles another, it was measured in a different year and no unique person-level crosswalk was published. These are playback events, **not a census of creators or potential buyers**.

## Reproducible next measurement

For a true segment comparison, pre-register content codes, a primary category plus explicitly overlapping secondaries, language, video format, release month and fixed-duration public view windows. Require a mutually compatible population before calculating growth. Owner-authorized viewer retention, YouTube product conversions, advertising earnings or commissions are separate sources and **remain unknown**. No viewer demographic identities were inferred.

The study identifies concrete existing fashion- and lifestyle-video styles, yet neither YouTube's published editorials nor these view-event totals establishes a typical creator's pay, conversion rate or skill/production costs.

## Additional beauty-adjacent subcultures: three incompatible historical view frames

These published figures strengthen **content taxonomy evidence**, but cannot be used as a unique audience census or longitudinal growth estimate:

| Topic and primary source | Observation period | Published, bounded activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Nail art](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/2023-nail-art-day/) | Global, calendar **2022** | More than **5 billion views** of videos *related to nail art*, across beauty, art and learning |
| [Drag community](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/drag-yoodle/) | Global, calendar **2023** | More than **2 billion views** of videos related to drag queens and drag kings (beauty, performance and other genres) |
| [Halloween makeup](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/halloween-makeup/) | Global, **Jan 1–Oct 5, 2025** | More than **1 billion views** of videos with both **halloween** and **makeup** in their titles |

Nail art, drag performance and seasonal makeup can **overlap** but use different topical filters and observation years. One is a publisher-defined nail-art topic, another a broad performance community, the last an exact two-keyword title filter. These historical video view events cannot be summed into unique humans, used as an all-beauty view series, or divided by unmatched channel-index counts to infer a scarce profitable niche. The drag topic includes activity beyond makeup and is not a demographic classification of any viewer.

Neither these numbers nor the earlier GRWM and sewing observations document **actual unique viewers, active producers, gender composition, full-year 2025 nail or makeup views, consumer payments, or typical creator cash profit**. A reproducible next study would align inclusion rules, observation window, video format, and owner-authorized audience/sales data where available.
