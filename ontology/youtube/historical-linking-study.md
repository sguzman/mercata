# Historical high-subscriber YouTube linking: an observed behavior, not income

**Source publication:** June 29, 2023. **Underlying channel snapshot:** late 2019. [Machine-readable measurements](historical-linking-study.json).

The open-access journal article [Making a Living in the Creator Economy: A Large-Scale Study of Linking on YouTube](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/20563051231180628), by Rieder and colleagues, examines how highly subscribed channels linked to marketplaces, crowdfunding and other websites through YouTube video descriptions.

## Study population and units

The researchers drew on a prior crawl of **more than 36 million channels**, then selected **153,770 channels with at least 100,000 subscribers** for the linking analysis. Their video dataset covered **136,909,228 video records** published from January 2007 to December 1, 2019. They counted **93,186,712 descriptions containing links** and approximately **571 million extracted URLs**, but those are descriptions and link events, not unique creators or purchases.

The article explicitly notes that the high-subscriber focus **does not represent all YouTube videos**. These figures are historical; they do not establish the number of channels at any subscriber tier in October 2026.

## Reported use of promotion links

| Category, across the sampled high-subscriber channels | No such URLs | URLs in fewer than 20% of videos | URLs in at least 80% of videos |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marketplace links | 53,256 (34.6%) | 115,904 (75.37%) | 10,221 (6.6%) |
| Crowdfunding links | 127,805 (83.1%) | 144,521 (94%) | 2,675 (1.74%) |

The rows' columns are **nested frequency groups**, not a mutually exclusive partition to sum to 100%. A channel with no qualifying links also belongs to the fewer-than-20% group. The underlying measures are descriptive counts and rounded shares from the original paper.

These URLs can indicate promotional and social-capital activities, but **do not disclose a paid purchase, sponsor contract, crowdfunding revenue, profit, conversion rate or even actual monetization**. Off-platform earnings can occur with no description link. Descriptions can also be edited after upload, so historical publication dates are not equivalent to original-link insertion dates.

## The revenue table in the source is modeled, not measured payouts

The authors also provide a **2019 model** estimating advertising revenue by subscriber tier from view shares and a public aggregate revenue disclosure. That worksheet does **not** use observed creator-level bank payments. Mercata does not convert it into a 2026 RPM forecast, median creator salary or probability of successfully joining YPP. The machine record retains the fact that the model exists while leaving all creator-income targets as `null`.

## Valid research use

This study establishes that linking practices differed among already successful channels in a defined historical sample. It is useful for designing comparative off-platform strategy samples, not for estimating the behavior, earnings or population size of **new entrants** in 2026.
