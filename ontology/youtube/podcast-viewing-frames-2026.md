# Podcast video and background use: platform hours versus people and survey formats

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-25. Primary sources: YouTube [February 2025 podcast audience announcement](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/1-billion-monthly-podcast-users/), [December 2025 living-room review](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/podcasts-living-room-in-2025/), [May 2026 Premium feature update](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/new-youtube-premium-features-podcast-fans/) and [Edison Research's June 2026 podcast consumer publication](https://www.edisonresearch.com/the-podcast-consumer-2026/).

| Source-defined measure | Observation | Population or device restriction |
|---|---:|---|
| YouTube global monthly podcast viewers, January 2025 | **More than 1 billion** | Active platform podcast viewers, not U.S. weekly consumers or paying creators |
| YouTube October 2024 living-room podcast time | **About 400 million hours** | Television/living-room device watch time, not unique people |
| YouTube October 2025 living-room podcast time | **More than 700 million hours** | Same broad device category and month of year as 2024, different measured period |
| YouTube Premium April 2026 podcast viewing | **More than 800 million hours** | Premium users on supported devices, **not solely living-room devices** |
| Edison Q3 2025 weekly podcast consumption | **82% watched video; 78% listened to audio-only** | U.S. weekly podcast consumers across platforms; respondents could do both |

The October 2025 YouTube living-room headline reflects substantially more watch hours than the year-ago headline, but since one is a lower bound and the other rounded, no *exact growth rate* is published here. The April 2026 **Premium** hours belong to a different calendar month and population (paying product rather than TV devices). **Do not subtract or add these hours** to create a platform total.

The Edison weekly U.S. survey discusses video and audio-only *behavior*, not an either/or classification: people can both watch video podcasts **and** listen to audio-only episodes. 82% plus 78% does not equal a meaningful 160% mutually exclusive audience. Edison’s U.S. weekly survey is neither the same geography nor the same population frame as YouTube’s global monthly active viewers.

YouTube [podcast charts](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-charts-top-us-podcasts/) order selected U.S. podcast shows based on watch time; rankings are not a direct platform-wide census, and a top show’s engagement is not evidence of a typical creator hourly profit.

## What remains unknown

No source above supplies individual-person podcast watch minutes, background-play versus visually attentive sessions, paid creator allocations, median creator revenue, a global podcast listener census, per-format labor costs or mobile-versus-TV overlap. A real research panel would require matching product definitions, country, age base, viewing device, period, and duplicated-person rules, then separating owned-channel analytics from independent survey response weights.
