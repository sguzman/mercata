# YouTube channel ownership, delegation, transfer and continuity

**Evidence review: October 23, 2026.** [Structured ownership and export model](channel-ownership-continuity.json). These are general official rules; Mercata has not accessed, transferred or recovered anyone's account.

A YouTube channel can be a business-facing asset without the creator necessarily having unilateral rights to sell subscribers, assign advertising contracts, preserve monetization after relocation, or move it freely. **Channel role delegation, Brand Account primary ownership and moving a channel into another Brand Account are separate operations.**

## Three different levels of access

| Operation | Administrative surface | Effect and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delegate routine work | [YouTube Studio channel permissions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9481328?hl=en) | Assign owner, manager, editor or viewer roles; owners here **cannot transfer primary ownership** |
| Change controlling primary owner | [Google Brand Account owner permissions](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/7311601?hl=en) | Changes primary owner after documented tenure conditions |
| Move channel to another Brand Account | [YouTube account transfer settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3056283?hl=en) | Moves the channel and videos but can delete an existing target channel |

YouTube recommends [role-based channel permissions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9367690?hl=en) to avoid password sharing. An Editor (Limited) or Viewer (Limited) role cannot access revenue data. A Studio manager may have more ability to edit or remove drafts, but is not necessarily permitted to delete the channel. These permissions do not automatically authorize every API operation.

## Primary ownership timing

Google's [Brand Account documentation](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/7311601?hl=en) requires the person initiating a change to have held an **owner** role for **at least seven days**. The prospective primary owner must have held a **manager or owner** role for at least seven days. The account must always have a primary owner.

This is not an estimate that a complete channel transfer will take seven days in all cases. Access invites, channel-permissions mode, identity verification and account eligibility can create separate steps.

## Irreversible destination-transfer risk

[YouTube's move-channel guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3056283?hl=en) warns that a channel on the destination Brand Account may be permanently **deleted** when replaced. Target-side videos, messages, playlists, channel history and verification badge can disappear. The moved-from account may lose its watch/search history, submitted creator demographic data, and gift settings. Before a transfer, operators may need to opt out of Studio channel permissions and remove delegated users.

There is no safe generic shortcut to “move to a different account”; matching a destination ID incorrectly can destroy assets. The policy describes a migration within associated Brand Accounts and not a universal, unqualified sale or legal assignment of creator accounts.

## Backup is not the same as platform portability

[YouTube Studio](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/56100?hl=en) permits downloads of videos the account uploaded, sometimes as **720p or 360p** MP4, subject to restrictions (removed content, strikes, preapproved audio and per-video download frequency). Google Takeout is separately presented as a way to export uploaded videos.

Neither interface promises a reproduction of the editable production project, all paid rights, subscribers as portable individuals, comments, customer contacts, YPP revenue contracts, or a working channel on another platform. Creators maintaining a long-term business should preserve **original local files and independent license records**, and should secure their own account recovery methods.

YouTube's [termination procedure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802168?hl=en) allows an appeal up to **one year** after channel termination and indicates a limit of **two appeals within one year**, with other per-channel limits. Copyright counter-notification is a distinct legal process. Following termination, YouTube says normal download access to its own video content is no longer available, though access to Google data can remain.

## What has not been established

The transferability of a channel's exact monetization contract, advertising approval, established subscribers as a legally transferable customer base, accumulated business goodwill, expected transfer downtime, and successful recovery probability are **not** established by general role-permission help pages. They remain unknown.

This research is intentionally **not** an operational transfer script or an invitation to disclose account credentials. No private account identifiers, security factors, contact information or uploads are stored in Mercata.
