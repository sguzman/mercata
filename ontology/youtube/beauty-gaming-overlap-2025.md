# Gaming and Beauty audience overlap on YouTube: a bounded 2025 panel

**Study window:** July 2024–June 2025. **Published:** September 2025. **Reviewed:** October 24, 2026. [Structured evidence](beauty-gaming-overlap-2025.json).

Tubular Labs' [*Unexpected Partnerships* research report](https://www.themeasure.net/content/files/2025/09/Tubular-2025-Report--Research-Featuring-Insights-and-Strategy-Team-Unexpected-Collaborators.pdf), page 9, examines a **provider-defined panel of 74,276 U.S. female viewers aged 25–34 who engaged with YouTube Gaming videos at least five times** in that dated window. This is a selected **audience engagement sample**, not a count of all U.S. female gamers, YouTube viewers, or gaming creators. Neither the recruitment probabilities, vendor's classification of sex/gender, nor population weights are available in the published chart.

## Provider-reported overlap and affinities

| Measurement | Figure | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Beauty/Gaming audience overlap | **5%** | Report's highlighted overlap figure in that gaming-focused U.S. female age-25–34 panel; exact matched numerator and percentage denominator not independently disclosed |
| YSL Beauty relative affinity | **6.4×** | Selected Beauty channel's relative viewing affinity in that report |
| Olay relative affinity | **5.7×** | Same provider-defined panel |
| NYX Professional Makeup relative affinity | **5.4×** | Same provider-defined panel |

An affinity multiple is **not** the percent of gaming viewers who watch that brand. The 5% is reported by the vendor, but the panel report does not expose enough raw matched-person detail to decide whether its base is the selected gaming-engager panel, the beauty channel audience, or another matched category definition. Consequently **do not multiply 5% by 74,276** or publish the resulting value as a deduplicated viewer count.

Tubular's [October 14, 2025 explanation](https://tubularlabs.com/?p=26144) describes the same cross-category project and points to high Beauty viewing affinity among selected female gaming viewers. [The Measure's September 17, 2025 coverage](https://www.themeasure.net/beauty-partnerships-can-help-break-through-with-female-gamers-tubular-fenty-arcane/) is **secondary commentary**, not a second independent sample. Other social-platform engagement during brand collaborations does not establish YouTube viewer overlap or campaign causal lift.

## What this can and cannot establish

The finding is useful evidence against a simplistic assumption that YouTube Gaming and Beauty audiences never intersect. It supports **investigating** related categories in a defined demographic segment, not assuming the same behavior across all genders, ages, places or channels.

A true all-age beauty/game **unique-person audience union** would require a matched common frame, exact group sizes, an independently verifiable intersection, comparable engagement definitions, documented weighting and privacy-compatible aggregate access. Existing [audience-overlap accounting rules](audience-overlap.md) still reject an exact union, because none of these necessary counts are established here.

No private engagement histories, individual identities or sensitive attribute classifications have been collected. The machine record keeps national viewer totals, absolute overlap, incremental reach, return on sponsorship and population generalizability as `null`. Audience segments are **those reported by the commercial study**; they are not individual classifications assigned by Mercata.

The [topic taxonomy](taxonomy.json) treats beauty and gaming as potentially intersecting content categories. This is an observed, limited panel description, not a gender-role assumption, natural segmentation rule, audience-size census, or expected creator income.
