# Software tutorials, technology reviews and workspace content are not one audience

**Public evidence snapshot: October 24, 2026.** [Machine-readable content segmentation](technology-subcultures-2026.json). This is a sourced taxonomy and two bounded platform viewing observations, not a census of software learners or a ranking of profitable niches.

## Six overlapping content purposes

The independent [YouTube topic taxonomy](taxonomy.json) contains `software`, `tech-reviews`, `desk-setups`, `crafts`, `education` and `gaming`. They should not be treated as mutually exclusive users or channels:

| Segment | Observable video types | Audience and economic limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Programming courses | Beginner lessons, web projects, game scripts | Programming learners and paying customers have not been independently enumerated |
| Software setup and troubleshooting | How to install, use or fix an application | Problem-solution searches differ from recurring community consumption |
| Professional workflows | Editor, design or developer tool demonstrations | May overlap tutorials, reviews and productivity |
| Hardware product reviews | Comparisons, benchmarks, long-term use | Promotional purchases and reviewer/sponsor relationships require separate examination |
| Repair and maker projects | Device repairs, modifications and diagnosis | Technical/how-to content overlaps physical crafts |
| Desk and workspace aesthetics | Tours, design changes and setup videos | May be lifestyle, productivity or gaming, not actual technical instruction |

YouTube's [2024 official coding guide](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/learn-to-code-on-youtube/) illustrates beginner web development, software fundamentals and game-scripting lessons. Those are editor-selected examples, not representative video counts or achieved learning outcomes.

## Two reported volumes with different populations

YouTube's [September 2, 2026 desk-setup article](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/desk-setup-videos/) reported **more than 250 million views worldwide from January through June 2026** for videos with *desk setup* or *#desksetup* in the title. This describes a **global title-query set of view events**, not all hardware reviews, unique people, creator income or purchase behavior.

The [April 28, 2026 learning announcement](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-empowering-learning-tools/) separately reports **more than 5.5 billion U.S. views in June 2025** for YouTube's broad learning/how-to category. It does not disclose what fraction involved coding, software setup or electronics repair.

The two numbers cannot legitimately be divided into a software-content market share: their **time periods, geographic denominators and content definitions differ**. They also precede the platform's August 24, 2026 public-view definition revision; current public playback starts must not be substituted for the original aggregates without harmonization.

## Minimum next measurements

A genuine technology-creator market study would predefine mutually auditable topic inclusion rules, sample all eligible newcomers rather than only successful channels, measure platform observation and potential overlap across subtopics, and obtain source-based gross receipts, cash production costs, time and zero-earner outcomes. It would also separate advertising, sales and sponsor compensation, and compare country-language combinations rather than assuming U.S. software-learning viewers represent the globe.

The machine study leaves programming view totals, unique learners, channel populations, earning distributions and profitability **unknown**. Editorially featured creators are neither a random sample nor evidence that a newcomer can earn a similar amount.
