# YouTube search demand: different proxies, no absolute counts

**Reviewed October 24, 2026.** [Machine provenance and method](search-demand-proxy-crosswalk.json).

[YouTube Studio Trends](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11962757?hl=en) reports top searches, breakout videos and Shorts content gaps using recent **28-day** audience/save context. Its ordinal audience-interest indicators derive from videos exceeding **1,000 weekly views** and vary with country, language and device availability. A content gap is not the number of monthly searches or a guaranteed profitable niche.

[Google Trends](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533?hl=en) uses a **sampled and normalized 0–100** interest index. [Google's advanced guide](https://newsinitiative.withgoogle.com/en-gb/resources/trainings/advanced-google-trends/) documents the **YouTube Search** filter, which differs from ordinary Web Search. Index 100 is the maximum normalized interest within the chosen comparison and period, **not 100 searches**. Changing the date, country, query/topic type or comparison panel can rescale the index. Equal index values in different countries do not imply equal numbers of searches.

[YouTube search predictions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9872296?hl=en) depend on typed words, trending searches, region and potentially signed-in history. An autocomplete suggestion is not a census of distinct searchers. The public YouTube Data API search-result total describes approximate indexed video supply, not actual viewer demand, creator count or conversion.

A reproducible research panel would preregister the keyword/topic, YouTube Search type, country, language, period, comparison panel and collection dates. Repeated captures may describe relative shifts **within comparable frames**; they cannot produce absolute buyer volumes or creator earnings without independently sourced, consented sales data.

No query panel was collected in this revision. True monthly searches, distinct people, buyer conversion and niche-level profit remain **unknown**.
