# YouTube ontology — audiences, content and earning

The independent [machine model](model.json) defines **184 concepts** and **175 relationships**. [Policy ledger](policies.json) captures cited eligibility, platform-fee and child-directed restrictions. [Measurement dictionary](measurements.json) explains which statistics exist, what they measure and whether the public can access them. The [topic taxonomy](taxonomy.json) describes **33 candidate overlapping subcultures**, without pretending their populations have been measured.

**Source snapshot: 2026-10-23.** Contemporary policy assertions refer to the verified official links in [YouTube's dossier](../../specimens/youtube.json). These documents distinguish official facts from research definitions, models and unknown economic estimates.

## Legal service provider and economic counterparties

The [dated operator and transaction map](operator-market-roles.md) and [machine taxonomy](operator-market-roles.json) cite YouTube's current legal terms identifying Google LLC as the covered service provider, while separating public video viewing, advertiser-funded placements, conditional YPP revenue sharing, paid viewer subscriptions and purchases, sponsorships, rights holders and channel delegates. Free viewers are not necessarily paying customers; AdSense for YouTube is a payment-routing account, not an independent source of creator demand. No seller or buyer population sizes, deal volumes or typical incomes are inferred.

## Participants, accounts and audiences

A viewer, registered account, channel, subscriber, publisher, monetized creator, view and advertising impression are not interchangeable units. One person can operate channels; subscribers may never watch; viewers can be unregistered. The rolling 28-day unique audience of a channel must not be called YouTube's monthly global user base.

Platform demographics (age, sex, gender, race or ethnicity, political affiliation, country and language) require survey or platform-wide denominators. A creator's authorized YouTube Analytics may give age, gender or country for that channel but does not make a population census. Demographic reporting can be suppressed.

## Community ontology

The 33 topic definitions include beauty, feminine lifestyle, youth culture, child-directed animation, general animation, gaming, memes, civic/political commentary and other cultural interests. They are open, overlapping coding categories. Labels do not establish that any person belongs to a group, and a topic's video count is not a topic's number of unique viewers. Until an appropriate sampled or aggregate source is found, *every* audience and creator size estimate remains unknown.

Children's animation requires separating broadly suitable animation from material deliberately directed at children. That classification affects advertisement and social features under YouTube's rules, and individual children must not be profiled.
