# Comparing English, Spanish, Hindi and Russian YouTube videos

**Review: October 26, 2026.** Independent peer-reviewed study: [McGrady, Zheng and Zuckerman, *Social Media + Society*, August 13, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1177/20563051251363216). [Machine-readable cohort record](four-language-video-cohorts-2025.json) · [tests](../../tests/four-language-video-cohorts.test.mjs).

The authors sampled **246,381 candidate YouTube video objects** using ten random-prefix batches collected between September 2023 and March 2024, then calibrated automated audio language detection against 90 manually coded videos per language. Inclusion confidence thresholds were **0.8 for English/Russian** and **0.6 for Spanish/Hindi**. This is a historical public-video sample, **not a survey of individual YouTube users, viewers, creators, or commenters**.

| Dominant audio language | Median public views/video | Median duration | Median likes | Video-view Theil concentration | Share of 2024 high-confidence sample uploaded in 2023 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| English | 38 | 119 seconds | 1 | 4.85 | 26.25% |
| Spanish | 39 | 148 seconds | 1 | 4.78 | 25.74% |
| Hindi | 37 | 29 seconds | 2 | 6.04 | 50.92% |
| Russian | 34 | 98 seconds | 1 | 4.88 | 30.71% |

The paper reports **more than 70% of sampled videos received no comments** in each of the four language cohorts, with *median comment count zero*. It expressly does not establish which languages the **comments** were written in. Video medians are not medians of viewers or creators. Theil measures **concentration of video views**, not creator income inequality.

Hindi was the shortest-duration group in the historical sample. It contained **58.31% Shorts** and **2.51% videos longer than 20 minutes**; its top 0.1% of videos accounted for **78.60% of its sample views**. These are selected historical format and video-event facts, not a modern platform user count or proof that Shorts produced the difference. Sampled videos span original upload years 2006–2024, and format definitions have since changed.

## Explicit interpretation boundary

A detected spoken/sung video language is not a creator's first language, nationality, ethnicity, home country or viewer language. The sample excludes some ambiguous, noisy, music-dominant, unavailable, and code-switched material; final audio-filtered per-language cohort sizes are not entered without numeric table evidence. Other studies based on U.S./Mexico fan **survey respondents** are not comparable to this video-object sample.

This evidence strengthens comparative content and visible interaction research. It **does not resolve** how many unique people participate in each language community, how many viewers comment, active audience sizes by country, growth since 2024, wages, or profits. The [country-language advertiser-reach matrix](country-language-matrix-2026.md) and [public-comment protocol](public-comment-language-protocol-2026.md) retain their separate denominators and unknown viewer language populations.
