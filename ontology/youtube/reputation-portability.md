# Feedback, reputation and creator switching on YouTube

**Review date: October 24, 2026.** [Machine evidence matrix](reputation-portability.json). This describes platform rules rather than measuring channel resale values, verified-buyer satisfaction or creator profit.

## Visible comments are not independent customer reviews

YouTube lets channel managers reply to, like, heart or pin comments, while held, removed or hidden comments are not equally visible. [Comment settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9483359?hl=en) permit moderation, pausing or disabling comments; held comments can remain in Studio up to 60 days. [Creator reply controls](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9482367?hl=en) and [community moderation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16622701?hl=en) also cover creator curation, chat and posts. A collection of positive comments is therefore **not** a representative buyer-review distribution, unique customer count or quantified conversion effect.

## What stays attached to the platform?

| Mechanism | Documented capability | Unsupported interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video comments | Publish, hold, react, pin and moderate | Verified buyer score, independently transferable reputation |
| Community/live chat | Moderate posts and chat | Statistically representative engagement survey |
| Subscriptions | Owner-visible subscriber metric | List of consenting, reachable external customers |
| Monthly/returning viewers | Studio aggregates for defined dates | Contact identities or cross-platform migration rate |
| Uploaded videos | [Download eligible uploads](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/56100?hl=en) via Studio or Takeout | Export of recommendation history or subscriber relationships |
| Brand Account channel | [Conditional internal move](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3056283?hl=en), possible destructive replacement | Transfer of audience to a competitor |
| Collaborator roles | [Delegation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9481328?hl=en) through Studio | Copyright assignment or completed business sale |

[Studio's audience guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314416?hl=en) separates subscribers, estimated monthly audience and returning viewers. They have different denominators and cannot be added or exchanged.

## Measurement gate for switching costs

An empirical switching study would need a dated cohort including unsuccessful and inactive channels; original media availability and rights; actual time and cash to re-publish; consented off-platform audiences; documented attrition and reach; and before/after *net* earnings. Account-role changes, within-YouTube Brand Account moves, uploaded-file copies and migration to another distribution service require different measures.

No representative verified-buyer rating, median switching cost, follower migration fraction, copyright sale valuation or reputation-caused sales lift has been established. Absence of a described export in reviewed documentation is treated as a **documentation limit**, not a universal prohibition.
