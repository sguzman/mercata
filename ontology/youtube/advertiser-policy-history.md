# Dated YouTube advertiser-suitability revisions in 2026

**Review date: October 23, 2026.** These are advertising-eligibility **policy changes**, not measured income increases. [Machine-readable ledger](advertiser-policy-history.json).

Two official sources anchor the ledger: [YouTube Help's recent guideline updates](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9725604?hl=en) and its [current advertiser-friendly content guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278?hl=en). YouTube's change log supplies the **month**, but not an exact effective day, for each of these entries. No precise daily transition date is invented.

## What changed

| Reported month | Area | Officially described allowance or restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **October 2026** | Public interest, harmful/unreliable and derogatory content | Certain public-interest material, including legislative proceedings and discussion of current government actions, can be eligible for advertising revenue |
| **September 2026** | Recreational drugs in games and drama | Gaming/scripted portrayals of use, sale and promotion may earn ads; non-glorifying education and public-service discussions of trafficking may qualify |
| **September 2026** | Graphic gaming | Graphic gaming material between seconds 8 and 15 may earn advertising revenue, while material in the first 7 seconds remains ineligible |
| **August 2026** | Depictions of death | Updated contextual distinctions for educational, documentary and news reporting |
| **March 2026** | Young-appearing distress imagery | Graphic or shocking content portraying young-appearing subjects in distress is ineligible for ads under the clarified rule |
| **January 2026** | Controversial issues | Non-graphic dramatizations may earn ads; certain sensitive categories remain ineligible for full monetization |

These are bounded summaries. Other content details may override these general categories. The current full advertiser-friendly guidelines include distinctions concerning real and simulated violence, editorial context, titles, thumbnails, hateful speech, unsafe content, and advertiser restrictions.

## Four separate decisions

1. **May this video earn advertising revenue?** Content suitability is evaluated per video or Short and can be full, limited, or no ads.
2. **Does the channel qualify for the YPP and relevant module?** Meeting [program thresholds](policies.json), approval and contract acceptance is distinct from ad suitability.
3. **Is the content allowed on YouTube at all?** Community Guidelines and legal rights rules may remove or restrict content independently.
4. **How much does the creator actually receive?** Even green/eligible videos can produce no or low revenue. Audience monetized playbacks, advertiser demand, region, copyright and revenue allocation remain separate.

An initial machine suitability decision is not always final. [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278?hl=en) provides for human review of eligible automated determinations. Mercata does not assign a success probability or promise reversal.

## Genre implications without invented rankings

Gaming, animation, public-interest journalism, historical documentary, and educational channels should record **format and content context** rather than applying a generic permanent RPM penalty to an entire genre. For example, gaming violence in one specific part of a video differs from explicit gore in a thumbnail; a political debate differs from a misleading election claim; an animated young-appearing character cannot reveal the audience's age.

These policy updates do **not** prove that gaming or journalism became statistically more profitable during 2026. The number of affected videos, any realized ad-income lift, typical RPM by content type, review error rates and appeal outcome distributions are unknown. Their fields remain `null` in the machine ledger.

## Reproducibility

For any legitimate comparison of creator income over policy revisions, separately record upload dates, policy month, per-video suitability decision, whether decision was appealed, creator eligibility, audience region and qualified ad exposure. A change in published guidance is a **potential confounder**, not an observed causal effect on earnings. The [YPP transition scheduled for February 2027](ypp-transition-2027.md) is another separate policy layer.
