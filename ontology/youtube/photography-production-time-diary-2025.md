# Real creator time diary: 61 completed photography videos

**Source published:** 15 September 2025. [How Long Does It Really Take to Make a YouTube Video?](https://zenagainst.com/2025/09/15/how-long-to-make-a-youtube-video/) is a first-person production log from one photography creator. The author recorded time spent on scripts, shoots, editing, publishing, promotion and administration across **61 completed videos**. This is an unusually concrete case record, not a probability sample of creators.

## Observed labor for this one channel

| Measure | Hours per completed video |
|---|---:|
| Minimum | 1.87 |
| Median | 7.37 |
| Mean | 9.74 |
| Maximum | 60.92 |

The author also reports a mean of **169 minutes of work for each published minute of video**, a median of 147, and a range of 48 to 359. This unit is work minutes **per published video minute**, not the number of hours in a viewer session and not a generic 169-hour production requirement.

| Logged work stage | Mean hours | Share of logged work |
|---|---:|---:|
| preproduction | 1.33 | 13.7% |
| production | 2.95 | 30.3% |
| postproduction | 4.87 | 50.0% |
| publishing | 0.43 | 4.4% |
| marketing | 0.12 | 1.3% |
| other | 0.03 | 0.3% |

Six stage averages total **9.73 hours** versus the author's **9.74-hour** overall average, consistent with rounding individual stage values. Do not silently change either published measurement to force agreement. Half the logged labor was attributed to postproduction; this is specific to the photographic workflow, not a causal law about video genres.

## Valid costs and invalid earnings claims

This is **completed video production labor**. It does not capture all rejected or abandoned scripts, invisible contractor effort, monetary software bills, product marketing margin or all legal/accounting work. Although the diary includes some promotion and administrative categories, the creator's total attempts and period cash costs are not available. The author is not a representative sample of creators and one channel cannot define typical labor hours for gaming, music, Shorts, documentaries or tutorials.

**No creator revenue is reported in this diary.** It cannot determine net hourly wages, ROI, the probability of channel monetization, typical production cost in dollars, or revenue produced by extra editing. For hypothetical scenario accounting, combine *independently entered* owner receipts and costs with owner-recorded hours, never transform this self-tracked case into projected income.
