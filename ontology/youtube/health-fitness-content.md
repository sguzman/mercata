# Fitness, wellness and health information: YouTube topic and policy research

**Source snapshot: October 24, 2026.** [Machine-readable content matrix](health-fitness-content.json). This is a study of publishing formats, source standards and policy boundaries, **not** a health treatment guide, a representative creator-income estimate or a measured niche census.

## Topic categories must remain separate

The parent [fitness topic](taxonomy.json) can include routine workouts, sport-specific conditioning, yoga or mobility, nutrition education, clinical explainers, first aid, mental wellness, body-image discussions and commercial equipment reviews. These are **candidate research categories**, not mutually exclusive audience groups or standardized YouTube reporting categories.

A channel may produce more than one type of content. A query containing "health" or "fitness" does not establish that a viewer has a condition, that a creator holds a clinical license, that a video provides medical treatment, or that it can monetize. Country, language, date, audience age and format matter.

## Four independent policy and discovery layers

| Layer | Source-backed effect | Invalid shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [YouTube medical misinformation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13813322?hl=en) | Prohibits specified high-risk misinformation about prevention or treatments contrary to local health authority guidance; educational/documentary context may matter. Videos, descriptions, livestreams and external links can be covered. | Not all exercise, health education or lived-experience content is prohibited |
| [Self-harm and eating-disorder policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802245?hl=en) | Distinguishes promotion of dangerous conduct from certain supportive and recovery discussions, with additional risks for minors. | Not every discussion of eating disorders is disallowed |
| [Advertiser-friendly guidelines](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278?hl=en) | Independently evaluates ad suitability for harmful medical claims and sensitive eating-disorder details. A video can face limited/no ads without automatically being deleted. | Monetization eligibility is not identical to Community Guidelines compliance |
| [YouTube Health features](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9795167?hl=en) and [applications](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12796915?hl=en) | Certain professionally vetted health sources can appear in health search shelves or source-information panels, subject to country, channel and licensing rules. | These are not generic fitness badges, a universal traffic boost or cash payments |

For eligible professional applications, the source currently describes **more than 1,500 valid public watch hours in the previous 12 months, or 1.5 million qualified public Shorts views over 90 days**, alongside eligible professions and other conditions. Those are a specific health-feature gate, **not** the YouTube Partner Program's general advertising thresholds. The reviewed application policy lists US, UK, Germany, Brazil, Indonesia, Japan, France, South Korea, Canada and Mexico for qualified individual or institutional applications; some institution types have separate qualification treatment. Feature availability can differ further by language and condition. Approval and display are not guaranteed.

## Evidence rules for content claims

A descriptive movement class is not automatically a treatment recommendation. The specific **claim** matters: for example, a statement promising to treat a diagnosed illness by replacing established therapy warrants an evidence and platform policy review, while a lesson on exercise form has a different content purpose. Do not use a diagnosis inferred from viewers' comments, names or visible bodies.

Content about weight, body image or mental health requires extra care in the presence of eating-disorder triggers, vulnerable audiences and age-related restrictions. The [official eating-disorder page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802245?hl=en) expressly distinguishes awareness or recovery contexts from promotion of harm. YouTube's advertiser suitability may still differ from whether a video stays online.

Commercial supplement, coaching and equipment offers also require separate consideration of [paid-promotion disclosure](brand-disclosure-risk.md), applicable product rules and creator transaction expenses. Product sales, content views and channel revenue cannot be substituted for one another.

## Measuring this sector without inventing an audience

A reproducible study needs a declared time/country window; topic definitions; legal public sampling; deduplicated creator or channel units; long-form/Shorts separation; pre/post-August-2026 public-view definitions; metadata/claim classification reviewed with context; and uncertainty around incomplete search/discovery samples.

This record has **no observed fitness-category unique-person audience, active-channel population, typical production hours, new-entrant profit or policy enforcement rate**. The machine fields remain `null`. An official policy source establishes rules, not how often they are enforced or how profitable a creator is.
