# YouTube product-by-product earning eligibility

**Checked 2026-10-23.** This is a [machine-readable eligibility matrix](eligibility-matrix.json) for the 16 monetization routes in Mercata's [revenue catalog](revenue-pathways.md). It does **not** establish that any specific person or channel qualifies.

## Detailed checklists

### Watch-page advertising

- Meet the advertising/Premium YPP threshold and pass review
- Accept the Watch Page Monetization Module
- Ads must be suitable and eligible; rights and regional restrictions apply

**Sources:** [How to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en); [YouTube Partner Program overview and eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en); [YouTube partner earnings overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en). **Status:** Documented YouTube product eligibility.

### Shorts Feed advertising

- Meet the ads YPP threshold and pass review
- Accept the Shorts Monetization Module
- Only eligible engaged Shorts views count for pooled revenue

**Sources:** [How to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en); [YouTube Partner Program overview and eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en); [YouTube Shorts monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en). **Status:** Documented YouTube product eligibility.

### YouTube Premium allocation

- Have advertising and Premium revenue-sharing eligibility
- Premium viewers must watch eligible content
- Premium allocation is not a per-view fixed price

**Sources:** [How to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en); [YouTube Partner Program overview and eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en). **Status:** Documented YouTube product eligibility.

### Channel memberships

- Meet expanded YPP eligibility and be approved
- Creator age at least 18
- Live in a memberships-eligible country
- Accept Commerce Product Module
- Channel is not made for kids or significantly child-directed
- Not a music channel under SRAV

**Sources:** [How to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en); [YouTube partner earnings overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en). **Status:** Documented YouTube product eligibility.

### Super Chat and Super Stickers

- Be accepted into eligible YPP tier
- Creator age at least 18
- Live in eligible country
- Accept Commerce Product Module
- Live chat and individual stream must support paid interactions

**Sources:** [How to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en); [YouTube partner earnings overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en); [Set your channel or video's audience](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9527654?hl=en). **Status:** Documented YouTube product eligibility.

### Super Thanks

- Be accepted into eligible YPP tier
- Creator age at least 18
- Live in eligible country
- Accept Commerce Product Module
- Check Super Thanks restrictions and music channel requirements

**Sources:** [How to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en); [YouTube partner earnings overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en); [Set your channel or video's audience](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9527654?hl=en). **Status:** Documented YouTube product eligibility.

### Live gifts, Jewels and Rubies

- Channel is in YPP
- Creator at least 18 with eligible verified AdSense identity
- Accept Virtual Items Module and meet individual feature rules
- Eligible country and stream; private, unlisted, age-restricted, made-for-kids and fundraiser streams excluded
- Comments or live chat must remain available
- Vertical and horizontal live streams may support gifts where eligible

**Sources:** [Virtual Items Program Policies for Creators](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15427201?hl=en); [Gifts eligibility and availability](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15535963?hl=en). **Status:** Documented YouTube product eligibility.

### Shopping: own products

- Channel in YPP
- Meet relevant subscriber threshold or be an Official Artist Channel
- Not made for kids or substantially child-directed
- No hate-speech-related Community Guidelines strikes
- Connect an eligible storefront; independent merchant handles fulfillment and payment

**Sources:** [Get started with YouTube Shopping](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12257682?hl=en); [Connect, manage, and disconnect your store](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12258186?hl=en); [How to earn money on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en). **Status:** Documented YouTube product eligibility.

### Shopping: affiliate products

- Channel in YPP and meeting applicable subscriber threshold
- Based in an eligible affiliate creator country
- Not a music channel, Official Artist Channel or associated with music partners
- Not made for kids or significantly child-directed
- Accept affiliate terms and tag participating seller products
- Sale, refund and commission conditions depend on merchant and program

**Sources:** [YouTube Shopping affiliate program overview and eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en); [YouTube Shopping affiliate launch announcement for Mexico and Argentina](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/449002752?hl=es-419). **Status:** Documented YouTube product eligibility.

### YouTube Creator Partnerships

- Creator age at least 18
- In YPP and eligible for advertising revenue sharing
- Located in available country
- No active Community Guidelines strikes
- In Mexico, brand inquiries may arrive via external email or social handles
- Being discoverable does not guarantee a paid brand deal

**Sources:** [Get started with YouTube Creator Partnerships: Mexico](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9385307?co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DMX&hl=en). **Status:** Documented YouTube product eligibility.

## Important differences

A channel in YPP is **not** automatically eligible for memberships, Gifts, affiliate Shopping or Creator Partnerships. Native product rules may require adulthood, additional agreement modules, geography, account/identity verification and restrictions on music or child-directed channels. A channel's audience location does not necessarily confer the creator's product country access.

Official YouTube Help supports two monetization entry thresholds for different features and adds individual conditions. [How to earn on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857?hl=en), [affiliate shopping](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en), [own-product shopping](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12257682?hl=en), [virtual gifts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15535963?hl=en) and [Creator Partnerships](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9385307?co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DMX&hl=en) have different rules and rollout timelines.

Unknown values stay unknown. The matrix does not request account credentials or estimate creator profit. Any eligibility answer for an actual channel requires current rules and the channel owner's authorized records.
