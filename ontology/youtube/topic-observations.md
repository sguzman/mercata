# YouTube subculture activity: initial evidence

These are carefully scoped numbers published by YouTube in official reports. They are **not** unique viewer counts, estimates of the number of people in a subculture, typical creator earnings or forecasts.

Every observation is linked to its original report, with the timeframe and method stated. Lifetime totals, annual totals, sample survey percentages and watch hours are deliberately kept separate.

## Interpretation

A video can appear under multiple topics. One person can watch several videos, return many times and follow several creators. The observations therefore cannot be added together to estimate a number of people or even a mutually exclusive total of topic engagement.

The animation-fan percentages were obtained from commissioned online fan samples in the stated countries and years. They describe those surveyed fans, not all residents or all YouTube users.

Selected virtual creators are not a representative sample of all channel operators. News hours on connected televisions are part of all news watch hours, not additional hours. A high view total says nothing by itself about margins, subscriptions, production costs or profitability.
