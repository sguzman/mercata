## Interpretation and boundaries

The 2006 acquisition is a corporate transaction, not a creator payment. Early 2007 partnerships were selective, December 2007 applications covered the US and Canada, and the July 2008 change added Brazil and Spain. The 2013 paid-channel product was a pilot; the 2015 YouTube Red launch was a specific US subscription service, not a current pricing snapshot.

The 2017 10,000-lifetime-view safeguard and January 2018 1,000-subscriber/4,000-hour announcement describe historical YPP access, not cumulative modern entry requirements. The September 2022 announcement anticipated Shorts sharing in early 2023. June 2023's earlier entry route applies to fan funding and selected products, not unconditional advertising. March 2026 Shopping access remains conditional.

The August 2026 announcement sets a **future February 1, 2027** effective date for new ads/Premium YPP rules. As of this study's date, those conditions are not yet current. The announcement's subscriber floor is not inferred.

**Not measured:** representative creator earnings before or after each change, median net profit, zero-earner rate, account approvals, country-specific realized availability, or causal policy effects.
