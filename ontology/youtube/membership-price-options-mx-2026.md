Across **25 allowed price options**, **6** have increased nominal MXN prices and **19** are unchanged. These are menu-option counts, not a transaction histogram.

The newest listed Mexico options range from MXN 19 to MXN 9,999, but those endpoints are not observed minimum or maximum **paid** prices. An individual creator can configure [up to six levels](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7544492?hl=en), rather than all 25. No sampled channel pricing, subscriber counts, customer conversion, churn, App Store adjustments, taxes, creator receipts, or economic profit are available in this source. A price menu is not an income distribution.
