# Offer and listing controls on YouTube: metadata is not a price

**Official product review: October 25, 2026.** [Machine surface matrix](offer-listing-controls-2026.json). Three distinct creator-facing surfaces are compared: ordinary video publication, paid membership tiers, and YouTube Shopping connected-store product tags.

| Surface | What the creator can edit | What a buyer pays for | Who determines price? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Ordinary video settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57404?hl=en) | Title (up to **100 characters**), description (up to **5,000**), thumbnail, tags and publishing/audience metadata | There is no inherent item sale from a video-view event | No native item price is set by ordinary upload metadata |
| [Channel memberships](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7544492?hl=en) | Up to **six levels**, **one to five perks per level**, benefits stacking for higher tiers | Eligible viewer makes a recurring member payment | Channel chooses from supported tier prices, with country/platform and member-cohort rules |
| [Shopping products](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12257682?hl=en) | Link store, display eligible store inventory and tag products in videos, Shorts or live | Customer completes an applicable merchant/store purchase flow | Connected merchant/store governs sale price, stock, payment and fulfillment under its agreements |

## Limits on comparing these surfaces

A video title, thumbnail or tag is metadata for discovery, not a guarantee that someone watches or pays. A watch-page view count is not a count of paying buyers. A membership level represents a recurring paid benefit, while an external connected product listing represents a different merchant settlement and refund responsibility. Gross merchant order totals, YouTube advertising revenue and net membership transfers cannot be summed without separately disaggregating fees and double counts.

Price choices may change according to eligibility, country, device and platform pricing updates; this study deliberately records no synthetic average membership price or net purchase value. Merchant/product access, maker's rights, disclosures, child-directed content restrictions and YouTube review still apply.

**Not measured:** actual product-title click-through, gross or net merchant sales, members enrolled, buyer conversion, typical price distributions or true creator profit. This source-defined listing taxonomy does not involve account access, manual QA or posting commercial offers.
