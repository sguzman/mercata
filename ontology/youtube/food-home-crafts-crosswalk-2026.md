# Food, cooking and home crafts: five distinct activities, incompatible public count units

**Reviewed October 26, 2026.** [Machine crosswalk](food-home-crafts-crosswalk-2026.json) and [validation tests](../../tests/food-home-crafts-audit.test.mjs). This advances the approved content-community classification rather than introducing new markets or claiming a representative population census.

Five analytical families remain separate: **home cooking / food preparation** (technique, baking, meal prep); **food entertainment** (food challenges, eating and cultural formats); **restaurant profiles / dining** (restaurant visits, chef profiles, food tourism); **kitchen and home product reviews** (equipment evaluation and potential affiliate intent); and **handmade crafts** (making, repairs and art/tutorial formats). They may overlap, and none specifies a distinct viewer identity.

YouTube's [ramen editorial article](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/ramen/) describes mukbang, restaurant profiles and cooking videos as **different formats**. But its **620M+ "buldak" AND "ramen" views** do *not* break out the percentage for restaurants, challenges or recipes. Restaurant and equipment-review audience/creator counts therefore remain **unknown**.

It reports **over 500 million view events**, not distinct baking viewers. The remaining ten observations reuse previously registered YouTube evidence and original source-frame records; no duplicate platform source count is invented.

Video-view events and new video uploads are different denominators; a Jan–Sep 2024 cake-decorating upload statistic cannot be compared directly with full-year origami uploads or Jan–Jun 2026 bento *view* events. Sources employ both YouTube's unpublished related-topic classification and text-filtered title predicates. One upload or viewer may contribute to multiple categories.

## Missing observations for a valid business analysis

Restaurant visit creators, home-kitchen product reviewers and cooking educators have different costs, potential monetization routes, disclosure/rights obligations and audience needs. None of these 11 narrow aggregates measures **paid creator earnings, distinct active channels, net profit, purchase conversion, sampled work hours or income viability**. Source dates, observation periods, category overlap and those unknowns are recorded independently, without using counts as genre sizes or recommending what a new entrant should produce.
