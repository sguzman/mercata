# From starting a channel to its first possible payout

YouTube's [Partner Program application overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en) and [monetization module guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/94522?hl=en) distinguish accumulating eligible activity, requesting review, being approved, enabling a revenue product and **actually receiving money**. These are different milestones.

## Published program stages

1. **Establish eligible subscribers, qualifying public uploads, watch hours or Shorts engaged-view thresholds; meet policies and regional requirements.**  Paid campaign-generated views/watch time don't count; organic follow-on views can under specific rules.

2. **Submit the YPP application from Studio and accept relevant base terms.**  Terms can change and additional module acceptance is required for specific earning products.

3. **Set up or link an active AdSense for YouTube account.**  Valid identity, country, age/payment and verification requirements may apply; no banking information is stored here.

4. **Automated and human review assesses the channel's policy compliance.** typically about 1 month Volume, repeat review or disagreement can extend the review process. Meeting numerical thresholds does not guarantee approval.

5. **A first rejection can be appealed within the published window.** Window: 21 days A valid appeal is not guaranteed to succeed.

6. **First-time rejected applicants can continue compliant uploads and request another review after the waiting period.** Waiting period: 30 days Rules depend on case history and published program policy.

7. **Later repeat applications usually have a longer cooldown.** Waiting period: 90 days Do not assume every rejection automatically uses the same path.

8. **Accept product modules and satisfy feature-specific conditions for ads, Supers, memberships, Shopping or gifts.**  Not all products are automatically available after base YPP approval.

9. **Earning activity is subject to adjustments, payout thresholds, verification, withholding and payment timing.**  Cash settlement may occur much later than view accrual or program approval.

YouTube says a decision typically takes about **one month** once the application is ready for review, but cases can take longer. Appeal and cooldown windows vary by application history. Reaching 500/1,000 subscribers and the applicable watch or Shorts thresholds is **not** channel approval. Ads, Commerce, Shopping and gifts have additional eligibility criteria.

## What paid promotion cannot substitute

[YouTube's module guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/94522?hl=en) says views and watch time from paid advertising campaigns don't count directly toward Partner Program application thresholds. Qualifying organic *follow-on* views after a promoted video are a different category; their eligibility depends on the platform's rules.

## Cash-flow consequences

A creator must account for potentially substantial labor before eligibility, review delay, product activation, time spent attracting advertisers or buyers, reporting adjustments, and payment thresholds and bank settlement. There is no reliable generic time-to-first-payment number in the available sources. The [payment calendar](payment-gates.md) covers the later monthly settlement cycle once an account is earning.

Mercata records the direct joining fee as **unknown** rather than casually declaring it zero without an explicit verified fee schedule. Out-of-pocket equipment costs and production labor remain separate. The [machine model](application-lifecycle.json) preserves uncertain stages without assigning a fake average.
