## What makes these hypotheses falsifiable

Each record states a viewer problem, comparison format, possible owner/consented aggregate metric, sampling frame and an explicit negative or inconclusive outcome criterion. Only a **preregistered** experiment with actual allowable data can establish whether the format difference appears in its sampled context. Low exposures, suppression, missing analytics or mismatched channels cannot be called zero consumer interest.

The dated public examples are descriptive *content activity or survey reports*, with different time, question and geography. They do not establish a paid market, that the suggested niche is undersupplied, a global preference share, or new-creator profitability.

Each plan's sample count, exposure threshold, observation calendar, spending ceiling and labor limit remains `null` until explicitly decided. **No account access, publishing, paid promotion, participant recruitment or experiment has been authorized or conducted.** Creator cash earnings, unpaid effort, consent and child-audience safeguards must be checked separately before any business interpretation.
