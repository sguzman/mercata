# Five distinct YouTube discovery surfaces

**Checked October 26, 2026.** [Machine record](recommendation-surface-evidence.json) and [automated evidence audit](../../site/recommendation-evidence-audit.mjs).

[YouTube's recommendation guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16089387?hl=en) distinguishes **Home**, which relies strongly on watch history, and **Up Next**, where the currently playing video is an important context. The [regular-video discovery guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dvideo&hl=en) names topic interest, competing videos and seasonality as reach factors and describes viewer satisfaction and watch duration as relevant signals. These are published system descriptions, not released coefficient weights.

[Shorts discovery guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en) distinguishes the Shorts tab, Home, subscriptions, search and sound/hashtag pages. It also says that **no minimum Shorts posting cadence** is required and that Shorts performance does not inherently harm ordinary-video recommendations.

YouTube **Search** considers query relevance across title, description, video content and engagement. It should not be equated with Home personalization or a most-viewed-video leaderboard. Subscriptions are follows, not guaranteed served impressions or an active user census.

## Dated recommendation update: Shorts originality, October 1, 2026

An [official TeamYouTube staff announcement](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/470890423/prioritizing-original-content-on-shorts?hl=en) on **October 1, 2026** describes an update intended to **prioritize original Shorts** and reduce Shorts-feed distribution for channels *primarily aggregating or reuploading others' videos without meaningful additions*. It recommends original commentary, substantial editing, original storytelling or a unique perspective, including when using another creator's clips. The announcement does not say every use of third-party footage is banned.

**Evidence type:** dated first-party statement of the intended recommendation direction, **not** a released ranking coefficient or observed causal experiment. The source does not quantify affected channels, traffic changes, deployment completion, a creator-level before/after panel, net income or the effect of any particular editing technique. Do not treat this as proof that reuse is copyright-authorized, that an account qualifies for YPP, or that all reused Shorts lose distribution.

This October 2026 discovery announcement is separate from YouTube's July 2025 **YPP inauthentic-content eligibility clarification**, the August 2026 **public view-count definition revision**, and advertiser-suitability rules. The [dated crosswalk](policy-measurement-change-crosswalk.md) preserves those policy boundaries.

## September 2026: prompt-created Custom Feeds, but only for eligible U.S.-English viewers

[YouTube's September 23, 2026 feature announcement](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-viewer-custom-feeds-shopping-tools/) introduced topic- or mood-prompted Custom Feeds for the Home surface and described forthcoming U.S. availability across viewer devices. The subsequently retrieved [official Help documentation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/18323430?hl=en) says they are currently **available in English in the United States**, require a **signed-in account**, enabled **watch and search history**, and an **English YouTube device language**. Custom feeds are private, expire after **30 days without use**, and viewing content through them may inform the viewer's standard Home recommendations.

The September announcement was **prospective**, while the undated Help page is a **current product-availability statement** as retrieved October 26. Neither reports a worldwide rollout, paid placements, qualified creator traffic, first-year reach, ranking weights, unique audiences, per-language engagement, or creator earnings. There is no basis to project that Spanish-language or non-U.S. viewers can access the same prompt interface under the documented eligibility rules. This is an **audience discovery surface and language-access restriction**, not a new payout route or proof of available demand in an unmeasured niche.

## Unmeasured outcomes

Fixed ranking weights, algorithm version dates, channel-entrant impression distribution, relative importance of any one signal, verified creator economic effects and a single universal posting schedule remain unknown. These documents do not justify ranking hacks, invented growth forecasts or adding public view events into deduplicated users.
