# EU27 creator consultation and ecosystem-impact evidence (October 2026)

**First-party publication:** [YouTube Government Affairs EMEA, October 12, 2026](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/europe-creator-consultation-creative-capital-report/). [Machine-readable evidence and limits](eu-creator-consultation-2026.json).

YouTube reports that the **Creative Capital** consultation heard from **more than 20,000 creators across all 27 European Union member states**. The public article describes selected question responses but does **not** provide a recruitment frame, country-by-country respondent counts, per-question sample sizes, weighting, nonresponse adjustment or probability-sampling design. The percentages therefore describe **respondents in that platform-supported consultation**, not all EU27 creator businesses.

## Questions with separately bounded denominators

| Reported result | Percentage | Who the percentage describes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Little or no support when interacting with government | 63% | Creator respondents to this question |
| More recognition from established industries would help | 64% | Creator respondents to this question |
| Insufficient training and workforce-development support | 60% | Creator respondents to this question |
| Lack of public grant access | 65% | Creator respondents to this question |
| Lack of access to dedicated studios | 65% | **Professional creators only**, item sample not specified |
| Recommendation algorithms considered vital to growth and revenue | 83% | Creator respondents to this question |
| Reduced algorithmic visibility perceived as livelihood threat | 71% | Creator respondents to this question |

The two **65%** figures have **different question populations**, so they are not interchangeable and must not be averaged, summed or applied to the 20,000+ participants as though the same subgroup answered each question. Exact counts for each question and the share of respondents classifying as professional remain unknown.

Reported algorithm dependence reflects **self-described importance and vulnerability**. It does not demonstrate measured actual revenue losses after a recommendation change, the probability of successful newcomer discovery, or that paid traffic can substitute for organic recommendations.

## Three other economic and distribution claims have different units

In the same publication, YouTube states that **more than 70% of watch time** on EU27-channel content came from **outside** the region. The time period and calculation details are not disclosed in the excerpt. This is a *share of watch time*, not a share of distinct foreign viewers, a country population, or export earnings.

The article also cites a **separate Oxford Economics 2025 impact study** which it says estimates over **EUR 8.4 billion in EU GDP contribution** and more than **220,000 full-time-equivalent jobs supported** by the YouTube ecosystem. These are *modeled economic impacts* as relayed by the platform. This review has not verified the underlying full Oxford methodology; do not treat them as observed channel payouts, creator employment headcount, a creator-income distribution, or a direct result of the consultation survey.

## Research applicability

These findings inform several *potential* business barriers: access to capital, training, workspace, distribution and regional policy. They do not establish that any intervention will increase revenue for a specific creator or that recommendation dependence is unique to one content genre.

A defensible future EU27 economic survey would require a declared universe of new entrant creators (including nonmonetized and inactive attempts), regional strata and coverage, representative selection or transparent nonprobability limits, gross and net earnings, costs, labor hours, funding access, channel activity and confidence/uncertainty measures.

Mercata's [newcomer-income protocol](newcomer-income-cohort.md), [geographic scope](geographic-scope.md), [production cost model](production-costs.md), and [traffic-source concentration guide](traffic-concentration.md) preserve these boundaries. No individual EU creator profiles or accounts were collected.
