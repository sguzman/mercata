# First-upload vlog channels: views measured, earnings and labor modeled

**Checked October 24, 2026.** [Machine record](first-upload-vlog-study-2026.json) and [regression tests](../../tests/first-upload-vlog-study.test.mjs).

An [author preprint published September 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32925) analyzes daily-life vlogging channels found through genre-specific YouTube searches. The authorship is **Samiha Tariq**. The first-upload-date subset includes **107 channels**, beginning between November 2025 and August 2026, including low-view channels visible in search. The source reports **51.4% had fewer than 1,000 lifetime views** and **11.2% had three or fewer uploads**. Because the sample depends on search discoverability and two vlog genres with varying follow-up durations, it is **not** a representative distribution of all YouTube entrants.

A separate full-history panel includes **420 channels and 56,419 videos**. It finds a post-first-breakout view association of approximately **164.2%**, not an experimental causal revenue estimate. The wider panel and 107-channel cold-start cohort cannot be added as disjoint creator samples.

## Model values are not payments

The often-quoted **$1.61 median "revenue"** is a *simulated long-form ad-value equivalent*, calculated from public views assuming **$2.30 per 1,000 views**. The study did **not** access channel payout statements, verified monetization status or individual channel RPM. Its **185 median "labor hours"** are also *estimated*, using **three hours per finished uploaded video minute** rather than measured creator timesheets. The implied **$0.01 per hour** is hypothetical, not take-home profit or actual creator pay.

The author reports alternate median modeled ad values of **$0.70** at $1.00 long-form RPM, **$2.81** at $4.00 RPM, and **$9.08** when adding hypothetical Shorts payouts at $0.15 per thousand Shorts views to the $2.30 baseline. These are sensitivity assumptions, not observed earnings distribution points; 79.4% below $100 refers to *modeled* value, not actual zero earners.

The work is a preprint, not a peer-reviewed creator income census. Real cash revenues, platform eligibility, expenses, actual production hours, inactive/deleted never-seen creators, and a generalizable first-year income median remain unmeasured. The [separate vidIQ 360-day subscriber study](channel-cohort-observations-2026.md) samples a fundamentally different channel population and cannot close these gaps.

## Exceptional creator businesses are not a typical earnings sample

The [Forbes 2026 Top Creators ranking](https://www.forbes.com/sites/stevenbertoni/2026/06/23/forbes-top-creators-2026/) (June 23, updated August 6) estimates **$1.02 billion** of **gross earnings** across 50 selected highly prominent cross-platform creators for **March 2025–March 2026**. Its exceptional examples include **MrBeast ($300 million)**, **Dhar Mann ($65 million)**, and **Markiplier ($38 million)**. Forbes's methodology counts estimated income across social/video media and associated off-platform enterprises, excluding company equity valuations. These are **not audited YouTube Studio payouts**, a typical creator wage, or net earnings after production, payroll, taxes and other costs.

The [Forbes 2025 ranking](https://www.forbes.com/sites/stevenbertoni/2025/06/16/forbes-top-creators-2025/) instead estimates **$853 million** for a 50-person list over **April 2024–April 2025**. The two lists are not verified as the same 50 people and cover different periods, so their aggregate difference cannot establish growth in one representative cohort.

Both Forbes lists condition selection on exceptional visibility, business scale and editorial criteria, excluding the unobserved majority of less prominent, failed, new, and non-earning creators. In particular, comparing their millions with the **$1.61** *hypothetical ad value* in the 107 search-selected vloggers above is **not** a contrast of two comparable earning populations. The first is estimated cross-platform **gross business income**, the second modeled video ad value from public view counts and an assumed RPM, with no verified receipts. Representative YouTube income distributions, net profit, individual success probabilities, and matched calendar-period outcomes stay **unknown**.

The [machine register](first-upload-vlog-study-2026.json) stores these selected cases as cautionary evidence with separately linked sources and explicit non-comparability, not as a leaderboard, target income, or newcomer forecast.
