# Fraud, artificial engagement and creator-channel security on YouTube

**Reviewed October 24, 2026.** [Machine risk evidence](creator-fraud-security.json), [regression audit](../../site/creator-fraud-security-audit.mjs), and [tests](../../tests/creator-fraud-security-audit.test.mjs).

[YouTube's fake engagement policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3399767?hl=en) prohibits artificially inflating video views, comments, likes or subscribers using automation, deceptive delivery or paid metric manipulation. A creator can ask people to subscribe, but coordinated sub-for-sub or purchased fabricated views are not legitimate growth; a contractor hired to promote a channel can still expose that creator's channel to enforcement.

[Channel security guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16281336?hl=en) explicitly warns of fake sponsor/brand-deal approaches, links and attachments. YouTube recommends independently verifying brand representatives, protecting accounts with a passkey and two-step verification, and reviewing channel managers. The [creator safety guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802848?hl=en) advises avoiding untrusted software and phishing, and using delegated channel permissions rather than sharing Google credentials.

[YouTube's scams and spam rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en) separately prohibit fraudulent offers, repetitive/deceptive engagement posts and misleading links. Real third-party services and advertisements must be checked for policy compliance; their use alone does not quantify ad impressions, real customer interest or legitimate new buyers.

## Separate the event types

| Type | Official risk mechanism | What remains unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artificially purchased engagement | Policy sanction or channel removal | Its prevalence, creator ROI, and specific enforcement likelihood |
| Fake sponsorship attachment/link | Credential theft or malware | Confirmed annual attacks and financial loss |
| Compromised credentials | Hijacked accounts and unauthorized access | Recovery time or effectiveness of security controls |
| Scam offer or off-site link | Viewer harm and policy enforcement | Real conversions and fraudulent buyer counts |
| Untrusted collaborator role | Access beyond creator intention | Verified owner permission histories |

No sampled creator-level incidence, verified fraud payouts/losses, conditional probabilities, or measured outcome from a particular security control was collected. The platform's documented precautions are **controls**, not actuarial findings; percentages remain unknown rather than being guessed.
