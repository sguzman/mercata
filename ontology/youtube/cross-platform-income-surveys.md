# Cross-platform creator-income surveys: comparison boundaries

**Review date: 2026-10-23.** [Structured evidence](cross-platform-income-surveys.json).

Two distinct research groups published self-reported creator-income results for 2025. They help test what Mercata knows about the broader creator economy, but **neither is a representative YouTube income sample**, and neither can establish a typical new YouTube channel's earnings, costs, or profit.

## NeoReach, 2025 creator-earnings report

The [NeoReach Creator Earnings Report 2025](https://neoreach.com/reports/creator-earnings-report-2025/) says **more than 3,000 cross-platform creators** were surveyed. Of these respondents, **50.71%** reported annual creator earnings below **USD 15,000**. It also says approximately **57% of its full-time creator subgroup** earned below USD 44,000 from content.

The [Influencer Marketing Hub publication](https://influencermarketinghub.com/creator-earnings-report-2025/), dated July 6, 2026, is a **partner publication of this same NeoReach dataset**, not an independently recruited survey. The publicly presented method does not establish probability sampling from all creators, YouTube-only results, the survey's exact fieldwork dates, the share earning zero, audited profit, or subgroup denominators. NeoReach's named "Monetization Barrier" around USD 15,000 is its commercial interpretation, **not** a demonstrated causal law.

## Creator Spotlight, December 2025

The independently conducted [Creator Spotlight monetization survey](https://www.creatorspotlight.com/p/monetization-report-2025) has **427 voluntary responses** from a newsletter readership. Its team sent a 28-question questionnaire to nearly 300,000 engaged subscribers, primarily North American creators who work across newsletters, video, podcasting and social media.

For calendar-year 2025 creator-work earnings, the report describes **nearly half** of respondents earning under USD 500. That phrase does not identify an exact percentage. It directly reports **37 of 427 (about 8.7%)** earning six figures or more, and **92** in an annual USD 1,000–5,000 range. These are **sample counts**, not estimates of all YouTube channels. No separate, representative YouTube-only subgroup or verified net-profit distribution is reported.

The publisher observes correlations between multiple income streams and high earnings; selection and business maturity could explain those patterns. They are **not experimental evidence** that adopting more revenue channels will cause a specified increase.

## Do not pool the findings

| Evidence population | Measured or described | Not established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NeoReach mixed-platform respondents (over 3,000) | Self-reported share below an annual USD 15,000 threshold | Worldwide YouTube-only distribution, true zero-earner share, creator net profit |
| Creator Spotlight newsletter respondents (427) | Selected yearly income brackets and number of six-figure earners | Representative new YouTube creator expectation or rate of monetization |
| [AIR Media-Tech already-monetized channels](small-channel-income.md) (297) | First six monetized months for a selected channel group | Income of failed/nonmonetizing new channels |
| [Patreon-affiliated YouTube creators](creator-income-evidence.md) (23,435 monthly observations) | Selected monthly **Patreon** income in three historical March periods | Native YouTube advertising or a creator's total income |

Neither the years nor the earnings constructs are interchangeable. Broad commercial surveys may include sponsorship and outside businesses. Patreon observations omit those who do not use Patreon; AIR starts **after** a channel is monetized. A sampled six-figure count does not establish a population success probability.

## Research still required

A credible YouTube newcomer earnings distribution needs an identifiable universe of first-time channel operators, including **nonuploaders or channels that never monetize** if the estimand covers entrants; consistent entry dates and follow-up windows; verified native creator payouts and outside receipts; cash and labor costs; and detailed missingness/nonresponse accounting. Consent-based or legitimately public aggregates must avoid collecting personal account credentials or sensitive user profiles.

All YouTube population median, first-year profit and zero-earner estimands remain **unknown**. The [income evidence review](creator-income-evidence.md) and [production-cost framework](production-costs.md) document additional data collection constraints.
