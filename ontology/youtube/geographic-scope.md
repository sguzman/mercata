# Geographic scope and country comparison protocol

**Research snapshot: October 23, 2026.** This is a source-bounded study design, not a claim to have measured every country or a unique global YouTube audience. [Structured model](geographic-scope.json).

## Five independent geographic axes

The viewer's geography, creator's residence, payout account's country, advertising market and content language are separate fields. A creator's location or spoken language cannot establish the location of their viewers, nor entitlement to another jurisdiction's products.

The Reuters report specifically cautions against nationally representative interpretations for some surveyed online populations, including India, Kenya, Nigeria and South Africa; it also describes the March 2026 repoll of questions on social networks. These methodological limitations matter when comparing reported use across countries and time.

## Country-by-country eligibility register

For every territory and date, inspect ordinary YPP entry, expanded YPP entry, each named monetization feature, age and module restrictions, the creator's legally applicable residency requirement, payout account country, currency and thresholds, and payment or tax conditions. Record the official source and the verification date for each separate field. Set anything not verified to `null`; never treat absence of a country row as zero participation or proof of ineligibility.

## Research procedure

1. Predeclare an exact estimand: people, creators, accounts, events or money; relevant population, countries, age, device, content format and time window.
2. Record survey frame, quotas, weightings, nonresponse, country coverage, question wording, and data access rights; segregate official platform claims and commercial measurement.
3. Compare only compatible measurement families, including the 2026 change in public view counting. Do not pool separate surveys into a fabricated global denominator.
4. Preserve overlap of topics and languages without assuming demographic identities or deduplicated community populations.
5. For income studies, include non-monetized channels and zero earners, cash expenses and labor hours; do not extrapolate samples of approved earning channels to new entrants.

See [country reach and device measures](country-reach-devices.md), [platform-scale definitions](platform-scale.md), [product eligibility](eligibility-matrix.md) and [community sampling procedures](public-sampling.md) for complementary evidence.

**Unresolved:** total distinct monthly YouTube viewers globally, most national creator population estimates, overlapping niche populations, newcomer profit including zero earners, and representative production costs. These remain unknown.
