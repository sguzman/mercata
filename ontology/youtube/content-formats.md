# YouTube formats: distribution, revenue and sales links

**Public policy snapshot: 2026-10-25.** This [machine catalog](content-formats.json) separates 8 publication or distribution formats. **No format has a measured average creator profit, success probability, startup cost or required work hours.**

## Ordinary watch-page video

Upload published as an ordinary video rather than classified as a Short.

**Access:** Channel can publish eligible video; YPP ad eligibility is separate.

**Discovery:** search, home, suggested, external, playlists.

**Candidate earning paths:** watch-page-ads, premium-revenue, super-thanks, channel-memberships, shopping-own, shopping-affiliate, direct-sponsorship. This list is a compatibility research checklist, not proof every channel receives every feature.

**Links:** long-form descriptions and comments subject to feature access.

**Limitations:** No guaranteed ads on every view. External clicks and viewer payments require separate attribution.

## Shorts

Standard-channel videos after 2024-10-15 that are square or vertical and up to three minutes generally qualify; artist channel cutoff differs.

**Access:** Eligible standard-channel square or vertical video up to three minutes; rights and channel status apply.

**Discovery:** shorts_feed, search, home, related_video.

**Candidate earning paths:** shorts-ads, premium-revenue, super-thanks, shopping-affiliate, shopping-own, direct-sponsorship. This list is a compatibility research checklist, not proof every channel receives every feature.

**Links:** related-video link can be clickable; ordinary URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments are not clickable.

**Limitations:** A video start is not necessarily a paid engaged view. Music and content claims can affect eligibility. A Shorts description is not equivalent to a clickable sales-page link.

## Webcam or encoded live stream

Real-time transmitted audiovisual content, distinct from scheduled playback of a prerecorded video.

**Access:** Verified eligible channel with no live restriction during the previous 90 days, and compliant creator age.

**Discovery:** live_surfaces, home, search, notifications, external.

**Candidate earning paths:** watch-page-ads, super-chat, live-gifts, channel-memberships, direct-sponsorship. This list is a compatibility research checklist, not proof every channel receives every feature.

**Links:** stream description and authorized live-chat surfaces have their own link rules.

**Limitations:** Minimum live creator age currently 16. Live attendance, replay views and repeat viewers are different populations. Paid live features require additional eligibility.

## Mobile live stream

Real-time broadcast from supported mobile app and device.

**Access:** Mobile-specific 50-subscriber minimum, verification, no live restriction in previous 90 days and activation.

**Discovery:** live_surfaces, home, notifications.

**Candidate earning paths:** super-chat, live-gifts, channel-memberships, direct-sponsorship. This list is a compatibility research checklist, not proof every channel receives every feature.

**Links:** subject to live link and channel feature rules.

**Limitations:** Initial activation may take 24 hours. Small mobile channels can face audience caps or archive privacy defaults. Subscriber minimum for mobile is not the webcam minimum.

## Video Premiere

Scheduled shared playback event of uploaded video, which can include live chat.

**Access:** Video publishing and Premiere eligibility; real-time chat/product access checked separately.

**Discovery:** upcoming_page, channel, notifications, watch_page.

**Candidate earning paths:** super-chat, super-thanks, watch-page-ads, channel-memberships. This list is a compatibility research checklist, not proof every channel receives every feature.

**Links:** ordinary watch-page surface; links and paid chat still subject to access rules.

**Limitations:** A Premiere is not a freshly filmed live stream. Real-time interaction depends on content and chat eligibility.

## Podcast show and episodes

A podcast is a playlist of full episodes, with special YouTube Music listening and discovery options.

**Access:** Eligible video playlist designated as a podcast; episodes must be videos rather than MP3-only files.

**Discovery:** podcast_search, youtube_music, watch_page, recommendations.

**Candidate earning paths:** watch-page-ads, premium-revenue, channel-memberships, direct-sponsorship, external-support. This list is a compatibility research checklist, not proof every channel receives every feature.

**Links:** watch-page/episode descriptions and channel profile links subject to policy.

**Limitations:** A show-level playlist is not itself a unique viewer or additional standalone video. Copyright-ineligible content may lose podcast distribution.

## Community Post

Text, images, polls, quizzes, music or videos distributed through supported channel and feed surfaces.

**Access:** Eligible channel not supervised or designated made for kids.

**Discovery:** channel_post_tab, home, subscriptions, some_shorts_feed.

**Candidate earning paths:** indirect_audience_retention, channel-memberships, shopping-own. This list is a compatibility research checklist, not proof every channel receives every feature.

**Links:** Post distribution and linking permissions must be checked for each feature.

**Limitations:** A Post is not automatically a direct paid product. Posts are unavailable on made-for-kids channels.

## Archived live replay

Recording of a live event preserved for later on-demand watching.

**Access:** Original stream and archive visibility must permit replay.

**Discovery:** watch_page, search, channel, external.

**Candidate earning paths:** watch-page-ads, premium-revenue, super-thanks, direct-sponsorship. This list is a compatibility research checklist, not proof every channel receives every feature.

**Links:** subject to replay and watch-page feature policy.

**Limitations:** Do not sum the live and replay audience into unique people without deduplication. Archive retention, availability and visibility are channel-specific.

## Format-specific questions that affect economic results

A standard-channel square or vertical upload up to three minutes has normally been classified as a Short since October 15, 2024, but older uploads and artist channels have different dates ([official three-minute Shorts guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en)). Shorts Feed revenue is allocated differently from Watch Page ads and is not a fixed per-start rate.

YouTube's [link-sharing guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en) says written URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments are **not clickable**, while a Shorts related-video link can be clicked. That makes a Shorts-to-long-form-to-store journey structurally different from placing an eligible clickable link under an ordinary video. Neither route comes with a published conversion rate.

For **mobile live streams**, [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9228390?hl=en) lists 50 subscribers, channel verification, a clean live status and activation; these must not be applied to all computer/webcam/encoder streaming. The [general live guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2474026?hl=en) also distinguishes live creator age rules.

For **podcasts**, [YouTube's model](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12751636?hl=en) treats a show as a playlist containing video episodes, some eligible for YouTube Music and podcast discovery. It is incorrect to count playlist and episode views as separate audiences. For **Posts**, [YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9409631?hl=en) supports images, polls, quizzes and several feeds, but no Posts on channels designated made for kids.

## What remains unknown

All 8 format income medians, entry-cohort outcomes, labor-hour distributions, equipment costs, share of total viewers reaching a store or membership, and first-year survival rates are explicitly `null`. Those questions require representative samples or a separately authorized, measured experiment, not intuition or success stories.

## Eight overlapping YouTube surface layers

These are **surface classifications**, not eight disjoint audiences. The [machine source-linked map](content-formats.json) joins the existing publication-format inventory to playback, playlist, device, social and transaction surfaces.

| Functional surface | Measurement boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Video watch page | Watch-page playback and revenue-eligible ad events are different quantities; replay does not create a new creator. |
| Shorts feed | Raw Shorts play starts, eligible engaged views and watch-page ad views have distinct definitions. |
| Live and Premiere interaction | Concurrent viewers are not paid interactions; Jewels purchased and Rubies earned are not interchangeable. |
| Music discovery and YouTube Music | Music charts, Content ID rights and music payouts have different units and permissions. |
| Podcast show and episodes | Podcast show playlist is a grouping of video episodes, not additional uploaded videos or unique viewers. |
| TV and living-room viewing | TV is a device/viewing frame, not a separate native upload or standalone creator earnings rate. |
| Posts and community interactions | Post displays and votes are neither video views nor deduplicated subscriber people. |
| Native products and external commerce | Payer transactions and creator net receipts are not views; module, rights and merchant eligibility differ. |

A single video may be played on a TV, appear on an ordinary watch page, and participate in a podcast playlist without becoming three creator publications. Memberships or gifts are independent transaction records, not additional video view events. The study therefore leaves unique people, profit and cross-surface conversion `null`.
