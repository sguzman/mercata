# YouTube creator competition and discovery: a commercial data sample

**Study:** [Metricool, July 29, 2026](https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/). The company analyzed **799,718 videos from 71,177 accounts** while comparing results from 2025 and 2026. It did not disclose enough public information to establish that these accounts form a representative random sample of YouTube creators, and its exact observation windows are not fully specified.

## Moving up a subscriber tier

| Account size at observation | Share moving into a higher size band |
|---|---:|
| all | 10.08% |
| tiny | 9.59% |
| small | 16.01% |
| medium | 7.01% |
| big | 2.11% |

Here, a *tier jump* is a change from one subscriber-size group to another during the study's observation period. It is **not** the share of newly created channels that eventually monetize, reach a thousand subscribers, or earn a living. A tiny channel could grow substantially without reaching the next group.

## Where its sampled views came from

| Traffic source | Percentage of sampled organic views |
|---|---:|
| shorts feed | More than 61% |
| subscribers | About 11% |
| youtube search | About 9% |
| related videos | About 5.77% |
| playlists | About 5.05% |
| direct channel | About 3% |

These are view-event counts within the commercial sample. They are **not** the worldwide share of all YouTube activity, a measure of monthly users or a guarantee of traffic for any channel.

## Reported long-form changes

Metricool reports roughly **76% more long-form views** and a decline in average view duration from **3.98 to 2.51 minutes**. It reports approximately **51% fewer ad impressions**, **59% fewer monetized playbacks** and **55% lower estimated ad revenue** across its compared long-form observations. These figures are not total YouTube ad revenue or the profit of all creators.

The study also observes that channels with no Shorts averaged 4,364 long-form views per video, while channels posting around five Shorts monthly averaged 8,789. The result is **correlational**: posting Shorts may coincide with higher activity or other channel qualities; it is not proof that making five Shorts causes a particular lift.

## The evidence boundary

The report predates YouTube's August 24, 2026 change to public view-count definitions. After that change, raw starts, engaged views and monetization-eligible views must remain separate. The study does not give a costed entry cohort, income distribution or a representative comparison among specific YouTube subcultures. Use it as *one sample with useful questions*, not an industry-wide earning forecast.
