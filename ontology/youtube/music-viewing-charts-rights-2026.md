# Music on YouTube: viewing time, charts and independent creator economics

**Reviewed October 24, 2026.** [Machine model](music-viewing-charts-rights-2026.json), [source checks](../../tests/music-viewing-charts-rights.test.mjs).

[Omdia's January 28, 2026 estimate](https://omdia.tech.informa.com/pr/2026/jan/youtube-reaches-29-billion-videos-as-music-and-shorts-dominate-viewing) assigns **33% of YouTube viewing time** to music videos. This is a research-provider estimate of watched time, **not** 33% of users, independent artist channels, new uploads, or creator income. Its press release does not provide a reproducible global individual-listener sample frame.

YouTube's [official Music Charts guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9014376?hl=en) defines different units:

| Report | Unit | Underlying content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly Top Songs | Song-attributed video views | Official music video + user-generated videos using the song + lyric videos |
| Daily/Weekly Top Music Videos | Official video views | Official music video viewing |
| Top Songs on Shorts | **Number of Shorts created using song** | Not music-video views or people listening |
| Weekly Top Artists | Artist-attributed catalog views | Multiple official and fan/recording-related formats |
| Music Insights | Song-attributed views **excluding Shorts** | Official recordings and user-generated/lyric video views |

A hit song can be represented in many other people's videos. Do not sum these rankings, infer new paying fans from the creation count, or treat an official video rank as a musician's own channel revenues.

## Which seller and rights questions differ?

Original independent music performances, official music videos, lyric videos, songs reused in Shorts, cover versions, derivative clips, educational tutorials and live shows have different combinations of song-composition, sound-recording, footage and performance rights. They may overlap. [Content ID](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797370?hl=en) can block, track or monetize matching uploads, and settings can differ by region and rights holder. YouTube royalties, claimant revenue, YPP uploader proceeds and gross video views are **not** one number.

**Unknown:** share of viewers who are unique music fans, number of active independent musicians, chart participants who earn nothing, normal artist net profit, song publishing royalties, genre-specific earnings per hour, and buyer conversion by format. The chart and Omdia evidence do not answer those questions.
