# Pew U.S. adult YouTube-news use: reported year-to-year point changes

**Reviewed October 25, 2026.** [Machine source-linked series](us-news-reported-change-2026.json), [constrained difference calculator](../../site/us-news-differences.mjs), and the existing [Pew demographic source-frame crosswalk](us-news-demographic-crosswalk.json).

[Pew's October 19, 2026 news fact sheet](https://www.pewresearch.org/journalism/fact-sheet/social-media-and-news-fact-sheet/) lists U.S. adults who **regularly get news from YouTube**, expressed as percentages of **all U.S. adults**. The figures are not the percentages of YouTube subscribers, monthly YouTube users, or news-channel followers.

| Survey year | U.S. adults regularly getting YouTube news | Difference from prior published year (percentage points) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2020 | 23% | — |
| 2021 | 22% | -1 |
| 2022 | 25% | +3 |
| 2023 | 26% | +1 |
| 2024 | 32% | +6 |
| 2025 | 35% | +3 |
| 2026 | 34% | -1 |

The 2026 estimate (**34%**) is **1 percentage point below** 2025's 35% and **11 percentage points above** 2020's 23%. These are differences of rounded published point estimates; **Pew's per-year variances and a harmonized cross-wave test are not established in this Mercata record**, so a statistically significant audience decline, a precisely measured growth slope or an explanatory cause is *not* claimed. The August 24–30, 2026 wave had **5,166 U.S. adult participants** in Pew's American Trends Panel. Older wave-specific sizes cannot be imputed from it.

## What this can and cannot say

These sources document a descriptive *survey self-report* series for one U.S. news-use question, not observed YouTube video attention, distinct people, channel revenue, advertising reach, news publishers' success or political persuasion. The series has no individual subscriber or party-identification records; no representative political-channel audience is measured.

A verified changing or declining content niche would require repeated comparable topic-specific video/watch-time series with stable formats, country, query definition and exposure frame. Pew's news-use answer is not an episode-level or category-level time series. The existing [public event-attention protocol](neutral-news-attention-protocol-2026.md) therefore remains unexecuted. This research does not prescribe political messaging or audience targeting.
