# Earnings among 297 small, already-monetized YouTube channels

**Source:** [AIR Media-Tech, updated October 23, 2026](https://air.io/en/monetization/how-much-does-youtube-pay-after-monetization). The company reports having reviewed YouTube Studio data from 297 channels under 10,000 subscribers, spanning 18 niches and 41 top-audience countries. Six-month sequences began between January 2024 and April 2026.

**Do not treat this as a representative estimate for all creators.** Channels that never reach monetization and nonparticipants are absent. The study is published by a commercial creator-services company; its sampling criteria are not fully disclosed or independently audited.

## The first six months

All figures in USD, with original study's separate median summaries:

| Month after monetization | Median earnings | Middle 50% | Median views | Earnings per 1,000 views | Channels under USD 100 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 (partial) | 30 | 6–141 | 50,400 | 0.88 | 69% |
| 2 | 88 | 18–348 | 60,500 | 1.75 | 53% |
| 3 | 64 | 11–290 | 45,400 | 1.71 | 56% |
| 4 | 55 | 9–247 | 37,000 | 1.56 | 62% |
| 5 | 39 | 7–186 | 32,500 | 1.49 | 67% |
| 6 | 32 | 6–127 | 28,000 | 1.40 | 72% |

Month 1 covers a partial calendar month. The first full month is month 2, with a median of USD 88 in this sample. Month 6 has a median of USD 32. These are not expected earnings for an arbitrary new channel. Also, medians for views, revenue and RPM are computed separately; multiplying marginal medians need not reproduce the reported earnings median.

## By leading audience country

The source groups each channel by the country contributing its largest share of views over the six months. Reported values are medians for months 2–6.

| Top viewer country | Channels | Median earnings USD/month | Earnings per 1,000 views USD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| United States | 70 | 106 | 3.46 |
| Mexico | 15 | 41 | 1.14 |
| Ukraine | 28 | 34 | 1.39 |
| Azerbaijan | 28 | 6 | 0.17 |
| India | 13 | 5 | 0.63 |

These are **viewer country**, not a setting a publisher can simply choose. Small country samples cannot establish a stable geographic pay rate.

Education and Science (10 channels) and Lifestyle (11 channels) are especially small groups. This is an observational comparison of selected monetized channels, not evidence that switching genres will cause a particular income.

## Implications and unanswered questions

The study provides direct examples of earnings volatility and how creator output, viewer geography and niche can coincide with different results. It does **not** include all channels, nonmonetized creators, reliably measured production costs or the full off-platform revenue mix. Its 24% six-month no-payout finding applies only to this cohort.

The independent [Patreon-linked study](creator-income-evidence.md) measures a **different revenue channel and sample**. Neither estimate should be pooled with the other to produce a false average.
