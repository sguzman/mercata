# Creator earnings evidence: Patreon accounts affiliated with YouTube

**Study:** Ilan Strauss, Jangho Yang, Mariana Mazzucato, [Rich-get-richer? Platform attention and earnings inequality using Patreon earnings data](https://doi.org/10.1093/icc/dtag044), *Industrial and Corporate Change* (2026).

This is a study of **monthly Patreon earnings** among Patreon creators linked exclusively to YouTube as an external social platform. It does **not** estimate what an average YouTuber earns from YouTube advertising or all income sources.

## Observed distribution

| Statistic | Monthly USD |
|---|---:|
| Sample observations | 23,435 |
| Mean | $250 |
| Median | $47 |
| 25th percentile | $28 |
| 75th percentile | $119 |
| Standard deviation | $1,490 |

The authors examined Patreon creator earnings in March of **2018, 2021 and 2024**. Their analysis includes only creators with more than $10 of reported or imputed monthly Patreon earnings and only one affiliated social platform. Missing Patreon earnings were partly imputed, reflecting creators' ability to conceal income.

The $47 median excludes YouTube channels without Patreon earnings and all accounts below the study threshold. It also excludes income earned directly from YouTube or through other channels. Therefore **it cannot answer Mercata's main question about the median earnings of YouTube creators as a whole**.

## What can be inferred

Within this selected paid-support sample, the mean is much higher than the median, consistent with a very uneven observed distribution. The measured dispersion is a useful warning against forecasting from highly successful creators. It does not establish causal effects of YouTube's algorithm or a reliable earnings projection for a new channel.

The research gaps remain native YouTube payout distributions, zero earners, channel-entry cohorts, geographic rates, genre-specific payouts, costs, hours and profit. [Machine dataset](creator-income-evidence.json) preserves population, time, units and limitations separately from [actual platform contract rules](policies.json).

No invented earnings estimates or private individual information have been added.
