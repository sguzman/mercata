# Independent channel-index comparison: CreatorDB and vidIQ are not censuses

**June/July 2026 source snapshots, reviewed October 24, 2026.** [Machine evidence register](creator-index-comparison-2026.json) and [tests](../../tests/creator-index-comparison.test.mjs).

[CreatorDB's June 27 report](https://creatordb.app/research/state-of-the-creator-economy/) analyzed **2,153,140 channels in its index with at least 1,000 subscribers**, including **601,232 indexed channels with at least 100,000 subscribers**. By comparison, the separate [vidIQ July 20 study](channel-cohort-observations-2026.md) indexed **61,186,246 channels with 1+ subscribers**, with **24,813,187 at 1,000+** and **792,376 at 100,000+**.

These are **different vendor-covered populations**, with unknown channel overlap and different dates and indexing rules. The disparity is not evidence that channels suddenly disappeared or that one researcher measured all YouTube channels. CreatorDB claims approximately complete coverage of the largest tiers, but that assertion was not independently audited in Mercata.

## Established channels' engagement and 30-day subscriber changes

| CreatorDB tracked size | Engagement measurement count | Median engagement (active followers) | Grew more than 10% in 30 days | Any positive growth |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10k–100k subscribers | 1,104,357 | 3.2% | 0.89% | 37.1% |
| 100k–1M subscribers | 523,619 | 3.1% | 0.81% | 38.0% |
| 1M+ subscribers | 76,736 | 2.5% | 0.50% | 36.3% |

The reported **median 30-day subscriber growth was approximately 0.7%** for each displayed tier. Engagement is CreatorDB's proprietary activity per *active followers*, not a fraction of all public subscribers, distinct fans or end-of-video completions. A single 30-day snapshot is **not** a birth-cohort trajectory and does not predict a new channel's growth or pay.

## Primary category counts within one vendor index

Selected broad primary categories were People & Blogs **322,869 channels**, Entertainment **288,977**, Gaming **222,645**, Education **198,440**, Music **196,340**, and Film & Animation **67,722**. These counts are **channels classified into broad CreatorDB categories**, not video uploads, active sellers with income, unique audiences or evidence of demand. They cannot be divided into watch-time by genre from an unrelated supplier to infer profitability.

For indexed 100k+ channels with country data, the provider reports United States **109,575**, India **103,548**, Brazil **29,495**, and Mexico **10,235** channels. Geography is a channel-classification count, not creator household residence or audience location.

**Remaining unknown:** 0-subscriber and sub-1000 coverage, exact matching with vidIQ channels, measured viewer-to-creator ratios, advertising/patron revenue distributions by channel tier and the net cost of production.
