# Seasonal discovery: observed windows are not automatically seasonal lift

**As-of October 24, 2026.** [Machine study](seasonal-discovery-comparison.json). This note compares five previously registered evidence examples using their own populations, periods and measured units. It is not a universal seasonal uploading strategy.

## A measured within-year partition

In its [February 5, 2026 NFL report](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/nfl-youtube-culture-trends-report/), YouTube says **over 30%** of global 2025 NFL-related viewing occurred between the end of the Super Bowl and start of the regular season. This is a useful **offseason share of same-year NFL-related views**, not 30% growth, 30% of distinct fans, a 30% creator profit margin, or evidence that the offseason *causes* an increase. The full month-by-month comparison and previous-years' offseason percentages remain unknown.

## Four importantly different published observations

| Source and time window | Bounded observation | Why it is not comparable seasonal lift |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Prom and GRWM](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/prom-youtube/), **all 2024** | More than **25 million** global video views of titles containing both words | A full-year title-filtered total, not a prom-week versus off-season comparison |
| [Halloween makeup](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/halloween-makeup/), **January 1–October 5, 2025** | More than **1 billion** global views of videos with both words in the title | Partial year, not total Halloween-month or all-year views, and not the same query as prom |
| [Splatoon 2 launch](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/gaming-quiz/), **July 21–28, 2017, Japan** | More than **50 million** game-related views over one release week | Single game-launch event, not repeating annual seasonality or worldwide views |

The underlying observations are already recorded independently in [NFL](nfl-view-seasonality-2026.md), [fashion queries](beauty-fashion-query-series.md), [game releases](gaming-release-events.md), and [children's media](children-media-seasonality.md). This comparison does not treat another page referring to the same underlying source as a new independent study.

## Method for a defensible seasonal discovery estimate

An actual seasonal uplift requires identical topic inclusion rules and video format, repeat monthly or weekly periods across **multiple complete calendar years**, consistent countries and viewer measurement populations, disclosed missing months, and a contemporaneous comparison category. Account for special events, new-video supply, language, school calendars, search indexing, and the **August 24, 2026 view definition break**. Use equal-length windows or explicitly normalize differing numbers of days.

Creator revenue adds still more conditions: monetization approval, advertiser market, costs, audience geography, and time worked. Viral view counts cannot yield a median payout, profit, or growth rate for new entrants.

**Present results:** one source-reported NFL offseason share, four other bounded temporal facts; no verified cross-category or recurrent seasonal lift, country-specific holiday effect, optimal upload month, or income seasonality. These fields remain `null` in the machine record. All publication gates for a repeatable predictive seasonality claim remain open.
