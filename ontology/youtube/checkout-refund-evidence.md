# YouTube buyer prices, payment paths and refund responsibility

**Reviewed October 24, 2026.** [Machine-readable buyer transaction audit](checkout-refund-evidence.json). This is a source-backed **transaction policy comparison**, not a sample of what real channels charge or proof of typical creator revenue.

## Three different kinds of checkout

| Product | Who runs checkout or fulfillment | Cancellation and refund boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Channel membership | YouTube or applicable purchasing platform, depending on device and enrollment | [Membership refund guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12016111?hl=en): cancel any time; access generally continues through billing cycle; no partial-period refund. Faults/unauthorized charges have support procedures, and Apple-billed purchases follow Apple policy. |
| Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks | YouTube-supported purchase routes (platform billing can vary) | [YouTube's policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12017323?hl=en) describes these as voluntary, non-refundable payments; unauthorized charges can be reported. |
| Shopping completed on a retailer or marketplace website | The **outside seller** sets total price, takes payment and handles fulfillment | [Shopping buyer policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12257787?hl=en): external retailer/marketplace is responsible for order support, returns and refunds; do not attribute retailer disputes to YouTube's own membership policy. |

These are **not** one uniform marketplace order system. An advertisement-funded view has an advertiser as payer, not a viewer checkout event, and creator sponsorship contracts require separate study.

## Published country membership price options, not sampled seller prices

YouTube's [US](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10119895?hl=en) and [Mexico](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10120159?hl=en) schedules list these illustrative *new-pricing options*:

| Listed USD tier | US schedule (USD) | Mexico schedule (MXN) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $0.99 | $0.99 | $19 |
| $4.99 | $4.99 | $69 |
| $9.99 | $9.99 | $219 |

These are official country **menu values** as checked on October 24, 2026, **not an exchange-rate calculation, current offer for a sampled channel, creator's net revenue, currency conversion, actual purchase price, or average customer spend**. YouTube says a price rollout began in May 2025 and an existing member's vintage can matter. The [tier configuration guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7544492?hl=en) permits up to six membership levels; [pricing guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15085977?hl=en) notes country differences and staggered price changes.

## What remains to measure

A repeatable real-offer sample needs the creator/channel, country, device, sign-up vintage, offered member level, checkout provider, date and actual displayed price. A transaction-outcome sample needs legal payment authorization, charge and refund dates, processor, tax deductions, reversal outcomes, merchant receipts, seller cash proceeds, and time spent managing disputes. Neither sample has been obtained.

**Unknown:** YouTube-wide median paid tier, distribution of creator-set prices, proportion of eligible viewers who buy, Supers refund incidence, external-retailer chargeback rates, creator margin after costs, and typical net profit. Zero observed transactions in this dataset means **no sample was collected**, not that creators earn zero.
