# Language market change: compatible panels versus mismatched proxies

**October 25, 2026.** [Machine study](language-market-comparability-2026.json), [compatibility audit](../../site/language-market-compatibility.mjs).

YouTube's [multilingual product data](multilingual-distribution.md) records **over 25% of watched minutes** from non-primary-language audio among *selected existing MLA creators* as of July 2025, and **27 supported auto-dubbing languages** in a May 2026 feature announcement. The first is a share of selected watch time, the second is a count of supported product languages; neither measures how many unique viewers speak a language in a specific country or a year-on-year demand increase.

Separately, [DataReportal's four 2025 country advertiser-planner readings](country-ad-reach-comparisons.md) describe addressable ad audiences in India, Indonesia, Japan and the Philippines, with selected planner-reported time changes. **Country is not language**, ad reach is not a person census, and planner denominator revisions prevent interpreting unrelated editions as true viewing growth.

For a **genuine normalized YouTube Search comparison**, preserve search query, country, language, service, category, definition version, time-window granularity and **joint normalization comparison panel**. The pure audit rejects separately normalized trend requests and mismatched countries. Even a compatible 0–100 index comparison yields only a **relative-index point change**, not absolute searches, unique viewers, annual creator income or causal localization effects.

No matched language-by-country-by-year viewer series, corrected comparable language-specific market volume, or verified incremental creator-profit data are present. All such values remain `null`.
