# YouTube country advertiser reach and reported changes

**Reviewed October 24, 2026.** [Machine data and caveats](country-ad-reach-comparisons.json). DataReportal's *Digital 2026* country reports were **published in late 2025**; these Google advertising-planner observations are from **October 2025**, not actual 2026 monthly-active-user readings.

| Country report | Oct 2025 advertiser reach | End-2024 to Oct-2025 reported change | July–Oct 2025 reported change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| [India](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-india) | 500 million | +9.0m (+1.8%) | -9.0m (-1.8%) |
| [Indonesia](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-indonesia) | 151 million | +8.0m (+5.6%) | +6.0m (+4.1%) |
| [Japan](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-japan) | 78.5 million | -0.2m (-0.3%) | +3.1m (+4.1%) |
| [Philippines](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-philippines) | 59.6 million | +1.9m (+3.3%) | +1.8m (+3.1%) |

**What changed:** the published audience *advertisers could potentially reach*, not necessarily people using YouTube. Ad planner revisions and periodic corrections can cause changes without actual viewer growth or loss. The source recommends using its internally calculated changes rather than subtracting numbers from older report editions. Do not add these four countries as a global unique user count.

The existing [audience scale crosswalk](audience-population-crosswalk.md) records separately sourced US, Mexico and Brazil ad reach plus surveys with different denominators. No verified national YouTube MAU, DAU, active creator census, actual buyer conversion or creator earnings data follow from these country figures.
