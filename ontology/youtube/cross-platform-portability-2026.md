# Creator switching and asset portability across video services

**Review date:** October 25, 2026. [Machine-readable crosswalk](cross-platform-portability-2026.json). This is a continuation of the [existing video comparison set](comparison-set.md) and [YouTube reputation/asset audit](reputation-portability.md); it does not admit any new marketplace specimens.

A creator can often move or copy **files** without moving a paying account, subscriber relationship, moderation history, recommendation position or monetization approval. Seller-exported customer records require separate privacy and marketing-consent restrictions.

| Service | Publicly documented route | Limits and unknowns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [youtube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/56100?hl=en) | Owner downloads eligible uploaded videos with Studio or Google Takeout. | Downloaded MP4 copies are not necessarily original editing projects or a complete channel archive. |
| [twitch](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/video-on-demand) | Enabled past broadcasts can be downloaded individually or exported directly to a linked YouTube account. | Past Broadcast storage must have been enabled before streaming; a past broadcast is not guaranteed to exist. |
| [tiktok](https://support.tiktok.com/en/account-and-privacy/personalized-ads-and-data/requesting-your-data) | Account holder may request an account data download through TikTok settings; specific video/audience transfer capability is not validated here. | The request workflow is not a documented platform-to-platform transfer mechanism. |
| [vimeo_ott](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/12427213530257-Managing-your-customers-on-Vimeo-OTT) | Authorized seller may export Customers and Transactions CSVs, including customer status and marketing opt-in fields. | A customer CSV is sensitive account data; this research records only the publicly documented schema, not real customers. |

## Retention clocks and customer rights

Twitch Help describes past-broadcast retention of **7 days** for ordinary broadcasters, **14 days** for Affiliates and **60 days** for Partners, Prime and Turbo users, with a **7-day** higher-quality/vertical variant in applicable dual-format cases. These are platform retention windows, not days of usable copyrighted footage or promised permanent storage. Recording must have been enabled and individual content downloaded or exported in time.

Vimeo OTT's [customer management Help](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/12427213530257-Managing-your-customers-on-Vimeo-OTT) documents seller-side Customers and Transactions CSV exports, including a *marketing opt-in status* field. That is different from a YouTube subscriber count and does not imply consent to unsolicited marketing or seamless transfer of subscriptions and app-store billing.

TikTok's [data request Help](https://support.tiktok.com/en/account-and-privacy/personalized-ads-and-data/requesting-your-data) documents requesting account data; file completeness and transfer to another video service remain unverified here. YouTube [uploaded media downloads](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/56100?hl=en) do not include recommendation history or a guaranteed portable subscriber list.

## Missing economic evidence

There are no matched creator records of cross-platform switching hours, abandonment, cost, subscriber conversion, lost earnings or representative net profit. The crosswalk therefore supports a **switching-cost research design**, not an income ranking or a claim that some host is universally best.

Only first-party, public feature descriptions were used. No real customer records, account credentials or private analytics were accessed.
