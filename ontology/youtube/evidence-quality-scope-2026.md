# Evidence quality depends on the claim, not a universal source score

**Review date: October 25, 2026.** [Machine-reviewed selected evidence](evidence-quality-scope-2026.json) and [regression audit](../../site/evidence-quality-audit.mjs). This crosswalk covers **8 deliberately different sources** out of the existing register of **387**; it is not a comprehensive recertification or a fresh full-text verification.

First-party YouTube documentation is authoritative about the platform's published **rules as retrieved**; an independent, properly scoped survey is preferable for measured usage within its sampling population. A vendor's channel index is evidence for the *vendor-covered channels*, not unique YouTube people or creator profits. An abstract can support what the abstract states but not unexamined methods. A first-person work diary can support that person's effort but not the typical creator.

## Six separate provenance axes

- **provenance:** originating organization and whether reporting its own platform/product.
- **sampling:** sampling frame, recruitment, inclusion/exclusions, nonresponse, weights and respondent unit where a population estimate is asserted.
- **denominator:** exact measured unit, country and observation period, including event versus person versus account.
- **independence:** first-hand data generation versus sponsor-selected publication, replicated by another dataset only after actual cross-source matching.
- **research access:** abstract-only, aggregate release, complete published method or verified underlying records.
- **applicability:** specific claim the document does and does not support, including policy snapshot versus income or causality.

Publication date and document retrieval date are different fields; undated, changing help pages cannot be treated as permanent program rules. Statistical observations also need selection frame, respondent or channel unit, time, geography and missing-data disclosure. Studies with the same respondents but different syndicated URLs are **not independent replications**.

The existing [normalized source-family audit](../../scripts/source-provenance.mjs) tracks repeated URLs and shared survey families. The [publication-rights policy](../../scripts/publication-rights.mjs) checks source metadata and asset declarations. This register adds **use-specific inference limits**, without replacing either test or claiming actual copyright clearance, scientific replication or all-source manual verification.

All individual creator profitability, representative worldwide creator-income distribution and missing full-text access remain unmeasured.
