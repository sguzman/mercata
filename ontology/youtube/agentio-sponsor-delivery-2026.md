# Paid sponsor integrations: actual long-tail viewing, clicks and repeated deals

**Research checked:** 2026-10-24. Original report: [Agentio, 6 March 2026](https://www.agentio.com/blog/how-agentio-turns-youtube-sponsorships-into-a-scalable-media-channel). Separate [EMARKETER methodology summary, 25 February 2026](https://www.emarketer.com/content/sticking-with-same-youtube-creator-nearly-doubles-affiliate-link-ctr) documents the January 2026 vendor playbook frame. **These two links describe the same underlying provider dataset, not two independent studies.**

## Published sample

The sponsor marketplace provider analyzed **more than 10,000 paid YouTube creator integrations placed through its own platform**, from **October 2023 to December 2025**, predominantly in the U.S. The marketing placements are brand messages embedded in creator video content, not all YouTube pre-roll ads or a random sample of creators. EMARKETER reports the vendor sample's creators as approximately **92% U.S., 5% Canada, 2% U.K. and less than 1% Australia**; these are rounded coverage descriptions, not a platform demographic census.

| Published vendor measurement | Result | Precise interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Views after day 30 | 38% | Share of measured sponsored-video view events after publication day 30 |
| Views after day 90 | 27% | Share after day 90, already contained in views after day 30 |
| Clicks after day 30 | Approximately 30% | Share of eligible tracked sponsor-link click events, not buyer conversions |
| Eighth repeat deal median CTR | 1.8× first | Conditional repeat-sponsorship pairs selected into eight deals |
| Sixth repeat deal conversion rate | 1.9× first | Relative rate for repeat pairs, with absolute base and verification gaps |
| Cumulative advertiser CPM change after a year | 54% decrease | Vendor-selected continuing brand campaign spending, not creator wages |

The 27% share after day 90 is **a subset of** the 38% after day 30; summing them is double-counting. View events and click events use different total denominators, so the 38% and 30% figures **must not be divided to produce a click-through rate**. Likewise the relative 1.8× and 1.9× multipliers do not disclose absolute paid order rates.

## Limits for creator economics

Agentio earns from and promotes the sponsorship system it measures; creators opt into a selected paying marketplace. Repeated sponsor-creator pairs and one-year continuous advertisers are selected survivors, likely unlike new creators with no accepted sponsorships. A higher observed CTR at an eighth collaboration may reflect prior contract selection, channel/topic changes and other non-random differences, not a causal advantage guaranteed by repeating integrations. Advertiser cumulative CPM may improve due to older views being counted, contract terms or new campaigns; it is **not** creator net profit or a verified change in creator fees.

The study provides a real observational comparison of **view delivery, sponsored clicks, advertiser response and retention in a paid channel** for the existing discovery and buyer-funnel tasks. It supplies **no representative zero-earner percentage, ordinary creator sponsor pay, net profit or general conversion-to-buyer probability**. No external advertiser accounts, viewers or buyer histories have been accessed.
