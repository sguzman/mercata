# Shorts-to-long-form referral: a measurable hypothesis, not a proven funnel

**Reviewed October 24, 2026.** [Machine research protocol](shorts-related-video-funnel.json). The study defines the conditions for measuring whether a Short actually leads people to another video, without inferring a journey from unrelated aggregate totals.

## Official link mechanism

[YouTube's related-video instructions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en) permit a creator to select a related Video, Short or Live item from the same channel. The clickable related-video link appears in the Shorts player beneath the channel handle. [Advanced feature access](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9890437?hl=en) is required; the selected video must be public or unlisted and obey Community Guidelines. This is a **technical availability path**, not a sampled viewer journey or proof the channel's account currently qualifies.

## Four different measurement levels

| Surface | Unit and source | Unsupported inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source Short | Views, engaged views or watch events in a named period | All viewers saw the link |
| Related video link | Eligible owner-selected connection in Shorts player | Everyone clicked; one exposure = one person |
| Destination video | Watch-page video view and assorted traffic-source reports | Every destination viewer arrived from that Short |
| Format-overlap report | Estimated viewer overlap across Shorts, videos and Live | Overlap proves directional sequence or referral |

[YouTube Studio Content analytics](https://support.google.com/youtubecreatorstudio/answer/12220281?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en) provides separate format-specific reports and a *Viewers across formats* report. Aggregate audience overlap cannot identify which upload preceded which other upload. Since August 24, 2026, YouTube has used playback-start counts across formats, while eligibility/revenue retain engaged/qualified metrics. Never combine one event-based numerator with an estimated-unique-people denominator and call the result a funnel probability.

## Attribution and economics acceptance gates

1. Verify the owner's advanced access and effective linked Short, target and dated observation window.
2. Obtain owner-authorized referral evidence that genuinely binds the **named Short** to the named **destination video**; if the analytics product does not expose the required link-level event data, record that limitation rather than reconstructing user journeys.
3. Preserve unit, epoch, device/geography boundaries, overlapping viewers and repeats. If link clicks cannot be counted from documented evidence, the click-through rate is unknown.
4. Design a preregistered comparison (for example, legally permitted randomized link assignment or appropriate matched control) that controls other upload promotions, search, seasonal release time, subscription notifications and external traffic.
5. Reconcile destination-specific watch value with the labor/cash costs of making and promoting both videos. Additional views are not necessarily incremental net creator earnings.

An unrelated 1,000 Short plays and 200 target-video views do **not** produce a 20% link-conversion rate. Even reported same-channel Shorts/video audience overlap only establishes common viewing, not that the Short caused the video view.

**Unknown:** total eligible link impressions, actual clicks, link-specific destination arrivals, deduplicated viewers who traversed the link, valid incremental watch hours, revenue lift, profit and first-time creator performance. No owner data were collected; no experiment has been run.
