# Dated policy and measurement changes are not algorithm updates

**Compiled October 24, 2026.** [Machine chronology](policy-measurement-change-crosswalk.json). This document consolidates existing independently cited rule and measurement changes without pretending to observe YouTube ranking weights.

- **October 15, 2024:** Standard channels' square/vertical videos up to three minutes uploaded on or after this date qualify as Shorts under the documented [classification rule](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en). This is *format eligibility*, not a guaranteed recommendation.
- **July 15, 2025:** YPP's *repetitious* content rule was renamed *inauthentic*, clarified as a restriction on mass-produced/repetitive content. It was *not* a blanket ban on AI videos and no causal viewer-ranking effect was observed.
- **July 2026:** YouTube began a gradual Analytics/Trends interface rollout. The [Trends guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11962757?hl=en) supplies the **month**, not one rollout date for every creator.
- **August 24, 2026:** The [public API video view definition](https://developers.google.com/youtube/data/reference/rest/v3/videos) changed to playback starts across formats. This is a **measurement discontinuity**, not by itself observed unique audience growth or increased payout.
- **January, March, August, September and October 2026:** YouTube's [advertiser suitability policy history](advertiser-policy-history.md) documents six changes. The original official updates usually specify only a *month*, so no exact effective day is invented.

The chronology distinguishes the audience/format classification, creator product eligibility, analytics presentation, public metric definition, and ad suitability. None of these source events discloses a ranking weight, paid creator before/after panel or a causal earnings effect.

**Unknown:** the size, dates and treatment effects of recommendation-ranking coefficient changes; creator-level realized payout effects; and like-for-like pre/post viewer measures for all channels.
