# YouTube marketplace: evidence-backed interim research synthesis

**Status as of October 24, 2026: an incomplete study, not an income forecast.** This report summarizes what is supported by cited primary/operator sources, independent surveys and scoped creator samples, without merging their denominators. The [machine-readable synthesis](research-synthesis-2026.json) records source IDs, relevant research artifacts, open evidence gates and explicit unknowns.

## What is measurable, and what is not

**Audience:** Pew's [2026 U.S. adult survey](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/fact-sheet/social-media/) reports self-reported platform usage for a defined U.S. adult sampling frame. [DataReportal/GWI's global platform-use report](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mid-year-global-update-report) covers surveyed internet users in selected economies, under different age and sampling definitions. These cannot be multiplied into an accurate worldwide unique-person census, nor can separate niche totals be added without overlap measurement. A creator's [Studio unique viewers](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7577916?hl=en) are owner-facing modeled numbers, not publicly available competitor or global user records. The [viewer/person metric register](viewer-person-metrics.md) distinguishes views, subscriptions and people.

**Platform supply:** [YouTube Press](https://blog.youtube/press/) reports more than 20 million uploaded videos daily. These are upload objects, not independent creators, accounts, economically viable channels, videos that remained monetized or people who viewed them. The [platform-scale study](platform-scale.json) keeps those separate units.

**Ways to earn and barriers:** The [16-pathway inventory](revenue-pathways.json) separates YouTube advertising/Premium, Shorts allocation, fan funding, shopping and off-platform contracts. A published channel eligibility rule is not actual approval or payout. YouTube announced changes effective **February 1, 2027**; the [transition record](ypp-transition-2027.md) explicitly distinguishes those *future* rules from the **October 2026 current** application and grandfathering cohorts. Live revenue shares and account-specific financial outcomes cannot be inferred from viewer totals.

**What creators earn:** A [2026 commercial CreatorIQ survey](https://www.creatoriq.com/whitepaper/state-of-creators) surveyed **5,095 cross-platform creators**, not a representative YouTube entry cohort. Other documented Patreon and already-monetized small-channel samples also omit nonmonetized people or measure off-platform receipts. Accordingly Mercata does **not** state a typical new YouTube creator salary, a representative zero-earner rate, median creator net profit or verified hourly payout. The [new-creator cohort protocol](newcomer-income-cohort.md) defines what those estimates require. Production labor and cash expenses remain separately unmeasured for the general entrant population.

**Discovery and time:** In its [performance FAQ](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805?hl=en), YouTube says ordinary upload timing is not known to influence long-term viewership, while simultaneous **Live and Premiere** timing matters for participation. The [publishing-time study](geographic-publishing-time.md) and DST-safe validator distinguish a chosen calendar time from a verified UTC instant; they do not invent an optimal slot. For seasonal discovery, [YouTube's NFL report](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/nfl-youtube-culture-trends-report/) reports **over 30% of 2025 NFL-related views during the offseason**. That is a same-year viewing share, *not* a causal seasonal lift or profitable upload calendar. The [seasonal comparison](seasonal-discovery-comparison.md) refuses to pool it with different full-year, partial-year, game launch, and child viewing units.

**Risks:** YouTube's [September 2026 disclosure-controls update](https://support.google.com/youtube/blog/458476782/giving-you-tools-to-easily-disclose-paid-partnerships-with-new-controls-automated-disclosures) and [October 21 security-pause update](https://support.google.com/youtube/blog/473851650/introducing-temporary-feature-pause-for-hacked-accounts) document product mechanisms and safeguards. A platform label alone does not satisfy all possible sponsorship legal requirements. A temporarily paused channel is not necessarily terminated; the support review is not guaranteed at a fixed duration. No valid aggregate incidence of hacking, sponsorship compliance failure, enforcement, lost creator earnings or brand profits is established.

**Discovery concentration:** The [owner analytics traffic-source taxonomy](traffic-concentration.md) separates search, browsing, suggested feeds, Shorts and direct/unknown events; their shares could measure a channel's dependency only after a complete matching report is available with consent. There is no representative all-entrant distribution of discovery-source concentration or profit from it.

## Evidence quality and population boundaries

| Evidence class | Suitable published use | What not to infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current first-party policy and product documentation | Eligibility requirements, application steps, reporting mechanisms and dated terms | Individual approval, actual income, guaranteed support results |
| Independent population survey with a defined frame | Conditional reported usage within the survey's country, age range, question and period | A complete global person census, child use, creator earnings |
| Commercial / vendor creator respondent survey | Bounded descriptions of people who actually answered | A probability-based all-newcomer YouTube salary, profit or zero-earner rate |
| Platform-reported view and upload aggregates | Dated activity of a defined content sample or title predicate | Distinct fans, creator population, audience overlap, causal lift or guaranteed sales |

Some official metrics are themselves privacy-limited or modeled. A numeric value is not better evidence merely because it is large.

## What must still be established for full research acceptance

1. **Income:** a defensibly recruited first-entrant cohort with non-earning, nonmonetized and inactive channels; measured gross receipts, costs, labor and missingness, by geography/format.
2. **People:** country and niche sampling with matching people units, dates, age coverage and overlap methods. Public views and advertiser reach alone cannot do this.
3. **Trends:** repeated equivalent topic samples across years and metric definition changes, not rankings or annual endpoints presented as complete time series.
4. **Cash flow:** independently verifiable approval rates, payment timing, feature-specific access, operating costs and after-cost returns; avoid predicting any individual's income.
5. **Policy currency:** recheck operative eligibility, tax and product policies when later decisions occur, especially announced **2027** terms.

For full reproducibility the [source provenance audit](source-provenance.md), [main research plan](../../plans/youtube.json), and [public source dossier](../../specimens/youtube.json) retain original source references, workstream status and acceptance evidence.

**Conclusion:** There is substantial evidence about YouTube's mechanics, use and selected cultural activity, but the general creator entry-to-profit distribution remains **unknown**. Mercata's progress ledger measures completion of research tasks, **not** the percent chance of creator success, and no unfinished empirical gate is counted as a successful population result.
