# U.S. YouTube use, 2026 — Pew Research Center

Source: [Americans' Use of Social Media in 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/fact-sheet/social-media/), published October 19, 2026. Survey of 5,511 U.S. adults January 30–June 18, 2026 by web, mail and telephone; overall margin of error ±1.8 percentage points. Subgroup error margins may differ.

**84%** of U.S. adults said they ever use YouTube. This is NOT a measure of global users, monthly active use or YouTube's audience composition.

Every percentage in the following tables is **the proportion of the named subgroup who report using YouTube**. These percentages must not be added to 100% or read as the makeup of the platform's audience.
