# Self-reported AI production changes: 384 U.S. creators, cross-platform

**Source:** [Adobe Express video creator survey, published 25 March 2026](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/ai-video-tools). A commercial creative software company surveyed **384 U.S. video creators through Prolific during December 1–5, 2025**. The study covers people creating for YouTube and other social networks; **86% worked solo**, 11% in teams of two or three, and 3% in teams of four or more.

## What respondents actually said

| Published response | Value | Denominator or measurement limit |
|---|---:|---|
| Had tried AI video generation or editing | 71% | Selected U.S. cross-platform respondents |
| Used AI tools weekly | 41% | **Among AI-tool users**, not the full sample |
| YouTube-posting respondents using AI | 74% | YouTube-posting subgroup, possibly also on other platforms |
| Said AI saves more than 30 minutes per video | 56% | Self-reported time savings, not stopwatch-verified |
| Said AI saves more than four hours per video | 10% | A nested subset of the over-30-minute category |
| Reported higher watch time | 19% | Self-report, not a randomized causal comparison |
| Reported higher engagement | 17% | Self-report, not a measured YouTube-only effect |
| Planned to increase investment in AI tools | 50% | Intention, not actual current spend or income |

The vendor also reports **24%** of respondents who had *previously completed brand deals* said they obtained more sponsorships after adopting AI. That statement is conditional on prior brand-deal experience, not a buyer-conversion rate or the probability an ordinary new creator will be paid.

## What this does not measure

This is a vendor-selected, retrospectively self-reported sample of **cross-platform video creators**, with no independently matched production records, before/after trial, random assignment, actual receipts or software invoices. Percentages within a subgroup cannot be multiplied by or casually combined with other source populations. The 56% and 10% thresholds overlap; adding them double-counts the latter.

The [one photography creator's 61-video time diary](photography-production-time-diary-2025.md) is a different creator, method, period and population. Subtracting vendor-reported time savings from that diary's 9.74-hour mean would manufacture a result that neither research source observed.

No minimum paid AI software requirement, net production cost decrease, net creator profit, marginal monetization ROI, or representative creator labor improvement can be estimated. The information is useful for defining what to measure in the existing creator labor and operating-cost research, not for promising productivity or revenue.
