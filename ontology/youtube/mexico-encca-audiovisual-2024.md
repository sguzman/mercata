# Mexico ENCCA 2024: conditional YouTube use versus online audiovisual consumption

**Primary source:** [IFT's December 9, 2024 national survey summary](https://www.ift.org.mx/sites/default/files/comunicacion-y-medios/comunicados-ift/comunicado120ift_0.pdf). [Machine crosswalk](mexico-encca-audiovisual-2024.json) and [regression tests](../../tests/mexico-encca-audiovisual.test.mjs).

Mexico's ENCCA 2024 reports 8,750 respondents, including 6,125 urban and 2,625 rural. Among the survey respondents, 55% consumed audiovisual content over the internet. **Among the subgroup consuming online audiovisual content, 66% named YouTube** as a used service. These figures do not share the same denominator.

The same conditional online-audiovisual group reported Netflix 32%, TikTok 29%, Facebook 29%, and Disney+ 13%. Respondents can name more than one platform. Thus these are not exclusive shares of views, advertising expenditure, buyer orders or platform revenue.

The regulator cites 26% using online audiovisual services in 2015 versus 55% in 2024, a descriptive 29-point difference across survey editions. This does not establish a YouTube-specific historical growth rate; survey questionnaire and sampling changes across nine years have not been harmonized.

**Unmeasured:** YouTube monthly/daily unique people in Mexico, the exact question-specific sample n, paid users, channel operators, YouTube creator earnings, and an equivalent series comparable with late-2025 advertising reach. No individual questionnaire responses were obtained.
