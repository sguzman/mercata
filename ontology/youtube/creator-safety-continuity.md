# Creator safety, channel recovery and October 2026 security pauses

**Research observation: October 23, 2026.** [Machine model](creator-safety-continuity.json). This file records official YouTube product controls and complaint procedures, **not** the incidence of harassment, hacking, successful recovery or psychological harm.

## The October 21 security-pause announcement

The [TeamYouTube first-party announcement dated October 21, 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/blog/473851650/introducing-temporary-feature-pause-for-hacked-accounts) describes automatic **temporary restrictions** on channels showing suspicious or unauthorized activity. Restricted operations can include publishing or changing videos/Shorts, starting livestreams, changing playlists or thumbnails, and changing private video visibility. During this security pause, YouTube says **existing video viewing and channel interaction remain available**, and the **channel's monetization status is not impacted by the pause itself**.

The announcement says the security review is **typically within 24 hours**. This is a reported typical period, **not a guaranteed deadline, an average over observed cases, or a measured recovery success rate**. An account can be flagged without proof that a hijack occurred; restoration and cleanup depend on case review. No public frequency, false-positive rate or distribution of actual review duration is established.

This temporary restriction differs from a copyright strike, a Community Guidelines penalty and a permanent channel termination. The consequences must not be merged in a single suspension-rate statistic.

## Channel takeover and prevention

[YouTube's hacked-channel recovery guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/76187?hl=en) says that a compromised YouTube channel entails a compromised associated Google Account. Its sequence is **recover and secure the Google Account**, revert unauthorized YouTube changes, and reduce repeat access risk. The help page warns that potential hacking support is limited to incidents within **nine months**, due to data retention; that window is not a promise of successful recovery.

[Channel security guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9701986?hl=en) covers malware scanning, passkeys and two-step verification, and an account-recovery plan. [YouTube's delegated role controls](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9481328?hl=en) allow channel work without sharing account passwords, but individual operations and delegation permissions vary by role.

## Moderation and privacy are different surfaces

| Surface | Documented options | Key limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Live chat](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9826490?hl=en) | Participation limits, blocked words, automatic hold settings, slow mode, trusted moderators, hiding users and timeouts | Actual abuse incidence and filter effectiveness are not measured |
| [Harassment policy and reporting](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802268?hl=en) | Report some threats, non-public personal-information exposure, impersonation and coordinated abuse | Not every unpleasant exchange necessarily violates policy, and a report does not guarantee removal |
| [Privacy and likeness complaints](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801895?hl=en) | Privacy complaint review and requests involving certain realistic synthetic depictions of a person | Eligibility, outcomes and timing are case-dependent |
| Account recovery and roles | Google Account recovery, channel cleanup, role-scoped permissions, device security | Account recovery cannot be replaced by simply moderating a public comment |

YouTube's harassment policy is stricter in several respects when minors are targeted. This project neither identifies individual targets nor mines user-level reports or complaints. A content safety study does not require collecting private account credentials, screenshots of victims, personally identifying records or moderation history.

## Unmeasured economic consequences

Security interruptions can block publishing and alter production schedules, but the source does **not** supply a distribution of monetization losses or downtime. A site continuing to show existing videos does not prove unaffected viewer traffic, ad income or paid subscriptions. Representative moderation work hours, security incident rates, creator losses and recovery success remain **unknown** in the machine model.

Before measuring this risk empirically, define account population, observation period, region, surface, incident type, published help basis and lawful, aggregate sampling methodology. Never convert a typical support time or an announced feature into an observed probability. See the [channel ownership and continuity study](channel-ownership-continuity.md) for delegation and export limitations.
