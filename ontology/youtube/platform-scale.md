# YouTube platform scale: four different measurement types

**Research snapshot 2026-10-23.** [Machine observations](platform-scale.json).

| Measure | Reported value | Denominator |
|---|---:|---|
| GWI past-month use, global covered sample including China and Russia | **55.3%** | Surveyed internet users aged 16+ in covered countries |
| GWI past-month use, covered sample excluding China and Russia | **70.6%** | Surveyed internet users aged 16+ after excluding those countries |
| YouTube videos uploaded each day | **More than 20 million** | Uploaded video events, global platform daily average |
| YouTube Shorts views each day | **More than 200 billion** | Shorts view events, global daily average |

GWI's percentage figures are described in [DataReportal's Digital 2026 Mid-Year Global Update](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mid-year-global-update-report). They represent self-reported use within the past month among internet users aged 16 and above, not a platform-verified headcount. The 70.6% figure uses a restricted geographic denominator and **does not** mean that 70.6% of YouTube viewers live outside China and Russia.

The upload and Shorts volume are headline statistics from [YouTube's official press page](https://blog.youtube/press/). That page does not publish an exact daily measurement window for either claim. Video uploads are not active creators. Shorts views are not engaged views for advertiser revenue, distinct people or monetized playbacks. The public view metric changed in August 2026, so comparisons with prior averages require definition checks.

## What these data do *not* say

None of these numbers measures how many unique people use YouTube worldwide, how many subscribers a typical channel has, the active supply in beauty or gaming, the proportion of viewers in any niche, or how much a channel can earn. The valid platform-scale answer is multiple scoped observations; the exact globally deduplicated YouTube user count remains **unknown** from the current public evidence.

For country-specific advertising reach, see [the separate country estimates](ad-reach-2025.md). For U.S. adult ever-use prevalence and survey history, see [Pew's research](us-audience-2026.md). These are independent datasets with different age ranges and periods.
