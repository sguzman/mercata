# YouTube discovery and revenue measurement

YouTube Search considers relevance, engagement and quality, including the relationship between a query and a video's title, description, tags and content. The full ranking weights are not public. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438?hl=en (checked 2026-10-23).

YouTube recommendations have several distinct surfaces: Home, Up Next, the Shorts Feed, destination pages and some channel-page shelves. Recommendations may use viewing history, search history, subscriptions, likes, dislikes and direct feedback. These are not interchangeable with search results. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16089387?hl=en (checked 2026-10-23).

Creator RPM and advertiser CPM use different denominators and revenue bases. RPM concerns creator revenue per thousand counted video views or applicable engaged views after revenue sharing; CPM concerns advertiser-side ad impressions. Neither figure is a fixed promise of future earnings. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314357?hl=en (checked 2026-10-23).

None of these descriptions establishes the size of a subculture, a new creator's chance of reaching an audience, or average creator income. These remain separate empirical research tasks.
